# D3 AML/CFT for digital assets at a bank

AML (anti-money laundering) and CFT (countering the financing of terrorism) rules exist to stop criminals using a bank to move or hide money. This module is for the Head of Compliance (digital assets) at a Hong Kong bank. After it, you can name the AML and CFT texts that actually bind the bank for virtual asset (VA) business. A virtual asset is a digital token such as a cryptocurrency. You can also point to where the rules written for virtual assets (VAs) sit. And you can tell which Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) material is only background. The same goes for Financial Action Task Force (FATF) material. Some of this material only sets expectations for the bank's counterparties. The FATF is the global standard-setter for anti-money laundering.

## In 60 seconds

The bank's AML rulebook for digital assets is the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) AML/CFT Guideline for Authorized Institutions (AIs). [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3; S:16fd7cfab136, Part III para 1] Authorized institutions are the banks and deposit-takers the HKMA supervises. The HKMA revised the Guideline in May 2023, and it took effect on 1 June 2023. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3; S:16fd7cfab136, Part III para 1] The bank follows this HKMA Guideline, not the SFC's guideline. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3; S:16fd7cfab136, Part III para 1]

The Guideline says little about VAs. It covers four points: [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1 fn 15, 8.4, 11.23]

- A HK$8,000 trigger for customer due diligence (CDD) on one-off VA transfers. CDD means checking who a customer is and what they do. The trigger applies to people with no business relationship with the bank. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1 fn 15, 8.4, 11.23]
- A footnote pointing to Schedule 2, section 13A of the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO). [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1 fn 15, 8.4, 11.23]
- Record keeping for five years. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1 fn 15, 8.4, 11.23]
- Correspondent-style checks on virtual asset service providers (VASPs) located outside Hong Kong. A VASP (such as a crypto exchange) offers VA services to others. Correspondent-style checks are the due diligence a bank does before serving another financial firm. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1 fn 15, 8.4, 11.23]

Other HKMA and SFC guidance calls the section 13A requirements the travel rule. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.1–6.2; S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.1–12.11.2] The travel rule means sender and recipient details go along with each VA transfer.

The HKMA's January 2022 circular sets further expectations. [S:642680fa4467, pp.3–5] It asks banks to:

- watch customers' flows to and from VA platforms; [S:642680fa4467, pp.3–5]
- assess the risk of each VASP customer; [S:642680fa4467, pp.3–5]
- talk to the HKMA before launching VA products or services. [S:642680fa4467, pp.3–5]

The SFC's AML guideline applies to licensed corporations (LCs) and SFC-licensed VASPs. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] LCs are firms licensed by the SFC. Chapter 12 of that guideline covers VAs. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] The bank's SFC-registered business is a registered institution (RI). An RI is a bank registered with the SFC to do securities business. The RI must take the SFC guideline into account. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1] The RI must comply with Chapter 12 when it handles client VA deposits or withdrawals. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]

Recent SFC circulars describe current money laundering patterns. [S:be46918f8aa2, paras 7–8; S:cfc4a0d9f1be, paras 1, 11] One is scam money layered through brokers and platforms. [S:be46918f8aa2, paras 7–8] Layering means moving money through many transactions to hide where it came from. Another is misuse of simplified Electronic Direct Debit Authorisation (eDDA) deposits. [S:cfc4a0d9f1be, paras 1, 11] eDDA lets a customer approve direct debits from a bank account online. The SFC also passed on a March 2026 FATF report on stablecoins and unhosted wallets. [S:00c9fbd119bf, p.3] A stablecoin is a token designed to keep a steady value, for example against a currency. An unhosted wallet is a crypto wallet the owner controls directly, not through a service provider.

## Key facts

- The HKMA Guideline is the AML rulebook for every AI, including AIs registered with the SFC. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.3, 1.6] The Guideline treats both "must" and "should" as signalling a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.3, 1.6]
- One-off VA transfers: an AI should complete CDD before carrying out an occasional VA transfer worth HK$8,000 or more. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4] It should also watch for linked smaller transfers that together reach that amount, and carry out CDD if they do. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4]
- For VA transfers, the Guideline points readers to AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A (footnote 15). [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1 fn 15; Chapter 10] Chapter 10 of the Guideline, on wire transfers, does not mention VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1 fn 15; Chapter 10]
- The HKMA has stated that the AMLO does not stop AIs making VA transfers for customers if they meet the section 13A requirements. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- The bank should keep records of occasional VA transfers of HK$8,000 or more for at least five years after the transaction. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4] For relationship records, the period is the life of the relationship plus at least five years after it ends. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]
- This point covers a bank that gives correspondent-like services to financial institutions (FIs) or VASPs located outside Hong Kong. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23, fn 78–79] That bank should apply the cross-border correspondent measures in paras 11.6 and 11.13 (where they apply). [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23, fn 78–79] FIs as defined in the AMLO are left out of this rule. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23, fn 78–79]
- For an overseas VASP customer, the bank should find out whether the account is for the VASP's own use or for serving its clients. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.24–11.25] The bank could consider encouraging or requiring separate accounts for the two. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.24–11.25]
- RIs are required to take the SFC's AML Guideline into account, including Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1 to para 1.3; S:8934af8cc884, para 4.16.1] The same applies to AIs that are associated entities of SFC licensees. An associated entity is a company linked to an SFC licensee that holds client assets for it. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1 to para 1.3; S:8934af8cc884, para 4.16.1]
- The HKMA expects AIs to watch for customers who often send money to or receive money from VA platforms. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4] AIs should understand those flows and, where there are grounds, file suspicious transaction reports (STRs). [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4]
- For VASP customers, the bank's risk assessments should tell individual VASPs apart. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4] Depending on the relationship, extra CDD may be needed. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4] Examples: [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
  - understand the VASP's business model;
  - check its licensing, and whether the overseas rules are comparable;
  - assess the VASP's own AML controls.
- An AI planning VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, 'AIs' VA-related proposals', p.5] It should also get the HKMA's feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, 'AIs' VA-related proposals', p.5]
- Intermediaries are LCs and RIs. Those that let clients deposit or withdraw VAs should comply with SFC Chapter 12 for those movements. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b); S:b79266f3da51, para 7(c)] The same applies when clients use VAs to subscribe for or redeem investment products. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b); S:b79266f3da51, para 7(c)]
- SFC Chapter 12 requires the ordering firm to get, record and pass on sender (originator) and recipient details for VA transfers. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.5–12.11.10, 12.14.2] The ordering firm is the one that sends the transfer for its customer. The full set of details applies at HK$8,000 or more, and a shorter set below that. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.5–12.11.10, 12.14.2] For unhosted wallets, the firm should collect similar details from its own customer. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.5–12.11.10, 12.14.2]
- Licensed stablecoin issuers that send transfers must collect full sender and recipient details for transfers of HK$8,000 or more. Fewer details apply below that. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.5–6.6, 5.11] The HKMA expects every holder's identity to be checked, unless an issuer shows the HKMA its controls work. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.5–6.6, 5.11]
- The SFC, citing police intelligence, reports scam money being layered through licensed firms. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 8] Some of it was turned into VAs and withdrawn to unhosted wallets. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 8]
- A Government reply to LegCo on 21 Feb 2024 gave the number of VA-related criminal cases: [S:4bd22ccf481d, reply (1)–(2)]
  - 2021: 1,397 cases, with HK$824 million involved;
  - 2022: 2,336 cases, with HK$1,704 million involved;
  - 2023: 3,415 cases, with HK$4,398 million involved.
- An HKMA speaker said Hong Kong recorded over 44,000 deception cases in 2024. That was a rise of close to 12% on the year before. [S:165d354a94bc, remarks on fraud and scams]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Complete CDD before any occasional VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more. Watch for linked transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4–4.2.5]
- Meet AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A for VA transfers the bank carries out. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15; S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Assess money laundering and terrorist financing risk before launching any VA-related product, service or new technology. Put controls in place to reduce that risk. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 2.10–2.11; S:642680fa4467, pp.2–3]
- Keep VA transfer and relationship records for the periods in para 8.4. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Monitor customers' flows to and from VA platforms, and look into what they are for. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4]
- Write down the settings and thresholds that trigger transaction-monitoring alerts. Review them regularly and have them checked independently. [S:7bfdad704174, para 5.8]
- In the RI business, apply SFC Chapter 12 when handling client VA deposits and withdrawals. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Discuss any planned VA activity with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, 'AIs' VA-related proposals', p.5]
- For the RI: tell the SFC and HKMA before it starts VA activities or changes them. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6] This covers dealing, advice and asset management, and changes to the type of clients served. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- File STRs with the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (JFIU) where the bank finds grounds for suspicion. The JFIU is the unit that receives these reports. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 5.10–5.12]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- For overseas VASPs that get correspondent-like services, do the following: [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.6, 11.23–11.24]
  - understand the business;
  - check its reputation and how it is supervised;
  - assess its AML controls;
  - get senior management approval.
- Confirm with each VASP customer that its operations do not break the laws that apply in Hong Kong or elsewhere. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- Note that the SFC expects licensed firms to register the client bank accounts used for deposits and withdrawals. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 20] It also expects them to whitelist client wallet addresses, meaning they keep a list of approved addresses. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 20]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Our main AML text for VAs is the HKMA Guideline for AIs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3, fn 1] Our RI business must take SFC Chapter 12 into account, and comply with it for client VA deposits or withdrawals. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3, fn 1; S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]
- For our own VA transfers, the Guideline points to AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A, not the wire-transfer chapter. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15]

**To business heads**
- Any VA product needs a written money laundering and terrorist financing risk assessment. [S:7bfdad704174, para 2.11] It also needs a conversation with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- If we serve an overseas VASP like a correspondent bank, we need correspondent-style checks and senior management approval. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.6, 11.23–11.24]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA has said the AMLO does not prohibit AIs from making VA transfers for customers if they meet the section 13A requirements. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- The Government told LegCo that money involved in VA-related crime reached HK$4,398 million in 2023. That was up from HK$824 million in 2021. [S:4bd22ccf481d, reply (1)–(2)]

**To Risk / CRO**
- The SFC's red flags for licensed firms include deposits and withdrawals with little or no trading. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 7] Another is a new eDDA setup followed by whitelisting a new wallet address. [S:cfc4a0d9f1be, para 11]
- An SFC circular notes that the FATF published a report on stablecoin and unhosted wallet risks on 3 March 2026. [S:00c9fbd119bf, p.3 item (3)(c)]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** SFC Chapter 12 binds the bank. **Fact:** It applies to LCs and SFC-licensed VASPs. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] The HKMA said AIs, including RIs, should keep complying with its own Guideline. RIs take the SFC text into account. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.2]
- **Myth:** Chapter 10 of the HKMA Guideline is our crypto travel rule. **Fact:** Chapter 10 covers wire transfers. The link to VA transfers is footnote 15, which points to section 13A. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15, Chapter 10]
- **Myth:** All VASP customers need correspondent banking measures. **Fact:** Para 11.23 targets VASPs located outside Hong Kong. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23] For other VASP customers, the HKMA expects a risk-based assessment, with extra CDD depending on the relationship. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- **Myth:** VASPs are automatically high-risk. **Fact:** The HKMA asks banks to assess each VASP's risks separately. There is no single approach. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- **Myth:** The 2026 custody guidance removed AML duties. **Fact:** The 2024 annex had its own AML paragraph, and the 2026 text does not. [S:86e064b2a8d0, para 23; S:ce05e67bcdce, sections (A)–(H)] But the AML Guideline still applies to all AIs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3]

## Read these first

1. **Guideline on AML/CFT (For Authorized Institutions), revised May 2023** — HKMA, 25 May 2023. Read footnote 1 (printed p.1) and paras 2.10–2.11. Then read paras 4.2.1–4.2.5 with footnotes 14–15. Then read paras 8.4, 11.6 and 11.23–11.25. Why: this is the text that binds the bank. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20230525-4-EN/AML-2.pdf
2. **Regulatory approaches to AIs' interface with VAs and VASPs** — HKMA, 28 Jan 2022. Read pp.3–5. Why: it sets out what the HKMA expects on VA flows, VASP customers and talks before launch. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/20220128-1-EN
3. **AML/CFT Guideline for LCs and SFC-licensed VASPs, Chapter 12** — SFC, 1 Jun 2023. Read 12.1, 12.11, 12.13–12.14. Why: it shows what the RI business must take into account, and what counterparties must do. https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/EN/assets/components/codes/files-current/web/guidelines/guideline-on-anti-money-laundering-and-counter-financing-of-terrorism-for-licensed-corporations/AML-Guideline-for-LCs-and-SFC-licensed-VASPs_Eng_1-Jun-2023.pdf

## Open items

- The 2023 consultation conclusions cite an HKMA circular of 27 April 2023. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3] That circular is not in the documents reviewed for this guide, so its content is unverified here.
- The text of AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A is not in the documents reviewed. So this guide does not restate the data fields it sets for banks.
- In May 2025, the HKMA said it was writing an extra AML/CFT guideline for digital asset activities. [S:0d6e6d0253ff, para 4.1] It was to cover AIs and stablecoin licensees. The HKMA said it would consult later in 2025. [S:0d6e6d0253ff, para 4.1] No consultation or final version appears in the documents reviewed.
- The HKMA's document page still lists the 16 Dec 2019 circular as current. [S:2b4123d71a07, landing page] The AML guidance in the 28 Jan 2022 circular follows it, updated for the FATF guidance of October 2021. [S:642680fa4467, p.3 fn 4]
- The documents reviewed contain no official VA crime statistics after 2023. The FATF report on stablecoins and unhosted wallets is also not among them.
- The SFC eDDA and layering circulars go to LCs, SFC-licensed VASPs and associated entities. The documents reviewed contain no matching HKMA circular for banks as payer banks. A payer bank is the bank that holds the customer's account being debited.
