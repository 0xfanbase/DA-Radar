# D3 AML/CFT for digital assets at a bank

For the Head of Compliance (digital assets) at a Hong Kong bank: after this module you can say which anti-money laundering (AML) and countering the financing of terrorism (CFT) texts actually bind the bank for virtual-asset (VA) business, where the VA-specific hooks sit, and which Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and Financial Action Task Force (FATF) material is context or counterparty expectation only.

## In 60 seconds

The bank's AML rulebook for digital assets is the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) AML/CFT Guideline for Authorized Institutions (AIs), revised in May 2023 and effective 1 June 2023 — not the SFC's guideline. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3; S:16fd7cfab136, Part III para 1] Its VA content is thin: a HK$8,000 trigger for customer due diligence (CDD) on one-off VA transfers for people with no business relationship with the bank, a footnote pointing to Schedule 2, section 13A of the AML Ordinance (AMLO), five-year record keeping, and correspondent-style checks on virtual asset service providers (VASPs) located outside Hong Kong. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1 fn 15, 8.4, 11.23] Other HKMA and SFC guidance describes the section 13A requirements as the travel rule. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.1–6.2; S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.1–12.11.2] The HKMA's January 2022 circular sets further expectations: watch customers' flows to and from VA platforms, risk-assess each VASP customer, and talk to the HKMA before launching VA products or services. [S:642680fa4467, pp.3–5] The SFC's AML guideline (with its Chapter 12 on VAs) applies to licensed corporations (LCs) and SFC-licensed VASPs; the bank's SFC-registered business (as a registered institution, RI) must have regard to it, and must comply with Chapter 12 when the RI handles client VA deposits or withdrawals. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2; S:7bfdad704174, fn 1; S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)] Recent SFC circulars describe live typologies — scam money layered through brokers and platforms, and misuse of simplified Electronic Direct Debit Authorisation (eDDA) deposits — and relay a March 2026 FATF report on stablecoins and unhosted wallets. [S:be46918f8aa2, paras 7–8; S:cfc4a0d9f1be, paras 1, 11; S:00c9fbd119bf, p.3]

## Key facts

- The HKMA Guideline is the AML rulebook for every AI, including AIs registered with the SFC; the Guideline treats both must and should as signalling a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.3, 1.6]
- One-off VA transfers: an AI should complete CDD before carrying out an occasional VA transfer worth HK$8,000 or more, and should watch for linked smaller transfers that together reach that amount. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4]
- For VA transfers the Guideline refers readers to AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A (footnote 15); Chapter 10 on wire transfers does not mention VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1 fn 15; Chapter 10]
- The HKMA has stated that the AMLO does not stop AIs carrying out VA transfers for customers if section 13A requirements are met. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Records for occasional VA transfers at or above HK$8,000 should be kept at least five years after the transaction; relationship records for the life of the relationship and at least five years after it ends. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]
- If the bank gives correspondent-like services to financial institutions (FIs) or VASPs located outside Hong Kong, it should apply the cross-border correspondent measures in paras 11.6 and 11.13 (where applicable); FIs as defined in the AMLO are carved out. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23, fn 78–79]
- For an overseas VASP customer the bank should establish whether the account is for the VASP's own use or for serving its clients, and could consider encouraging or requiring separate accounts for the two. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.24–11.25]
- RIs, and AIs that are associated entities of SFC licensees, are required to have regard to the SFC's AML Guideline, including Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1 to para 1.3; S:8934af8cc884, para 4.16.1]
- The HKMA expects AIs to watch for customers who make frequent transfers to or from VA platforms, understand those flows, and file suspicious transaction reports (STRs) where grounds exist. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4]
- For VASP customers, risk assessments should tell individual VASPs apart; depending on the relationship, extra CDD may be needed, such as understanding the business model, checking licensing and whether overseas rules are comparable, and assessing the VASP's own AML controls. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- An AI planning VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA and get feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, 'AIs' VA-related proposals', p.5]
- Intermediaries (LCs and RIs) that let clients deposit or withdraw VAs should comply with SFC Chapter 12 for those movements, and the same applies to VA-funded fund subscriptions and redemptions. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b); S:b79266f3da51, para 7(c)]
- SFC Chapter 12 requires the ordering firm to obtain, record and pass on originator and recipient details for VA transfers — the full set at HK$8,000 or more, a shorter set below that; for unhosted wallets it should collect similar details from its own customer. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.5–12.11.10, 12.14.2]
- Licensed stablecoin issuers acting as ordering institutions must collect full originator and recipient details for transfers of HK$8,000 or more (fewer below); unless an issuer shows the HKMA its controls work, the HKMA expects every holder's identity to be verified. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.5–6.6, 5.11]
- The SFC, citing police intelligence, reports scam proceeds being layered through licensed firms, some converted to VAs and withdrawn to unhosted wallets. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 8]
- A Government reply to LegCo (21 Feb 2024) gave VA-related criminal cases of 1,397 (2021), 2,336 (2022) and 3,415 (2023), with sums involved of HK$824m, HK$1,704m and HK$4,398m. [S:4bd22ccf481d, reply (1)–(2)]
- An HKMA speaker noted that Hong Kong logged over 44,000 deception cases in 2024, a rise of close to 12% from the year before. [S:165d354a94bc, remarks on fraud and scams]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Complete CDD before any occasional VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more, and watch for linked transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4–4.2.5]
- Meet AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A for VA transfers the bank carries out. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15; S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Run an ML/TF risk assessment and put mitigants in place before launching any VA-related product, service or new technology. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 2.10–2.11; S:642680fa4467, pp.2–3]
- Keep VA transfer and relationship records for the periods in para 8.4. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Monitor customers' VA-platform flows and look into their nature. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4]
- Keep transaction-monitoring parameters and thresholds documented, regularly reviewed and independently validated. [S:7bfdad704174, para 5.8]
- In the RI business, apply SFC Chapter 12 when handling client VA deposits and withdrawals. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Discuss any planned VA activity with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, 'AIs' VA-related proposals', p.5]
- For the RI: notify the SFC and HKMA when it intends to engage in VA activities (dealing, advisory, asset management) or to change them, including the type of clients served. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- File STRs with the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (JFIU) where suspicion is identified. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 5.10–5.12]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- For overseas VASPs receiving correspondent-like services: understand the business, check reputation and supervision, assess AML controls, get senior management approval. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.6, 11.23–11.24]
- Confirm with a VASP customer that its operations do not breach applicable laws in Hong Kong or elsewhere. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- Know that the SFC expects licensed firms to register client bank accounts used for deposits and withdrawals, and to whitelist client wallet addresses. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 20]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Our governing AML text for VAs is the HKMA AI Guideline; our RI business must have regard to SFC Chapter 12, and comply with it when handling client VA deposits or withdrawals. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3, fn 1; S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]
- For our own VA transfers the Guideline points to AMLO Schedule 2 s.13A, not the wire-transfer chapter. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15]

**To business heads**
- Any VA product needs a documented ML/TF risk assessment and an HKMA conversation before launch. [S:7bfdad704174, para 2.11; S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- Giving correspondent-like services to an overseas VASP means correspondent-style due diligence and senior management approval. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.6, 11.23–11.24]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA has said the AMLO does not prohibit AIs from VA transfers for customers if section 13A requirements are met. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- A Government reply to LegCo put sums involved in VA-related crime at HK$4,398m in 2023, up from HK$824m in 2021. [S:4bd22ccf481d, reply (1)–(2)]

**To Risk / CRO**
- SFC red flags for licensed firms include deposits and withdrawals with little or no trading, and a new eDDA setup followed by whitelisting a new wallet address. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 7; S:cfc4a0d9f1be, para 11]
- An SFC circular notes FATF published a focused report on stablecoin and unhosted-wallet risks on 3 March 2026. [S:00c9fbd119bf, p.3 item (3)(c)]

## Common mix-ups

- 'SFC Chapter 12 binds the bank.' It applies to LCs and SFC-licensed VASPs; the HKMA said AIs, including RIs, should continue to comply with its own Guideline, with RIs having regard to the SFC text. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.2; S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2]
- 'Chapter 10 of the HKMA Guideline is our crypto travel rule.' Chapter 10 covers wire transfers; the VA hook is footnote 15, pointing to s.13A. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15, Chapter 10]
- 'All VASP customers need correspondent-banking measures.' Para 11.23 targets VASPs located outside Hong Kong; for other VASP customers the HKMA expects a risk-based assessment, with extra CDD depending on the relationship. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23; S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- 'VASPs are automatically high-risk.' The HKMA asks banks to differentiate individual VASPs' risks; there is no single approach. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- 'The 2026 custody guidance removed AML duties.' The 2024 annex had its own AML paragraph and the 2026 text does not, but the AML Guideline still applies to all AIs. [S:86e064b2a8d0, para 23; S:ce05e67bcdce, sections (A)–(H); S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3]

## Open items

- The HKMA circular of 27 April 2023 (cited in the 2023 consultation conclusions) is not in the corpus; its content is unverified here. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- The text of AMLO Schedule 2 s.13A is not in the corpus, so its detailed data fields for banks are not restated here.
- The HKMA said in May 2025 it was developing a supplementary AML/CFT guideline for digital-asset activities of AIs and stablecoin licensees and would consult later in 2025; no consultation or final version appears in the corpus. [S:0d6e6d0253ff, para 4.1]
- The HKMA's repository page still lists the 16 Dec 2019 circular as current. The AML guidance in the 28 Jan 2022 circular follows it, updated for the FATF guidance of October 2021. [S:2b4123d71a07, landing page; S:642680fa4467, p.3 fn 4]
- No official VA-crime statistics after 2023 were found in the corpus; the FATF stablecoins/unhosted-wallets report itself is not in the corpus.
- The SFC eDDA and layering circulars are addressed to LCs, SFC-licensed VASPs and associated entities; no matching HKMA circular for banks as payer banks was found in the corpus.
