# D3 AML/CFT for digital assets at a bank

For the Head of Compliance (digital assets) at a Hong Kong bank: after this module you can say which anti-money-laundering (AML) texts actually bind the bank for virtual-asset (VA) business, where the VA-specific hooks sit, and which SFC and FATF material is context or counterparty expectation only.

## In 60 seconds

The bank's AML rulebook for digital assets is the HKMA's AML/CFT Guideline for Authorized Institutions (AIs), revised in May 2023 and effective 1 June 2023 — not the SFC's guideline. Its VA content is thin: a HK$8,000 trigger for customer due diligence (CDD) on walk-in VA transfers, a footnote pointing to the statutory travel rule at Schedule 2, section 13A of the AML Ordinance (AMLO), five-year record keeping, and correspondent-style checks on overseas virtual asset service providers (VASPs). The HKMA's January 2022 circular adds the expectations that matter day to day: watch customers' flows to and from VA platforms, risk-assess each VASP customer, and talk to the HKMA before launching any VA product. The SFC's AML guideline (with its Chapter 12 on VAs) binds licensed corporations and licensed platforms; it reaches the bank only for its SFC-registered (RI) business, as something to 'have regard to', and when the RI handles client VA deposits or withdrawals. Recent SFC circulars and FATF reports describe the live typologies — scam money layered through brokers and platforms, eDDA abuse, stablecoins moving through unhosted wallets.

## Key facts

- The HKMA Guideline is the AML rulebook for every AI, including AIs registered with the SFC; in it both 'must' and 'should' are mandatory. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.3, 1.6]
- Walk-in VA transfers: an AI should complete CDD before carrying out an occasional VA transfer worth HK$8,000 or more, including linked smaller transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4]
- For VA transfers the Guideline only points to AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A (footnote 15); its wire-transfer chapter does not mention VAs, so the statute is the operative travel-rule text for the bank. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1 fn 15; Chapter 10]
- The HKMA has stated that the AMLO does not stop AIs carrying out VA transfers for customers if section 13A conditions are met. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Records for occasional VA transfers at or above HK$8,000 should be kept at least five years after the transaction; relationship records five years after the relationship ends. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]
- If the bank gives correspondent-like services to FIs or VASPs located outside Hong Kong, it should apply the cross-border correspondent measures in paras 11.6 and 11.13; AMLO-defined FIs are carved out. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23, fn 78–79]
- For an overseas VASP customer the bank should establish whether the account is for the VASP's own use or for serving its clients, and may require separate accounts for the two. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.24–11.25]
- RIs, and AIs that are associated entities of SFC licensees, must have regard to the SFC's AML Guideline, including Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1 to para 1.3; S:8934af8cc884, para 4.16.1]
- The HKMA expects AIs to watch for customers who make frequent transfers to or from VA platforms, understand those flows, and file suspicious transaction reports (STRs) where grounds exist. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4]
- For VASP customers, due diligence should be risk-differentiated: understand the business model, check licensing and whether overseas rules are comparable, and assess the VASP's own AML controls. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- An AI planning any VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA and get feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, 'AIs' VA-related proposals', p.5]
- Intermediaries (LCs and RIs) that let clients deposit or withdraw VAs should comply with SFC Chapter 12 for those movements, and the same applies to VA-funded fund subscriptions and redemptions. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b); S:b79266f3da51, para 7(c)]
- SFC Chapter 12 requires the ordering firm to obtain and pass on originator and recipient details for VA transfers of HK$8,000 or more; for unhosted wallets it should collect similar details from its own customer. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.5, 12.14.2]
- Licensed stablecoin issuers run a full travel rule at HK$8,000 and, unless their controls are proven effective, are expected to verify the identity of every holder. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.5–6.6, 5.11]
- The SFC reports scam proceeds being layered through licensed firms, some converted to VAs and withdrawn to unhosted wallets. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 8]
- Police figures given to LegCo: VA-related crime cases rose from 1,397 (2021) to 2,336 (2022) and 3,415 (2023), with sums involved of HK$824m, HK$1,704m and HK$4,398m. [S:4bd22ccf481d, reply (1)–(2)]
- An HKMA speaker noted that Hong Kong logged over 44,000 deception cases in 2024, a rise of close to 12% from the year before. [S:165d354a94bc, remarks on fraud and scams]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Complete CDD before any walk-in VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more, and aggregate linked transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4–4.2.5]
- Meet AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A for VA transfers the bank carries out. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15; S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Run an ML/TF risk assessment and put mitigants in place before launching any VA-related product, service or new technology. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 2.10–2.11; S:2b4123d71a07, p.2]
- Keep VA transfer and relationship records for the periods in para 8.4. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Monitor customers' VA-platform flows and look into their nature. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (a), pp.3–4]
- Keep transaction-monitoring thresholds documented, reviewed and independently validated. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 5.4–5.8]
- In the RI business, apply SFC Chapter 12 when handling client VA deposits and withdrawals. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Discuss any planned VA activity with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- For the RI: notify the SFC and HKMA before starting or changing VA dealing, advisory or asset-management activity. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- File STRs with the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (JFIU) where suspicion remains. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 5.10–5.14]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- For overseas VASPs receiving correspondent-like services: understand the business, check reputation and supervision, assess AML controls, get senior management approval. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.6, 11.23]
- Confirm with a VASP customer that its operations do not breach applicable laws in Hong Kong or elsewhere. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- Expect licensed platforms and brokers to register client bank accounts and whitelist wallet addresses — useful when reconciling flows with them. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 20]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Our governing AML text for VAs is the HKMA AI Guideline; SFC Chapter 12 applies only to our RI business and to VA deposits or withdrawals it handles. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1; S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]
- The travel rule for our own VA transfers comes from AMLO Schedule 2 s.13A, not the wire-transfer chapter. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15]

**To business heads**
- Any VA product needs a documented ML/TF risk assessment and an HKMA conversation before launch. [S:7bfdad704174, para 2.11; S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- Banking an overseas exchange means correspondent-style due diligence and senior management sign-off. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA does not bar us from VA transfers if statutory conditions are met; the bar is controls, not permission. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Official data show VA crime sums rising to HK$4.4bn in 2023; fraud is the main threat to watch. [S:4bd22ccf481d, reply (1)–(2)]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Watch named red flags: rapid deposit-and-withdraw with no trading, and eDDA setups followed by new wallet whitelisting. [S:be46918f8aa2, para 7; S:cfc4a0d9f1be, para 11]
- FATF issued a focused report covering stablecoins and unhosted wallets in March 2026. [S:00c9fbd119bf, p.3]

## Common mix-ups

- 'SFC Chapter 12 binds the bank.' It binds LCs and licensed VASPs; the HKMA confirmed banks stay on its own Guideline, with RIs only having regard to the SFC text. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.2; S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2]
- 'Chapter 10 of the HKMA Guideline is our crypto travel rule.' Chapter 10 covers money wire transfers; the VA hook is footnote 15 to s.13A. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15, Chapter 10]
- 'All VASP customers need correspondent-banking measures.' Para 11.23 targets VASPs located outside Hong Kong; others get normal risk-based CDD. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23]
- 'VASPs are automatically high-risk.' The HKMA asks banks to differentiate individual VASPs' risks; there is no single approach. [S:642680fa4467, AML/CFT section (b), p.4]
- 'The 2026 custody annex removed AML duties.' The 2024 annex had its own AML paragraph and the 2026 text does not, but the AML Guideline still applies in full. [S:86e064b2a8d0, para 23; S:ce05e67bcdce, sections (A)–(H)]

## Open items

- The HKMA circular of 27 April 2023 on VA transfers (cited in the 2023 consultation conclusions) is not in the corpus; its content is unverified here. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- The text of AMLO Schedule 2 s.13A is not in the corpus, so its detailed data fields are not restated here.
- The HKMA said in 2025 it was drafting supplementary AML guidance for other digital-asset activities; no final version appears in the corpus. [S:0d6e6d0253ff, para 4.1]
- No official VA-crime statistics after 2023 were found; the FATF stablecoins/unhosted-wallets report itself is not in the corpus.
- The SFC eDDA and layering circulars bind LCs and VASPs; there is no matching HKMA circular for banks as payer banks in the corpus.
