# D2 Regulatory reporting and disclosure

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After this module, you can name the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) reports that now carry cryptoasset data. You can say when each change applied. You can also say which banks could use the six-month transition.

## In 60 seconds

New amendment rules bring the Basel cryptoasset standard into Hong Kong law. The Basel standard is the global rule set for how banks treat cryptoassets. The new rules took effect on 1 January 2026. [S:277820235891, paras 1, 6]

For reporting, the HKMA changed seven returns. A return is a regular data report that a bank files with the HKMA. Each return got new or revised templates (the forms) and completion instructions (the guide to filling them in). [S:30b686db29f9, p.3; S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1] The HKMA names its returns with codes such as MA(BS)3. The seven returns are:
- MA(BS)1E: liquidity (whether the bank has enough cash and easy-to-sell assets).
- MA(BS)1F: compliance certificate.
- MA(BS)3: capital. It has a new Part IIIf (a form for extra crypto risk-weighted amounts). A risk-weighted amount is an exposure scaled up or down for its risk. It drives how much capital the bank holds.
- MA(BS)3A: capital for market risk and credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk. CVA risk is the risk of loss when a trading counterparty's credit quality worsens.
- MA(BS)26: stable funding.
- MA(BS)27: leverage (capital against total exposures, without risk weights).
- MA(BS)28: large exposures (big exposures to a single counterparty).

[S:30b686db29f9, p.3; S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1]

Authorized institutions (AIs) are banks and other deposit-takers authorized by the HKMA. Some AIs had no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026. The HKMA calls these 'Transition AIs'. They could keep using the old templates for four of the seven returns until 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]

For disclosure, Pillar 3 is the public report in which a bank publishes data on its capital and risks. A new Part XII (the crypto section) adds four items to the Pillar 3 package. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218] They are:
- Table CAEA (a written description of crypto activities and risks).
- Template CAE1 (crypto exposures and risk-weighted amounts).
- Template CAE2 (accounting classification).
- Template CAE3 (liquidity).

[S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218] These items apply to AIs with cryptoasset exposures. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218] CAE1 needs extra rows for each material single holding. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218]

The Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) is the HKMA's book of supervisory guidance. SPM module CA-D-1 (the disclosure guideline) helps AIs understand the HKMA's approach to applying the Banking (Disclosure) Rules (BDR). [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

## Key facts

- The HKMA listed seven returns with new templates or instructions. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, 'Banking returns' list (a)-(g)] The list covers MA(BS)1E, MA(BS)1F(a) and (b), MA(BS)3, 3A, 26, 27 and 28. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, 'Banking returns' list (a)-(g)]
- The HKMA said the final templates would not differ materially from the consultation versions. The only exceptions were wording, formatting and changes agreed with the industry. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- A Transition AI is an AI with no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3] It could keep filing four returns on the old templates until 30 June 2026. These are MA(BS)26, MA(BS)1E, MA(BS)3 and MA(BS)3A. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- Even Transition AIs were expected to use some revised MA(BS)3 instructions from 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4] These cover Part II (capital base) and paragraph 6 of Part I. They deal with other changes unrelated to crypto. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
- A bank with crypto exposure could agree a temporary alternative arrangement with the HKMA if its systems were not ready. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4] So could a Transition AI that later acquired crypto exposure. The arrangement covered positions from 31 January to 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4]
- The points below come from the September 2025 consultation versions of the completion instructions. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3] The final versions went to AIs for download in December 2025. The HKMA said they would not differ materially. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- MA(BS)3 Part IIIf is new. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1] Its instructions say every Hong Kong-incorporated AI should complete it. The form works out extra risk-weighted amounts for cryptoassets. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1]
- The Basel standard sorts cryptoassets into groups. Group 1 assets meet four classification conditions. Group 1b is the stablecoin part of Group 1. Group 2 assets do not meet the conditions. Group 2a assets also meet certain hedging criteria. Group 2b is the rest of Group 2. Part IIIf covers four items: [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
  - An infrastructure add-on for Group 1 exposures in the banking book (assets not held for trading). The add-on is extra capital for risks in the underlying blockchain infrastructure. The HKMA sets it by written notice. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
  - Redemption-related credit risk. This is the risk that whoever redeems the tokens fails to pay. It covers Group 1b exposures in the trading book (assets held for trading) and Group 2a stablecoins. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
  - Each Group 2b cryptoasset. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
  - The Group 2 exposure ratio (Group 2 exposures measured against the bank's capital). All Hong Kong-incorporated AIs report it. Only global and domestic systemically important banks report its add-on. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
- The consultation version of the Part IIIf instructions carries a March 2026 version stamp (03/2026). [S:d1fbf6c0736a, header p.1]
- Part IIIa (basic approach) and Part IIIb (standardized approach) are credit-risk forms. Each gains a separate Division A2 for cryptoasset exposures. [S:2195417bfd31, para 4 and Section C.3, para 47; S:df1dbac9c51a, para 4 and Section C.3, para 60] Both also ask for the top five Group 1 cryptoassets by risk-weighted amount. [S:2195417bfd31, para 4 and Section C.3, para 47; S:df1dbac9c51a, para 4 and Section C.3, para 60]
- Part IIIc (a credit-risk form) covers the internal ratings-based approach (where a bank uses its own approved models for credit risk). [S:4b2b2befa66f, Section C.6] It adds a memorandum form, IRB_CRYPTO, for Group 1 exposures in the banking book. [S:4b2b2befa66f, Section C.6]
- MA(BS)1E is due no later than 14 days after each month-end. [S:f4e64fda4011, paras 5, 13.14] Category 1 institutions are AIs the HKMA designates to meet its main liquidity ratio. They report new memo items 1.7(a)-(b). One covers cryptoassets counted as high-quality liquid assets (assets easy to sell for cash in stress). The other covers tokenised claims on customers or counterparties. [S:f4e64fda4011, paras 5, 13.14]
- Tokenised deposits are bank deposits recorded as digital tokens. In MA(BS)1E, some tokenised deposits may be treated like ordinary deposits. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31] This covers those that meet the criteria in SPM LM-1 (the liquidity risk module). [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31] But they do not get the 3% or 5% stable-deposit outflow rate. That rate is the low run-off assumed for stable deposits in a stress. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
- MA(BS)26 is quarterly and due within one month. [S:767a346fbcaa, paras 4, 14] Category 1 institutions add items 3(a)-(b) on tokenised claims and cryptoassets. These feed the net stable funding ratio. This ratio checks that long-term assets have stable funding. [S:767a346fbcaa, paras 4, 14]
- MA(BS)27 includes cryptoassets in the leverage exposure measure. It values them at their financial-reporting value (the value in the bank's accounts). [S:3955600d53f0, attached completion instructions, para 6.5, pp.10-11]
- MA(BS)28 is quarterly and due within one month of quarter-end. [S:527c897d3eed, paras 4, 12] Some crypto exposures are not tied to a counterparty's credit or default risk. A bank need not include them when it reports exempted exposures. [S:527c897d3eed, paras 4, 12]
- Pillar 3: the revised disclosure package applies to any reporting period ending after 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- Table CAEA is annual. It covers crypto activities and risks. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA, pp.210-211] It also covers how the bank assessed each Group 1 classification condition. The bank includes public information it used, but not confidential or proprietary information. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA, pp.210-211]
- Hong Kong-incorporated banks with crypto exposures publish two templates twice a year. [S:9e8d84c87168, Templates CAE1 p.212, CAE2 p.216] CAE1 shows exposures and risk-weighted amounts by Group. CAE2 shows accounting classification. [S:9e8d84c87168, Templates CAE1 p.212, CAE2 p.216]
- CAE1 needs extra rows for each material stablecoin or unbacked cryptoasset. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214] A Group 2 exposure is material if two tests are met. The Group 2 ratio is at least 0.3%, and the single asset exceeds 5% of total Group 2 exposure. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214]
- The CAE1 narrative must state the market value of cryptoassets the bank holds in custody for clients, split by type. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 accompanying narrative, p.212]
- CAE3 (liquidity) is published twice a year in a fixed format. [S:9e8d84c87168, Template CAE3, p.218; S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, p.61-62] It applies to category 1 institutions with crypto exposures, whether incorporated locally or overseas. [S:9e8d84c87168, Template CAE3, p.218; S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, p.61-62]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] File the revised crypto items in MA(BS)1E, 3, 3A, 26, 27 and 28 on the stated schedules. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]; [S:f4e64fda4011, para 5]; [S:767a346fbcaa, para 4]; [S:527c897d3eed, para 4]
- [ ] Complete MA(BS)3 Part IIIf if the bank is incorporated in Hong Kong. Include the Group 2 exposure ratio. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 1, 11]
- [ ] Publish Table CAEA and Templates CAE1-CAE3 as they apply to the bank. [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, pp.61-62]; [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Use daily averages over the period for certain CAE1 columns. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (i), p.213] These are the Group 2a long and short columns, and the Group 2b total, long and short columns. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (i), p.213]
- [ ] Run the 0.3% / 5% materiality test at each period end. The test decides which Group 2 assets need their own CAE1 row. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214]
- [ ] Use the HKMA's explanatory notes, alongside the BDR, when filling in each standard template and table. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Agree a temporary arrangement with the HKMA if your systems are not ready for the revised returns. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Be ready to describe, for each Group 1 asset, how the bank assessed the four classification conditions. Include the public information used. Table CAEA asks for this. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA point (f), p.211]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Crypto reporting started on 1 January 2026. The transition window for banks with no crypto ended on 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- CA-D-1 is general guidance on the HKMA's approach. If it conflicts with the Disclosure Rules, the Rules prevail. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

**To business heads**
- A Transition AI that picked up crypto exposure after 1 January 2026 was grouped with exposed banks. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3-4] If its systems were not ready, it could agree a temporary arrangement with the HKMA. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3-4]
- The public CAE1 narrative shows the total market value of crypto we hold in custody for clients, split by type. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 accompanying narrative, p.212]

**To the CEO**
- If we have crypto exposures, our public Pillar 3 report has a crypto section. It has a yearly narrative plus numbers twice a year. [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, pp.61-62]
- Seven HKMA returns changed at once for the cryptoasset standard. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Every Hong Kong-incorporated AI reports its Group 2 exposure ratio in Part IIIf. Only global and domestic systemically important banks report the add-on. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 11-12]
- Tokenised deposits do not get stable-deposit outflow rates in the liquidity return. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** "No crypto, no reporting change." **Fact:** Transition AIs were still expected to apply some revised MA(BS)3 instructions. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4] These cover non-crypto items and applied from 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
- **Myth:** "The transition covered all seven returns." **Fact:** It covered only four: MA(BS)26, 1E, 3 and 3A. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- **Myth:** "Part IIIf redemption risk is a banking-book item." **Fact:** It sits in the trading book. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 5] The instructions place Group 1b redemption-related credit risk on trading-book exposures. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 5] The legal basis is section 376 of the Banking (Capital) Rules. [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(a)]
- **Myth:** "Banks incorporated overseas disclose nothing." **Fact:** Two items apply to them too. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA scope, p.210; CAE3 scope, p.218] CAEA covers their crypto activities that create liquidity risk. CAE3 applies if they are category 1. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA scope, p.210; CAE3 scope, p.218]
- **Myth:** "Every stablecoin needs its own row." **Fact:** Group 2 rows follow the 0.3% / 5% test. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214-215] Group 1b follows the general materiality test in the Disclosure Rules. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214-215]

## Check yourself

1. Which regulator changed the seven returns that now carry cryptoasset data?
   **Answer:** The HKMA. It gave each of the seven returns new or revised templates and completion instructions. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3; S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1]
2. What is a Transition AI, and until when could it use old return templates?
   **Answer:** An AI with no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3] It could use old templates for four returns until 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
3. If SPM CA-D-1 conflicts with the Banking (Disclosure) Rules (BDR), which one wins?
   **Answer:** CA-D-1 is general guidance on the HKMA's approach. If it conflicts with the Disclosure Rules, the Rules prevail. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]
4. Which part of the capital return MA(BS)3 do the instructions say every Hong Kong-incorporated AI should complete?
   **Answer:** Part IIIf, the new form for extra crypto risk-weighted amounts. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1] It includes the Group 2 exposure ratio, which all Hong Kong-incorporated AIs report. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 11-12]
5. Did Transition AIs face any reporting change on 1 January 2026?
   **Answer:** Yes. They were still expected to apply some revised MA(BS)3 instructions, covering non-crypto items. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
