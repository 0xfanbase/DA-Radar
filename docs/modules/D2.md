# D2 Regulatory reporting and disclosure

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can say which Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) returns and public (Pillar 3) disclosures now carry cryptoasset data, from when they applied, and who could use the six-month transition.

## In 60 seconds

The amendment rules that bring the Basel cryptoasset standard into Hong Kong law took effect on 1 January 2026. [S:277820235891, paras 1, 6] For reporting, the HKMA issued new or revised templates and instructions for seven returns: MA(BS)1E (liquidity), MA(BS)1F (compliance certificate), MA(BS)3 (capital, with a new Part IIIf for extra crypto risk-weighted amounts), MA(BS)3A (capital for market and credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk), MA(BS)26 (stable funding), MA(BS)27 (leverage) and MA(BS)28 (large exposures). [S:30b686db29f9, p.3; S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1] Authorized institutions (AIs) with no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026 ('Transition AIs') could keep using the old templates for four of these returns until 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3] For disclosure, a new Part XII adds one narrative table (CAEA) and three templates (CAE1-CAE3) to the Pillar 3 package. These apply to AIs with cryptoasset exposures, and CAE1 needs extra rows for material individual holdings. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218] Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) module CA-D-1 gives the HKMA's guidance on applying the Banking (Disclosure) Rules (BDR). [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

## Key facts

- The HKMA listed seven returns with new templates or instructions: MA(BS)1E, MA(BS)1F(a)/(b), MA(BS)3, MA(BS)3A, MA(BS)26, MA(BS)27 and MA(BS)28. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, 'Banking returns' list (a)-(g)]
- The HKMA said the final templates would not differ materially from the consultation versions, apart from textual and formatting changes and changes agreed with the industry. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- A Transition AI is one with no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026; it could keep filing MA(BS)26, MA(BS)1E, MA(BS)3 and MA(BS)3A on the old templates until 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- Even Transition AIs were expected to use the revised instructions for MA(BS)3 Part II and Part I paragraph 6 from 1 January 2026, for miscellaneous changes unrelated to crypto. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
- A bank with crypto exposure, or a Transition AI that later acquired some, could agree a temporary alternative arrangement with the HKMA for positions from 31 January to 30 June 2026 if its systems were not ready. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4]
- The points below come from the September 2025 consultation versions of the completion instructions. The final versions went to AIs for download in December 2025, and the HKMA said they would not differ materially. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- MA(BS)3 Part IIIf is new; the instructions say every Hong Kong-incorporated AI should complete it to work out additional risk-weighted amounts for cryptoassets. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1]
- Part IIIf covers: an infrastructure add-on for banking-book Group 1 exposures set by HKMA written notice; redemption-related credit risk for trading-book Group 1b exposures and Group 2a stablecoins; each Group 2b cryptoasset; and the Group 2 exposure ratio (all Hong Kong-incorporated AIs) plus its add-on (global and domestic systemically important banks (G-SIBs and D-SIBs) only). [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
- The consultation version of the Part IIIf instructions carries a March 2026 version stamp (03/2026). [S:d1fbf6c0736a, header p.1]
- Parts IIIa and IIIb gain a separate Division A2 for cryptoasset exposures; both also ask for the top five Group 1 cryptoassets by risk-weighted amount. [S:2195417bfd31, para 4 and Section C.3, para 47]; [S:df1dbac9c51a, para 4 and Section C.3, para 60]
- Part IIIc (internal ratings-based approach) adds a memorandum form, IRB_CRYPTO, for banking-book Group 1 exposures. [S:4b2b2befa66f, Section C.6]
- MA(BS)1E is due no later than 14 days after each month-end; category 1 institutions report new memo items 1.7(a)-(b) on cryptoassets counted as high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) and on tokenised claims from customers or counterparties. [S:f4e64fda4011, paras 5, 13.14]
- In MA(BS)1E, tokenised deposits that meet the SPM LM-1 criteria may be treated like ordinary deposits, but they do not qualify for the 3% or 5% stable-deposit outflow rate. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
- MA(BS)26 is quarterly, due within one month; category 1 institutions add items 3(a)-(b) on tokenised claims and cryptoassets for the net stable funding ratio (NSFR). [S:767a346fbcaa, paras 4, 14]
- MA(BS)27 includes cryptoassets in the leverage exposure measure at their financial-reporting value. [S:3955600d53f0, attached completion instructions, para 6.5, pp.10-11]
- MA(BS)28 is quarterly, due within one month of quarter-end; cryptoasset-related exposures not tied to a counterparty's credit or default risk need not be included when reporting exempted exposures. [S:527c897d3eed, paras 4, 12]
- Pillar 3: the revised disclosure package applies to any reporting period ending after 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- Table CAEA is annual and covers crypto activities, risks, and how the bank assessed each Group 1 classification condition, including public information used but not confidential or proprietary information. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA, pp.210-211]
- Template CAE1 (exposures and risk-weighted amounts by Group) and CAE2 (accounting classification) are semi-annual for Hong Kong-incorporated banks with crypto exposures. [S:9e8d84c87168, Templates CAE1 p.212, CAE2 p.216]
- CAE1 needs extra rows for each material stablecoin or unbacked cryptoasset; for Group 2 an exposure is material when the Group 2 ratio is at least 0.3% and the single asset exceeds 5% of total Group 2 exposure. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214]
- CAE1's narrative must state the market value of cryptoassets the bank holds in custody for clients, split by type. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 accompanying narrative, p.212]
- CAE3 (liquidity) is semi-annual and fixed-format, for category 1 institutions with crypto exposures, whether incorporated locally or overseas. [S:9e8d84c87168, Template CAE3, p.218]; [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, p.61-62]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] File the revised crypto items in MA(BS)1E, 3, 3A, 26, 27 and 28 on the stated schedules. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]; [S:f4e64fda4011, para 5]; [S:767a346fbcaa, para 4]; [S:527c897d3eed, para 4]
- [ ] If Hong Kong-incorporated, complete MA(BS)3 Part IIIf, including the Group 2 exposure ratio. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 1, 11]
- [ ] Publish Table CAEA and Templates CAE1-CAE3 as they apply to the bank. [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, pp.61-62]; [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII, pp.210-218]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] In CAE1, use daily mean values over the period for the specified Group 2a columns (long, short) and Group 2b columns (total, long, short). [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (i), p.213]
- [ ] Run the 0.3% / 5% materiality test at each period end to decide which Group 2 assets need their own CAE1 row. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214]
- [ ] Refer to the HKMA's explanatory notes, alongside the BDR, when filling in each standard template and table. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] If systems are not ready for the revised returns, agree a temporary arrangement with the HKMA. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For each Group 1 asset, be ready to describe how the bank assessed the four classification conditions, including the public information used, as CAEA asks for this. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA point (f), p.211]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Crypto reporting started on 1 January 2026; the no-exposure transition window ended on 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- CA-D-1 is general guidance on the HKMA's approach; if it conflicts with the BDR, the BDR prevail. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

**To business heads**
- A Transition AI that acquired crypto exposure after 1 January 2026 was grouped with exposed banks: if its systems were not ready, it could agree a temporary arrangement with the HKMA. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3-4]
- The aggregate market value of cryptoassets held in custody for clients, split by type, goes into the public CAE1 narrative. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 accompanying narrative, p.212]

**To the CEO**
- If we have cryptoasset exposures, our public Pillar 3 report has a crypto section: an annual narrative plus semi-annual numbers. [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, pp.61-62]
- Seven HKMA returns changed at once for the cryptoasset standard. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Every Hong Kong-incorporated AI reports its Group 2 exposure ratio in Part IIIf; only G-SIBs and D-SIBs report the add-on. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 11-12]
- Tokenised deposits do not qualify for stable-deposit outflow rates in the liquidity return. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]

## Common mix-ups

- "No crypto, no reporting change." Transition AIs were still expected to apply revised MA(BS)3 instructions for non-crypto items from 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
- "The transition covered all seven returns." It covered only four: MA(BS)26, 1E, 3 and 3A. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- "Part IIIf redemption risk is a banking-book item." The instructions place Group 1b redemption-related credit risk on trading-book exposures (Banking (Capital) Rules (BCR) section 376). [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 5]; [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(a)]
- "Overseas-incorporated banks disclose nothing." CAEA (liquidity-risk activities) and CAE3 (if category 1) apply to them too. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA scope, p.210; CAE3 scope, p.218]
- "Every stablecoin needs its own row." Group 2 rows follow the 0.3% / 5% test; Group 1b follows general BDR materiality. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214-215]
