# D2 Regulatory reporting and disclosure

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can say which HKMA returns and public (Pillar 3) disclosures now carry cryptoasset data, when the first filings fell due, and who could use the six-month transition.

## In 60 seconds

The Basel cryptoasset standard came with new reporting and disclosure duties, both effective 1 January 2026. On the reporting side, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) revised seven returns: liquidity (MA(BS)1E), the certificate of compliance (MA(BS)1F), capital adequacy (MA(BS)3, including a brand-new Part IIIf for crypto add-ons), market and CVA risk (MA(BS)3A), stable funding (MA(BS)26), leverage (MA(BS)27) and large exposures (MA(BS)28). Banks with no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026 ('Transition AIs') could keep using the old templates for four of these returns until 30 June 2026. On the disclosure side, a new Part XII adds one narrative table (CAEA) and three templates (CAE1-CAE3) to the Pillar 3 package. A bank must publish these if it has cryptoasset exposures, with extra rows for material individual holdings. SPM module CA-D-1 explains how to apply the Banking (Disclosure) Rules (BDR).

## Key facts

- The HKMA listed seven returns with new templates or instructions: MA(BS)1E, MA(BS)1F(a)/(b), MA(BS)3, MA(BS)3A, MA(BS)26, MA(BS)27 and MA(BS)28. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, 'Banking returns' list (a)-(g)]
- The HKMA said the final templates would not differ materially from the consultation versions, apart from textual, formatting and agreed changes. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- A Transition AI is one with no cryptoasset exposure on 1 January 2026; it could keep filing MA(BS)26, MA(BS)1E, MA(BS)3 and MA(BS)3A on the old templates until 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- Even Transition AIs had to use revised instructions for MA(BS)3 Part II and Part I paragraph 6 from 1 January 2026, for non-crypto changes. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
- A bank with crypto exposure, or a Transition AI that later acquired some, could agree a temporary alternative arrangement with the HKMA for positions from 31 January to 30 June 2026 if its systems were not ready. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4]
- MA(BS)3 Part IIIf is new and must be completed by every Hong Kong-incorporated AI to work out additional risk-weighted amounts for cryptoassets. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 1]
- Part IIIf covers: an infrastructure add-on set by HKMA written notice; redemption-related credit risk for trading-book Group 1b and Group 2a stablecoins; each Group 2b position; and the Group 2 exposure ratio (all AIs) plus its add-on (G-SIBs and D-SIBs only). [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 3-12]
- The Part IIIf instructions carry a March 2026 version stamp, pointing to the quarter ended 31 March 2026 as the first capital-return period with the new crypto items. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, footer p.1]
- Parts IIIa and IIIb gain a separate Division A2 for cryptoasset exposures; Part IIIa also asks for the top five Group 1 cryptoassets by risk-weighted amount. [S:2195417bfd31, para 4 and Section C.3]; [S:df1dbac9c51a, para 4]
- Part IIIc (internal ratings-based approach) adds a memorandum form, IRB_CRYPTO, for banking-book Group 1 exposures. [S:4b2b2befa66f, Section C.6]
- MA(BS)1E is due 14 days after each month-end; category 1 banks report new memo items 1.7(a)-(b) on cryptoassets counted as HQLA and on tokenised customer claims. [S:f4e64fda4011, paras 5, 13.14]
- In MA(BS)1E, tokenised deposits can be reported like ordinary deposits but never get the 3% or 5% stable-deposit outflow rate. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
- MA(BS)26 is quarterly, due within one month; category 1 banks add items 3(a)-(b) on tokenised claims and cryptoassets for the net stable funding ratio (NSFR). [S:767a346fbcaa, paras 4, 14]
- MA(BS)27 includes cryptoassets in the leverage exposure measure at their financial-reporting value. [S:3955600d53f0, para 6.5]
- MA(BS)28 is quarterly, due within one month; crypto exposures with no counterparty credit or default risk can be left out. [S:527c897d3eed, paras 4, 12]
- Pillar 3: the revised disclosure package applies to any reporting period ending after 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- Table CAEA is annual and covers crypto activities, risks, and how the bank assessed each Group 1 classification condition, using public information only. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA, p.210-211]
- Template CAE1 (exposures and risk-weighted amounts by Group) and CAE2 (accounting classification) are semi-annual for Hong Kong-incorporated banks with crypto exposures. [S:9e8d84c87168, Templates CAE1 p.212, CAE2 p.216]
- CAE1 needs extra rows for each material stablecoin or unbacked cryptoasset; for Group 2 an exposure is material when the Group 2 ratio is at least 0.3% and the single asset exceeds 5% of total Group 2 exposure. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214-215]
- CAE1's narrative must state the market value of cryptoassets the bank holds in custody for clients, split by type. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 accompanying narrative, p.212]
- CAE3 (liquidity) is semi-annual and fixed-format, for category 1 institutions with crypto exposures, whether incorporated locally or overseas. [S:9e8d84c87168, Template CAE3, p.218]; [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, p.61-62]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] File the revised crypto items in MA(BS)1E, 3, 3A, 26, 27 and 28 on the stated schedules. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]; [S:f4e64fda4011, para 5]; [S:767a346fbcaa, para 4]; [S:527c897d3eed, para 4]
- [ ] Complete MA(BS)3 Part IIIf, including the Group 2 exposure ratio. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 1, 11]
- [ ] Publish Table CAEA and Templates CAE1-CAE3 as they apply to the bank. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 12.2.4 and Annex A, p.61-62]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Use daily mean values over the period for Group 2a and 2b rows in CAE1. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (i), p.213]
- [ ] Run the 0.3% / 5% materiality test at each period end to decide which Group 2 assets need their own CAE1 row. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214-215]
- [ ] Follow the HKMA explanatory notes for each template, not just the BDR text. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] If systems are not ready for the revised returns, agree a temporary arrangement with the HKMA. [S:30b686db29f9, p.4]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For each Group 1 asset, keep the public evidence used to assess the four classification conditions, since CAEA asks for it. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA point (f), p.211]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Crypto reporting started on 1 January 2026; the no-exposure transition window ended on 30 June 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- CA-D-1 is interpretive only; the BDR prevails if they conflict. [S:e10295e5fe20, para 2.1]

**To business heads**
- The first crypto holding ends any Transition AI easement: the bank must move to the new templates. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3-4]
- Client custody holdings are disclosed publicly by market value and type. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 accompanying narrative, p.212]

**To the CEO**
- Our public Pillar 3 report now has a crypto section: an annual narrative plus semi-annual numbers. [S:e10295e5fe20, Annex A, p.61-62]
- Seven HKMA returns changed at once for the cryptoasset standard. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Every local bank reports its Group 2 exposure ratio; only G-SIBs and D-SIBs report the add-on. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 11-12]
- Tokenised deposits never get stable-deposit outflow rates in the liquidity return. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]

## Common mix-ups

- "No crypto, no reporting change." Transition AIs still had to apply revised MA(BS)3 instructions for non-crypto items from 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3, footnote 4]
- "The transition covered all seven returns." It covered only four: MA(BS)26, 1E, 3 and 3A. [S:30b686db29f9, p.3]
- "Part IIIf redemption risk is a banking-book item." The source text places Group 1b redemption risk in the trading book (BCR section 376); some summaries say otherwise. [S:d1fbf6c0736a, para 5]; [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(a)]
- "Overseas-incorporated banks disclose nothing." CAEA (liquidity-risk activities) and CAE3 (if category 1) apply to them too. [S:9e8d84c87168, Table CAEA scope, p.210; CAE3 scope, p.218]
- "Every stablecoin needs its own row." Group 2 rows follow the 0.3% / 5% test; Group 1b follows general BDR materiality. [S:9e8d84c87168, CAE1 points to note (iii), p.214-215]
