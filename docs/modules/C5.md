# C5 Tokenisation: tokenised products, deposits and bonds

For a bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After reading, you can explain which tokenisation rules bind the bank today, which HKMA projects the bank may join, and what is still only a stated plan.

## In 60 seconds

Tokenisation records a claim on an asset (such as a bond, fund unit, gold or a bank deposit) on a distributed ledger (DLT) or similar technology. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 2, 5; S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1] Hong Kong regulators apply the rules for the underlying product to its tokenised form, and add expectations on technology, ownership and custody risks. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-3; S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 7-8] For authorized institutions (AIs, i.e. banks), the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) set these expectations in a February 2024 circular. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1] The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has circulars on tokenised securities and on tokenised SFC-authorised investment products. They reach licensed corporations (LCs) and banks acting as registered institutions (RIs). [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8; S:58ea8520974d, para 18] On infrastructure, the HKMA's Project Ensemble entered a real-value pilot, EnsembleTX, in November 2025. Seven named Tokenised Deposit Banks are to offer tokenised deposits and settle interbank transactions through it. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3; S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A] The Government has made three tokenised bond issues (2023, 2024 and 2025), and the Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation (HKMC) priced its first digital bond in June 2026. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 5; S:6aa27ee8b400, para 1] The 2026 Policy Address lists next steps: settlement in central bank digital currency (CBDC) and round-the-clock (24/7) operation for EnsembleTX, a digital asset platform at CMU OmniClear (operator of the Central Moneymarkets Unit, CMU), tests of tokenised Exchange Fund Bills, and a second-phase legal review with the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB). These are stated targets, not rules. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii)-(iv), 37(i), 50]

## Key facts

- The HKMA circular covers tokenised real-world assets (e.g. non-SFO structured investment products, spot precious metal). It excludes stablecoins and products covered by the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO). AIs should also meet its standards when customers place tokenised deposits. It expects due diligence on issuers and vendors, plain-language risk disclosure, HKMA custody standards where relevant, and prior discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1; Sections A-D; p.6]
- General principle: the existing selling and investor-protection rules for a product also apply to its tokenised form. AIs should still assess whether tokenisation changes the product's nature (e.g. fractional interests becoming a collective investment scheme). [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.2]
- The SFC treats tokenised securities as traditional securities in a tokenisation wrapper. Whether one is a complex product is judged on the underlying security, provided the technology risks are effectively mitigated. The SFC no longer imposes the 2019 mandatory professional-investor (PI)-only restriction, though public-offering rules still apply. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 7, 21, 24, 26-27]
- RIs planning digital-securities activity should notify the SFC and the HKMA in advance. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8]
- For tokenised SFC-authorised investment products, primary dealing is allowed. Product providers remain responsible for the tokenisation arrangement, and should not use public-permissionless chains without extra controls such as a permissioned token. [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 10, 13]
- Since 20 April 2026, the SFC allows secondary trading of these products on SFC-licensed VATPs, subject to fair-pricing and other controls. RIs taking part should also notify the HKMA. [S:a64e364fd912, paras 4, 8, 12, 27 and fn 13]
- Project Ensemble initially focused on tokenised deposits (digital representations of commercial bank deposits). [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] EnsembleTX moved Ensemble from proof-of-concept to real-value transactions. Its initial focus is tokenised money market fund and treasury use cases, with interbank settlement first running on HKD Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS). [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3]
- The HKMA lists seven Tokenised Deposit Banks for EnsembleTX: Bank of China (Hong Kong), China Construction Bank (Asia), Fubon Bank (Hong Kong), Fusion Bank, Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong), The Bank of East Asia and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]
- Under the HKMA liquidity guideline LM-1 (V.5, Dec 2025), a category 1 bank may treat tokenised deposits it issues (a legally binding claim on the bank, redeemable at par in fiat) as deposits or unsecured funding for the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR). They get no stable-deposit or stable-funding treatment, whether held by retail or small business customers. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4]
- The DBGS covers 50% of eligible expenses: up to HK$1.25m (Half Grant) or HK$2.5m (Full Grant), for at most two issuances per issuer and its associates. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1]
- For the Full Grant, the DLT platform provider must not be an associate of the issuer. The issue must also be at least HK$1bn, go to five or more investors not associated with the issuer or platform provider, and be listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) or an SFC-licensed VATP. [S:5a0563de8142, s.3]
- The FSTB/HKMA phase-1 review found that Hong Kong's legal and regulatory framework is already flexible enough for tokenised bond issues. The Companies Registry's FAQs affirm that a DLT register of debenture holders can be deemed to meet Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) record-keeping rules. [S:279f10054c6e, opening para and 'Issuing clarifications' section]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Apply existing product-selling rules to tokenised products, plus the tokenisation-specific expectations. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-3]
- Apply the circular's standards when customers place tokenised deposits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, fn 1]
- As a distributor of tokenised SFC-authorised products, the bank should be a regulated intermediary (e.g. an RI) and follow existing onboarding and suitability rules. [S:58ea8520974d, para 18]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Carry out due diligence before offering and at intervals after, on the product, its issuer and third-party vendors, including key management and audits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4]
- Give customers plain-language tokenisation risk disclosures. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section B, pp.4-5]
- If providing custody of tokenised products, meet the HKMA's digital asset custody standards. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section D, p.6]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Discuss tokenised-product plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Implementation', p.6]
- As an RI, notify the SFC and the HKMA before digital-securities activity. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8]
- As an RI in secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products, also notify the HKMA. [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13]
- Optional: use the HKMA's Supervisory Incubator for DLT to reaffirm risk controls before a full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Assess the issuer's and tokenisation platform's track record, key-management and ownership-record controls. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4]
- For tokenised bonds, consider the product's legal status and whether settlement is final. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, p.4; Section B, p.5] For example, the HKMA states that CMU settlement of the Government digital green bonds has finality protected by statute. [S:4290ec1111e3, fn 2]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Existing product rules apply to tokenised products, plus HKMA expectations on due diligence and disclosure, and advance discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-6]
- As an RI, the bank should tell both the SFC and the HKMA before digital-securities work, and notify the HKMA too before secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, fn 8]; [S:a64e364fd912, fn 13]

**To business heads**
- EnsembleTX is to operate throughout 2026 with real-value tokenised-deposit settlement. The HKMA invites stakeholders with real-value tokenised-deposit use cases to take part. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3 and CE remarks]
- The DBGS Full Grant (up to HK$2.5m per issue) is not available if the DLT platform provider is an associate of the issuer. A Half Grant of up to HK$1.25m may still apply. [S:5a0563de8142, ss.1, 3]

**To the CEO**
- The Government has made three tokenised bond issues; the latest raised about HK$10bn. The HKMC priced about HK$12bn in June 2026. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 5]; [S:ac6cd085a325, para 1]; [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 1]
- The Policy Address targets CBDC settlement and 24/7 operation for EnsembleTX around end-2026, and a CMU OmniClear digital asset platform this year. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(iv), 50]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Tokenised deposits the bank issues cannot get stable-deposit treatment in the LCR. If the bank cannot always identify holders, it must treat them as other unsecured wholesale funding. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4]
- The HKMA's non-binding DLT risk considerations (April 2024) cover network choice, smart contracts, legal risks such as settlement finality, third parties, interoperability, cybersecurity and private-key security. [S:1884530ff235, Introduction and items 2-8]

## Common mix-ups

- 'Tokenised securities are automatically complex or professional-investor-only.' They are not. The SFC looks through to the underlying security and no longer imposes a mandatory PI-only restriction. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 24, 26]
- 'The HKMA tokenised-products circular covers stablecoins.' It expressly does not. [S:b2719f3dcb37, fn 1]
- 'Secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products is still barred.' That was the 2023 position. The revised April 2026 framework allows it on SFC-licensed VATPs, subject to conditions. [S:d422282a262d, paras 6-7]; [S:58ea8520974d, para 6]
- 'EnsembleTX is not an official name.' The HKMA uses it in its own November 2025 announcement and annex. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1]; [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]

## Open items

- No dedicated HKMA framework for issuing tokenised deposits was found among the sources reviewed. The current anchors are the February 2024 circular, the LM-1 liquidity treatment and the Supervisory Incubator, which has tokenised deposits as a core focus. [S:b2719f3dcb37, fn 1; S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4; S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2]
- The HKMA said it would soon publish an Ensemble Use Case Report and an Ensemble Taxonomy. Neither was found among the sources reviewed as of 25 Sep 2026. [S:3e5e256be485, Project Ensemble passage and 'The Road Ahead' section]
- Phase-2 legal review findings (e-execution, possession/transfer of tokenised instruments) are not yet published. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Next phase']
