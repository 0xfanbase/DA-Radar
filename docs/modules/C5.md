# C5 Tokenisation: tokenised products, deposits and bonds

For a bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After reading, you can explain which tokenisation rules bind the bank today, which HKMA projects the bank may join, and what is still only a stated plan.

## In 60 seconds

Tokenisation records a claim on a real asset (bond, fund unit, gold, bank deposit) on a distributed ledger (DLT). Regulators apply the rules of the underlying product to its tokenised form, plus extra checks for technology, custody and legal risk. For banks, the HKMA's February 2024 circular sets those expectations, and the SFC's circulars cover tokenised securities and tokenised SFC-authorised funds. On the infrastructure side, the HKMA runs Project Ensemble, which moved in November 2025 into a live pilot (EnsembleTX) where seven named banks settle real-value tokenised-deposit transactions. The Government has also issued three digital green bonds, and the Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation (HKMC) issued a digital bond in June 2026. The next steps (24/7 central-bank-money settlement, a CMU OmniClear digital-asset platform, tokenised Exchange Fund Bills tests, and a second-phase legal review) are stated targets in the 2026 Policy Address, not rules.

## Key facts

- The HKMA circular covers tokenised real-world assets (e.g. non-SFO structured products, spot precious metal). It excludes stablecoins and SFO-regulated instruments, but also applies to customers placing tokenised deposits. It expects due diligence on issuer and vendor, plain-language risk disclosure, HKMA custody standards where relevant, and prior discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 fn 1; Sections A-D; p.6]
- General principle: the existing selling and investor-protection rules for a product also apply to its tokenised form; AIs should still assess whether tokenisation changes the product's nature (e.g. fractional interests becoming a collective investment scheme). [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.2]
- The SFC treats tokenised securities as traditional securities in a tokenisation wrapper. Complex-product status is judged on the underlying security, and the 2019 professional-investor-only restriction was lifted. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 21, 24, 26]
- Registered institutions (RIs) planning digital-securities activity should notify both the SFC and the HKMA in advance. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and footnote 8]
- For tokenised SFC-authorised funds, primary dealing is allowed. Product providers remain responsible for the tokenisation arrangement, and should not use public-permissionless chains without extra controls such as a permissioned token. [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 10, 13]
- Since 20 April 2026, secondary trading of these products on SFC-licensed VATPs is allowed with fair-pricing controls; RIs engaging in it must also notify the HKMA. [S:a64e364fd912, para 12 and footnote 13]
- Project Ensemble centres on tokenised deposits (digital representations of commercial bank deposits). [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] EnsembleTX moved Ensemble from proof-of-concept to real-value transactions. It starts with tokenised money market fund and treasury use cases, with interbank settlement via HKD RTGS first. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3]
- The HKMA lists seven Tokenised Deposit Banks for EnsembleTX: Bank of China (Hong Kong), China Construction Bank (Asia), Fubon Bank (Hong Kong), Fusion Bank, Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong), The Bank of East Asia and HSBC. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]
- Tokenised deposits that are a legal claim on the bank can be reported like ordinary deposits for the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), but never get the 3%/5% stable-deposit outflow rate. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
- The DBGS pays 50% of eligible costs: up to HK$1.25m (Half Grant) or HK$2.5m (Full Grant), for at most two issuances per issuer group. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1]
- For the Full Grant, the DLT platform provider must not be an associate of the issuer. The issue must also be at least HK$1bn, go to five or more unrelated investors, and be listed on SEHK or an SFC-licensed VATP. [S:5a0563de8142, s.3]
- The FSTB/HKMA phase-1 review found the current legal framework flexible enough for tokenised bonds. The Companies Registry confirmed that a DLT register of debenture holders can meet Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) record-keeping rules. [S:279f10054c6e, opening para and 'Issuing clarifications' section]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Apply existing product-selling rules to tokenised products, plus the tokenisation-specific expectations. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.2]
- Apply the circular's standards when customers place tokenised deposits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1]
- As distributor of tokenised SFC-authorised products, act as a regulated intermediary under existing conduct and suitability rules. [S:58ea8520974d, para 18]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Carry out ongoing due diligence on the tokenised product, its issuer and technology vendors, including key management and audits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A]
- Give customers plain-language tokenisation risk disclosures. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section B]
- If providing custody, meet the HKMA custody standards. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section D]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Discuss tokenised-product plans with the HKMA before launch. [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Implementation', p.6]
- As an RI, notify the SFC and the HKMA before digital-securities activity. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35, footnote 8]
- As an RI in secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products, also notify the HKMA. [S:a64e364fd912, footnote 13]
- Optional: use the Supervisory Incubator for DLT to confirm risk management before full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Assess the tokenisation platform's track record, key-management and ownership-record controls. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4]
- For bonds you arrange or hold: check the settlement system and the legal-finality position (CMU settlement has statutory finality). [S:4290ec1111e3, footnote 2]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Tokenised products are not a new licence category. Existing product rules apply, plus HKMA tokenisation due diligence and disclosure, and advance discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-6]
- Our RI must tell both the SFC and the HKMA before digital-securities or tokenised-fund secondary-trading work. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, fn 8]; [S:a64e364fd912, fn 13]

**To business heads**
- EnsembleTX runs throughout 2026 with real-value tokenised-deposit settlement. The HKMA invites banks with real use cases to take part. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 2-3 and CE remarks]
- The DBGS can refund up to HK$2.5m per issue, but not if the issuer uses an associated DLT platform. [S:5a0563de8142, ss.1, 3]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong has done three Government digital bond issues, the latest about HK$10bn. The HKMC added about HK$12bn in June 2026. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 5]; [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 1]
- The Policy Address targets CBDC settlement and 24/7 operation for EnsembleTX around end-2026, and a CMU OmniClear digital-asset platform this year. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(iv), 50]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Tokenised deposits do not get stable-deposit liquidity treatment. If holders cannot always be identified, outflows are reported separately. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
- The HKMA expects DLT risk management covering network choice, smart contracts, settlement finality and key security. [S:1884530ff235, Annex items 2-8]

## Common mix-ups

- 'Tokenised securities are automatically complex or professional-investor-only.' They are not. The SFC looks through to the underlying security and dropped the 2019 PI-only restriction. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 24, 26]
- 'The HKMA tokenised-products circular covers stablecoins.' It expressly does not. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1]
- 'Secondary trading of tokenised SFC funds is still barred.' That was the 2023 position. The revised April 2026 framework allows it on SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:d422282a262d, paras 6-7]; [S:58ea8520974d, para 6]
- 'EnsembleTX is not an official name.' It is used in the HKMA's own November 2025 announcement and annex. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1]; [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]
