# C5 Tokenisation: tokenised products, deposits and bonds

This module is for a bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After reading it, you can explain which tokenisation rules bind the bank today. You can also explain which Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) projects the bank may join, and what is still only a stated plan.

## In 60 seconds

Tokenisation records a claim on an asset, such as a bond, fund unit, gold or a bank deposit. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 2, 5; S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1] The claim is recorded on distributed ledger technology (DLT) or similar technology. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 2, 5; S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1] A distributed ledger is a shared digital record kept in step across many computers.

Hong Kong regulators apply the rules for the underlying product to its tokenised form. They also add expectations on technology, ownership and custody risks. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-3; S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 7-8]

For authorized institutions (AIs), meaning banks, the HKMA set these expectations in a February 2024 circular. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1] A circular is a formal notice a regulator sends to the firms it supervises.

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has circulars on tokenised securities. It also has circulars on tokenised SFC-authorised investment products, such as funds the SFC has authorised for sale to the public. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8; S:58ea8520974d, para 18] These circulars reach SFC licensed corporations (LCs). They also reach banks acting as registered institutions (RIs), meaning banks registered with the SFC for securities business. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8; S:58ea8520974d, para 18]

The HKMA's Project Ensemble entered a real-value pilot, EnsembleTX (live transactions with real money), in November 2025. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3; S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A] Seven named Tokenised Deposit Banks are to offer tokenised deposits through it. They are also to settle payments between banks through it. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3; S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]

The Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR) Government has made three tokenised bond issues, in 2023, 2024 and 2025. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 5] The Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation (HKMC) priced its first digital bond in June 2026. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 1]

The 2026 Policy Address, the Chief Executive's yearly policy speech, lists these next steps. They are stated targets, not rules. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii)-(iv), 37(i), 50]
- Settlement in central bank digital currency (CBDC) for EnsembleTX, and round-the-clock (24/7) operation.
- A digital asset platform at CMU OmniClear, the operator of the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU). The CMU is Hong Kong's central system for holding and settling bonds.
- Tests of tokenised Exchange Fund Bills (short-term debt papers the HKMA issues).
- A second-phase legal review with the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB). [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii)-(iv), 37(i), 50]

## Key facts

- The HKMA circular covers tokenised real-world assets. Examples are spot precious metal and structured investment products outside the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO). [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1; Sections A-D; p.6] It excludes stablecoins (tokens built to hold a steady value) and products covered by the SFO. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1; Sections A-D; p.6] AIs should also meet its standards when customers place tokenised deposits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1; Sections A-D; p.6] The circular expects: [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.1 and fn 1; Sections A-D; p.6]
  - checks on issuers and vendors (due diligence)
  - plain-language risk disclosure
  - HKMA custody standards, where relevant
  - discussion with the HKMA in advance.
- Existing selling and investor-protection rules for a product also apply to its tokenised form. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.2] AIs should still assess whether tokenisation changes what the product is. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.2] For example, selling small fractions of an asset could turn it into a collective investment scheme (a pooled investment, like a fund). [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.2]
- The SFC treats tokenised securities as traditional securities in a tokenisation wrapper. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 7, 21, 24, 26-27] Whether one is a complex product is judged by looking at the underlying security. This holds as long as the technology risks are well managed. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 7, 21, 24, 26-27] A complex product is one whose risks are hard for retail investors to understand. The SFC no longer imposes its mandatory 2019 restriction that tokenised securities go to professional investors only. Public-offering rules still apply. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 7, 21, 24, 26-27]
- RIs that plan activity in digital securities should notify the SFC and the HKMA in advance. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8]
- The SFC allows primary dealing (buying new units and cashing them in) in tokenised SFC-authorised investment products. [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 10, 13] Product providers stay responsible for the tokenisation arrangement. [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 10, 13] They should not use public-permissionless chains (open networks anyone can join) without extra controls. One such control is a permissioned token, which only approved holders can hold. [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 10, 13]
- Since 20 April 2026, the SFC allows secondary trading of these products on SFC-licensed VATPs. Secondary trading means investors buying and selling among themselves after issue. [S:a64e364fd912, paras 4, 8, 12, 27 and fn 13] The trading is subject to fair-pricing and other controls. RIs taking part should also notify the HKMA. [S:a64e364fd912, paras 4, 8, 12, 27 and fn 13]
- Project Ensemble first focused on tokenised deposits, meaning commercial bank deposits held in digital token form. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] EnsembleTX moved Ensemble from proof-of-concept (a trial to show an idea works) to real-value transactions. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3] It starts with tokenised money market funds and treasury (cash management) uses. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3] Payments between banks first settle on the Hong Kong dollar Real Time Gross Settlement system. That system settles each payment in full, one by one, as it happens. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3]
- The HKMA lists seven Tokenised Deposit Banks for EnsembleTX: [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]
  - Bank of China (Hong Kong)
  - China Construction Bank (Asia)
  - Fubon Bank (Hong Kong)
  - Fusion Bank
  - Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong)
  - The Bank of East Asia
  - The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation
- The HKMA's liquidity guideline LM-1 (V.5, Dec 2025) covers tokenised deposits that a bank issues. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4] Here, a tokenised deposit is a legally binding claim on the bank, redeemable at face value (par) in ordinary currency (fiat). [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4] A category 1 bank may treat them as deposits or unsecured funding for the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR). [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4] The LCR tests whether a bank holds enough easy-to-sell assets to meet cash outflows under stress. The deposits get no stable-deposit or stable-funding treatment, even when retail or small business customers hold them. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4] That treatment is for money the rules assume is less likely to leave the bank.
- The DBGS pays 50% of eligible expenses, up to a cap. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1] The cap is HK$1.25m for a Half Grant and HK$2.5m for a Full Grant. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1] An issuer and its associates (related companies) can get grants for at most two issuances. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1]
- For a Full Grant, all of these conditions must hold: [S:5a0563de8142, s.3]
  - The DLT platform provider is not an associate of the issuer.
  - The issue is at least HK$1bn.
  - The issue goes to five or more investors not associated with the issuer or platform provider.
  - The issue is listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong or on one of the SFC-licensed VATPs.
- The FSTB and HKMA phase-1 review found Hong Kong's legal and regulatory framework already flexible enough for tokenised bond issues. [S:279f10054c6e, opening para and 'Issuing clarifications' section] A debenture is a company's written promise to repay a debt, such as a bond. The Companies Registry's FAQs confirm that a DLT register of debenture holders can be treated as meeting the record-keeping rules of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). [S:279f10054c6e, opening para and 'Issuing clarifications' section]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Apply existing product-selling rules to tokenised products. Add the HKMA's tokenisation-specific expectations on top. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-3]
- Apply the circular's standards when customers place tokenised deposits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, fn 1]
- As a distributor of tokenised SFC-authorised products, the bank should be a regulated intermediary, such as a registered institution (RI). [S:58ea8520974d, para 18] It should also follow existing onboarding and suitability rules (checking a product fits the customer). [S:58ea8520974d, para 18]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Carry out due diligence on the product, its issuer and third-party vendors. Do it before offering the product and at intervals after. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4] Cover key management (how the keys that control tokens are kept safe) and audits. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4]
- Give customers plain-language disclosures on tokenisation risks. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section B, pp.4-5]
- Meet the HKMA's digital asset custody standards if the bank holds tokenised products for customers (custody). [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section D, p.6]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Discuss plans for tokenised products with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Implementation', p.6]
- Notify the SFC and the HKMA before starting digital-securities activity as an RI. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8]
- Notify the HKMA as well when taking part, as an RI, in secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products. [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13]
- Consider the HKMA's Supervisory Incubator for DLT (optional). Banks can use it to reaffirm their risk controls before a full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Assess the track record of the issuer and the tokenisation platform. Also assess their controls over keys and ownership records. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4]
- Consider, for tokenised bonds, the product's legal status and whether settlement is final. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, p.4; Section B, p.5] Final settlement means a completed transfer is legally fixed and stays in place. For example, the HKMA states that a law protects the finality of CMU settlement for the Government digital green bonds. [S:4290ec1111e3, fn 2]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Existing product rules apply to tokenised products. The HKMA adds expectations on due diligence, disclosure and early discussion with it. [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.2-6]
- As an RI, the bank should tell the SFC and the HKMA before digital-securities work. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, fn 8] It should also notify the HKMA before secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products. [S:a64e364fd912, fn 13]

**To business heads**
- EnsembleTX is to run throughout 2026 with real-value tokenised-deposit settlement. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3 and CE remarks] The HKMA invites parties with real-value tokenised-deposit use cases to take part. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3 and CE remarks]
- The DBGS Full Grant pays up to HK$2.5m per issue. It is not available if the DLT platform provider is an associate of the issuer. [S:5a0563de8142, ss.1, 3] A Half Grant of up to HK$1.25m may still apply. [S:5a0563de8142, ss.1, 3]

**To the CEO**
- The Government has made three tokenised bond issues. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 5] The latest raised about HK$10bn. [S:ac6cd085a325, para 1] The HKMC priced a digital bond of about HK$12bn in June 2026. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 1]
- The Policy Address targets CBDC settlement and 24/7 operation for EnsembleTX around end-2026. It also targets a CMU OmniClear digital asset platform this year. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(iv), 50]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Tokenised deposits the bank issues cannot get stable-deposit treatment in the LCR. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4] If the bank cannot always identify the holders, it must treat them as "other unsecured wholesale funding". [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4]
- The HKMA's April 2024 DLT risk considerations are not binding. They cover network choice, smart contracts (code that runs by itself on a ledger), legal risks such as settlement finality, and third parties. They also cover interoperability (systems working together), cybersecurity and private-key security. [S:1884530ff235, Introduction and items 2-8]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** Tokenised securities are automatically complex or for professional investors only. **Fact:** The SFC looks through to the underlying security. It no longer imposes a mandatory professional-investor-only restriction. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, paras 24, 26]
- **Myth:** The HKMA tokenised-products circular covers stablecoins. **Fact:** The circular expressly excludes them. [S:b2719f3dcb37, fn 1]
- **Myth:** Secondary trading of tokenised SFC-authorised products is still barred. **Fact:** That was the 2023 position. [S:d422282a262d, paras 6-7] The revised April 2026 framework allows it on SFC-licensed VATPs, subject to conditions. [S:58ea8520974d, para 6]
- **Myth:** EnsembleTX is not an official name. **Fact:** The HKMA uses the name in its own November 2025 announcement and annex. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1]; [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A]

## Open items

- The sources reviewed contain no dedicated HKMA framework for issuing tokenised deposits. For now, the anchors are the February 2024 circular and the LM-1 liquidity treatment. [S:b2719f3dcb37, fn 1; S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4] A third anchor is the Supervisory Incubator, which has tokenised deposits as a core focus. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2]
- The HKMA said it would soon publish an Ensemble Use Case Report and an Ensemble Taxonomy (a shared set of terms). Neither appears in the sources reviewed as of 25 Sep 2026. [S:3e5e256be485, Project Ensemble passage and 'The Road Ahead' section]
- The findings of the phase-2 legal review are not yet published. That review covers electronic signing, and holding and transferring tokenised instruments. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Next phase']
