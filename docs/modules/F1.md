# F1 Where the money is

This module maps where a bank can earn money from digital assets in Hong Kong. After reading, you can walk a Chief Executive Officer (CEO) through seven stages of the value chain and say who pays at each one. You can name all 16 business lines on this site, with the bank's role and the status of the rules. You can also tell an official market figure apart from an industry estimate.

## In 60 seconds

Money in digital assets follows seven stages: issue, distribute, trade, hold, settle and pay, finance, and advise and manage. At each stage someone pays a fee, a spread or interest. A spread is the gap between a buying and a selling price. (concept)

On this site, 11 of the 16 business lines have rules already in force. Banks registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) may offer virtual asset (VA) dealing, such as trades in bitcoin. The SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) require them to work only with SFC-licensed platforms. [S:8a016a912528, para 17] The HKMA expects a bank to discuss VA custody plans with it first. It also expects the bank to show that it meets HKMA custody standards. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2] Three lines are still pilots. EnsembleTX (the HKMA's tokenised-deposit pilot) is to run throughout 2026. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]

Official figures give a sense of scale. The 11 VA spot exchange-traded funds (ETFs) had a total market value of US$451 million at end-June 2026. [S:c24d834af185, p.31] An exchange-traded fund (ETF) is a fund whose units trade on a stock exchange. Hong Kong's asset and wealth management business held HK$42,202 billion at end-2025. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 1]

## Key facts

**Market signals from official sources**
- The SFC's list showed 13 licensed VA trading platforms at its 29 May 2026 update. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table; Last update line]
- A money market fund invests in short-term, low-risk debt. (concept)
- Tokenised retail money market funds authorised by the SFC held HK$8.66 billion at end-2025. [S:c28638fc5ccc, para 4 and Note 3]
- At end-June 2026, 15 tokenised retail products held HK$10.3 billion in their tokenised classes, up 280% in a year. [S:c24d834af185, Highlights, p.6]
- The 2026 Policy Address says digital bonds issued in Hong Kong made up nearly 50% of the global market in 2025 and the first half of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35]
- The Government's November 2025 digital green bond raised about HK$10 billion. Orders passed HK$130 billion. [S:ac6cd085a325, Expanding scale section]
- The Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation (HKMC) priced about HK$12 billion of digital bonds in June 2026. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 1]
- In April 2026 the HKMA licensed two stablecoin issuers from 36 first-batch applicants. [S:193316443831, Assessment of licence applications] A stablecoin is a token built to hold a steady value against a currency.
- On 28 April 2026 both licensed issuers confirmed they had not yet issued regulated stablecoins. [S:275e7319a841, paras 2-3]
- The total assets of Hong Kong's private banking and private wealth management business rose 24% to HK$12,945 billion at end-2025. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 5]
- The Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU) is Hong Kong's central securities depository. It holds and settles securities such as bonds. Its assets under custody were about HK$5 trillion at end-September 2025. [S:9c8855ec55a1, About CMU OmniClear]
- Exchange Fund Bills are short-term debt papers issued for Hong Kong's Exchange Fund. (concept) The Policy Address puts them at more than HK$1.3 trillion. It says the HKMA will test tokenised versions by the end of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- In an HKMA stock-take run in January 2025 and published in July 2025, 45% of respondents had adopted distributed ledger technology (DLT), up from 30% in 2022. [S:738405ae090d, p.11] DLT is a shared digital record, such as a blockchain.

**Fees the rules already touch**
- Hong Kong banks on mBridge, a shared platform for cross-border central bank money, set their own payment fees. They base them on operating costs and commercial factors. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- The Digital Bond Grant Scheme (DBGS) covers half of an issuer's eligible costs for a digital bond issue. The cap is HK$1.25 million, or HK$2.5 million if all extra conditions are met. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1, p.2]
- The Stablecoins Ordinance says a licensed stablecoin issuer must not pay interest to holders. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1]

**Capital rules**
- The Basel Committee on Banking Supervision sets global bank capital standards. (concept)
- The HKMA put the Basel standards on banks' crypto-asset exposures into effect in Hong Kong on 1 January 2026. It did this by amending the Banking (Capital), Banking (Disclosure) and Banking (Exposure Limits) Rules. [S:100b982aadca, p.90]

## Industry view

These are estimates or firms' own reported figures. Most are global. None is an official Hong Kong statistic.

- **Stablecoins.** The Bank for International Settlements (BIS), a body owned by central banks, put the total market value of stablecoins at about US$320 billion at end-May 2026. This is from its 2026 Annual Economic Report (global, reported estimate). [I:efa37f6d77e9, p. 88]
- **Tokenised money funds.** A November 2025 BIS staff bulletin put tokenised money market funds on public blockchains at almost US$9 billion by end-October 2025. This is a global, reported estimate. [I:c1c1a36b00a2, PDF p.6]
- **Tokenised bonds.** A July 2025 BIS staff bulletin counted about US$8 billion of tokenised bonds issued up to then (global, reported estimate). [I:44ec1038876d, PDF p.3]
- **Trading take rates.** HashKey Holdings Limited (HashKey), a Hong Kong-listed platform group, reported 2025 revenue of HK$522.8 million from its trading services segment (group, reported). [I:786f45f6ea11, PDF p.11] Its 2025 trading volume for those services was HK$590.8 billion (group, reported). [I:786f45f6ea11, PDF p.11] A take rate is fee income as a share of trading volume. (concept) This site's own arithmetic on those two cited figures: HK$522.8 million of revenue on HK$590.8 billion of volume is about 0.09%. HashKey does not publish this ratio, and it is not a publisher's estimate.
- **Custody.** OSL Group Limited (OSL), also Hong Kong-listed, reported custody income of about HK$16.1 million for 2025 in its 2025 annual report (group, reported). [I:e2df03a8c8a6, PDF p.126, Note 5]
- **Fund fees.** Bosera Asset Management (International) issued a Hong Kong prospectus in March 2026. It states a current management fee of 0.60% a year for its bitcoin ETF's listed units (HK, reported). [I:d81073907a14, PDF p.96] China Asset Management (Hong Kong)'s April 2026 key facts document says its ether ETF pays up to 1% of net asset value (NAV) a year for custody and administration (HK, reported). [I:ee50e4efff6f, PDF p.11] NAV is the value of a fund's assets minus its debts.
- **Staking.** HashKey's 2025 results report a staking "monetization rate" of 0.2%, on average staked assets of HK$22.4 billion (group, reported). This is HashKey's own measure; the results do not give its formula. [I:786f45f6ea11, PDF p.4]
- **Payments.** The Financial Stability Board (FSB), a global standard setter, put the 2025 global average cost of sending a US$200 remittance at 6.5% of the amount. This is from its October 2025 progress report (global, reported estimate). [I:0c779bbbb00d, p.27, section 3.3]

## How it works commercially

**The value chain and who pays**

The table is a general map of where fees arise. It gives no figures. (concept)

| Stage | What happens | Who pays the bank | Main business lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Issue | A token, bond or deposit is created on a ledger | The issuer pays arranging and platform fees | Digital bonds; tokenised deposits; stablecoin roles |
| Distribute | Products reach clients | The client pays a commission, or the product maker shares its fee | VA distribution; tokenised funds; ETF roles |
| Trade | Assets change hands | The client pays a commission or a spread | VA dealing; ETF roles |
| Hold | Assets and keys are kept safe | The client or fund pays a yearly fee on value held | Custody; staking |
| Settle and pay | Money moves between parties | The payer pays a fee or a foreign exchange (FX) spread; balances earn margin | Cross-border payments; banking the industry |
| Finance | The bank lends against assets | The borrower pays interest above funding cost | VA financing; collateral; trade finance |
| Advise and manage | The bank gives advice or runs portfolios | The client pays advice, management or performance fees | Advice and asset management |

Net interest margin is the gap between what a bank earns on money and what it pays for it. (concept)

**Every business line in one list**

Each line has its own page with facts, levers and an analysis box.

- **[Virtual-asset dealing](#b-va-dealing).** Stages: Trade. Role: Broker that deals for clients. Status: In force. Banks deal only through SFC-licensed platforms. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- **[Virtual-asset distribution and brokerage](#b-va-distribution).** Stages: Distribute, Trade. Role: Distributor; broker through a licensed platform; stablecoin offeror. Status: In force. The joint circular covers VA-related products sold by intermediaries. [S:8a016a912528, para 5]
- **[Roles in virtual-asset ETFs](#b-va-etf-roles).** Stages: Distribute, Trade, Hold. Role: Participating dealer; custodian or sub-custodian; distributor; trading counterparty. Status: In force. The trustee of an SFC-authorised VA fund should delegate VA custody only to set types of firm. These include a bank that meets the HKMA's custody standards. [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 20]
- **[Virtual-asset custody](#b-custody).** Stages: Hold, Settle and pay. Role: Custodian; custody delegate for funds and other firms. Status: In force. The HKMA expects a bank to discuss its custody plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- **[Staking services](#b-staking).** Stages: Hold. Role: Custodian that stakes client VAs; staking provider to brokers and funds. Status: In force. Staking means locking up tokens to help run a blockchain in return for rewards. (concept) In September 2025 the SFC and the HKMA said they are now prepared to let intermediaries offer staking to clients. [S:b79266f3da51, para 4]
- **[Virtual-asset financing and margin](#b-va-financing).** Stages: Finance, Trade. Role: Lender to its own dealing clients. Status: In force. A bank may lend for VA dealing based on the client's finances and the collateral the client posts. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (1), p.2]
- **[Collateral and tokenised financing](#b-collateral-financing).** Stages: Finance, Hold, Settle and pay. Role: Lender; repo counterparty; collateral agent; custodian of pledged assets. Status: In force. A repo is a sale of securities with an agreement to buy them back. (concept) The HKMA may revise the VA collateral haircut after giving banks notice. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4), pp.3-4] A haircut is a cut to collateral value.
- **[Stablecoin roles for banks](#b-stablecoin-roles).** Stages: Issue, Distribute, Trade, Hold, Settle and pay, Finance. Role: Issuer; reserve bank and custodian; distributor and dealer; lender that takes coins as collateral. Status: In force. The HKMA requires licensed issuers to place their reserve assets with banks in Hong Kong. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (1)]
- **[Tokenised deposits and payments](#b-tokenised-deposits).** Stages: Issue, Settle and pay. Role: Issuer; settlement bank; payment provider. Status: Pilot. EnsembleTX started the pilot phase in November 2025. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-3]
- **[Tokenised funds and money market fund settlement](#b-tokenised-funds).** Stages: Distribute, Trade, Hold, Settle and pay. Role: Distributor; connecting broker; settlement bank; custodian. Status: In force. The SFC allows primary dealing in tokenised SFC-authorised products. [S:58ea8520974d, para 5]
- **[Digital bonds: arranging and custody](#b-digital-bonds).** Stages: Issue, Distribute, Hold, Settle and pay. Role: Arranger; distributor; investor custodian; investor. Status: In force. Existing securities rules continue to apply to tokenised securities. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 7]
- **[Cross-border payments, FX and mBridge](#b-cross-border-payments).** Stages: Settle and pay. Role: Payment bank; FX provider; wallet top-up bank; settlement bank. Status: Pilot. The HKMA is testing CBDC networks, tokenised deposits and linked fast payment systems. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (2)] CBDC means central bank digital currency.
- **[Trade finance and electronic bills of lading](#b-trade-finance).** Stages: Finance, Settle and pay. Role: Trade financier; payment bank; data user; pilot participant. Status: Exploratory. The HKMA is to study trade-finance uses of tokenised deposits with the Mainland, with pilot deals by end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 99]
- **[Infrastructure as a service](#b-infrastructure-service).** Stages: Issue, Hold, Settle and pay. Role: Platform operator; technology provider; settlement bank; custodian for other firms. Status: Pilot. EnsembleTX is to move step by step to round-the-clock settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]
- **[Banking the digital-asset industry](#b-banking-da-industry).** Stages: Hold, Settle and pay. Role: Account bank; client-money bank; reserve bank and custodian; payment provider. Status: In force. The HKMA expects banks to discuss new digital-asset activities before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- **[Advice and asset management under the new regimes](#b-advisory-management).** Stages: Advise and manage. Role: Adviser; discretionary portfolio manager; fund manager. Status: Conclusions published. The Government and the SFC have not yet set a start date. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64] Licensed and registered firms already giving these services are to get a faster approval process. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 67]

In total, 11 lines are In force, three are Pilot, one is Exploratory and one has Conclusions published.

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** Which stages of the value chain could our bank serve with licences and systems it already has?
> **How to think about it:**
> - At which stages does the bank already hold the permission it needs? VA dealing through licensed platforms is one example [S:8a016a912528, para 17].
> - At which stages does a new regime or a pilot come first? The planned bill for VA dealing and custody is one example [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (3), 'In addition' para].
> - Which lines share one client group and one system? Custody, staking and ETF roles are one possible group.
> - Separate fee income from balance income, since each depends on different drivers. (concept)
> **What it depends on:** the bank's current licences; client demand; the start dates of new regimes; how pilots turn into live services.
> **Official signposts:** the planned VA bill [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (3), 'In addition' para]; EnsembleTX's move to 24/7 operation [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]; the SFC platform list [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table].
> **What this is not:** Not a forecast, not advice, and not the regulator's view.

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** Industry market sizes are official Hong Kong data. **Fact:** Most are global estimates by firms or international bodies. This page labels each one.
- **Myth:** A listed platform's take rate is a market price. **Fact:** It is one group's revenue divided by its volume. Scope and period differ by firm. (concept)
- **Myth:** Stablecoins are already live in Hong Kong. **Fact:** On 28 April 2026 both licensed issuers said they had not yet issued any. [S:275e7319a841, paras 2-3]
- **Myth:** Every line on this page is a live business today. **Fact:** Three are pilots and one is exploratory. EnsembleTX, for example, is a pilot running through 2026. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]

## Open items

- Regulators publish no figures for bank revenue, fees or client numbers in any digital-asset line.
- No official source gives fee levels for bank VA custody, dealing or staking in Hong Kong.
- The sources reviewed include no official list of banks registered with the SFC for VA dealing or distribution.
- Industry benchmarks are mostly global or group-wide. No reliable Hong Kong-only figure was found for tokenised deposits, trade finance or VA financing.
- The sources reviewed do not show whether regulated stablecoins were issued after 28 April 2026.
