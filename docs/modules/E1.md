# E1 What's coming: the pipeline

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can list what is still coming in Hong Kong digital assets, say how far along each item is, and quote only the targets the government and regulators have stated.

## In 60 seconds

Four new licensing regimes for virtual assets (VAs) are coming: dealing, custody, advisory and management. [S:4a0342268bf2, paras 2, 4] All four have consultation conclusions, and the government says one bill will go to the Legislative Council (LegCo) in 2026. [S:ff3df4326e0f, para 2]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 3]; [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)] The earlier plan for an over-the-counter (OTC) licence run by Customs was revised into the wider dealing proposal under Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licensing. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.10] On the market side, the SFC has set out a framework for licensed platforms to propose perpetual contracts for professional investors, a pilot for secondary trading of tokenised funds, and standards for platforms dealing in stablecoins from Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)-licensed issuers. [S:089ec86e2407, para 3]; [S:bbd10edd41cf, paras 1-2]; [S:19d52ade6849, paras 2, 4-5] The HKMA plans central bank money settlement and 24/7 operation in EnsembleTX by around end-2026, and tests of tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end; CMU OmniClear is to set up a digital asset platform in 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii), 35(iv), 50] The Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) bill sets its CARF part to start on 1 January 2027; the bill is before LegCo. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]; [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1] Customs plans digital-asset analytics systems by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex (I) New Indicators, item 209]

## Key facts

- The February 2024 OTC proposal would have created a CCE-run licence for businesses offering spot trading of VAs, with banks and other SFC- or HKMA-regulated firms proposed to be exempt. [S:efcdb0ae3849, paras 2.6, 2.23]
- The June 2025 dealing paper widened that plan. It proposed covering simple conversions and more complex services such as brokerage and block trading, after the consultation found more varied business models. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.8-1.10]
- For dealing and custody, the SFC will be the standard setter; the HKMA will be frontline regulator for registered banks and stored value facilities. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]
- The December 2025 conclusions align VA dealing with Type 1 (dealing in securities). The SFC will require VA dealers to keep client VAs with SFC-regulated VA custodians. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3-5, 26]
- No deeming arrangement is planned for the dealing or custody regimes; each takes full effect on commencement. An expedited approval process is planned for VATPs, LCs and RIs already providing VA dealing, and for VATP associated entities and banks already assessed for VA custody. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
- Commencement dates are not set. The government and SFC will consider how long the market needs to adjust. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- VA advisory will align with Type 4 and VA management with Type 9. For these two services, banks alone could take the SFC registration route rather than a licence. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]
- VA management will have no de minimis threshold. Type 9 firms now managing portfolios with VA exposure below the current threshold will need a licence or registration, or should wind down that business by commencement. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 68]
- In June 2026 the government said the bill for all four regimes, covering stablecoins among other VAs, would go to LegCo that year. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- Under the SFC framework, perpetual contracts on VATPs should be offered only to professional investors. Margin collateral should be fiat, or stablecoins or tokenised deposits regulated by the HKMA. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management']
- The first products under the SFC's tokenised-fund trading framework are expected to be tokenised money market funds. The SFC will then assess how they operate before considering a wider product range. [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6]
- For Relevant Stablecoins, the SFC does not require VATPs and LCs to apply the retail liquidity and index requirements, but their disclosures should cover the stabilisation mechanism and redemption arrangements. [S:19d52ade6849, paras 4-5]
- EnsembleTX settles interbank tokenised deposit transactions at first via HKD RTGS (the Hong Kong dollar real-time gross settlement system), with planned upgrades to 24/7 settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]
- The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA will test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end, to help banks use the more than $1.3 trillion of these bills round the clock. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The SFC plans to start running its digital-asset custody surveillance system starting in the second half of 2026 and to switch on CrypTech market and AML surveillance components in 2027. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)]
- C&ED plans to launch its Digital Asset Transaction Analytics System (DATAS) and AML Analytics System (AMLAS) by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex (I) New Indicators, item 209]
- The FATF approved publication of a seventh targeted update on implementing its VA/VASP standards and a DeFi report at its June 2026 Plenary; both are to be published in due course. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Inventory current and planned VA dealing, custody, advisory and management activity. Intermediaries now doing VA activities under SFC licensing or registration conditions will need a licence or registration under the new regimes. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]
- [ ] Plan for a fixed start date with no transition: no deeming arrangement is planned, and dealers that have not contacted the SFC or HKMA may have to stop operations on that date. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31-32, 34]
- [ ] Note that, once in force, the regimes will prohibit actively marketing VA advisory or management services to the Hong Kong public without a licence or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 70]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Note the SFC's stated plan to start running its digital-asset custody surveillance system starting in the second half of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)]
- [ ] Track the CARF start date (1 January 2027 in the bill) for any group entity that may be an RCASP. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] The regulators encourage firms interested in VA dealing to approach the SFC (or the HKMA, where relevant) early to begin pre-application. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]
- [ ] Existing and would-be VA advisers and managers are strongly encouraged to start pre-application talks with the SFC early. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 7]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Under the dealing regime, check that VA custodians used for client assets are SFC-regulated. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]
- [ ] The SFC's 27 May 2026 circular covers only Relevant Stablecoins: specified stablecoins issued by an HKMA-licensed issuer and authorised under its licence. It does not cover other stablecoins. [S:19d52ade6849, preamble, para 2]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- All four VA intermediary regimes have conclusions; one bill is targeted for 2026, with no deeming arrangement. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 63]
- For dealing and custody, banks register with the SFC and the HKMA is frontline regulator. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]; [S:27d27650e820, para 1.10]

**To business heads**
- VA management will have no de minimis threshold, so even a small VA portion of a managed portfolio will need a licence or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 68]
- Under the SFC framework, VATP perpetuals may take HKMA-regulated tokenised deposits as margin collateral. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management']

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA plans EnsembleTX central bank money settlement and 24/7 operation by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]

**To Risk / CRO**

- The FATF's next VA/VASP update and a DeFi report are approved but not yet published. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)]

## Common mix-ups

- "The Customs OTC licence is still coming." The 2025 dealing proposal revised that plan and put VA dealing under SFC licensing. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.9-1.10]
- "Conclusions mean the regimes are law." A bill is still to be introduced; commencement dates are not set. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- "Retail clients can trade VATP perpetuals." Under the SFC framework, only professional investors should be offered them. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets']
- "VA managers can use any custodian, like dealers." It is the other way round: dealers will have to use SFC-regulated custodians, while private funds managed by VA managers keep the flexibility to appoint qualified custodians globally. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 56]
- "Policy Address items are rules with legal force." They are stated plans: the Policy Address says what the SFC and HKMA will do or are planning to do. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49-51]
