# E1 What's coming: the pipeline

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can list what is still coming in Hong Kong digital assets, say how far along each item is, and quote only the targets the government and regulators have stated.

## In 60 seconds

Four new licensing regimes for virtual assets (VAs) are coming: dealing, custody, advisory and management. All four have consultation conclusions, and the government says one bill will go to the Legislative Council (LegCo) in 2026. The earlier plan for an over-the-counter (OTC) licence run by Customs was folded into the wider dealing regime. On the market side, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has opened perpetual contracts for professional investors, secondary trading of tokenised funds, and licensed stablecoins on trading platforms. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) targets 24/7 settlement and central bank money settlement in EnsembleTX, tokenised Exchange Fund Bills tests and a CMU OmniClear digital-asset platform by around end-2026. Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) tax duties start in 2027 if the bill passes, and Customs plans digital-asset analytics systems by 2029.

## Key facts

- The February 2024 OTC proposal would have created a Customs-run licence for VA-for-cash trading, with banks exempt. [S:efcdb0ae3849, paras 2.6, 2.23]
- The June 2025 dealing paper widened that plan. It now covers brokerage, block trading and custody-linked dealing, after the consultation found more varied business models. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.8-1.9]
- For dealing and custody, the SFC will license and set standards; the HKMA is frontline regulator of banks and of stored value facilities. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]
- The December 2025 conclusions align VA dealing with Type 1 (dealing in securities). VA dealers must use SFC-regulated VA custodians for client VAs. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3-5, 26]
- No deeming or grandfathering applies at commencement. Firms already vetted by the SFC or HKMA get an expedited track. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
- Commencement dates are not set. The government and SFC will consider how long the market needs to adjust. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- VA advisory will mirror Type 4 and VA management Type 9. Only banks may register, rather than be licensed, for these two services. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]
- VA management has no de minimis threshold. Any discretionary VA exposure needs a licence or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 32]
- In June 2026 the government said the bill for all four regimes, covering stablecoins among other VAs, would go to LegCo later that year. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- Perpetual contracts on VATPs are for professional investors only. Margin collateral is limited to fiat or HKMA-regulated stablecoins or tokenised deposits. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements']
- The SFC's tokenised-fund trading framework will start with tokenised money market funds, and the SFC plans a review before widening it. [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 7]
- For Relevant Stablecoins, VATPs need not apply the retail liquidity and index tests, but must disclose stabilisation and redemption details. [S:19d52ade6849, paras 4-5]
- EnsembleTX settles interbank tokenised deposits through HKD RTGS (real-time gross settlement) at first, with a stated path to 24/7 tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]
- The 2026 Policy Address targets tokenised Exchange Fund Bills tests by year-end, covering more than $1.3 trillion of bills. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The SFC plans to run a digital-asset custody surveillance system from H2 2026 and switch on CrypTech surveillance in 2027. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)]
- Customs plans to launch the Digital Asset Transaction Analytics System (DATAS) and Anti-Money Laundering Analytics System (AMLAS) by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Part II item 209]
- The FATF approved a seventh targeted update on VA/VASP standards and a DeFi report at its June 2026 Plenary, to be published later. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Inventory current and planned VA dealing, custody, advisory and management activity against the four coming regimes. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 4]
- [ ] Treat commencement as a hard stop: no deeming arrangement is planned. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]
- [ ] Do not actively market VA advisory or management services to the Hong Kong public without a licence or registration once the regime applies. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 70]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Track the SFC's stated custody surveillance system (H2 2026) for impact on custody counterparties. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)]
- [ ] Track CARF commencement dates for any group entity that may be an RCASP. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Start pre-application talks with the SFC, or the HKMA as applicable, early for the dealing regime. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]
- [ ] Existing and would-be VA advisers and managers are urged to talk to the SFC early. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 6]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Under the dealing regime, check that VA custodians used for client assets are SFC-regulated. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]
- [ ] Check that a stablecoin offered on a VATP is a Relevant Stablecoin from an HKMA-licensed issuer. [S:19d52ade6849, para 2]

## Common mix-ups

- "The Customs OTC licence is still coming." The 2025 dealing proposal revised and replaced that plan under SFC licensing. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.9-1.10]
- "Conclusions mean the regimes are law." A bill is still needed; commencement dates are not set. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- "Retail clients can trade VATP perpetuals." Only professional investors can. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets']
- "VA managers can use any custodian, like dealers." The rules differ: dealers must use SFC-regulated custodians, while private VA fund managers may appoint qualified custodians globally. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 56-57]
- "Policy Address items are commitments with legal force." They are stated targets, delivered later through separate instruments. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49-51]
