# E1 What's coming: the pipeline

This module is for the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After reading it, you can list what is still coming in Hong Kong digital assets. You can say how far along each item is. You can also quote the targets that the government and regulators have stated, and only those.

## In 60 seconds

Four new licensing regimes for virtual asset (VA) services are coming. They cover dealing, custody (holding assets for clients), advisory and management. [S:4a0342268bf2, paras 2, 4] All four now have consultation conclusions (the published outcome of a public consultation). [S:ff3df4326e0f, para 2]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 3] The government says one bill for all four will go to the Legislative Council (LegCo) in 2026. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 3]; [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]

An earlier plan proposed an over-the-counter (OTC) licence, run by Customs, for firms that trade virtual assets directly with customers. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.10] That plan was revised into a wider dealing proposal under Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licensing. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.10]

For trading platforms, the SFC has set out three things:

- a framework for licensed platforms to propose perpetual contracts for professional investors. [S:089ec86e2407, para 3] Perpetual contracts are derivatives with no expiry date. Professional investors are institutions and wealthy investors who meet legal thresholds.
- a pilot for secondary trading (buying and selling after first issue) of tokenised funds (fund units recorded as digital tokens). [S:bbd10edd41cf, paras 1-2]
- standards for platforms dealing in stablecoins from issuers licensed by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA). [S:19d52ade6849, paras 2, 4-5] Stablecoins are tokens designed to hold a steady value against a currency.

The 2026 Policy Address sets three dated plans for market infrastructure. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii), 35(iv), 50] The HKMA plans two upgrades to EnsembleTX (its project for settling tokenised deposits between banks) by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50] Tokenised deposits are bank deposits recorded as digital tokens. The upgrades are settlement in central bank money (money held at the central bank) and 24/7 operation. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50] The HKMA will also test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills (short-term HKMA debt) by year-end. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)] CMU OmniClear (the company that runs the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU) bond settlement system) is to set up a digital asset platform in 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(iv)]

The Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) is a tax reporting standard for crypto-assets. A bill sets 1 January 2027 as the start date for its CARF rules. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)] The bill is before LegCo. [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1] Customs plans digital-asset analytics systems by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex (I) New Indicators, item 209]

## Key facts

- The February 2024 OTC proposal would have created a licence run by the CCE. [S:efcdb0ae3849, paras 2.6, 2.23] The licence was for businesses offering spot trading of virtual assets (VAs), meaning buying and selling for immediate delivery. [S:efcdb0ae3849, paras 2.6, 2.23] Banks and other firms regulated by the SFC or the HKMA were proposed to be exempt. [S:efcdb0ae3849, paras 2.6, 2.23]
- The June 2025 dealing paper widened that plan. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.8-1.10] It proposed covering simple conversions (one VA for another, or for ordinary money). [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.8-1.10] It also proposed covering more complex services, such as brokerage and block trading (large trades arranged privately). [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.8-1.10] The reason was that the consultation found more varied business models. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.8-1.10]
- For dealing and custody, the SFC will be the standard setter (the body that writes the rules). [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4] The HKMA will be the frontline regulator (day-to-day supervisor) for registered banks and stored value facilities (licensed e-wallet and prepaid card operators). [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]
- The December 2025 conclusions align VA dealing with Type 1 (dealing in securities), an existing SFC licence type. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3-5, 26] The SFC will require VA dealers to keep client VAs with VA custodians regulated by the SFC. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3-5, 26]
- No arrangement is planned to let existing dealing or custody firms carry on while they apply. Each regime takes full effect on its start date. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51] A faster approval process is planned for two groups: [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
  - VATPs, LCs and RIs already providing VA dealing.
  - VATP associated entities (the platform's group company that holds client assets) and banks already assessed for VA custody.
- Start dates are not set yet. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32] The government and the SFC will consider how long the market needs to adjust. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- VA advisory will align with Type 4 (advising on securities). VA management will align with Type 9 (asset management). [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2] For these two services, only banks could register with the SFC instead of getting a licence. [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]
- VA management will have no small-amount exception. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 68] Some Type 9 firms now manage portfolios whose VA share is below the current threshold. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 68] By the start date, these firms will need a licence or registration, or should wind down that business. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 68]
- In June 2026, the government said the bill for all four regimes would go to LegCo that year. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)] The bill is to cover stablecoins, among other VAs. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- Under the SFC framework, VATPs should offer perpetual contracts only to professional investors. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management'] Margin collateral (assets a client posts to back its trades) should be one of these: [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management']
  - fiat money (ordinary government-issued currency)
  - stablecoins regulated by the HKMA
  - tokenised deposits regulated by the HKMA
- The first products under the SFC's tokenised-fund trading framework are expected to be tokenised money market funds (funds that hold short-term debt). [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6] The SFC will then assess how they work before it considers a wider range of products. [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6]
- Relevant Stablecoins are specified stablecoins from HKMA-licensed issuers. [S:19d52ade6849, preamble, para 2] For these coins, the SFC does not require VATPs and LCs to apply the retail liquidity and index requirements. [S:19d52ade6849, paras 4-5] Those are the tests a token normally has to pass before retail clients can trade it. [S:19d52ade6849, paras 4-5] Disclosures to clients should still cover two things: [S:19d52ade6849, paras 4-5]
  - the stabilisation mechanism (how the coin keeps its value steady)
  - the redemption arrangements (how holders swap coins back for money)
- At first, EnsembleTX settles transfers of tokenised deposits between banks through the Hong Kong dollar (HKD) real-time gross settlement (RTGS) system. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3] RTGS means each payment settles in full, one by one, as it happens. Planned upgrades would move EnsembleTX to 24/7 settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]
- The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA will test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)] The aim is to help banks use the more than HK$1.3 trillion of these bills round the clock. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The SFC plans to launch its system for monitoring digital-asset custody in the second half of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)] The CrypTech market and AML surveillance components are to follow in 2027. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)]
- C&ED plans to launch two systems by 2029: DATAS and AMLAS. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex (I) New Indicators, item 209]
- At its June 2026 Plenary (full meeting), the FATF approved two papers for publication. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)] One is a seventh targeted update on how countries apply its VA/VASP standards. The other is a DeFi report. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)] Both are to be published in due course. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] List your bank's current and planned VA dealing, custody, advisory and management activity. Firms now doing VA activities under SFC licence or registration conditions will need a licence or registration under the new regimes. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]
- [ ] Plan for a fixed start date with no transition. No arrangement is planned to let existing firms carry on while they apply. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31-32, 34] Dealers that have not contacted the SFC or HKMA may have to stop operating on that date. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31-32, 34]
- [ ] Note the marketing ban that applies once the regimes are in force. Firms without a licence or registration will be prohibited from actively marketing VA advisory or management services to the Hong Kong public. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 70]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Note the SFC's stated plan to launch its system for monitoring digital-asset custody in the second half of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(iii)]
- [ ] Track the CARF start date for any group company that may be an RCASP. The bill sets that date as 1 January 2027. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Note that the regulators encourage firms interested in VA dealing to approach the SFC early, or the HKMA where relevant. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33] The aim is to begin pre-application talks (informal talks before a formal application). [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]
- [ ] Note that existing and would-be VA advisers and managers are strongly encouraged to start pre-application talks with the SFC early. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 7] This matters if your bank gives VA advice or manages VAs, or plans to.

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Under the dealing regime, check that the VA custodians holding client assets are regulated by the SFC. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]
- [ ] Know the scope of the SFC's 27 May 2026 circular. It covers only Relevant Stablecoins. [S:19d52ade6849, preamble, para 2] These are specified stablecoins issued by an HKMA-licensed issuer and authorised under its licence. [S:19d52ade6849, preamble, para 2] The circular does not cover other stablecoins. [S:19d52ade6849, preamble, para 2]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** The Customs OTC licence is still coming. **Fact:** The 2025 dealing proposal revised that plan. It put VA dealing under SFC licensing instead. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.9-1.10]
- **Myth:** Conclusions mean the regimes are law. **Fact:** A bill is still to be introduced. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)] Start dates are not set. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- **Myth:** Retail clients can trade VATP perpetual contracts. **Fact:** Under the SFC framework, only professional investors should be offered them. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets']
- **Myth:** VA dealers will have the same custodian choice as VA managers. **Fact:** Dealers will have to use custodians regulated by the SFC. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26] Private funds managed by VA managers, by contrast, keep the flexibility to appoint qualified custodians anywhere in the world. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 56]
- **Myth:** Policy Address items are rules with legal force. **Fact:** They are stated plans. The Policy Address says what the SFC and HKMA will do or are planning to do. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49-51]

## Open items

- As of 25 September 2026, the documents reviewed for this guide include no bill text for the four VA regimes.
- Start dates for the VA regimes are not set. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- The SFC intends to align custodian requirements with those now applied to SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:f41a543388bc, para 38] It will look at a flexible approach to custody technology and storage ratios (how much client VA is kept offline versus online). [S:f41a543388bc, para 38] The detailed requirements are not in the documents reviewed for this guide.
- The CARF bill was introduced into LegCo on 3 June 2026. [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1] The Second Reading debate (where LegCo debates the bill's main principles) was adjourned. [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1] The documents reviewed for this guide do not record whether the bill has passed.
- The FATF seventh targeted update and DeFi report were not yet published as of the SFC circular of 14 July 2026. [S:43fd79de14c1, section (3)(e)]
