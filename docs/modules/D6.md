# D6 Travel Rule, counterparty VASPs and unhosted wallets

Virtual asset service providers (VASPs) are firms, such as crypto exchanges, that offer virtual asset services to others. A virtual asset (VA) is a digital token such as a cryptocurrency. This module is for the Head of Compliance (digital assets) at a Hong Kong bank. After it, you can:
- Explain what the Travel Rule is and which Hong Kong texts set it out.
- Say what a bank must do before it sends or receives a VA transfer for a customer.
- Describe the checks on the firm at the other end of a transfer, and on self-custody wallets.
- Explain the sunrise issue and how the regulators have handled it.

## In 60 seconds

The Travel Rule means that sender and recipient details move with each VA transfer between firms. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.2, fn 143] It adapts a wire-transfer standard of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF), the global anti-money laundering (AML) standard-setter. [S:08a762f7233e, fn 143] In Hong Kong the legal hook is section 13A of Schedule 2 to the AML statute (AMLO). [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.1] Its full name is the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance.

For the bank, the binding text is the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) AML Guideline for authorized institutions (AIs). [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3] AIs are the banks and deposit-takers the HKMA supervises. That Guideline sets a HK$8,000 trigger for checks on one-off VA transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1(b)(iii)] It then points to section 13A in a footnote. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1(b)(iii), fn 15]

The detailed rulebook sits in Chapter 12 of the AML guideline of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11–12.14] Chapter 12 binds SFC-licensed VASPs, and licensed corporations (LCs) in their VA business. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] The bank's SFC-registered business must take it into account. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1] Chapter 12 also covers checks on the firm at the other end (the counterparty) and transfers with unhosted wallets. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.13–12.14] An unhosted wallet is one where the owner holds the private key, the secret code that moves the assets. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.9, fn 116] The HKMA's AML guideline for licensed stablecoin issuers applies section 13A to transfers of the stablecoins they issue. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, para 6.1]

## Status board

As of 25 September 2026. A registered institution (RI) is a bank registered with the SFC for securities business. Registered institutions (RIs) take SFC AML rules into account. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1] A licensed corporation (LC) is a firm licensed by the SFC. Licensed corporations (LCs) are bound by them. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] A VA trading platform (VATP) is a crypto exchange licensed by the SFC. CFT means counter-financing of terrorism (CFT).

## Key facts

- The HKMA Guideline treats both 'must' and 'should' as mandatory. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6] So each 'should' below from the HKMA Guideline is a requirement for the bank. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6] The SFC and stablecoin guidelines use the same rule for the firms they bind. [S:08a762f7233e, para 1.8; S:4d98c6a80b4d, para 1.9]
- An AI should carry out customer due diligence (CDD) before an occasional VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1(b)(iii)] CDD means checking who a customer is. An occasional transfer is one for a person with no ongoing relationship with the bank. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.3] The AI should watch for linked smaller transfers that together reach HK$8,000. [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.4] A footnote sends the reader to section 13A for VA transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15]
- The HKMA stated that the AMLO does not bar AIs from making VA transfers for customers if they meet section 13A. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- RIs are required to take the SFC guideline, including Chapter 12, into account. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1] Some LCs and RIs with VA dealing services let clients deposit or withdraw virtual assets (VAs). [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)] Those firms should also comply with Chapter 12 for those movements. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)] The same applies to VA subscriptions and redemptions of investment products. [S:b79266f3da51, para 7(c)]
- Chapter 12 names three roles in a transfer. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.4] The ordering institution sends for its customer, the originator. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.4] The beneficiary institution receives for the recipient. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.4] An intermediary institution passes the transfer along in between. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.4] A firm that sends or receives for itself is outside the Travel Rule for that transfer. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.1]
- For transfers of HK$8,000 or more, Chapter 12 says the ordering institution must get and record five items. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.5] They are the originator's name and account number, and the recipient's name and account number. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.5] The fifth is one of: the originator's address, customer number or identity document (ID) number, or, for an individual, date and place of birth. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.5]
- Below HK$8,000, only the two names and two account numbers are needed. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.6] A wallet address can serve as the account number. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.7]
- The ordering institution must send the details to the beneficiary institution securely and immediately. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.9–12.11.10] Immediately means before the transfer or at the same time. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.13] The details may travel separately from the on-chain transfer. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.11]
- To send securely, the ordering institution should check the receiving firm and use tools such as data-sharing agreements and strong encryption. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.12] It should not send the transfer if it cannot pass the details on securely. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.12]
- For transfers of HK$8,000 or more, the ordering institution must make sure the originator details are accurate. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.15] It should not send any transfer until all these steps are met. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.17] The SFC says its LCs and licensed VASPs should not carry out occasional VA transfers at all. [S:08a762f7233e, fn 152]
- An intermediary institution must keep the details with the transfer and pass them on. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.18] A beneficiary institution must get and keep a record of the details it receives. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.20] For HK$8,000 or more, it should check the recipient's name and account match its own records. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.21]
- A receiving firm must have procedures to spot transfers with missing details. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.22] It must seek the missing details as soon as reasonably practicable. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.23] If it cannot get them, it should consider limiting or ending VA transfers with the sending firm. Or it should take reasonable steps to reduce the money laundering and terrorist financing risk. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.23]
- A firm may use any technology to exchange Travel Rule data if it meets the rule. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.12.1] The firm stays responsible, and should check the tool first. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.12.2] Factors include whether the tool works with other firms' tools and copes with high volumes. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.12.3]
- The SFC's consultation paper of 20 February 2023, now superseded, described the sunrise issue. [S:75f3d8a5e93e, fn 31] Countries bring in the Travel Rule at different times. So a firm may deal with a foreign firm not yet under the rule. [S:75f3d8a5e93e, fn 31]
- Some respondents asked the SFC for a 12- to 24-month transition because of the sunrise issue. [S:35906d3416df, para 88] The SFC answered that the FATF wants fast adoption, since the issue lasts until major markets comply. [S:35906d3416df, para 92] For licensed platforms, the SFC accepted sending the details as soon as practicable after the transfer. That stop-gap ran until 1 January 2024, when the duty to send immediately took effect. [S:35906d3416df, para 96, fn 10] All other Travel Rule duties, including secure sending, applied from 1 June 2023. [S:35906d3416df, para 96]
- As part of counterparty checks, a firm should assess whether the counterparty faces a rule similar to the Travel Rule where it operates. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.8(a)] A counterparty not yet licensed or supervised for AML is a sign of higher risk. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.9(b)] The HKMA stablecoin guideline sets matching checks for licensed issuers. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.34–6.35]
- A firm should check a counterparty before sending or releasing a transfer. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.3] It need not repeat the check for each transfer unless risk rises or it suspects crime. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.4] The checks draw on those used for cross-border correspondent banking. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.7]
- A firm must not make VA transfers with a shell VASP (a VASP with no real presence where it is licensed) or shell financial institution. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.13] A shell VASP is incorporated and authorised in a place outside Hong Kong but has no physical presence in that place. It is also not part of a regulated financial group under effective group-wide supervision. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.6.7]
- Before a transfer to or from an unhosted wallet, a firm should get and record sender and recipient details from its own customer. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.14.2] Below HK$8,000, the address or ID item is not needed. [S:08a762f7233e, fn 167] The firm should also monitor these transfers more closely. It should accept transfers only with wallets it has assessed as reliable and, where appropriate, set transaction limits. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.14.3]
- To prove a customer owns a wallet, a firm can use a small test payment or a signed message. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.10.6, fn 141] For whitelisted unhosted wallets, the firm should recheck ownership from time to time, based on risk. [S:08a762f7233e, fn 170]
- For overseas VASP customers that get correspondent-like services, an AI should apply its correspondent banking checks. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.23] It should find out whether the account is for the VASP's own use or for its clients. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.24] The AI could consider asking for two separate accounts. [S:7bfdad704174, para 11.25]
- Licensed stablecoin issuers face matching Travel Rule duties, the same HK$8,000 threshold and counterparty checks, including a ban on transfers with shell VASPs. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.5–6.6, 6.28–6.39] They should check that a customer owns or controls its wallet. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, para 4.36] They should screen unhosted wallets used at issue or redemption. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, para 4.39] They should get and record customer details before transfers to or from customers' unhosted wallets. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 6.40–6.41] Peer-to-peer transfers between non-customers are outside that duty, but still need monitoring. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, para 6.42]

## What your bank must do

The HKMA Guideline binds the whole bank, and the RI business takes Chapter 12 into account. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3, fn 1] The joint circular says intermediaries, including RIs, should comply with Chapter 12 when they handle client VA deposits and withdrawals. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)] Other items citing Chapter 12 state what the SFC requires of its licensees. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2]

**Obligations**
- [ ] Complete CDD before any occasional VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more, including linked transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4]
- [ ] Meet AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A for VA transfers the bank carries out. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15; S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- [ ] In the RI business, apply Chapter 12 when clients deposit or withdraw VAs. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b); S:b79266f3da51, para 7(c)]
- [ ] Keep records of occasional VA transfers of HK$8,000 or more for at least five years. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Build procedures to find incoming transfers with missing details and decide whether to hold, return or release them. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.22]
- [ ] Check any Travel Rule technology before use, and keep records of that check. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.12.2, fn 150]
- [ ] Monitor transfers with each counterparty, and review its file regularly or when risk changes. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.10]
- [ ] Test customer ownership of unhosted wallets, and apply closer monitoring or limits. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.10.6, 12.14.3]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Keep records that show the regulator whether and how details were sent. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.14]
- [ ] Approach the usual HKMA supervisory contacts in its AML and Financial Crime Risk Division with any VA transfer questions. The HKMA says AIs may do so. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- [ ] For the RI, tell the SFC in advance, and the HKMA where relevant, before clients subscribe or redeem with VAs. [S:b79266f3da51, para 7(a)]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Before a transfer, find out whether the other side is a firm or an unhosted wallet, and identify the firm. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.13.3, 12.13.5]
- [ ] Understand the counterparty's business, its reputation, its supervision and its AML controls. Get senior management approval. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.6]
- [ ] Assess whether the counterparty is under a similar Travel Rule and can protect personal data. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.8]
- [ ] Refuse transfers with shell VASPs or shell financial institutions. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.13]
- [ ] For overseas VASP customers, apply correspondent banking checks and learn how the account is used. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23–11.24]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Our binding AML text is the HKMA Guideline, where 'should' is mandatory. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6] It points to section 13A for VA transfers. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 15]
- Our RI business must take SFC Chapter 12 into account. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1] For client VA deposits and withdrawals, the joint circular says it should comply with Chapter 12. [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]

**To business heads**
- Under Chapter 12, the sending firm must get, record and pass on full sender and recipient details for a VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.5, 12.11.9] If the details cannot go securely and on time, the transfer should not go. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.11.12, 12.11.17]
- Under SFC Chapter 12, a transfer with a customer's unhosted wallet needs a check that the customer controls it. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.10.6, 12.14.3]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA has said the law allows banks to make VA transfers for customers if section 13A is met. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- Chapter 12 asks firms to check whether a counterparty is under a similar Travel Rule where it operates. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.8(a)] A counterparty not yet licensed or supervised for AML is a higher-risk sign. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.13.9(b)]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Counterparty checks draw on the correspondent banking approach and need senior management approval. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.13.6–12.13.7]
- Higher-risk counterparties can mean enhanced reviews, closer monitoring or limits. If risk cannot be managed, the firm should not transfer. [S:08a762f7233e, paras 12.13.11–12.13.12]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** SFC Chapter 12 binds the whole bank. **Fact:** It applies to SFC-licensed VASPs, and to LCs in their VA business. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] AIs follow the HKMA Guideline, and RIs take Chapter 12 into account. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.2]
- **Myth:** The Travel Rule starts at HK$8,000. **Fact:** A shorter set of details applies below HK$8,000. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.6]
- **Myth:** The details must be written into the blockchain transfer. **Fact:** They can be sent separately, if sent securely and on time. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.11]
- **Myth:** Unhosted wallets are banned. **Fact:** Firms may deal with wallets they have assessed as reliable, with extra controls. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.14.3]
- **Myth:** The sunrise issue excuses a missing Travel Rule message. **Fact:** A receiving firm must seek missing details. If it cannot get them, it should consider limiting the relationship or take steps to reduce the risk. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.23]

## Open items

- The text of AMLO Schedule 2, section 13A is not in the documents reviewed. This module describes it only as the SFC and HKMA guidelines set it out.
- The HKMA Guideline for AIs has no Travel Rule chapter of its own. The documents reviewed show no HKMA text on how banks should handle the sunrise issue.
- The SFC said it would issue an FAQ on the interim measure. [S:35906d3416df, fn 10] That FAQ is not in the documents reviewed.
- The HKMA circular of 27 April 2023 is cited by the HKMA but is not in the documents reviewed. [S:16fd7cfab136, Part II para 4.3]
- The HKMA stablecoin consultation conclusions (July 2025) also discuss the sunrise issue. They are not yet in the source registry, so this module does not rely on them.

## Check yourself

1. Which text binds the bank on VA transfers, and which binds SFC licensees?
   **Answer:** The HKMA AML Guideline for AIs binds the bank. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3] Chapter 12 binds SFC-licensed VASPs, and LCs in their VA business. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.1.2] RIs take Chapter 12 into account. [S:7bfdad704174, fn 1]
2. Under Chapter 12, what details must the ordering institution get for a VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more?
   **Answer:** The originator's name and account number, and the recipient's name and account number. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.5] The fifth is the originator's address, customer number or ID number, or, for an individual, date and place of birth. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.5]
3. Can an SFC-licensed platform still send Travel Rule details after the transfer under the stop-gap?
   **Answer:** No. That stop-gap ran until 1 January 2024, when the duty to send immediately took effect. [S:35906d3416df, para 96, fn 10]
4. What should the bank do before an occasional VA transfer of HK$8,000 or more?
   **Answer:** An AI should carry out CDD first, and watch for linked smaller transfers that together reach HK$8,000. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 4.2.1(b)(iii), 4.2.4] It should keep the records for at least five years. [S:7bfdad704174, para 8.4]
5. Does the sunrise issue excuse a transfer that arrives without Travel Rule details?
   **Answer:** No. A receiving firm must seek missing details. If it cannot get them, it should consider limiting the relationship or take steps to reduce the risk. [S:08a762f7233e, para 12.11.23]
