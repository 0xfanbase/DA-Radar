# C1 Selling, dealing and advising on virtual assets

For the Head of Compliance at a bank that is also a Registered Institution (RI) of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC): after this module you can explain what the joint regime of the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) lets the bank sell, deal in, advise on and stake, and what it must check first.

## In 60 seconds

Since January 2022 the SFC and the HKMA have set their expectations for intermediaries, including banks registered with the SFC, in a joint circular on virtual-asset (VA) activities [S:e4828fcba126, p.1]. The current base text is dated 22 December 2023 and replaced the two earlier versions [S:8a016a912528, para 3]. A supplemental circular of 30 September 2025 brought in refinements and relaxations [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 2], and HKMA guidance of 27 May 2026 eased the rules for licensed stablecoins and allowed VA financing [S:a0bd48559762, Background; S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2]. VA-related products are very likely complex, and complex ones should generally go to professional investors (PIs) only [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.1]. Clients other than institutional and qualified corporate PIs should be assessed for VA knowledge, and dealing should be done through SFC-licensed platforms (or, for licensed stablecoins, the licensed issuer) [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.2, 17; S:a0bd48559762, para (1)]. The detailed conduct rules are imposed as conditions on the bank's SFC registration, and the SFC (and the HKMA where applicable) should be told in advance of new VA activities or changes [S:8a016a912528, paras 19, 31].

## Key facts

- The 22 December 2023 circular replaced both earlier joint circulars in full [S:8a016a912528, para 3].
- VA-related products are those that mainly invest in VAs, get their value mainly from VAs, or track VA returns. The regulators say such products are very likely complex, so suitability applies even without a solicitation or recommendation, apart from the exchange-traded products in paras 7 and 9 [S:8a016a912528, para 5].
- Complex VA-related products should be offered to PIs only, apart from a limited set of products [S:8a016a912528, para 6.1]. These are certain VA derivatives traded on exchanges specified by the SFC and SFC-authorised VA funds. For those traded on exchange (the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) for the funds), an order placed without solicitation or recommendation can be executed without a suitability check, but the knowledge test still applies [S:8a016a912528, paras 7, 9, 9.1].
- Everyone except institutional PIs and qualified corporate PIs should be assessed for VA knowledge. A one-off test is acceptable. A client without the knowledge may trade only after the firm has trained them [S:8a016a912528, para 6.2 and fn 10].
- Firms should partner only with SFC-licensed platforms for VA dealing, either by introducing clients or through an omnibus account, and should offer VA dealing only to clients of their Type 1 (dealing in securities) business [S:8a016a912528, paras 17–18].
- Before serving retail dealing clients, a firm should set an exposure limit for each client with reference to the client's financial situation, including net worth, and should restrict them to tokens the platform offers to retail investors [S:8a016a912528, para 20(a)(ii), (iv)].
- Advice on VAs is limited to Type 1 or Type 4 (advising on securities) clients. Any VA recommended to a retail client should be included in at least two acceptable indices from at least two different index providers [S:8a016a912528, paras 26, 28(a)].
- A portfolio triggers the Appendix 7 (RA9, asset management) terms if it has a stated VA objective or plans to put 10% or more of gross asset value (GAV) into VAs. Ancillary discretionary VA dealing under Type 1 should stay below 10% of GAV [S:8a016a912528, paras 23–24].
- The September 2025 supplement allows staking for clients the firm already deals VAs for. Staking should go only through segregated accounts at an SFC-licensed platform or an AI [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4–5].
- The same supplement allows trades through a licensed platform's off-platform trading services. Subscribing for or redeeming products with VAs, including in-kind fund flows, is not treated as providing VA dealing services [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 6–7].
- The net-worth check and the VA-futures risk disclosure do not apply to institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 8–9].
- From May 2026, RIs may partner with the licensed issuer of a Relevant Stablecoin (a specified stablecoin issued under a Stablecoins Ordinance licence) as well as with VATPs. Paragraph 20(a)(iii) is removed, so retail omnibus dealing may go through a PI-only VATP if retail clients trade only tokens that VATP has admitted for retail trading [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (1)].
- For clients whose accounts are clearly marked as Relevant-Stablecoin-only, no knowledge test or exposure limit is needed. Relevant Stablecoin holdings also need not be counted when setting any client's exposure limit [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a)–(b); S:31b4f67e0233, paras 6.2A, 6.4, 6.4A].
- RIs providing VA dealing may now offer VA financing (credit for VA dealing), but only to their securities margin clients. Where VA collateral is relied on, only bitcoin, ether and Relevant Stablecoins are eligible, with a haircut of at least 60% on bitcoin and ether [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (1), (2), (4) and fn 5].
- In January 2026 the SFC reprimanded and fined a licensed broker HK$4 million. Between November 2018 and November 2022 it let retail clients trade VA products that the 2018 and 2022 circulars limited to PIs. The 1,446 trades involved 32 VA products. It did not assess VA knowledge or give VA-specific warnings, and had no specific product due diligence procedures for VA products [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–5].

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Classify every VA-linked product and apply the PI-only, suitability and knowledge-test rules that follow from that classification [S:8a016a912528, paras 5–10].
- Carry out extra due diligence on any VA fund that is not SFC-authorised, using Appendix 4 [S:8a016a912528, para 12.3].
- Give VA risk disclosures and warning statements to clients other than institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:8a016a912528, para 15].
- Receive or withdraw client VAs only through segregated accounts at the partner platform or at an AI that meets HKMA custody standards [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)].
- For Relevant Stablecoins, disclose material information on the coin's stabilisation mechanism and redemption arrangements. Commission rebates or other benefits should not be the main basis for soliciting or recommending one [S:a0bd48559762, paras (5), (8), (10)].

**Controls & monitoring**
- Have adequate policies, procedures and controls, and train staff, for Relevant Stablecoin activities [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9].
- Keep Relevant-Stablecoin-only clients to Relevant Stablecoins. Before dealing in any other VA for them, apply the full rules, including the knowledge test [S:a0bd48559762, fn 4].
- Keep the listed records on client VAs, staking and VA financing for at least seven years. Records of orders and instructions on client VAs need be kept for at least two years [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 9.6–9.8].
- Obtain an executed staking-risk acknowledgement from clients other than institutional or qualified corporate PIs before staking [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.7(b), 8.8].
- When relying on VA collateral, monitor its volatility in real time. Treat loans secured only by VAs as clean loans, because the Banking (Capital) Rules do not recognise VA collateral [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (5)(b), (9)].

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Tell the SFC in advance, and the HKMA where applicable, about any new VA activity or change to one, including a change in the type of client served [S:8a016a912528, para 31].
- Notify before the first client VA deposit or withdrawal, the first staking service, or the first VA-funded subscription or redemption [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 10].
- A bank that is an AI but not an RI should follow the same standards and discuss any Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management with the HKMA before starting [S:1babdb609fc8, pp.1–2].
- Report any material breach of the terms, whether actual or suspected, to the SFC and the HKMA as soon as practicable [S:31b4f67e0233, para 12.1].

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Confirm that the platform holds an actual SFC licence. Platforms that are only deemed licensed under AMLO Schedule 3G do not count (AMLO = Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance) [S:8a016a912528, para 17 and fn 20].
- Use only an SFC-licensed platform or an AI as the staking service provider. Confirm it has SFC approval or has discussed the service with the HKMA [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.2–8.3].
- For in-kind or VA-funded subscriptions, hold the VAs only at SFC-licensed platforms or AIs, and comply with the VA chapter (Chapter 12) of the SFC's anti-money-laundering (AML) guideline for those transfers [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(b)–(c)].

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The regime reaches us as an RI through registration conditions that the SFC imposes, consulting the HKMA where applicable. The HKMA is our frontline, day-to-day supervisor [S:8a016a912528, para 19; S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3].
- Our AML baseline is the HKMA AML Guideline. As an RI we are also required to have regard to the SFC guideline's VA chapter (Chapter 12) [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 fn 1].

**To business heads**
- SFC-authorised VA funds are not PI-only, and retail can buy listed ones on SEHK. We should still assess VA knowledge first. Overseas non-derivative VA exchange-traded products remain PI-only [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 6.2, 9, 9.1].
- Since May 2026, clearly ring-fenced Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts need no knowledge test and no exposure limit. The coin must come from an HKMA-licensed issuer [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (3)].

**To the CEO**
- Parts of the regime were relaxed in December 2023, September 2025 and May 2026 [S:8a016a912528, para 9; S:d5ebca32c92b, para 2; S:a0bd48559762, Background]. Complex-product rules, knowledge tests and licensed venues remain [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.2, 17].
- In January 2026 the SFC fined a broker HK$4 million for VA distribution failures that ran from 2018 to 2022 [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–2].

**To Risk / CRO**
- Margin clients can get VA financing. Eligible VA collateral is limited to bitcoin, ether and Relevant Stablecoins, with at least a 60% haircut on bitcoin and ether. VA collateral gets no capital recognition [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (4), (9) and fn 5].
- Discretionary VA exposure under Type 1 should stay below 10% of the client account's GAV [S:31b4f67e0233, para 4.7].

## Common mix-ups

- "All VA products are PI-only." Not so. SFC-authorised VA funds are not PI-only, and certain VA derivatives traded on specified exchanges are exempt. A knowledge test is still needed [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 7, 9].
- "VA financing is banned." For RIs that was true until May 2026. RIs providing VA dealing may now offer it to their securities margin clients under HKMA conditions [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background and paras (1)–(2)].
- "The 2026 stablecoin relief covers any stablecoin and any bank." It covers only Relevant Stablecoins and only RIs. It does not cover a licensed issuer for its own coin [S:a0bd48559762, p.2 and fn 3]. AIs that are not RIs are told by a separate letter to follow the same standards [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1].
- "Deleting para 20(a)(iii) only matters for stablecoins." The deletion lets RIs serve retail clients in any VA through PI-only VATPs, as long as each token has been admitted for retail trading [S:a0bd48559762, para (1)].
- "The 2022 circular still sets the rules." Not so. The December 2023 circular replaced the 2022 and October 2023 circulars [S:8a016a912528, para 3]. Existing clients found to have VA knowledge by tests done before 22 December 2023 could continue to be served [S:8a016a912528, fn 40].

## Open items

- The standalone VA dealing, advisory and management licensing regimes are at the conclusions stage. The conclusions state that they will replace the current approach of imposing terms and conditions on SFC registrations [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]. No deeming arrangement is planned for existing VA advisory or management providers, but an expedited approval process is planned for LCs and RIs already providing those services [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 63, 67]. No bill text or commencement date is in the sources reviewed.
- A June 2026 reply to a Legislative Council (LegCo) question said a bill to set up VA dealing, custodian, advisory and management regimes would go to LegCo within 2026 [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]. The sources reviewed do not show that it has been introduced.
- The SFC's Statement of Disciplinary Action behind the January 2026 fine is referenced in the press release but has not been reviewed here [S:415e4629a80f, Notes 3 and 5].
- Footnote 25 of the 2023 circular says firms should not provide financial accommodation for clients to acquire VAs [S:8a016a912528, fn 25]. For RIs, the May 2026 terms now allow it where the firm engages in securities margin financing [S:31b4f67e0233, para 4.3; S:e64b7b0267b2, p.5]. Registry summaries of the 2023 circular that still describe financing as prohibited are out of date.
