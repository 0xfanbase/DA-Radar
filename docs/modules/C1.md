# C1 Selling, dealing and advising on virtual assets

For the Head of Compliance at a bank that is also an SFC Registered Institution (RI): after this module you can explain what the joint SFC–HKMA circular regime lets the bank sell, deal in, advise on and stake, and what it must check first.

## In 60 seconds

Since January 2022 the SFC and the HKMA have used one joint circular to set the rules for intermediaries, banks included, that distribute virtual-asset (VA) products or offer VA dealing, advice or portfolio management. The current base text is dated 22 December 2023. A supplemental circular (30 September 2025) and a May 2026 package on licensed stablecoins have since relaxed parts of it. The core ideas stay the same. Most VA-linked products are treated as complex, and most are for professional investors (PIs) only. Clients other than institutional PIs must pass a VA-knowledge test. Actual dealing may only go through SFC-licensed trading platforms. The detailed conduct rules sit in terms and conditions attached to the bank's SFC registration. For a bank, the HKMA is the frontline supervisor, and the HKMA or SFC must be told before any VA activity starts or changes.

## Key facts

- The 22 December 2023 circular replaced both earlier joint circulars in full [S:8a016a912528, para 3].
- VA-related products are those that mainly invest in VAs, get their value mainly from VAs, or track VA returns. The regulators say such products are very likely complex, so suitability applies even without a recommendation [S:8a016a912528, para 5].
- Complex VA-related products should be offered to PIs only, apart from a small group of exchange-traded products [S:8a016a912528, para 6.1]. The exceptions are VA futures on specified exchanges and SFC-authorised VA funds traded on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK). Where there is no solicitation, these can be executed for retail clients without a suitability check, but the knowledge test still applies [S:8a016a912528, paras 7, 9.1].
- Everyone except institutional PIs and qualified corporate PIs should be assessed for VA knowledge. A one-off test is acceptable. A client without the knowledge may trade only after the firm has trained them [S:8a016a912528, para 6.2 and fn 10].
- Dealing must be done with SFC-licensed platforms, either by introducing clients or through an omnibus account, and only for the bank's Type 1 (dealing in securities) clients [S:8a016a912528, paras 17–18].
- Before serving retail dealing clients, a firm should set a net-worth-based VA exposure limit for each client and should restrict them to tokens the platform offers to retail investors [S:8a016a912528, para 20(a)(ii), (iv)].
- Advice on VAs is limited to Type 1 or Type 4 clients. Any VA recommended to a retail client must appear in at least two acceptable indices from two different index providers [S:8a016a912528, paras 26, 28].
- A portfolio triggers the Appendix 7 (RA9) terms if it has a stated VA objective or plans to put 10% or more of gross asset value (GAV) into VAs. Ancillary discretionary VA dealing under Type 1 should stay below 10% of GAV [S:8a016a912528, paras 23–24].
- The September 2025 supplement allows staking for existing VA-dealing clients. The staked assets must sit in segregated accounts at an SFC-licensed platform or an AI [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4–5].
- The same supplement allows trades through a licensed platform's off-platform (OTC) desk. Paying for or redeeming products with VAs, including in-kind fund flows, does not count as VA dealing [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 6–7].
- The net-worth check and the VA-futures risk disclosure no longer apply to institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 8–9].
- From May 2026, RIs may deal in a Relevant Stablecoin (a fiat-referenced coin issued under a Stablecoins Ordinance licence) through its licensed issuer as well as through a VATP. Paragraph 20(a)(iii) is removed, so retail omnibus dealing may go through a PI-only VATP if retail clients trade only tokens that VATP has cleared for retail [S:a0bd48559762, para (1)].
- For accounts clearly ring-fenced as Relevant-Stablecoin-only, no knowledge test or exposure limit is needed. Relevant Stablecoin holdings are also left out of the exposure calculation for everyone else [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a)–(b); S:31b4f67e0233, paras 6.2A, 6.4, 6.4A].
- RIs providing VA dealing may now offer VA-backed margin financing, but only to existing securities margin clients. Only bitcoin, ether and Relevant Stablecoins count as eligible collateral, with a haircut of at least 60% on bitcoin and ether [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (2), (4) and fn 5].
- The SFC fined a licensed broker HK$4 million. Between November 2018 and November 2022 it had let retail clients trade 32 VA products that the circulars restricted to PIs, without VA due diligence, knowledge tests or VA warnings [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–4].

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Classify every VA-linked product and apply the PI-only, suitability and knowledge-test rules that follow from that classification [S:8a016a912528, paras 5–10].
- Carry out extra due diligence on any VA fund that is not SFC-authorised, using Appendix 4 [S:8a016a912528, para 12.3].
- Give VA risk disclosures and warning statements to clients other than institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:8a016a912528, para 15].
- Receive client VAs only through segregated accounts at the partner platform or at an AI that meets HKMA custody standards [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)].
- For Relevant Stablecoins, disclose the coin's stabilisation mechanism and redemption arrangements. Do not let commission rebates be the main reason for a recommendation [S:a0bd48559762, paras (5), (8)].

**Controls & monitoring**
- Have policies, procedures, controls and staff training in place for Relevant Stablecoin activities [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9].
- Keep Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts clearly separate. If another VA is added, the full rules apply, including the knowledge test [S:a0bd48559762, fn 4].
- Keep records on client VAs, orders and staking for at least seven years [S:31b4f67e0233, para 9.7].
- Obtain a signed staking-risk acknowledgement from non-institutional clients before staking [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.1–8.8].
- When relying on VA collateral, monitor its volatility in real time. Book loans secured only by VAs as clean loans for capital purposes [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (5), (9)].

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Give the SFC advance notice of any new VA activity or change to one, including a change in the type of client served, and copy the HKMA where relevant [S:8a016a912528, para 31].
- Notify before the first client VA deposit or withdrawal, the first staking service, or the first VA-funded subscription [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 10].
- A bank that is an AI but not an RI should discuss any Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management with the HKMA before starting [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2].
- Report any actual or suspected material breach of the terms to the SFC and the HKMA as soon as practicable [S:31b4f67e0233, para 12.1].

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Confirm that the platform holds an actual SFC licence. Platforms that are only deemed licensed under AMLO Schedule 3G do not count [S:8a016a912528, para 17 and fn 20].
- Use only an SFC-licensed platform or an AI as the staking service provider. Confirm it has SFC approval or has discussed the service with the HKMA [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.2–8.3].
- For in-kind or VA-funded subscriptions, hold the VAs only at SFC-licensed platforms or AIs, and follow the SFC AML Guideline's VA chapter (Chapter 12) for those transfers [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(b)–(c)].

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The regime reaches us as an RI through registration conditions that the SFC imposes after consulting the HKMA. The HKMA supervises us day to day [S:8a016a912528, para 19; S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3].
- Our VA anti-money-laundering (AML) baseline remains the HKMA AML Guideline. As an RI we must also have regard to the SFC Guideline's VA chapter (Chapter 12) [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 fn 1].

**To business heads**
- Retail may buy SFC-authorised VA ETFs on the exchange, but only after passing a VA-knowledge test. Overseas spot VA ETPs are still for PIs only [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 9.1].
- Since May 2026, stablecoin-only accounts need no knowledge test and no exposure limit. The account must be ring-fenced and the coin must be HKMA-licensed [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)].

**To the CEO**
- The rules have loosened three times, in 2023, 2025 and 2026. The core safeguards stayed: complex-product treatment, knowledge tests and licensed venues only [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 2; S:a0bd48559762, Background].
- The SFC's January 2026 fine shows that VA distribution failures can be sanctioned years after they happened [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–2].

**To Risk / CRO**
- VA margin lending is now possible, but only against bitcoin, ether and licensed stablecoins. Bitcoin and ether take a haircut of at least 60%, and the VA collateral gets no capital credit [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (4), (9)].
- Discretionary VA exposure under Type 1 should stay below 10% of portfolio GAV [S:31b4f67e0233, para 4.7].

## Common mix-ups

- "All VA products are PI-only." Not so. VA futures on specified exchanges and SFC-authorised VA funds on SEHK can go to retail on an unsolicited basis, but a knowledge test is still needed [S:8a016a912528, paras 7, 9].
- "VA financing is banned." That was true before 2026. Since May 2026, RIs may offer it to margin clients under HKMA conditions [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background and para (1)].
- "The 2026 stablecoin relief covers any stablecoin and any bank." It covers only Relevant Stablecoins and only RIs. It does not cover an issuer dealing in its own coin [S:a0bd48559762, p.2 and fn 3]. AIs that are not RIs are pointed to the same standards by a separate letter [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1].
- "Deleting para 20(a)(iii) only matters for stablecoins." The deletion lets RIs serve retail clients in any VA through PI-only VATPs, as long as each token has been cleared for retail [S:a0bd48559762, para (1)].
- "A 2022 client knowledge test is still good enough." The 2022 and October 2023 circulars are superseded. Only tests done before the December 2023 circular were carried forward [S:8a016a912528, para 3 and fn 40].

## Open items

- The standalone VA dealing, advisory and management licensing regimes are at the conclusions stage. When they commence they will replace the circular-plus-conditions approach, and no grandfathering is planned [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 62–63]. No bill text or commencement date is in the corpus.
- A June 2026 LegCo reply said a bill was planned for later in 2026 [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]. The corpus does not show that it has been introduced.
- The SFC's Statement of Disciplinary Action behind the January 2026 fine is referenced but not in the corpus.
- Canon conflict: the key points for 8a016a912528 still state that financing is prohibited. That was correct for the 2023 text. The May 2026 terms (paras 4.2–4.3) and the HKMA financing circular now allow it for RIs [S:31b4f67e0233; S:e64b7b0267b2].
