# C1 Selling, dealing and advising on virtual assets

This module is for the Head of Compliance at a bank that is also a Registered Institution (RI). An RI is a bank registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) for securities business. After this module you can explain what the bank can do with virtual assets (VAs), such as bitcoin. That covers selling, dealing, advising and staking. You can also explain what the bank must check first. The rules come from a joint regime of the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA).

## In 60 seconds

Since January 2022, the SFC and the HKMA have set out their expectations in a joint circular on virtual-asset (VA) activities [S:e4828fcba126, p.1]. The circular covers intermediaries, including banks registered with the SFC [S:e4828fcba126, p.1]. The current base text is dated 22 December 2023. It replaced the two earlier versions [S:8a016a912528, para 3]. A supplemental circular of 30 September 2025 added refinements and relaxations [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 2]. HKMA guidance of 27 May 2026 eased the rules for licensed stablecoins [S:a0bd48559762, Background]. Licensed stablecoins are tokens pegged to one or more official currencies [S:a0bd48559762, Background]. The same guidance allowed VA financing, meaning credit for VA dealing [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2]. The regulators say VA-related products are very likely complex [S:8a016a912528, para 5]. Complex ones should generally go only to professional investors (PIs), who meet set wealth or institutional tests [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.1]. Firms should test the VA knowledge of clients other than institutional and qualified corporate PIs [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.2, 17]. Dealing should go through SFC-licensed platforms [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.2, 17]. For licensed stablecoins, dealing can also go through the licensed issuer [S:a0bd48559762, para (1)]. The SFC imposes the detailed conduct rules as conditions on the bank's SFC registration [S:8a016a912528, paras 19, 31]. The bank should tell the SFC in advance of new VA activities or changes, and the HKMA where applicable [S:8a016a912528, paras 19, 31].

## Key facts

- The 22 December 2023 circular fully replaced both earlier joint circulars [S:8a016a912528, para 3].
- VA-related products mainly invest in VAs, get their value mainly from VAs, or track VA returns [S:8a016a912528, para 5]. The regulators say such products are very likely complex [S:8a016a912528, para 5]. So suitability checks (does the product fit the client?) apply even without a solicitation or recommendation [S:8a016a912528, para 5]. The exception is the exchange-traded products in paras 7 and 9 [S:8a016a912528, para 5].
- Complex VA-related products should be offered only to PIs, apart from a limited set of products [S:8a016a912528, para 6.1]. The set covers certain VA derivatives traded on exchanges the SFC specifies, plus SFC-authorised VA funds [S:8a016a912528, paras 7, 9, 9.1]. For the funds, the exchange is the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK). For these exchange-traded products, a firm can execute a client's order without a suitability check if it made no solicitation or recommendation [S:8a016a912528, paras 7, 9, 9.1]. The knowledge test still applies [S:8a016a912528, paras 7, 9, 9.1].
- Firms should assess the VA knowledge of every client except institutional PIs and qualified corporate PIs [S:8a016a912528, para 6.2 and fn 10]. A one-off test is acceptable [S:8a016a912528, para 6.2 and fn 10]. A client without the knowledge may trade only after the firm has trained them [S:8a016a912528, para 6.2 and fn 10].
- Firms should partner only with SFC-licensed platforms for VA dealing [S:8a016a912528, paras 17–18]. They can introduce clients to the platform or use an omnibus account (one account holding many clients' assets) [S:8a016a912528, paras 17–18]. They should offer VA dealing only to clients of their Type 1 (dealing in securities) business [S:8a016a912528, paras 17–18].
- Before serving retail dealing clients, a firm should set an exposure limit for each client [S:8a016a912528, para 20(a)(ii), (iv)]. The limit should reflect the client's financial situation, including net worth [S:8a016a912528, para 20(a)(ii), (iv)]. The firm should also restrict these clients to tokens the platform offers to retail investors [S:8a016a912528, para 20(a)(ii), (iv)].
- Advice on VAs is limited to Type 1 or Type 4 (advising on securities) clients [S:8a016a912528, paras 26, 28(a)]. Any VA recommended to a retail client should appear in at least two acceptable indices [S:8a016a912528, paras 26, 28(a)]. Those indices should come from at least two different index providers [S:8a016a912528, paras 26, 28(a)].
- Appendix 7 sets extra terms for managing some portfolios that invest in VAs (RA9: Type 9 regulated activity, asset management) [S:8a016a912528, paras 23–24]. A portfolio triggers these terms if it has a stated VA objective [S:8a016a912528, paras 23–24]. It also triggers them if it plans to put 10% or more of gross asset value (GAV) into VAs [S:8a016a912528, paras 23–24]. Discretionary VA dealing that is only incidental to Type 1 business should stay below 10% of GAV [S:8a016a912528, paras 23–24].
- The September 2025 supplement allows staking for clients the firm already deals VAs for [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4–5]. Staking means locking up tokens to help run a blockchain in return for rewards. Staking should go only through segregated accounts at an SFC-licensed platform or an AI [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4–5]. Segregated accounts keep client assets apart from the firm's own.
- The same supplement allows trades through a licensed platform's off-platform trading services [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 6–7]. These are services the platform runs outside its main trading venue. The regulators do not treat a client's subscription for, or redemption of, products using VAs as providing VA dealing services [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 6–7]. The same applies to in-kind fund flows (paying in or taking out VAs instead of cash) [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 6–7].
- The net-worth check and the VA-futures risk disclosure do not apply to institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 8–9].
- From May 2026, RIs may partner with the licensed issuer of a Relevant Stablecoin as well as with VATPs [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (1)]. A Relevant Stablecoin is a specified stablecoin issued under a Stablecoins Ordinance licence [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (1)]. The same guidance removes paragraph 20(a)(iii) [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (1)]. So retail omnibus dealing may now go through a VA trading platform (VATP) that serves PIs only [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (1)]. The condition is that retail clients trade only tokens that VATP has admitted for retail trading [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (1)].
- Some clients have accounts clearly marked as Relevant-Stablecoin-only. For them, no knowledge test or exposure limit is needed [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a)–(b); S:31b4f67e0233, paras 6.2A, 6.4, 6.4A]. For any client, Relevant Stablecoin holdings need not count towards the exposure limit [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a)–(b); S:31b4f67e0233, paras 6.2A, 6.4, 6.4A].
- RIs providing VA dealing may now offer VA financing, but only to their securities margin clients [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (1), (2), (4) and fn 5]. Where the RI relies on VA collateral, only bitcoin, ether and Relevant Stablecoins are eligible [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (1), (2), (4) and fn 5]. Bitcoin and ether get a haircut of at least 60% [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (1), (2), (4) and fn 5]. A haircut is a cut to the value the lender counts.
- In January 2026 the SFC reprimanded and fined a licensed broker HK$4 million [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–5]. Between November 2018 and November 2022, the broker let retail clients trade VA products that the 2018 and 2022 circulars limited to PIs [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–5]. The 1,446 trades involved 32 VA products [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–5]. The broker did not assess VA knowledge or give VA-specific warnings [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–5]. It had no specific product due diligence procedures for VA products [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–5].

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Classify every VA-linked product [S:8a016a912528, paras 5–10]. Then apply the PI-only, suitability and knowledge-test rules that follow from that classification [S:8a016a912528, paras 5–10].
- Carry out extra due diligence, using Appendix 4, on any VA fund the SFC has not authorised [S:8a016a912528, para 12.3].
- Give VA risk disclosures and warning statements to clients other than institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:8a016a912528, para 15].
- Receive or withdraw client VAs only through segregated accounts [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)]. Use accounts held at the partner platform or at an AI that meets HKMA custody standards [S:8a016a912528, para 20(b)].
- For Relevant Stablecoins, disclose material information on how the coin keeps its value and how redemption works [S:a0bd48559762, paras (5), (8), (10)]. Commission rebates or other benefits should not be the main basis for soliciting or recommending a coin [S:a0bd48559762, paras (5), (8), (10)].

**Controls & monitoring**
- Have adequate policies, procedures and controls for Relevant Stablecoin activities, and train staff [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9].
- Keep Relevant-Stablecoin-only clients to Relevant Stablecoins [S:a0bd48559762, fn 4]. Apply the full rules, including the knowledge test, before dealing in any other VA for them [S:a0bd48559762, fn 4].
- Keep the listed records on client VAs, staking and VA financing for at least seven years [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 9.6–9.8]. Keep records of orders and instructions on client VAs for at least two years [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 9.6–9.8].
- Get a signed staking-risk acknowledgement before staking for clients other than institutional or qualified corporate PIs [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.7(b), 8.8].
- Monitor the volatility of VA collateral in real time when relying on it [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (5)(b), (9)]. Treat loans secured only by VAs as clean loans, meaning unsecured for capital purposes [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (5)(b), (9)]. The reason is that the Banking (Capital) Rules do not recognise VA collateral [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (5)(b), (9)].

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Tell the SFC in advance, and the HKMA where applicable, about any new VA activity or change to one [S:8a016a912528, para 31]. That includes a change in the type of client served [S:8a016a912528, para 31].
- Notify before the first client VA deposit or withdrawal, the first staking service, or the first VA-funded subscription or redemption [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 10].
- A bank that is an AI but not an RI should follow the same standards [S:1babdb609fc8, pp.1–2]. It should discuss any Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management with the HKMA before starting [S:1babdb609fc8, pp.1–2].
- Report any actual or suspected material breach of the terms to the SFC and the HKMA as soon as practicable [S:31b4f67e0233, para 12.1].

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Confirm that the platform holds an actual SFC licence [S:8a016a912528, para 17 and fn 20]. Some platforms are only treated as licensed under Schedule 3G of the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO). Those platforms do not count [S:8a016a912528, para 17 and fn 20].
- Use only an SFC-licensed platform or an AI as the staking service provider [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.2–8.3]. Confirm it has SFC approval or has discussed the service with the HKMA [S:31b4f67e0233, paras 8.2–8.3].
- For in-kind or VA-funded subscriptions, hold the VAs only at SFC-licensed platforms or AIs [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(b)–(c)]. For those transfers, comply with the VA chapter (Chapter 12) of the SFC's anti-money-laundering (AML) guideline [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(b)–(c)].

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The regime reaches us as an RI through conditions the SFC puts on our registration, consulting the HKMA where applicable [S:8a016a912528, para 19; S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]. The HKMA is our frontline, day-to-day supervisor [S:8a016a912528, para 19; S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3].
- Our AML baseline is the HKMA AML Guideline [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 fn 1]. As an RI we are also required to take into account the VA chapter (Chapter 12) of the SFC guideline [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 fn 1].

**To business heads**
- SFC-authorised VA funds are not PI-only, and retail clients can buy listed ones on SEHK [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 6.2, 9, 9.1]. We should still assess VA knowledge first. Overseas VA exchange-traded products that are not derivatives remain PI-only [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 6.2, 9, 9.1].
- Since May 2026, clearly ring-fenced Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts need no knowledge test and no exposure limit [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (3)]. The coin must come from an HKMA-licensed issuer [S:a0bd48559762, p.1 and para (3)].

**To the CEO**
- The regulators relaxed parts of the regime in December 2023, September 2025 and May 2026 [S:8a016a912528, para 9; S:d5ebca32c92b, para 2; S:a0bd48559762, Background]. Complex-product rules, knowledge tests and licensed venues remain [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.2, 17].
- In January 2026 the SFC fined a broker HK$4 million for VA distribution failures from 2018 to 2022 [S:415e4629a80f, paras 1–2].

**To Risk / CRO**
- Margin clients can get VA financing [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (4), (9) and fn 5]. Only bitcoin, ether and Relevant Stablecoins count as VA collateral, with at least a 60% haircut on bitcoin and ether [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (4), (9) and fn 5]. VA collateral gets no capital recognition [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (4), (9) and fn 5].
- Discretionary VA exposure under Type 1 should stay below 10% of the client account's GAV [S:31b4f67e0233, para 4.7].

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** All VA products are PI-only. **Fact:** SFC-authorised VA funds are not PI-only [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 7, 9]. Certain VA derivatives traded on specified exchanges are also exempt. A knowledge test is still needed [S:8a016a912528, paras 6.1, 7, 9].
- **Myth:** VA financing is banned. **Fact:** For RIs, that was true until May 2026 [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background and paras (1)–(2)]. RIs providing VA dealing may now offer it to their securities margin clients under HKMA conditions [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background and paras (1)–(2)].
- **Myth:** The 2026 stablecoin relief covers any stablecoin and any bank. **Fact:** It covers only Relevant Stablecoins and only RIs [S:a0bd48559762, p.2 and fn 3]. It does not cover a licensed issuer for its own coin [S:a0bd48559762, p.2 and fn 3]. A separate letter tells AIs that are not RIs to follow the same standards [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1].
- **Myth:** Deleting para 20(a)(iii) only matters for stablecoins. **Fact:** The deletion lets RIs serve retail clients in any VA through PI-only VATPs [S:a0bd48559762, para (1)]. This works only if each token has been admitted for retail trading [S:a0bd48559762, para (1)].
- **Myth:** The 2022 circular still sets the rules. **Fact:** The December 2023 circular replaced the 2022 and October 2023 circulars [S:8a016a912528, para 3]. Firms could keep serving existing clients found to have VA knowledge by tests done before 22 December 2023 [S:8a016a912528, fn 40].

## Open items

- The standalone licensing regimes for VA dealing, advisory and management are at the conclusions stage [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]. The conclusions state that they will replace the current approach of putting terms and conditions on SFC registrations [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62].
- No arrangement is planned to let existing VA advisory or management providers carry on while they apply for a licence [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 63, 67]. Instead, a faster approval process is planned for LCs and RIs already providing those services [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 63, 67]. The sources reviewed contain no bill text or start date.
- A June 2026 reply to a Legislative Council (LegCo) question covered a bill for VA dealing, custodian, advisory and management regimes [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]. The reply said the bill would go to LegCo within 2026 [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]. The sources reviewed do not show that it has been introduced.
- The SFC press release on the January 2026 fine refers to a Statement of Disciplinary Action [S:415e4629a80f, Notes 3 and 5]. We have not reviewed that statement here.
- Footnote 25 of the 2023 circular says firms should not provide financial accommodation (credit) for clients to acquire VAs [S:8a016a912528, fn 25]. For RIs, the May 2026 terms now allow it where the firm engages in securities margin financing [S:31b4f67e0233, para 4.3; S:e64b7b0267b2, p.5]. Registry summaries of the 2023 circular that still describe financing as prohibited are out of date.
