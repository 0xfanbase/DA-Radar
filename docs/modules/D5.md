# D5 Tax and information reporting

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After this module you can explain three things:
- what the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) bill would require;
- where CARF overlaps with the bank's existing Common Reporting Standard (CRS) duties;
- what the government has said about tax concessions (tax breaks) for digital-asset funds.

CRS is the global standard under which banks and other financial institutions report accounts held by foreign tax residents to the tax authority.

## In 60 seconds

CARF is a global tax standard from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD). [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] Under CARF, tax authorities exchange tax data on crypto-asset transactions once a year. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] Hong Kong committed to CARF in December 2024. [S:ea81f92ea327, para 1] The government consulted on it in December 2025. [S:252a7f86684e, para 1]

The bill is the Inland Revenue (Amendment) (CARF and Amended CRS) Bill 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] The bill appeared in the Gazette (the government's official journal) on 22 May 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] It had its First Reading in the Legislative Council (LegCo) on 3 June 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] The documents reviewed do not show the bill passing as of 25 September 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview]

If passed, CARF duties would start on 1 January 2027, and amended CRS duties on 1 January 2028. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)-(4)]

The bill calls a business that carries out crypto exchange transactions for customers a reporting crypto-asset service provider (RCASP). [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, ss.50M, 50O-50Q, 80R] An RCASP with a Hong Kong link would have to register, check its customers (due diligence), report and keep records. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, ss.50M, 50O-50Q, 80R] Failures could lead to fines. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, ss.50M, 50O-50Q, 80R]

CRS already covers bank accounts. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.3-4.5] The amended CRS adds crypto-linked assets and digital money products. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.3-4.5]

Separately, the government said it would extend fund and family-office tax breaks to digital assets. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]; [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 98] It is aiming at the 2025/26 tax year (year of assessment). [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]; [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 98]

## Key facts

- On 13 December 2024, the government told the OECD's Global Forum on tax transparency that it would implement CARF. [S:ea81f92ea327, paras 1, 4] It aimed to have local law in place by 2026 and first data exchanges from 2028. [S:ea81f92ea327, paras 1, 4] It committed to two-way exchanges, and only with partners that protect data. [S:ea81f92ea327, paras 1, 4]
- The December 2025 consultation paper proposed amending Hong Kong's main tax law, the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112). [S:252a7f86684e, paras 4, 7] The changes covered both CARF and the amended CRS. [S:252a7f86684e, paras 4, 7] Comments closed on 6 February 2026. [S:252a7f86684e, paras 4, 7]
- The consultation described CARF and the amended CRS as finalised global standards. [S:275d5cc93a88, para 8, PDF p.8 (printed p.4)] It asked for views only on choices for putting them in place locally. [S:275d5cc93a88, para 8, PDF p.8 (printed p.4)]
- The bill appeared in the Gazette on 22 May 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] It went to LegCo for its First Reading (the formal first step in LegCo) on 3 June 2026. [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1] LegCo's notice for that sitting said the Second Reading debate would be adjourned (put off to a later date). [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1]
- If passed, Part 2 of the bill (CARF) would start on 1 January 2027, and Part 3 (amended CRS) on 1 January 2028. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(2)-(4)] The rest would start on the day the new law is published in the Gazette. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(2)-(4)]
- The bill defines an RCASP as any individual or entity that, as a business, carries out crypto exchange transactions for customers. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M "reporting crypto-asset service provider"] This covers acting as the other side of the trade, acting as a broker, or running a trading venue. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M "reporting crypto-asset service provider"]
- Under the bill, an RCASP with any of the following Hong Kong links would be a Hong Kong RCASP (HKRCASP): [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50N(1)-(2)]
  - it is tax resident in Hong Kong;
  - it is set up under Hong Kong law, and is either a legal person or a tax-return filer with the IRD;
  - it is managed from Hong Kong;
  - it has a regular place of business in Hong Kong;
  - it carries out the relevant transactions through a Hong Kong branch.
- The bill would require an HKRCASP to register with the Commissioner of Inland Revenue (the head of the IRD). [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50O(1)-(2)] The deadline is 31 January of the year after it first meets a Hong Kong link test (a "nexus" test). [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50O(1)-(2)]
- CARF covers three kinds of reportable transaction: crypto-to-fiat exchanges (crypto for ordinary money), crypto-to-crypto exchanges, and transfers. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Scope of crypto-assets covered' and 'Transactions subject to reporting'] Central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) and specified electronic money products (certain digital forms of ordinary money) are outside CARF. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Scope of crypto-assets covered' and 'Transactions subject to reporting']
- RCASPs would have to get a tax-residence self-certification from each new customer and check that it is reasonable. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements'] A self-certification is the customer's own statement of where they are tax resident. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements'] For existing users, they would have 12 months after the rules start. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements']
- The bill proposes that records be kept for six years after the related return's due date. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Record keeping requirements'] If a former RCASP entity is dissolved (closed down), its last directors would have to make sure the records are still kept. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Record keeping requirements']
- An HKRCASP would commit an offence if it failed, without reasonable excuse, to register, do due diligence or file a return. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(1)-(3)] On conviction, the fine would be at level 3 (a band on Hong Kong's standard scale of fines). [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(1)-(3)] Using a service provider would not, on its own, be a reasonable excuse. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(1)-(3)]
- Extra fines would apply. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)] A registration, return or notice failure would cost a further HK$500 a day while it continues after conviction. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)] A due-diligence breach could instead bring a fine of HK$1,000 per affected user or controlling person. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)] This applies if that total is higher than the level 3 fine. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)] A controlling person is someone who controls a customer that is an entity.
- The bill also proposes a financial penalty, set by the Commissioner, in place of prosecution. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Penalty provisions'] The firm could make its case in writing and appeal to the Board of Review (an independent tax appeals body). [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Penalty provisions']; [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, ss.82E-82F]
- Under the amended CRS, CBDCs and specified electronic money products join the "depository account" category (the CRS term for deposit-type accounts). [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.3-4.5] Derivatives that reference crypto-assets become financial assets. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.3-4.5] Crypto-assets become eligible investments for investment entities, such as funds. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.3-4.5]
- The consultation covered entities that are both a CRS reporting financial institution and an RCASP. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.14-4.17] It proposed that they report crypto sale or redemption proceeds under both regimes, not once. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.14-4.17]
- The government's Policy Statement 2.0 on digital assets said it would propose a change to profits tax concessions. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15] The change was to add specified digital assets to the qualifying transactions for privately offered funds and FIHVs. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15] It was to take effect from the 2025/26 year of assessment, if LegCo approves. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- The 2026-27 Budget said a bill was due in the first half of 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 98] That bill would class digital assets as qualifying investments for these concessions. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 98] The target is the 2025/26 year of assessment. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 98]

## What your bank must do

These duties bind only once the bill passes and takes effect. The bill is not yet law.

**Obligations**
- [ ] Map whether any group entity carries out crypto exchange transactions for customers as a business. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M] Include acting as a go-between. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M]
- [ ] Test each such entity, including branches, against the Hong Kong link (nexus) rules. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50N(1)-(2)]
- [ ] Plan to register by 31 January of the year after the entity first meets a nexus test. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50O(2)]
- [ ] Plan for due diligence from 1 January 2027. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)] Collect self-certifications from existing users within 12 months. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements']
- [ ] Prepare CRS systems for amended CRS due diligence from 1 January 2028. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(4)]; [S:275d5cc93a88, para 4.18]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep CARF due-diligence evidence and reporting records for six years after each return's due date. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Record keeping requirements']
- [ ] Keep oversight of any outsourced CARF work. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(2)] Under the bill, using a service provider would not in itself be a reasonable excuse. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(2)]
- [ ] Plan to send test data files to the IRD for checking if the bank uses its own in-house software. [S:275d5cc93a88, Chapter 6 item (1)(c)] The consultation's timetable set the fourth quarter of 2027 for this testing. [S:275d5cc93a88, Chapter 6 item (1)(c)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Note that CARF registration and returns would go to the IRD, not to the HKMA or SFC. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Reporting portal and mandatory registration requirement'] The IRD's planned CARF Portal is the channel for them. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Reporting portal and mandatory registration requirement'] Watch for the portal and the data schema (the technical format for the data). [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Reporting portal and mandatory registration requirement']

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Note that a crypto trading platform or broker the bank partners with may itself be an RCASP with its own reporting duties. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Individuals and entities subject to due diligence and reporting requirements']

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The CARF bill had its First Reading on 3 June 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview] If passed, CARF duties would start on 1 January 2027, and amended CRS duties on 1 January 2028. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview]
- Using a vendor for CARF work is not, in itself, a reasonable excuse for failing to register, check customers or report. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(2)]

**To business heads**
- Offering crypto exchange services to customers as a business could make an entity an RCASP. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M] That is true even when it only acts as a go-between. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M]
- New crypto customers will need to give a tax-residence self-certification. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements'] Existing customers will need to give one within 12 months of the start date. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements']

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong committed to CARF in December 2024. [S:ea81f92ea327, para 4] It targets first data exchanges from 2028. [S:ea81f92ea327, para 4]
- The government linked CARF to Hong Kong's reputation as a financial centre. [S:ab980a841782, para 3]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Under the bill, fines could add up. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)] A failure that continues after conviction could cost HK$500 a day. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)] A due-diligence breach could instead cost HK$1,000 per affected user or controlling person. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)]
- The consultation proposed that entities in both regimes report crypto proceeds under both CARF and CRS. [S:275d5cc93a88, para 4.17]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** CARF is already law. **Fact:** CARF is in a bill before LegCo. The IRD says the dates apply only if the bill passes. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview]
- **Myth:** Registration is due by 31 December of the year the link is first met. **Fact:** That date was the consultation's main proposal. [S:275d5cc93a88, Chapter 6 item (1)(b) and footnote 45] The bill sets 31 January of the year after the link is first met. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50O(2)]
- **Myth:** Amended CRS starts in 2029. **Fact:** Its duties start on 1 January 2028. [S:275d5cc93a88, para 4.18]; [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(4)] The first exchange of amended CRS data is in 2029. [S:275d5cc93a88, para 4.18]
- **Myth:** CARF covers CBDCs and e-money. **Fact:** CARF leaves them out. The amended CRS brings them in instead. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 1.5, 4.3]
- **Myth:** The HKMA or SFC supervises CARF. **Fact:** The IRD runs registration, returns and penalties. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Reporting portal' and 'Penalty provisions']

## Open items

- The documents reviewed for this guide do not record the CARF bill passing as of 25 September 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview]; [S:d5097f447cf9, para 1]
- The consultation also proposed compulsory CRS registration for all reporting financial institutions (RFIs) from January 2027. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 5.3, 5.9-5.12, 5.15, Chapter 6 item (2)(a)] It also proposed heavier CRS penalties. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 5.3, 5.9-5.12, 5.15, Chapter 6 item (2)(a)] The bill's list of provisions in the documents reviewed shows no registration clause for RFIs. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Contents] The documents do not say whether that proposal moved to other legislation.
- The IRD page does not say whether the final bill keeps the proposed default of reporting under both regimes.
- The IRD page says two lists will come in due course: reportable jurisdictions and partner jurisdictions. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Lists of reportable jurisdictions' and 'Reporting portal'] The CARF Portal and the data schema will also come in due course. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Lists of reportable jurisdictions' and 'Reporting portal']
- Concessions for funds and FIHVs: the 2026 Policy Address says a bill easing taxes has already passed. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 40] That bill covers fund vehicles, single-family offices and people who earn carried interest (fund managers' share of profits). [S:ef3648ff1610, para 40] No document reviewed for this guide has a bill text confirming how the enacted law treats digital assets.
- The documents reviewed do not say whether tokenised deposits (bank deposits recorded as digital tokens) count as specified electronic money products for CRS.
