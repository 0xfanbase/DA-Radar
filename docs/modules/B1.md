# B1 Your bank's legal status for digital assets

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain which legal hat the bank wears for each digital-asset activity, who supervises it, and which rulebook binds which entity in the group.

## In 60 seconds

A bank in Hong Kong is an Authorized Institution (AI) under the Banking Ordinance, supervised by the HKMA. When it carries on SFC-regulated activities such as dealing in or advising on securities, it registers with the SFC and becomes a Registered Institution (RI); the HKMA stays its day-to-day supervisor under SPM module SB-1. A group subsidiary licensed by the SFC is a Licensed Corporation (LC) and is supervised by the SFC directly. The joint SFC–HKMA virtual-asset (VA) circulars reach the bank in its RI capacity, through the HKMA. Under the coming VA dealing and custody regimes, banks will register with the SFC while the HKMA remains their frontline regulator; bank subsidiaries doing VA custody will be SFC-regulated. Some HKMA circulars bind all AIs, others only RIs, so scope checks matter.

## Key facts

- An AI that carries on regulated activities must register with the SFC as an RI under SFO s.119. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2, 2.2.1]
- The SFC holds ultimate responsibility, but the HKMA is the RI's front-line supervisor, and the SFC may rely on HKMA supervision under SFO s.5(3). [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- Each RI appoints at least two executive officers per regulated activity, each needing the Monetary Authority's prior written consent; one must always be available. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.4]
- Every relevant individual performing a regulated function must be on the HKMA Register before starting. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.1, 4.3.5]
- An associated entity of an RI is a company in a controlling-entity relationship with it that receives or holds the RI's client assets in Hong Kong. [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1]
- An RI should notify the SFC and HKMA if it intends to engage in any VA activity or change it, including the client type. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- The HKMA AML Guideline binds all AIs, including RIs; RIs and AI associated entities must also have regard to the SFC AML guideline, including its Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 and footnote 1]
- The joint circular addresses intermediaries, meaning LCs and RIs; it requires advance notice to the SFC and, where applicable, the HKMA. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- The Sep 2025 supplement lets LCs and RIs offer staking through segregated accounts with an SFC-licensed platform or an AI. [S:b79266f3da51, paras 4-5]
- The May 2026 Relevant Stablecoin relief applies to RIs only; it does not reach AIs that are not RIs or an issuer's dealings in its own coin. [S:a0bd48559762, footnote 3, p.2]
- A separate HKMA letter asks all AIs doing Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management to meet the RI standards and to discuss with the HKMA first. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1; Implementation, p.2]
- Under the Stablecoins Ordinance, only specified licensed institutions may offer a fiat-referenced stablecoin in Hong Kong. [S:35e1ac2b815a, para 3]
- A bank can itself be a licensed issuer: HSBC holds licence FRS02. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- For an issuer that is an AI, the one-third independent-director expectation does not apply; the Banking Ordinance governs instead. [S:240be6833157, para 7.1.3]
- Under the proposed VA dealing regime, banks must register with the SFC, in consultation with the HKMA. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 1.10]
- The HKMA would be frontline regulator for banks' VA dealing, with powers to inspect premises and investigate. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 2.36]
- For VA custody, the HKMA would be frontline regulator for banks, but VA custodians that are subsidiaries of locally incorporated banks would be regulated by the SFC. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]
- Banks doing VA custody would rely on existing HKMA capital requirements instead of the HK$10m/HK$3m custodian baseline. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 41-42]
- Only banks may register (rather than be licensed) for VA advisory or VA management services. [S:6186c01c6649, footnotes 9-10]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Hold SFC registration for each regulated activity carried on, with at least two HKMA-consented executive officers per activity. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.1, 2.2.4]
- [ ] Register relevant individuals on the HKMA Register before they start. [S:8934af8cc884, para 4.3.1]
- [ ] Apply the HKMA AML Guideline bank-wide; for RI and associated-entity business, also have regard to SFC AML Chapter 12. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- [ ] Make sure subsidiaries of the locally incorporated bank that do custody or staking comply with HKMA standards. [S:ed61d7c335e7, p.2]; [S:4641e6c404d2, p.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Map every digital-asset activity to its entity and capacity (AI, RI, LC subsidiary, associated entity) before applying a circular. [S:a0bd48559762, footnote 3, p.2]
- [ ] Keep the relevant-individual database current and notify changes within 7 business days. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.6, 5.5]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the SFC and HKMA before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- [ ] Notify the HKMA in advance of an intended SFC registration application. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.3]
- [ ] Engage the SFC or HKMA early on pre-application for the coming dealing regime. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Confirm a stablecoin issuer counterparty is on the HKMA register. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register note]
- [ ] Confirm VA dealing partners are SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Our VA conduct rules reach us as an RI; the HKMA supervises, the SFC keeps ultimate responsibility. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- Scope matters: some May 2026 relief applies only to RIs, while the stablecoin-activity letter covers all AIs. [S:a0bd48559762, fn 3]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

**To business heads**
- New VA services need advance notice to the SFC and HKMA through our RI registration. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- A custody subsidiary would be SFC-regulated under the coming custodian regime, not HKMA-supervised as the bank is. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]

**To the CEO**
- Under the coming regimes, the bank registers with the SFC while the HKMA stays frontline regulator. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- Executive officers can lose HKMA consent for misconduct, which could affect the RI's ability to operate. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 6.3, 6.7]

**To Risk / CRO**
- A bank VA custodian would use existing HKMA capital rules, not the custodian capital baseline. [S:f41a543388bc, para 41]
- VA-only secured loans count as clean loans for capital purposes. [S:e64b7b0267b2, para (9), p.5]

## Common mix-ups

- "SFC AML Chapter 12 binds the whole bank." It applies to RIs and AI associated entities on a have-regard basis only. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- "The joint circular is an SFC rule aimed at brokers, not us." It addresses RIs too, and reaches banks through HKMA frontline supervision. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- "All AIs get the May 2026 stablecoin relief." That circular covers RIs only. [S:a0bd48559762, footnote 3, p.2]
- "A bank subsidiary custodian is supervised like the bank." It would be regulated by the SFC. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]
- "VA financing rules apply to every AI." The May 2026 financing circular applies to RIs. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.1-2]
