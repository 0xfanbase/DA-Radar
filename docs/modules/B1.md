# B1 Your bank's legal status for digital assets

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After this module you can explain the bank's legal role in each digital-asset activity. You can also say who supervises each activity and which rules bind which company in the group.

## In 60 seconds

A bank in Hong Kong is an Authorized Institution (AI). The Banking Ordinance governs how it is authorized and supervised. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.1] Some banks carry on "regulated activities", a term the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) defines. A bank doing so must register with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) as a Registered Institution (RI). [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the RI's front-line (day-to-day) supervisor. Its Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) module SB-1 explains how. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] A company the SFC licenses under SFO section 116 or 117 is a Licensed Corporation (LC). [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1] The joint SFC–HKMA circulars on virtual-asset (VA) activities address "intermediaries". That term covers both licensed corporations (LCs) and registered institutions (RIs). [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1; S:8a016a912528, para 31] Under the proposed VA dealing and custody regimes, banks would register with the SFC, with the HKMA as their frontline regulator. [S:f673ef6dcb63, paras 1.10, 2.36; S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)] VA custodians owned by locally incorporated banks would be regulated by the SFC instead. [S:f673ef6dcb63, paras 1.10, 2.36; S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)] Some May 2026 HKMA circulars go to all authorized institutions (AIs), and others only to RIs. So always check which firms a circular covers. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1; S:a0bd48559762, p.1]

## Key facts

- An AI that carries on business in regulated activities is required to register with the SFC as an RI. The SFC registers AIs under SFO section 119. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2, 2.2.1]
- The SFC holds ultimate responsibility for RIs. The HKMA is the RI's front-line supervisor. Under SFO section 5(3), the SFC may rely on the HKMA's supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- Each RI needs at least two executive officers for each regulated activity. Executive officers are the senior staff who directly supervise that activity. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 1.1.1, 2.2.4]
- Each executive officer needs the written consent of the Monetary Authority (MA). At least one of them must be available at all times. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 1.1.1, 2.2.4]
- Only people entered on the HKMA Register (the HKMA's public list of RI staff) may perform a regulated function for an RI. So the RI should submit their details before they start. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.1, 4.3.5]
- An associated entity is a company linked to an RI through control that receives or holds the RI's client assets in Hong Kong. [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1]
- An RI should tell the SFC and HKMA if it plans to start any VA activity. It should also tell them before changing one, including the type of clients it serves. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- The HKMA AML/CFT Guideline applies to all AIs, including RIs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 and footnote 1]
- RIs, and associated entities that are AIs, must also take into account the SFC's AML/CFT guideline. That includes its Chapter 12 on virtual assets. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 and footnote 1]
- The joint circular addresses intermediaries, meaning LCs and RIs. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1]
- The joint circular reminds intermediaries to tell the SFC (and the HKMA, where relevant) before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1]
- The September 2025 supplement lets LCs and RIs offer staking to clients they deal in virtual assets for. Staking means locking up clients' tokens to help run a blockchain, in return for rewards. [S:b79266f3da51, paras 4-5]
- Firms may offer staking only through segregated (ring-fenced) accounts. Those accounts are held with an SFC-licensed platform, an AI, or a subsidiary of a locally incorporated AI. [S:b79266f3da51, paras 4-5]
- The May 2026 Relevant Stablecoin circular is addressed to RIs. Relevant Stablecoins are stablecoins issued by HKMA-licensed issuers. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]
- That circular's VA dealing, advisory and asset management rules do not apply to AIs that are not RIs. The same goes for the joint circulars' rules. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]
- Those rules also do not apply to an HKMA-licensed issuer for the coin it issues itself. The HKMA expects these firms to meet similar standards that it issues separately. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]
- A separate HKMA letter covers all AIs that deal in, advise on or manage portfolios of Relevant Stablecoins. They should follow the RI standards. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1; Implementation, p.2]
- The same letter reminds these AIs to discuss their plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1; Implementation, p.2]
- Under the Stablecoins Ordinance, only specified licensed institutions may offer a fiat-referenced stablecoin (one tied to an official currency) in Hong Kong. [S:35e1ac2b815a, para 3]
- The HKMA register of licensed stablecoin issuers lists The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited. Its licence is FRS02, effective 10 Apr 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- The HKMA's guideline for licensed stablecoin issuers sets board expectations in para 7.1.3. These include having at least one-third independent non-executive directors. Those expectations do not apply to a licensed issuer that is an AI. [S:240be6833157, para 7.1.3]
- Such an issuer should follow the Banking Ordinance and related guidance instead. [S:240be6833157, para 7.1.3]
- The June 2025 VA dealing proposal said banks would have to register with the SFC. The SFC would register them in consultation with the HKMA. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 1.10]
- The proposal also said the HKMA would be the frontline regulator for banks' VA dealing, with powers to inspect premises and investigate suspected breaches. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 2.36]
- For VA custody, the proposal made the HKMA the frontline regulator for banks. VA custodians owned by locally incorporated banks would be regulated by the SFC. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]
- The December 2025 custody conclusions describe a minimum capital level (a floor) for custodians: HK$10m paid-up capital and HK$3m liquid capital. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 41-42]
- The conclusions exempt banks from that floor because existing HKMA capital requirements already apply to them. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 41-42]
- The December 2025 further consultation proposed that only banks could register with the SFC, rather than be licensed, for VA advisory or VA management services. [S:6186c01c6649, footnotes 9-10]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Hold SFC registration for each regulated activity the bank carries on. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.1, 2.2.4]
- [ ] Have at least two executive officers per activity, each with HKMA consent. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.1, 2.2.4]
- [ ] Register relevant individuals (staff who carry out the RI's regulated activities) on the HKMA Register before they start. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.1, 4.3.5]
- [ ] Apply the HKMA AML/CFT Guideline across the whole bank. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- [ ] For the RI and any associated entities that are AIs, also take into account Chapter 12 of the SFC AML/CFT guideline. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- [ ] If the bank is locally incorporated, check that its subsidiaries meet the HKMA standards when they provide digital-asset custody or staking from custody. [S:ed61d7c335e7, p.2]; [S:4641e6c404d2, p.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Before applying a circular, map each digital-asset activity to the company and role doing it: AI, RI, LC subsidiary or associated entity. [S:a0bd48559762, footnote 3, p.2]
- [ ] Keep the relevant-individual database up to date. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.3, 4.3.6, 5.5]
- [ ] Report Register changes and specified fitness information within 7 business days. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.3, 4.3.6, 5.5]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Tell the SFC and HKMA before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- [ ] Tell the HKMA in advance before applying to the SFC for registration. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.3]
- [ ] Consider early contact with the SFC or HKMA before applying under the coming dealing regime. The conclusions encourage this. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Check that any stablecoin issuer you deal with is on the HKMA register. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register note]
- [ ] Check that VA dealing partners are SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]; [S:a0bd48559762, para (1), p.3]
- [ ] For Relevant Stablecoins only, treat the HKMA-licensed issuer of that coin as an allowed dealing partner too. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]; [S:a0bd48559762, para (1), p.3]

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- Our VA conduct rules apply to us because we are an RI. The HKMA supervises us; the SFC keeps ultimate responsibility. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- Check who each paper covers. The May 2026 Relevant Stablecoin circular is addressed to RIs. The separate stablecoin letter covers all AIs. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

**To business heads**
- As an RI, we should tell the SFC and HKMA when we plan to start or change VA services. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- Under the proposed custodian regime, the SFC would regulate a custody subsidiary. The HKMA would be frontline regulator for the bank itself. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]

**To the CEO**
- Under the proposed dealing and custody regimes, the SFC would license and register providers. The HKMA would be frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- The MA can withdraw or suspend an executive officer's consent for misconduct. Each regulated activity must always have at least one executive officer available. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.4, 6.3]

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- A bank VA custodian would use existing HKMA capital rules, not the custodian capital floor. [S:f41a543388bc, para 41]
- The RI financing circular requires loans secured only by virtual assets to be treated as clean (unsecured) loans for capital purposes. [S:e64b7b0267b2, para (9), p.5]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** "SFC AML Chapter 12 binds the whole bank." **Fact:** It reaches only RIs, and associated entities that are AIs. They must take it into account, but it does not bind the rest of the bank. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- **Myth:** "The joint circular is an SFC rule for brokers, not us." **Fact:** It addresses RIs too. It reaches banks through HKMA frontline supervision. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:8934af8cc884, paras 1.1.1, 2.1.3]
- **Myth:** "The May 2026 RI stablecoin circular covers all AIs." **Fact:** It is addressed to RIs. A separate HKMA letter covers AIs that are not RIs. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- **Myth:** "A custodian owned by a bank is supervised like the bank." **Fact:** Under the proposal, the SFC would regulate it. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]
- **Myth:** "VA financing rules apply to every AI." **Fact:** The May 2026 financing circular is addressed to RIs. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.1-2]

## Open items

- On 24 Jun 2026 the Government said it would put a bill to the Legislative Council this year. The bill would cover the VA dealing, custodian, advisory and management regimes. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- The documents reviewed hold no bill text. So the final rules on how banks register are not yet in law. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- The HKMA and SFC documents reviewed do not say how registration under the new VA regimes will fit with an RI's existing SFO registration. They mention only fast-track approval. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 35-36]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 67]
- The documents reviewed do not include the Banking Ordinance or SFO text on executive officers. SB-1 is the only source used here.

## Check yourself

1. Which regulator is the front-line, day-to-day supervisor of a bank that is a Registered Institution (RI)?
   **Answer:** The HKMA is the RI's front-line supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] The SFC holds ultimate responsibility but may rely on that supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
2. How many executive officers must an RI have for each regulated activity?
   **Answer:** At least two executive officers per regulated activity, each with the Monetary Authority's written consent. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 1.1.1, 2.2.4]
3. Does the May 2026 Relevant Stablecoin circular bind every authorized institution (AI)?
   **Answer:** No. That circular is addressed to RIs only. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2] A separate HKMA letter covers all AIs that deal in, advise on or manage Relevant Stablecoins. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
4. What should your RI do before starting or changing a VA dealing, advice or asset-management activity?
   **Answer:** Tell the SFC and HKMA in advance, including for any change in the type of clients it serves. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
5. Under the proposed custodian regime, would a VA custodian owned by a locally incorporated bank be regulated like the bank?
   **Answer:** No. Under the proposal, the SFC would regulate that custodian, while the HKMA would be frontline regulator for the bank. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)] This is a proposal, not yet law.
