# B1 Your bank's legal status for digital assets

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain which legal hat the bank wears for each digital-asset activity, who supervises it, and which rulebook binds which entity in the group.

## In 60 seconds

A bank in Hong Kong is an Authorized Institution (AI), authorized and supervised under the Banking Ordinance. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.1] When it carries on business in regulated activities (a term the Securities and Futures Ordinance, or SFO, defines), it must be registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) as a Registered Institution (RI). The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the RI's front-line supervisor, and its Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) module SB-1 explains how. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] A company licensed by the SFC under SFO s.116 or s.117 is a Licensed Corporation (LC). [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1] The joint SFC–HKMA virtual-asset (VA) circulars address intermediaries, a term that covers both LCs and RIs. [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1; S:8a016a912528, para 31] Under the proposed VA dealing and custody regimes, banks would register with the SFC while the HKMA would be their frontline regulator; VA custodians that are subsidiaries of locally incorporated banks would be regulated by the SFC. [S:f673ef6dcb63, paras 1.10, 2.36; S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)] Some May 2026 HKMA circulars are addressed to all AIs and others only to RIs, so scope checks matter. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1; S:a0bd48559762, p.1]

## Key facts

- An AI carrying on business in regulated activities is required to be registered with the SFC as an RI; the SFC registers AIs under SFO s.119. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2, 2.2.1]
- The SFC holds ultimate responsibility, but the HKMA is the RI's front-line supervisor, and the SFC may rely on HKMA supervision under SFO s.5(3). [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- Each RI needs at least two executive officers to supervise each regulated activity directly. Each needs the Monetary Authority's (MA's) written consent, and at least one of them must be on hand at all times. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 1.1.1, 2.2.4]
- Only individuals entered on the HKMA Register may perform a regulated function for an RI, so they should be submitted before they start. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.1, 4.3.5]
- An associated entity of an RI is a company in a controlling-entity relationship with it that receives or holds the RI's client assets in Hong Kong. [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1]
- An RI should notify the SFC and HKMA if it intends to engage in any VA activity or change it, including the type of clients served. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- The HKMA AML/CFT Guideline applies to all AIs, including RIs; RIs and associated entities that are AIs must also have regard to the SFC AML/CFT guideline, including its Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.3 and footnote 1]
- The joint circular addresses intermediaries (LCs and RIs) and reminds them to give the SFC (and, where relevant, the HKMA) advance notice before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 1.1.1]
- The Sep 2025 supplement lets LCs and RIs offer staking to clients they deal in VAs for, only through segregated accounts with an SFC-licensed platform or an AI (or a subsidiary of a locally incorporated AI). [S:b79266f3da51, paras 4-5]
- The May 2026 Relevant Stablecoin circular is addressed to RIs. It states that the VA dealing, advisory and asset management requirements in the joint circulars and in it do not apply to AIs that are not RIs, nor to an HKMA-licensed issuer for its own coin; those are expected to meet similar standards the HKMA issues separately. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]
- A separate HKMA letter says all AIs doing Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management should follow the RI standards, and reminds them to discuss with the HKMA in advance. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1; Implementation, p.2]
- Under the Stablecoins Ordinance, only specified licensed institutions may offer a fiat-referenced stablecoin in Hong Kong. [S:35e1ac2b815a, para 3]
- The HKMA register of licensed stablecoin issuers lists The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited under licence FRS02, effective 10 Apr 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- For a licensed issuer that is an AI, the para 7.1.3 board expectations (including one-third independent non-executive directors) do not apply; it should follow the Banking Ordinance and related guidance instead. [S:240be6833157, para 7.1.3]
- The June 2025 VA dealing proposal said banks would have to register with the SFC, with the HKMA consulted. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 1.10]
- It proposed that the HKMA be frontline regulator for banks' VA dealing, with powers to inspect premises and investigate suspected non-compliance. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 2.36]
- For VA custody, the proposal made the HKMA frontline regulator for banks, while VA custodians that are subsidiaries of locally incorporated banks would be regulated by the SFC. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]
- The Dec 2025 custody conclusions exempt banks, which are subject to existing HKMA capital requirements, from the HK$10m paid-up capital and HK$3m liquid capital custodian baseline. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 41-42]
- The Dec 2025 further consultation proposed that only banks could register with the SFC (rather than be licensed) for VA advisory or VA management services. [S:6186c01c6649, footnotes 9-10]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Hold SFC registration for each regulated activity carried on, with at least two HKMA-consented executive officers per activity. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.1, 2.2.4]
- [ ] Register relevant individuals on the HKMA Register before they start. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.1, 4.3.5]
- [ ] Apply the HKMA AML/CFT Guideline bank-wide; for the RI and any associated entities that are AIs, also have regard to SFC AML/CFT guideline Chapter 12. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- [ ] If the bank is locally incorporated, make sure its subsidiaries that provide digital-asset custody, or staking from custody, meet the HKMA standards. [S:ed61d7c335e7, p.2]; [S:4641e6c404d2, p.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Map every digital-asset activity to its entity and capacity (AI, RI, LC subsidiary, associated entity) before applying a circular. [S:a0bd48559762, footnote 3, p.2]
- [ ] Keep the relevant-individual database current; notify Register changes and specified fitness information within 7 business days. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 4.3.3, 4.3.6, 5.5]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the SFC and HKMA before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- [ ] Notify the HKMA in advance of an intended SFC registration application. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.3]
- [ ] Consider early pre-application contact with the SFC or HKMA for the coming dealing regime, which the conclusions encourage. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Confirm a stablecoin issuer counterparty is on the HKMA register. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register note]
- [ ] Confirm VA dealing partners are SFC-licensed VATPs or, for Relevant Stablecoins only, the HKMA-licensed issuer of that coin. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]; [S:a0bd48559762, para (1), p.3]

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- Our VA conduct rules reach us as an RI; the HKMA supervises, the SFC keeps ultimate responsibility. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- Scope matters: the May 2026 Relevant Stablecoin circular is addressed to RIs, while the separate stablecoin-activity letter covers all AIs. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

**To business heads**
- As an RI, we should notify the SFC and HKMA when we intend to start or change VA services. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- Under the proposed custodian regime, a custody subsidiary would be regulated by the SFC, while the HKMA would be frontline regulator for the bank itself. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]

**To the CEO**
- Under the proposed dealing and custody regimes, the SFC would license and register providers, while the HKMA would be frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- The MA can withdraw or suspend an executive officer's consent for misconduct, and each regulated activity must always have at least one executive officer on hand. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.4, 6.3]

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- A bank VA custodian would use existing HKMA capital rules, not the custodian capital baseline. [S:f41a543388bc, para 41]
- The RI financing circular requires loans secured solely by VAs to be handled as clean loans for capital purposes. [S:e64b7b0267b2, para (9), p.5]

## Common mix-ups

- "SFC AML Chapter 12 binds the whole bank." It applies to RIs and associated entities that are AIs on a have-regard basis only. [S:7bfdad704174, footnote 1]
- "The joint circular is an SFC rule aimed at brokers, not us." It addresses RIs too, and reaches banks through HKMA frontline supervision. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:8934af8cc884, paras 1.1.1, 2.1.3]
- "The May 2026 RI stablecoin circular covers all AIs." It is addressed to RIs; AIs that are not RIs are covered by a separate HKMA letter. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; footnote 3, p.2]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- "A bank subsidiary custodian is supervised like the bank." Under the proposal it would be regulated by the SFC. [S:30b60438f017, para 2.12(a)]
- "VA financing rules apply to every AI." The May 2026 financing circular is addressed to RIs. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.1-2]
