# D1 Capital and prudential treatment of cryptoassets

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain how Hong Kong turned the Basel cryptoasset standard into binding capital, liquidity and exposure rules, how a cryptoasset gets classified, and what the bank must notify to the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA).

## In 60 seconds

The global bank standard setter, the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS), published a standard on banks' cryptoasset exposures in December 2022 and amended it in July 2024. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.1] The HKMA consulted on it (CP 24.01, February 2024), sent draft rules to the banking associations in January 2025, and the government gazetted three sets of amendment rules in July 2025. [S:27f8bc2f6b2a, paras 1-2; S:ebc5f9b5ee0d, p.1; S:277820235891, para 1] The rules, plus a new guidance module CRP-1 and nine revised Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) modules, took effect on 1 January 2026. [S:277820235891, closing para; S:30b686db29f9, pp.1-2] Every cryptoasset a bank is exposed to is sorted into Group 1a (tokenised traditional assets), 1b (stablecoins with an effective stabilisation mechanism), 2a (Group 2 assets that meet hedging criteria, so limited hedging is recognised) or 2b (everything else, at a 1,250% risk weight). [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.2-1.3.3, 2.1.8; S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, column (c), pp.214-215] New cryptoasset types must be notified to the HKMA and sit in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] In June 2026 the HKMA clarified that one Group 1 condition for tokenised traditional assets on permissionless chains can also be met another way. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]

## Key facts

- CP 24.01 proposed bringing the December 2022 Basel standard into Hong Kong rules no earlier than 1 July 2025, with comments due by 6 May 2024. [S:27f8bc2f6b2a, paras 2, 4, 11]
- In January 2025 the HKMA sent draft rule amendments to the industry, asking for comments by 20 February 2025 and aiming for a 1 January 2026 start to match Basel. [S:ebc5f9b5ee0d, p.1]
- The three amendment rules were gazetted on 11 July 2025, were due to be tabled at LegCo on 16 July 2025, and took effect on 1 January 2026. [S:277820235891, paras 1-2 and closing para]
- The HKMA finalised CRP-1 and revisions to nine other modules and a code of practice on 27 November 2025, all effective 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, pp.1-2]
- CRP-1 is guidance; the binding classification rules sit in Part 12 of the Banking (Capital) Rules (BCR), which prevails if the two differ. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- Group 1 status needs all four classification conditions: same rights and risks as the traditional asset (or an effective stabilisation mechanism); clearly defined, legally enforceable rights and settlement finality; network risks that are sufficiently mitigated and managed; and entities performing key functions that are regulated and supervised (or subject to suitable risk management standards). [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 2.2.1, 2.3.1, 2.4.1, 2.5.1]
- Group 1a is tokenised traditional assets and Group 1b is stablecoins with an effective stabilisation mechanism; anything else is Group 2. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1-1.3.2]
- A Group 2 asset reaches Group 2a only if it meets three hedging criteria, including average market capitalisation of at least HKD 78 billion and 100 or more real price observations. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 3.1.1]
- Group 2b exposures take a 1,250% risk weight, which is also meant to cover their market risk and their credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, column (c), pp.214-215]; [S:5d845665ba83, para 4.3.2]
- Before acquiring a new type of cryptoasset, a bank must notify the HKMA and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's own classification assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- Exposures held before 1 January 2026 must sit in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.4.2]
- If the HKMA disagrees with a bank's classification, it will override it. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.6]
- On permissionless chains, stablecoins issued under the HKMA's licensing regime can be deemed to meet the extra measures; other stablecoins must be under a regime the HKMA considers comparable to the Stablecoins Ordinance. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.6.3]
- The June 2026 letter says the approval condition for tokenised traditional assets on permissionless chains can also be met where a financial market regulator supervises the tokenisation business. The letter took effect immediately. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- CA-G-1 section 8.7 says Group 1a banking-book exposures generally follow the underlying asset's rules, while Group 1b needs separate capital for the reference asset and the redeemer. [S:5d0c13e09312, paras 8.7.1-8.7.2]
- Group 2 exposure limit: a global or domestic systemically important bank (G-SIB or D-SIB) faces an add-on when its Group 2 exposure exceeds 1% of Tier 1 capital, and a different, heavier treatment above 2%. [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(d)]; [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, rows 8-10, p.215]
- Under LM-1 Annex 3, a category 1 institution may count a cryptoasset as high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) only if it is a Group 1a token of an HQLA-eligible asset class and meets the HQLA requirements; an issuer of a stablecoin redeemable within the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) period must generally apply a 100% outflow. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 paras 1, 7]
- Market risk: any cryptoasset exposure removes the de-minimis exemption, the internal models approach cannot be used for instruments referencing Group 2 cryptoassets, and Group 2a position risk is 100% of the net position. [S:b8d2d2a0f9c8, footnote 80, paras 6.1.9, 6.2.8]
- CR-G-8 values crypto exposures under new Division 5A of the Banking (Exposure Limits) Rules; exposures not linked to a counterparty's credit or default risk are disregarded when measuring large exposures to a counterparty or group. [S:0cd3c1a74e5e, paras 2.8.8-2.8.9 and footnote 13]
- CR-L-5 treats a token carrying the economics of equity as share capital for the major-acquisitions consent rules. [S:13365a31e794, footnote 1]
- CA-G-5 says the HKMA may respond to crypto-related weaknesses with capital add-ons, provisions, limits or other measures. [S:349a543ae2f0, para 3.6.6]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Classify every cryptoasset exposure under BCR Part 12 and CRP-1, and keep assessing it on an ongoing basis. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 2.1.1]
- [ ] Move an asset agreed as Group 1a, 1b or 2a to Group 2b at once if it stops meeting its conditions or criteria. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.3]
- [ ] As an RI, treat loans secured only by VA collateral as clean loans for capital purposes. [S:e64b7b0267b2, para (9), p.5]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep policies, staff and IT able to assess cryptoasset risk, and document the evidence for each classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.1]
- [ ] For stablecoins, keep a monitoring framework that checks the stabilisation mechanism works as intended. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.2.4]
- [ ] If the bank is a G-SIB or D-SIB, track the Group 2 exposure ratio against the 1% and 2% thresholds used in disclosure. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, rows 8-10, p.215]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the HKMA before acquiring any new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Give the HKMA classification evidence on request. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.1]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For Group 1 assets, check that every entity redeeming, transferring, storing or settling the asset (or managing its reserves) is regulated and supervised or meets suitable risk standards, and discloses a governance framework. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.5.1]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The capital rules are law from 1 January 2026; CRP-1 is guidance, and the BCR wins if they differ. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1]
- New cryptoasset types need HKMA notification before purchase. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]

**To business heads**
- Until the HKMA agrees a classification, a new cryptoasset sits in Group 2b at a 1,250% risk weight. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]; [S:5d845665ba83, para 4.3.2]
- Stablecoins under the HKMA licensing regime can be deemed to meet the permissionless-chain measures, but the four classification conditions still apply. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1, 2.6.3]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong's rules started on 1 January 2026, matching the Basel timeline. [S:277820235891, closing para]
- The HKMA can override our classifications and add capital or limits if it sees weaknesses. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.6]; [S:349a543ae2f0, para 3.6.6]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Any crypto exposure ends our market-risk de-minimis exemption. [S:b8d2d2a0f9c8, footnote 80]
- For a category 1 institution, a stablecoin it holds is generally treated as non-HQLA, with a 0% LCR inflow and an 85% required stable funding factor under the net stable funding ratio (NSFR). [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 7]

## Common mix-ups

- "The June 2026 CRP-1 letter tightened the rules." It clarified that the para 2.6.4 approval condition can also be met where a financial market regulator supervises the tokenisation business. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- "Internal models are only barred for Group 2a." MR-1 bars them for all instruments referencing Group 2 cryptoassets. [S:b8d2d2a0f9c8, para 6.1.9]
- "The 1%/2% Group 2 limit applies to every bank." The HKMA's return instructions have all Hong Kong-incorporated AIs report the ratio, but only G-SIBs and D-SIBs face the add-on. [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(d)]; [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 11-12]
- "Legacy holdings keep their old treatment." They sit in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.4.2]
- "CBDCs are cryptoassets." They sit outside the standard and are generally treated like the central bank's fiat. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.2.3 and footnote 4]

## Open items

- The April 2026 BCR proposals (for example, removing cryptoassets from the Part 6 "cash item" definition, and rules on hedging counterparty credit risk for netting sets with Group 2b cryptoasset derivatives) closed for comment on 1 June 2026; no final rules are in the corpus. [S:1e9b351e81e7, Items 19, 45]; [S:f6b5ade120fe, landing page]
- The June 2026 letter says CRP-1's wording will reflect the clarification in due course; the corpus holds no updated CRP-1 text. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- The gazetted Banking (Capital) Rules Part 12 text itself is not in the corpus; section references come from HKMA modules and templates.
- The corpus copies of the HKMA's December 2022 BCBS relay letter and the July/August 2025 gazettal letters are catalogue pages only.
