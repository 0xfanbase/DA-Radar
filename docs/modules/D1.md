# D1 Capital and prudential treatment of cryptoassets

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain how Hong Kong turned the Basel cryptoasset standard into binding capital, liquidity and exposure rules, how a cryptoasset gets classified, and what the bank must notify to the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA).

## In 60 seconds

The global bank standard setter, the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS), published a standard on banks' cryptoasset exposures in December 2022 and amended it in July 2024. The HKMA consulted on it (CP 24.01, 2024), sent draft rules to the industry in January 2025, and the government gazetted three sets of amendment rules in July 2025. The rules, plus a new guidance module CRP-1 and nine revised modules, took effect on 1 January 2026. Every cryptoasset a bank holds is sorted into Group 1a (tokenised traditional assets), 1b (stablecoins that pass strict tests), 2a (Group 2 assets that meet hedging criteria, so some hedges count) or 2b (everything else, at a 1,250% risk weight). New cryptoasset types must be notified to the HKMA and sit in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees otherwise. In June 2026 the HKMA eased one Group 1 condition for tokenised assets on permissionless chains.

## Key facts

- CP 24.01 proposed bringing the December 2022 Basel standard into Hong Kong rules no earlier than 1 July 2025, with comments due by 6 May 2024. [S:27f8bc2f6b2a, paras 2, 4, 11]
- In January 2025 the HKMA sent draft rule amendments to the industry, asking for comments by 20 February 2025 and aiming for a 1 January 2026 start to match Basel. [S:ebc5f9b5ee0d, p.1]
- The three amendment rules were gazetted on 11 July 2025, tabled at LegCo on 16 July 2025, and took effect on 1 January 2026. [S:277820235891, paras 1-2 and closing para]
- The HKMA finalised CRP-1 and revisions to nine other modules and a code of practice on 27 November 2025, all effective 1 January 2026. [S:30b686db29f9, p.1-2]
- CRP-1 is guidance; the binding classification rules sit in Part 12 of the Banking (Capital) Rules (BCR), which prevails if the two differ. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- Group 1 requires all four classification conditions: same rights and risks as the traditional asset (or an effective peg); legally enforceable rights and settlement finality; adequately controlled network risks; and regulated entities performing key functions. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 2.2.1, 2.3.1, 2.4.1, 2.5.1]
- Group 1a is tokenised traditional assets and Group 1b is stablecoins with an effective stabilisation mechanism; anything else is Group 2. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1-1.3.3]
- A Group 2 asset reaches Group 2a only if it meets three hedging criteria, including average market capitalisation of at least HKD 78 billion and 100 or more real price observations. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 3.1.1]
- Group 2b exposures take a 1,250% risk weight, which also covers their market and CVA risk. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, columns (c)-(e), p.215]; [S:5d845665ba83, para 4.3.2]
- Before acquiring a new type of cryptoasset, a bank must notify the HKMA and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees a better classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- Exposures held before 1 January 2026 must sit in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.4.2]
- If the HKMA disagrees with a bank's classification, it will override it. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.6]
- On permissionless chains, stablecoins licensed by the HKMA are deemed to meet the extra measures; other stablecoins need a comparable regime. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.6.3]
- The June 2026 letter says the approval condition for tokenised traditional assets on permissionless chains can also be met where a financial market regulator supervises the tokenisation business. This widens the route to Group 1. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- CA-G-1 section 8.7 says Group 1a banking-book exposures follow the underlying asset's rules, while Group 1b needs separate capital for the reference asset and the redeemer. [S:5d0c13e09312, paras 8.7.1-8.7.2]
- Group 2 exposure limit: a global or domestic systemically important bank (G-SIB or D-SIB) faces an add-on above 1% of Tier 1 capital and a harsher treatment above 2%. [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(d)]; [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, rows 8-10, p.215]
- Under LM-1 Annex 3, only a Group 1a token of an asset that already qualifies as high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) can count as HQLA; a bank issuing a stablecoin redeemable within the LCR period applies a 100% outflow. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 paras 1, 7]
- Market risk: any cryptoasset exposure removes the de-minimis exemption, internal models cannot be used for Group 2 instruments, and Group 2a position risk is 100% of the net position. [S:b8d2d2a0f9c8, footnote 80, paras 6.1.9, 6.2.8]
- CR-G-8 values crypto exposures under new Division 5A of the Banking (Exposure Limits) Rules; exposures with no counterparty default risk are ignored for large-exposure limits. [S:0cd3c1a74e5e, paras 2.8.8-2.8.9 and footnote 13]
- CR-L-5 treats a token carrying the economics of equity as share capital for the major-acquisitions consent rules. [S:13365a31e794, footnote 1]
- CA-G-5 says the HKMA may respond to crypto-related weaknesses with capital add-ons, provisions, limits or other measures. [S:349a543ae2f0, para 3.6.6]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Classify every cryptoasset exposure under BCR Part 12 and CRP-1, and keep assessing it on an ongoing basis. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 2.1.1]
- [ ] Move an asset to Group 2b at once if it stops meeting its conditions or criteria. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.3]
- [ ] As an RI, book loans secured only by VA collateral as clean loans for capital. [S:e64b7b0267b2, para (9), p.5]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep policies, staff and IT able to assess cryptoasset risk, and document the evidence for each classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.1]
- [ ] For stablecoins, keep a monitoring framework that checks the stabilisation mechanism works as intended. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.2.4]
- [ ] If the bank is a G-SIB or D-SIB, track the Group 2 exposure ratio against the 1% and 2% thresholds. [S:9e8d84c87168, Part XII CAE1 explanatory note, rows 8-10, p.215]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the HKMA before acquiring any new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Give the HKMA classification evidence on request. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.1]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For Group 1 assets, check that every entity redeeming, transferring, storing or settling the asset is regulated or meets suitable risk standards. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.5.1]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The capital rules are law from 1 January 2026; CRP-1 is guidance, and the BCR wins if they differ. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1]
- New cryptoasset types need HKMA notification before purchase. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]

**To business heads**
- Until the HKMA agrees a classification, a new cryptoasset sits in Group 2b at a 1,250% risk weight. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]; [S:5d845665ba83, para 4.3.2]
- HKMA-licensed stablecoins are deemed to meet the permissionless-chain measures, but must still pass conditions 1-4. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.6.3]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong implemented the Basel standard on the Basel date, 1 January 2026. [S:277820235891, closing para]
- The HKMA can override our classifications and add capital or limits if it sees weaknesses. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.6]; [S:349a543ae2f0, para 3.6.6]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Any crypto exposure ends our market-risk de-minimis exemption. [S:b8d2d2a0f9c8, footnote 80]
- A stablecoin we hold is non-HQLA with a 0% LCR inflow and an 85% NSFR required-funding factor. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 7]

## Common mix-ups

- "The June 2026 CRP-1 letter tightened the rules." It loosened para 2.6.4 by accepting supervised tokenisation activity. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- "Internal models are only barred for Group 2a." MR-1 bars them for all instruments referencing Group 2 cryptoassets. [S:b8d2d2a0f9c8, para 6.1.9]
- "The 1%/2% Group 2 limit applies to every bank." All locally incorporated banks calculate the ratio, but only G-SIBs and D-SIBs face the add-on. [S:5d0c13e09312, para 8.7.4(d)]; [S:d1fbf6c0736a, paras 11-12]
- "Legacy holdings keep their old treatment." They sit in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.4.2]
- "CBDCs are cryptoassets." They sit outside the standard and are generally treated like the central bank's fiat. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.2.3 and footnote 4]
