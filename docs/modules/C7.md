# C7 VATP counterparties and the SFC platform regime

VATP stands for virtual asset trading platform. SFC stands for the Securities and Futures Commission. This module is for the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After it, you can explain how the SFC licenses these platforms and what its rulebook requires of them. You can also use that rulebook to check a platform that is your bank's partner, customer or counterparty.

## In 60 seconds

Since 1 June 2023, a platform needs an SFC licence to run a virtual asset (VA) exchange in Hong Kong. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1] A VA is a digital token, such as a cryptocurrency. The same applies to a platform that actively markets such an exchange to Hong Kong investors. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1] The licence comes under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO), a Hong Kong law. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1] Before that date, licensing was opt-in. The SFC could only license platforms that offered at least one security token (a token that counts as a security). [S:024089b01825, paras 4, 32] The SFC's Guidelines for VATP Operators (the Guidelines) are the platforms' rulebook. They cover which tokens a platform lists and which tokens retail clients can buy. They also cover 98% cold storage (keys kept offline), compensation cover and key bans. The Guidelines apply to platform operators, not to banks. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 1.2, 7.1, 7.8, 10.6(c), 10.22] But the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) require banks and other intermediaries to partner only with SFC-licensed platforms for VA dealing. [S:8a016a912528, para 17] So the Guidelines become your benchmark for due diligence (the checks you run on a partner). [S:8a016a912528, para 17] Since November 2025, the SFC has widened what licensed platforms may do. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 3; S:f009f1a6d615, para 3] In May 2026, the HKMA told registered institutions (RIs) which of these SFC standards it expects them to follow. [S:e64b7b0267b2, pp.1, 6] RIs are banks registered with the SFC to carry on securities business.

## Key facts

- In 2018, the SFC chose to watch selected platforms in its Regulatory Sandbox (a supervised test setting). [S:e7f89463ceae, Section II] The SFC would then decide whether to license any of them. [S:e7f89463ceae, Section II]
- Under its 2019 position paper, the SFC could only license a centralised Hong Kong platform. [S:024089b01825, paras 4, 32] Centralised means the platform trades, clears and settles for clients and controls their assets. That platform also had to trade at least one security token. [S:024089b01825, paras 4, 32] Platforms that traded only non-security tokens were outside the SFC's powers. [S:024089b01825, paras 4, 32]
- The SFC granted the first VATP licence in December 2020. [S:60682e9fd7fa, main text and Note 1] The licence covered Type 1 (dealing in securities) and Type 7 (automated trading services) regulated activities. [S:60682e9fd7fa, main text and Note 1] The platform could serve professional investors only. [S:60682e9fd7fa, main text and Note 1]
- The AMLO licensing regime started on 1 June 2023. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1] The AMLO requires every platform that runs a VA exchange in Hong Kong to hold an SFC licence. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1] This also covers platforms that actively market a VA exchange to Hong Kong investors. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1]
- Platforms already operating before the regime could carry on until 31 May 2024. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 12] A platform that applied by 29 February 2024 and met set conditions could be treated as licensed from 1 June 2024. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 12] This let existing platforms keep operating while they waited for a licence decision. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 12]
- Being treated as licensed is not the same as holding a licence. [S:c787f990209d, 'Reminder for investors' and 'Reminder for deemed-to-be-licensed VATP applicants'] The SFC warns investors that such platforms are not formally licensed. [S:c787f990209d, 'Reminder for investors' and 'Reminder for deemed-to-be-licensed VATP applicants'] The SFC does not expect them to take on new retail clients until they prove their controls work and are formally licensed. [S:c787f990209d, 'Reminder for investors' and 'Reminder for deemed-to-be-licensed VATP applicants']
- The SFC publishes lists of licensed platforms and of applicants. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table and note] It also lists removed applicants and platforms that are closing down. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table and note] At 29 May 2026, the licensed list showed 13 platforms. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table and note] Being on a list does not mean a platform is creditworthy. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table and note]
- Token admission: a platform should have a token admission and review committee. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.6] The platform should also do documented due diligence before listing any token. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.6]
- Retail access: a token offered to retail clients should be highly liquid (easy to buy and sell). [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.7-7.8] At a minimum, it should be in at least two acceptable indices from at least two different index providers. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.7-7.8]
- Custody: a platform and its Associated Entity (the group company that holds client assets) should keep 98% of client virtual assets in cold storage. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22] They should also hold an SFC-approved compensation arrangement (cover that pays clients if assets are lost). [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22] That cover is 50% of client virtual assets in cold storage. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22] It is 100% of those in hot storage (keys kept online) and other storage. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22]
- Prohibitions: outside narrow exceptions, the June 2023 Guidelines say platforms should not do six things. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.23-7.26, 13.2-13.4] Three are lending clients money to buy virtual assets, dealing in VA futures or derivatives, and offering clients algorithmic (computer-run) trading. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.23-7.26, 13.2-13.4] The other three are earning returns on client virtual assets, trading for their own account, and market making (quoting prices) with their own money. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.23-7.26, 13.2-13.4] Two later SFC documents open limited routes. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Background'; S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 4, 7] The February 2026 perpetuals framework invites platforms to propose perpetual contracts (futures-style contracts with no end date). [S:49f642bdee28, 'Background'] A February 2026 circular lets a company in the platform's group make markets on the platform, under conditions. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 4, 7]
- The SFC inspected all applicants treated as licensed. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1] It checked three areas: cybersecurity, the safety of client assets, and know-your-client (KYC) checks on client identity. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1] The SFC then told every platform operator to review its systems and fix gaps at once. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1]
- In November 2025, the SFC dropped the 12-month track record rule for some tokens. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 5] Under that rule, a token needed a 12-month history before a platform offered it. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 5] The rule no longer covers tokens offered to professional investors. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 5] Nor does it cover stablecoins (tokens meant to hold a steady value) from licensed issuers offered to retail clients. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 5] It still applies to other tokens offered to retail clients. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 5]
- Shared liquidity: licensed platforms may share an order book (the list of waiting buy and sell orders) with affiliated overseas platforms. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 1, 3, 26] They need prior written SFC approval. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 1, 3, 26] The first step allows only liquidity from within the same group. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 1, 3, 26]
- Since February 2026, a platform may let an affiliated market maker (a group company that quotes buy and sell prices) trade on it. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 7, 9] The platform first notifies the SFC and files a report from an independent firm. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 7, 9] Client orders keep priority over the market maker's orders at the same price. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 7, 9]
- The SFC's perpetual contracts framework says these products should go to professional investors only. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets' and 'Margin arrangements' sections] It says margin collateral (assets posted to back a trade) should take one of three forms. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets' and 'Margin arrangements' sections] These are fiat money (ordinary currency), or stablecoins or tokenised deposits (bank deposits recorded as tokens) regulated by the HKMA. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets' and 'Margin arrangements' sections]
- The SFC said JPEX (which presented itself as a VA trading platform) was unregulated. [S:c8dcca119dab, main text, paras 3, 5-6] The SFC said it put JPEX on its Alert List (a public warning list) in July 2022. [S:c8dcca119dab, main text, paras 3, 5-6] It said JPEX never applied for a licence. [S:c8dcca119dab, main text, paras 3, 5-6] The SFC referred the matter to the Police on suspicion of fraud. [S:c8dcca119dab, main text, paras 3, 5-6]
- The SFC expects SFC-licensed VASPs to adopt phishing-resistant login by 8 July 2027. [S:724f4d9f786e, paras 7, 21(c)] Phishing-resistant means fake websites cannot trick users into handing over login details. The same deadline covers device binding (tying an account to a trusted device). [S:724f4d9f786e, paras 7, 21(c)] The SFC does not treat a one-time password (OTP) as phishing-resistant. [S:724f4d9f786e, paras 7, 21(c)]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Partner only with SFC-licensed platforms if the bank offers VA dealing. [S:8a016a912528, para 17] Either introduce clients to the platform, or trade through an omnibus account (one account that pools many clients' orders). [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- [ ] Offer VA dealing only to clients of the bank's Type 1 (dealing in securities) business. [S:8a016a912528, para 18]
- [ ] Follow the SFC's guidance for VA brokers if the bank trades for clients on a shared order book and also gives retail clients access to it. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6] The HKMA expects this of RIs. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6]
- [ ] Apply the SFC's safeguards for client VA withdrawals if the bank, as an RI, lets clients withdraw virtual assets. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Client VA withdrawals', p.6] These safeguards apply on top of the HKMA's e-banking and technology rules for RIs. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Client VA withdrawals', p.6]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Re-check a platform's status on the live SFC lists before onboarding it and at each review. The lists can lag between updates. [S:dddd919625e9, Note 1]; [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section Note 1]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Tell the SFC in advance before the bank starts or changes VA activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31] Tell the HKMA too, where relevant. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Run a risk-based anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-financing of terrorism (CFT) assessment when the bank takes VASPs as customers. [S:642680fa4467, section (b) 'Banking relationships with VASPs', p.4] The HKMA says extra checks may be needed, depending on the relationship. [S:642680fa4467, section (b) 'Banking relationships with VASPs', p.4] These are like the checks used in correspondent banking (where one bank provides accounts to another). [S:642680fa4467, section (b) 'Banking relationships with VASPs', p.4] They include understanding the customer's business, licensing status and AML/CFT controls. [S:642680fa4467, section (b) 'Banking relationships with VASPs', p.4]
- [ ] Find out, for VASPs based outside Hong Kong, whether each account is for the firm's own use or for its clients. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23-11.24] If the account serves clients, apply the correspondent-banking measures. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23-11.24]
- [ ] Benchmark a partner platform against the VATP Guidelines. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.8, 10.6(c), 10.22] Check its token committee and its retail index test. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.8, 10.6(c), 10.22] Check its 98% cold storage and its compensation cover. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.8, 10.6(c), 10.22]
- [ ] Ask whether the platform has fixed the gaps listed in the SFC's inspection findings. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1 and Appendix 2]

## Talking points

**To the chief compliance officer (CCO)**
- The VATP Guidelines bind platforms, not the bank. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.2] We use them as a due diligence benchmark, because our VA dealing must run through SFC-licensed platforms. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- For customers that are VASPs, we follow the HKMA's AML/CFT Guideline. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23-11.24] That includes correspondent-banking-style checks on overseas VASPs. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23-11.24]

**To business heads**
- Platforms can now share order books with SFC approval. [S:f009f1a6d615, para 26] They can also use affiliated market makers after notifying the SFC. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, para 9] The SFC's framework says perpetual contracts should go to professional investors only. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets']
- If we trade for clients on a platform's shared order book and give retail clients access to it, the HKMA expects us to follow the SFC's broker guidance. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6]

**To the CEO**
- From 2019 to 2020, Hong Kong platform licensing was opt-in and for professional investors only. [S:024089b01825, para 32]; [S:60682e9fd7fa, Note 1] Since June 2023, every platform in or marketing to Hong Kong has needed a licence. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1]
- The SFC's licensed list showed 13 platforms at 29 May 2026. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table]

**To Risk / chief risk officer (CRO)**
- The Guidelines say licensed platforms should keep 98% of client virtual assets in cold storage. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22] Their compensation cover should equal 50% of cold-stored and 100% of hot-stored client assets. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22]
- The SFC's inspections of applicants treated as licensed covered cybersecurity, client assets and KYC. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1] The SFC said platforms should fix gaps at once. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** A platform treated as licensed has been vetted.
  **Fact:** In 2023, the SFC said it had not vetted whether these platforms were fit and proper (suitable to hold a licence). [S:dddd919625e9, Note 4] The SFC also said they may never get a licence. [S:dddd919625e9, Note 4]
- **Myth:** The 50%/100% compensation rule applies to our custody.
  **Fact:** That rule is for licensed trading platforms. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22] The HKMA's May 2026 custody guidance for banks takes a different approach. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)] It expects the bank to be liable for losses caused by the bank, and to hold enough financial resources. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)]
- **Myth:** A one-time password sent by text message (SMS) is enough.
  **Fact:** The SFC does not treat an OTP as phishing-resistant for login or device binding. [S:724f4d9f786e, para 7]

## Open items

- The SFC calls its perpetuals framework an initial approach that may be refined. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Way forward'] The documents reviewed for this guide show no approvals of specific perpetual products. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Way forward']
- The SFC press release on the first licence is dated 16 December 2020. [S:60682e9fd7fa, dateline] The SFC's licensed list gives 15 December 2020 as the earliest platform's licence date. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table] The documents reviewed do not explain the one-day gap.
- The SFC's VATP lists are a live page. The counts here are a snapshot from 29 May 2026.

## Check yourself

1. Which regulator licenses virtual-asset trading platforms operating in or marketing to Hong Kong?
   **Answer:** The SFC, under the AMLO licensing regime that started 1 June 2023 [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1].
2. What share of client virtual assets do the SFC's VATP Guidelines say a licensed platform should keep in cold storage?
   **Answer:** 98%. The platform and its Associated Entity should keep 98% of client virtual assets in cold storage [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22].
3. Do the VATP Guidelines bind banks directly, or only platform operators?
   **Answer:** Only platform operators [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.2]. Banks use them as a due diligence benchmark, because the SFC and HKMA require bank VA dealing to run through SFC-licensed platforms [S:8a016a912528, para 17].
4. What should a bank do before onboarding a VA trading platform as a partner?
   **Answer:** Re-check the platform's status on the live SFC lists before onboarding and at each review, since the lists can lag between updates [S:dddd919625e9, Note 1; S:b14be2203d39, applicant section Note 1].
5. Does being "treated as licensed" mean the SFC has fully vetted a platform?
   **Answer:** No. In 2023 the SFC said it had not vetted whether these platforms were fit and proper, and that they may never get a licence [S:dddd919625e9, Note 4].
