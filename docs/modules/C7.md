# C7 VATP counterparties and the SFC platform regime

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain how the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licenses virtual asset trading platforms (VATPs), what the rulebook requires of them, and how to use that rulebook when a platform is your bank's partner, customer or counterparty.

## In 60 seconds

Since 1 June 2023, any platform running a virtual asset (VA) exchange in Hong Kong, or actively marketing one to Hong Kong investors, needs an SFC licence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO). [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1] Before that, the SFC only had an opt-in route for platforms that offered at least one security token. [S:024089b01825, paras 4, 32] The SFC's VATP Guidelines are the platforms' rulebook: token admission, retail gates, 98% cold storage, compensation cover and key prohibitions. They apply to platform operators, not banks. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 1.2, 7.1, 7.8, 10.6(c), 10.22] But the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) require intermediaries, including banks, to partner only with SFC-licensed platforms for VA dealing, so the Guidelines become your due-diligence benchmark. [S:8a016a912528, para 17] Since November 2025 the SFC has widened what licensed platforms may do, and in May 2026 the HKMA told registered institutions (RIs) which related SFC standards it expects them to follow. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 3; S:f009f1a6d615, para 3; S:e64b7b0267b2, pp.1, 6]

## Key facts

- In 2018 the SFC chose to observe selected platforms in its Regulatory Sandbox before deciding whether to license any. [S:e7f89463ceae, Section II]
- Under the 2019 position paper, the SFC could only license a centralised Hong Kong platform that traded at least one security token; platforms trading only non-security tokens sat outside its powers. [S:024089b01825, paras 4, 32]
- The SFC granted the first VATP licence in December 2020, for Type 1 and Type 7 regulated activities, limited to professional investors. [S:60682e9fd7fa, main text and Note 1]
- The AMLO regime started on 1 June 2023 and requires every platform running a VA exchange in Hong Kong, or actively marketing one to Hong Kong investors, to be SFC-licensed. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1]
- Pre-existing platforms could keep operating until 31 May 2024; those that applied by 29 February 2024 and met the deeming conditions could be deemed licensed from 1 June 2024. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 12]
- Deemed status is not a licence. In the SFC's words, such platforms are "NOT formally licensed by the SFC", and they should not onboard new retail clients before proving their controls. [S:c787f990209d, 'Reminder for investors' and 'Reminder for deemed-to-be-licensed VATP applicants']
- The SFC publishes lists of licensed platforms, applicants, removed applicants and closing-down platforms; at 29 May 2026 the licensed list showed 13 platforms, and listing does not guarantee creditworthiness. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table and note]
- Token admission: a platform should run a token admission and review committee and do documented due diligence before listing any token. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.6]
- Retail access: a token offered to retail clients should be highly liquid; at a minimum, it should be included in at least two acceptable indices from at least two different index providers. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.7-7.8]
- Custody: a platform and its Associated Entity should keep 98% of client VAs in cold storage, and hold an SFC-approved compensation arrangement covering 50% of client VAs in cold storage and 100% of those in hot and other storage. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22]
- Prohibitions: the June 2023 Guidelines say platforms should not, outside narrow exceptions, finance client VA purchases, deal in VA futures or derivatives, provide algorithmic trading services to clients, use client VAs to generate returns, trade for their own account, or make markets on a proprietary basis. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.23-7.26, 13.2-13.4] Two later SFC documents open limited routes: the February 2026 perpetuals framework invites platforms to propose perpetual contracts, and the February 2026 circular lets an affiliated company make markets on a platform under conditions. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Background'; S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 4, 7]
- After inspecting all deemed applicants on cybersecurity, client-asset safeguarding and know-your-client (KYC) processes, the SFC told every Platform Operator to review its systems and fix gaps at once. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1]
- In November 2025 the SFC dropped the 12-month track record for tokens offered to professional investors, and for stablecoins from licensed issuers offered to retail; it still applies to other retail tokens. [S:e9cab4f52431, para 5]
- Shared liquidity: licensed platforms may, with prior written SFC approval, share an order book with affiliated overseas platforms, starting with intra-group liquidity only. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 1, 3, 26]
- Since February 2026 a platform may let an affiliated market maker trade on it, after notifying the SFC and filing an independent firm's report; client orders keep priority at the same price. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 7, 9]
- The SFC's perpetual-contracts framework says these products should be offered only to professional investors, and margin collateral should be fiat, or stablecoins or tokenised deposits regulated by the HKMA. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets' and 'Margin arrangements' sections]
- The SFC named JPEX as unregulated, said it was placed on the SFC Alert List in July 2022, said it never applied for a licence, and referred the matter to the Police on suspicion of fraud. [S:c8dcca119dab, main text, paras 3, 5-6]
- The SFC expects SFC-licensed VASPs to adopt phishing-resistant login and device binding by 8 July 2027; it does not treat one-time passwords (OTPs) as phishing-resistant. [S:724f4d9f786e, paras 7, 21(c)]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] If the bank offers VA dealing, partner only with SFC-licensed platforms, via an omnibus account or by introducing clients. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- [ ] Offer VA dealing only to clients of the bank's Type 1 (dealing in securities) business. [S:8a016a912528, para 18]
- [ ] As an RI, the HKMA expects you to follow the SFC's shared-order-book guidance for brokers when trading for clients on such a book or giving retail clients access. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6]
- [ ] As an RI permitting client VA withdrawals, apply the SFC's withdrawal safeguards on top of HKMA e-banking and technology rules. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Client VA withdrawals', p.6]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Re-check a platform's status on the live SFC lists before onboarding and at review; the lists can lag between updates. [S:dddd919625e9, Note 1]; [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section Note 1]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Give the SFC, and the HKMA where relevant, advance notice before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] When banking a VASP, run a risk-based anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) assessment; depending on the relationship, the HKMA says extra correspondent-style checks may be needed, such as understanding its business, its licensing status and its AML/CFT controls. [S:642680fa4467, section (b) 'Banking relationships with VASPs', p.4]
- [ ] For a VASP located outside Hong Kong, find out whether the account serves its own use or its clients; if clients, apply the correspondent-banking measures. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23-11.24]
- [ ] Benchmark a partner platform against the VATP Guidelines: token committee, retail index test, 98% cold storage, compensation cover. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 7.1, 7.8, 10.6(c), 10.22]
- [ ] Ask whether the platform has fixed the gaps in the SFC's inspection findings. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1 and Appendix 2]

## Talking points

**To the chief compliance officer (CCO)**
- The VATP Guidelines bind platforms, not the bank. For us they are a due-diligence yardstick, because our VA dealing must run through SFC-licensed platforms. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.2]; [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- On our own AML duties for VASP customers, we follow the HKMA AML/CFT Guideline, including correspondent-style checks on overseas VASPs. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 11.23-11.24]

**To business heads**
- Shared order books and affiliated market makers are now allowed for platforms, with SFC approval or notice; the SFC's perpetuals framework says those should go to professional investors only. [S:f009f1a6d615, para 26]; [S:84b3c1a0cee1, para 9]; [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets']
- If we give clients access to a platform's shared order book, the HKMA expects us to follow the SFC's broker guidance. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong moved from an opt-in, professional-only model (2019-2020) to mandatory licensing for all platforms from June 2023. [S:024089b01825, para 32]; [S:60682e9fd7fa, Note 1]; [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 1]
- The SFC’s licensed list showed 13 platforms at 29 May 2026. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table]

**To Risk / chief risk officer (CRO)**
- The Guidelines say licensed platforms should hold 98% of client VAs cold and carry compensation cover of 50% of cold-stored and 100% of hot-stored client VAs. [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 10.6(c), 10.22]
- The SFC's inspections of deemed applicants covered cybersecurity, client assets and KYC; it said platforms should act at once to fix gaps. [S:0c93d9a066e6, p.1]

## Common mix-ups

- "Deemed licensed means vetted." No: the SFC said in 2023 that it had not vetted the fitness and properness of deemed platforms, and that they may never get a licence. [S:dddd919625e9, Note 4]
- "The 50%/100% compensation rule applies to our custody." It is a VATP rule; the HKMA's May 2026 custody guidance for banks instead expects the bank to be liable for losses attributable to it and to hold adequate financial resources. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22]; [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)]
- "Text-message (SMS) OTP is enough." The SFC does not consider OTP phishing-resistant for login or device binding. [S:724f4d9f786e, para 7]
