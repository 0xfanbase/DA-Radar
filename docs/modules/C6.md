# C6 Financing, shared order books and client withdrawals

For a bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets) whose Registered Institution (RI) offers, or plans to offer, virtual asset (VA) dealing. After reading, you can explain what the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) changed in February 2026, how the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) applied it to banks in May 2026, and which controls the regulators expect.

## In 60 seconds

Under the omnibus-account terms and conditions, licensed corporations (LCs) and RIs dealing in VAs for clients could not provide financing for clients to buy VAs. [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background, p.1] On 11 February 2026 the SFC let VA brokers that already do securities margin financing extend VA financing to their securities margin clients. [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 3, 5] If a broker relies on VA collateral, the SFC expects it to be limited to bitcoin and ether, with a haircut of 60% or more. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 7] On 27 May 2026 the HKMA let RIs offer VA financing if they observe similar standards. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2] It also allowed stablecoins from HKMA-licensed issuers as collateral, and said loans secured only by VA must be treated as clean loans for capital purposes. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (4), (9)] The same circulars let brokers and RIs trade for clients on a VA trading platform's (VATP's) shared order book, which pools orders with affiliated platforms abroad. Retail clients get access only after a clear risk explanation and an express election. [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 15-17]; [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6] They also set anti-fraud standards where clients can withdraw VAs. [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 18-20] The SFC circular is addressed to LCs; the HKMA applies the standards to RIs. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 1]; [S:e64b7b0267b2, pp.2, 6]

## Key facts

- Before the SFC's 11 February 2026 circular, neither LCs nor RIs could give clients financial accommodation to acquire VA under the omnibus-account terms and conditions. [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background, p.1]
- The revised terms of 27 May 2026 keep the ban, with an exception. An LC or RI may give such financing only if it engages in securities margin financing and holds a Type 1 licence or registration carrying these terms. [S:31b4f67e0233, Part I para 4.3, p.10]
- The SFC framed VA financing as a first step under Pillar P of its ASPIRe roadmap. It is limited to VA brokers that already provide securities margin financing. [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 3-5]
- The HKMA allows RIs providing VA dealing to offer VA financing if they observe standards similar to the SFC's. It sets out the standards for RIs as items (1)-(9). [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', p.2]
- Eligible borrowers are limited to the RI's securities margin financing clients. The RI is not expected to raise a client's credit limit just because VA financing is available. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (2)-(3), p.2]
- Where an RI counts a client's VA holdings toward financial capability, its assessment should cover seven risk factors. These include price-gap risk, digital-asset sector concentration and correlation with equities under stress. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (3)(a)-(g), p.3]
- If an RI chooses to rely on VA collateral, only bitcoin, ether and stablecoins from HKMA-licensed issuers should count as eligible collateral ('Relevant Stablecoins'), with a prudent haircut. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4), pp.3-4]
- The haircut for bitcoin and ether should be 60% or more. The HKMA may revise the haircut on prior notice. No numeric haircut is set for Relevant Stablecoins. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4) and footnote 5, p.4]
- The RI is generally expected to value VA collateral at prices on the VATP where the client's orders execute. For Relevant Stablecoins it may instead use the licensed issuer's prices. RIs should keep a contingency plan for VATP outages. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (6) and footnote 6, p.4]
- VA financing counts as part of the margin loan. Concentration controls should cover VA collateral, and the RI should not repledge or reuse VA collateral except to enforce it on client default. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (7)(a)-(c), pp.4-5]
- If the loan exceeds collateral value, or a client has an outstanding margin call and a poor record of meeting them, the RI should suspend advances, stop VA purchases on margin and review the client's credit and trading limits. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (8), p.5]
- Because the Banking (Capital) Rules do not recognise VA collateral, loans secured solely by VA must be treated as clean loans to the client. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (9), p.5]
- Until the SFC revises capital requirements, the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules (FRR) apply a 100% haircut to VA collateral held by LCs. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 14]
- Tokenised products fall outside the HKMA circular, because the financing ban does not apply to acquiring them. On their sale and distribution, RIs should refer to HKMA guidance such as its 20 February 2024 tokenised-products circular. [S:e64b7b0267b2, footnote 4, p.2]
- A shared order book lets an SFC-licensed VATP combine its order book with those of affiliated platforms abroad. The overseas platform should operate in a Financial Action Task Force (FATF) or FATF-style regional body member jurisdiction with substantially aligned regulation. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 1, 3, 7]
- A shared order book should accept only fully pre-funded orders, with delivery-versus-payment settlement at the centre. The VATP is required to hold, on trust in Hong Kong, a client-compensation reserve no smaller than the Unsettled Trade Limit. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 10, 13, 15, 17]
- RIs doing agency trading on a shared order book, or offering retail access, should follow SFC 26EC5 paras 15-17. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6] Before trading, a broker should assess conflicts, settlement, default handling, reserve funds and insurance, and client recourse. Retail access needs a prominently communicated risk explanation and an express election. [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 16-17]
- Where client VAs stay in custody at the VATP, a broker's withdrawal function adds cyber and operational risk: a compromised broker system could let an impostor send fraudulent withdrawal instructions. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 19]
- SFC 26EC5 paras 18-20 also apply to RIs that permit client VA withdrawals, on top of HKMA requirements such as e-banking and technology risk management. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Client VA withdrawals', p.6] These expect robust authentication, continuous monitoring with suspension inside and outside office hours, and working with VATPs on withdrawal limits. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 20]
- 26EC6 lets a VATP accept an affiliated market maker, subject to conditions. These include functional independence, client-order priority at the same price, post-trade identification and disclosure. The VATP must notify the SFC first with an independent firm's report. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 7, 9-10]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Offer VA financing only to securities margin financing clients, based on financial capability and posted collateral. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (1)-(2), p.2]
- Treat loans secured solely by VA as clean loans for capital. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (9), p.5]
- Do not repledge, reuse or encumber VA collateral except to enforce it on client default. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (7)(c), p.5]

**Controls & monitoring**
- If relying on VA collateral, document VA risk-assessment methods in the margin lending policy, and monitor vulnerable VA exposures on an ongoing basis. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (5)(a), p.4]
- If relying on VA collateral, keep the ability to monitor its volatility in real time and act promptly. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (5)(b), p.4]
- Set prudent margin-call and stop-advance triggers, and act on them. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (8), p.5]
- For withdrawals: monitor continuously, with escalation and suspension also outside office hours. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]; [S:73dac3a5a832, para 20(b)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- The HKMA circular sets no separate notification step. For new digital-asset activity, the HKMA's existing expectation is that banks discuss plans with it before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body para 5, p.2]
- Contacts for queries are named in the circular, by topic. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Before shared order book trading, assess the operators' conflicts of interest, the settlement mechanism, failure and default processes, reserve fund and insurance, and client recourse. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]; [S:73dac3a5a832, para 16]
- Work with the partner VATP as needed on withdrawal limits and blocking suspicious withdrawals. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]; [S:73dac3a5a832, para 20(c)]
- Keep an outage contingency plan for the VATP used to value collateral. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (6), p.4]

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- The HKMA's 27 May 2026 circular lets our RI offer VA margin financing if we observe standards similar to the SFC's. If we rely on VA collateral, it should be limited to bitcoin, ether and stablecoins from HKMA-licensed issuers. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (1)-(4), pp.2-4]
- Withdrawal and shared-order-book standards come from SFC 26EC5 paras 15-20, applied to RIs by the HKMA. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]

**To business heads**
- Only securities margin financing clients qualify, and limits are not expected to rise just because VA financing exists. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (2)-(3), p.2]
- Retail clients can use a shared order book only after a prominent explanation of the overseas risks and an express election. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]; [S:73dac3a5a832, para 17]

**To the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- The SFC calls this a first step to bring margin trading into VA, aimed at liquidity in a risk-controlled setting. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 3]
- The Banking (Capital) Rules give VA collateral no recognition, so loans secured solely by VA are treated as clean loans. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (9), p.5]

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- Bitcoin and ether collateral should get a haircut of 60% or more. For stablecoins the HKMA asks only for a prudent haircut. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4), footnote 5, pp.3-4]
- The HKMA notes that VAs have a limited track record in major systemic shocks, and even the most traded VA has seen large falls within a day and over several days. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (3), closing paragraph, p.3]

## Common mix-ups

- 'The SFC circular binds banks directly.' It is addressed to LCs. Banks, as RIs, are brought in by the HKMA's 27 May 2026 circular. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 1]; [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2]
- 'Bank collateral rules are identical to the SFC's.' The HKMA adds Relevant Stablecoins as eligible collateral and uses Banking (Capital) Rules clean-loan treatment rather than the FRR 100% haircut. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (4), (9)]; [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 7, 14]
- 'The financing ban covered tokenised products.' The HKMA says the prohibition does not apply to acquiring tokenised products. [S:e64b7b0267b2, footnote 4, p.2]
- 'A shared order book can link to any overseas exchange.' As a first step it is limited to intra-group platforms. [S:f009f1a6d615, para 3]

## Open items

- Paragraph-number difference: SFC 26EC5 cites the financing ban as para 4.4 of the omnibus terms and conditions. The HKMA cites para 4.3 'as renumbered on 30 September 2025', and the revised terms of 27 May 2026 also number it 4.3. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 2]; [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.1 footnote 3]; [S:31b4f67e0233, Part I para 4.3, p.10]
- Date discrepancy: the HKMA's footnote 4 dates the joint circular 23 December 2023. The SFC's version, and the HKMA's own stablecoin circular of 27 May 2026, give 22 December 2023. [S:e64b7b0267b2, footnote 4]; [S:8a016a912528, p.1]; [S:a0bd48559762, p.1]
- The SFC capital-requirements consultation for VA activities has no published date. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 14]
- Of the revised terms and conditions (Appendices A1 and A2 to the HKMA's 27 May 2026 stablecoin circular), only para 4.3 of Appendix A1 was reviewed for this module. [S:553c7d718bec, landing page]
- No numeric haircut is specified for Relevant Stablecoin collateral. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4) and footnote 5, p.4]
