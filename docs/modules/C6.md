# C6 Financing, shared order books and client withdrawals

For a bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets) whose Registered Institution (RI) offers, or plans to offer, virtual asset (VA) dealing. After reading, you can explain what the SFC changed in February 2026, how the HKMA applied it to banks in May 2026, and which controls must be in place first.

## In 60 seconds

Until February 2026, brokers and banks dealing in VAs for clients could not lend clients money to buy VAs. On 11 February 2026 the SFC let VA brokers that already do securities margin lending extend VA financing to existing margin clients. Collateral in VA form is limited to bitcoin and ether, with a haircut of at least 60%. On 27 May 2026 the HKMA let RIs do the same on equivalent terms. It also allowed HKMA-licensed stablecoins as collateral and required loans backed only by VA to be treated as clean (unsecured) loans for capital. The same packages let clients reach a VA trading platform's (VATP's) shared order book, which pools liquidity with overseas affiliate platforms. Retail clients get access only after an explicit opt-in. They also set anti-fraud controls where clients can withdraw VAs. The rules bind LCs through the SFC and RIs through the HKMA as frontline regulator.

## Key facts

- Before 11 February 2026, neither LCs nor RIs could give clients financial accommodation to acquire VA under the omnibus-account terms and conditions. [S:e64b7b0267b2, Background, p.1]
- The SFC framed VA financing as a first step under Pillar P of its ASPIRe roadmap. It is limited to VA brokers that already provide securities margin financing. [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 3-5]
- The HKMA allows RIs providing VA dealing to offer VA financing if they observe standards similar to the SFC's. It restates those standards for RIs as items (1)-(9). [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', p.2]
- Eligible borrowers are only the RI's existing securities margin clients. Credit limits should not rise just because VA financing is available. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (2)-(3)]
- Where a client's VA holdings count toward financial capability, the RI should assess seven risk factors. These include price-gap risk, digital-asset sector concentration and correlation with equities under stress. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (3)(a)-(g)]
- If an RI chooses to rely on VA collateral, it is limited to bitcoin, ether and stablecoins issued under HKMA licences ('Relevant Stablecoins'), with a prudent haircut. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4)]
- The haircut for bitcoin and ether should be 60% or more. The HKMA may revise it on prior notice. No numeric haircut is set for Relevant Stablecoins. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4) and footnote 5]
- Collateral is generally valued at prices on the VATP where the client's orders execute (or, for Relevant Stablecoins, the licensed issuer). RIs should keep a contingency plan for VATP outages. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (6) and footnote 6]
- VA financing counts as part of the margin loan book. Concentration controls cover VA collateral, and VA collateral may not be repledged or reused except on client default. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (7)(a)-(c)]
- If the loan exceeds collateral value, or a client has an outstanding margin call and a poor payment record, the RI should suspend advances, stop VA purchases on margin and review the client's limits. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (8)]
- Because the Banking (Capital) Rules do not treat VA as eligible collateral, loans secured solely by VA must be treated as clean loans to the client. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (9)]
- For LCs, VA collateral gets a 100% haircut under the Financial Resources Rules until the SFC consults on revised capital rules. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 14]
- Tokenised products fall outside this circular. The financing ban does not apply to them, and RIs follow the HKMA's February 2024 tokenised-products circular instead. [S:e64b7b0267b2, footnote 4]
- A shared order book lets an SFC-licensed VATP integrate its order book with affiliated overseas platforms. The overseas platform must be in a FATF or FATF-style regional body member jurisdiction with aligned regulation. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 3, 7]
- VATPs operating a shared order book should accept only fully pre-funded orders, settle on delivery-versus-payment, and hold a Hong Kong reserve fund on trust at least equal to the Unsettled Trade Limit. [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 10, 13, 17]
- RIs doing agency trading on a shared order book, or offering retail access, follow SFC 26EC5 paras 15-17. Before trading they assess conflicts, settlement, default handling, reserve funds and client recourse. Retail access needs a prominent risk explanation and an express election. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Shared Order Book', p.6]; [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 16-17]
- Where client VAs stay in custody at the VATP, a withdrawal function adds cyber risk: a compromised broker system could send fraudulent withdrawal instructions. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 19]
- RIs permitting withdrawals must apply SFC 26EC5 paras 18-20 on top of HKMA e-banking and technology-risk rules. These cover robust authentication, round-the-clock monitoring with suspension, and working with VATPs on withdrawal limits. [S:e64b7b0267b2, 'Client VA withdrawals', p.6]; [S:73dac3a5a832, para 20]
- 26EC6 lets a VATP accept an affiliated market maker, subject to conditions. These include functional independence, client-order priority at the same price, post-trade identification and disclosure. The VATP must notify the SFC first with an independent firm's report. [S:84b3c1a0cee1, paras 7, 9-10]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Offer VA financing only to existing securities margin clients, on financial capability and posted collateral. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (1)-(2)]
- Book VA-only-secured loans as clean loans for capital. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (9)]
- Do not repledge, reuse or encumber VA collateral except on default. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (7)(c)]

**Controls & monitoring**
- Document VA risk-assessment methods in the margin lending policy, and monitor vulnerable VA exposures on an ongoing basis. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (5)(a)]
- Keep real-time capability to monitor VA collateral volatility and act promptly. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (5)(b)]
- Set margin-call and stop-advance triggers, and act on them. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (8)]
- For withdrawals: monitor continuously, with escalation and suspension also outside office hours. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 20(b)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- The circular itself sets no new notification step. For new VA activity, the HKMA's standing expectation is to discuss plans before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body para 5]
- Contacts for queries are named in the circular, by topic. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Before shared order book trading, assess the operators' conflicts of interest, the settlement mechanism, failure and default processes, reserve fund and insurance, and client recourse. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 16]
- Agree withdrawal limits and suspicious-withdrawal blocking with the partner VATP. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 20(c)]
- Keep an outage contingency plan for the VATP used to value collateral. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (6)]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The HKMA's 27 May 2026 circular lets our RI offer VA margin financing on SFC-equivalent terms. Collateral is limited to bitcoin, ether and HKMA-licensed stablecoins. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (1)-(4)]
- Withdrawal and shared-order-book standards come from SFC 26EC5 paras 15-20, applied to RIs by the HKMA. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.6]

**To business heads**
- Only existing securities margin clients qualify, and limits should not rise just because VA financing exists. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (2)-(3)]
- Retail clients can use a shared order book only after an explained overseas-risk warning and an express opt-in. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 17]

**To the CEO**
- This is a regulator-described first step to bring margin trading into VA, aimed at liquidity with risk controls. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 3]
- The HKMA gives VA collateral no capital credit, so VA-backed lending carries clean-loan capital. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (9)]

**To Risk / CRO**
- Bitcoin and ether collateral needs at least a 60% haircut. Stablecoin haircuts are left to the RI's prudent judgement. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (4), footnote 5]
- The HKMA warns that VAs have a limited track record in systemic shocks and can fall sharply within a day. [S:e64b7b0267b2, item (3)]

## Common mix-ups

- 'The SFC circular binds banks directly.' It binds LCs. Banks, as RIs, are brought in by the HKMA's 27 May 2026 circular. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 1]; [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2]
- 'Bank collateral rules are identical to the SFC's.' The HKMA adds Relevant Stablecoins as eligible collateral and uses Banking (Capital) Rules clean-loan treatment rather than the Financial Resources Rules 100% haircut. [S:e64b7b0267b2, items (4), (9)]; [S:73dac3a5a832, paras 7, 14]
- 'The financing ban covered tokenised products.' It never did. The HKMA says the prohibition does not apply to acquiring tokenised products. [S:e64b7b0267b2, footnote 4]
- 'A shared order book can link to any overseas exchange.' At this stage it is limited to intra-group (affiliated) platforms. [S:f009f1a6d615, para 3]

## Open items

- Paragraph-number conflict: SFC 26EC5 cites the financing ban as para 4.4 of the omnibus terms and conditions. The HKMA cites para 4.3 'as renumbered on 30 September 2025'. Both are official. Check the revised terms attached to the HKMA stablecoin circular of 27 May 2026 for the current numbering. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 2]; [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.1 footnote 3]
- Date discrepancy: the HKMA's footnote 4 dates the joint circular 23 December 2023. The SFC's version is dated 22 December 2023. [S:e64b7b0267b2, footnote 4]; [S:8a016a912528, p.1]
- The SFC capital-requirements consultation for VA activities has no published date. [S:73dac3a5a832, para 14]
- The revised terms and conditions (appendices to the HKMA's 27 May 2026 stablecoin circular) are catalogued, but their full text was not reviewed for this module. [S:553c7d718bec, landing page]
- No numeric haircut is specified for Relevant Stablecoin collateral.
