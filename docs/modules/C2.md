# C2 VA funds and ETFs

This module covers virtual asset (VA) funds and exchange-traded funds (ETFs). It is for the Head of Compliance at a bank that sells funds or acts as a participating dealer. A participating dealer is a firm allowed to create and redeem units in an exchange-traded fund. Tokenised funds are funds whose units are recorded as digital tokens. After this module, you can explain which crypto funds and tokenised funds may be sold in Hong Kong, and on what terms. You can also explain what the bank checks before it sells, creates or redeems units.

## In 60 seconds

In October 2022 the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) began taking applications to authorise VA ETFs [S:51dac2cabe78, para 6; S:c79c4686c5e0, para 5]. These ETFs got their exposure to virtual assets (VAs) mainly through futures (contracts to buy or sell at a set price on a future date) [S:51dac2cabe78, para 6; S:c79c4686c5e0, para 5]. A December 2023 circular let authorised funds invest directly in spot VAs (the crypto itself, not contracts on it) [S:5b6530666cc2, paras 2, 5]. An April 2025 revision added staking (locking up tokens to help run a blockchain in return for rewards) and other VA-related activities [S:353535602a83, paras 1–2, 27]. A new version, reference 26EC31, replaced the April 2025 revision on 27 May 2026 [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 3]. A fund that wants more than 10% of its net asset value (NAV) in VAs needs to consult the SFC and get its approval first [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 31]. Such a fund should invest only in tokens that the Hong Kong public can trade on SFC-licensed virtual asset trading platforms (VATPs) [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 15]. The fund should not use leverage (borrowing or derivatives that magnify exposure) to increase its VA exposure at fund level [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 15]. The fund's trustee may delegate VA custody (safekeeping) only to three types of firm [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 20]. These are licensed VATPs, qualifying authorized institutions (AIs), and other firms the SFC accepts [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 20]. A qualifying AI is a bank or other AI that meets the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) custody standards [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 20]. Separate SFC circulars allow tokenised versions of authorised products, including tokenised fund classes [S:58ea8520974d, para 5 and fn 8]. Since the 20 April 2026 revision, investors may also trade these tokenised products with each other on licensed VATPs [S:58ea8520974d, para 6; S:a64e364fd912, para 8]. When a bank sells SFC-authorised VA funds, the joint SFC–HKMA circular sets the selling rules [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 27; S:8a016a912528, para 9]. Participating dealers (PDs) for spot VA ETFs should be SFC-licensed corporations (LCs) or registered institutions (RIs), meaning banks registered with the SFC [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8]. The SFC may add extra terms for these PDs [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8].

## Key facts

- The first VA ETFs that the SFC allowed were based on futures [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 10, 12]. At first, the only futures allowed were bitcoin and ether futures traded on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 10, 12]. The fund manager needed at least three years of experience managing ETFs [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 10, 12].
- The 2022 circular also capped each fund's net derivative exposure (its overall derivatives position after offsets) at 100% of NAV [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 14, 16]. It also required intermediaries to run a VA-knowledge test (a check that the client understands VAs) when serving clients in these ETFs [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 14, 16].
- Under the December 2023 circular, funds could invest directly in spot VAs [S:5b6530666cc2, para 5]. The first spot VA ETFs listed on the Hong Kong stock exchange on 30 April 2024 [S:e4236ed2600f, para 1].
- The current circular, 26EC31, covers public funds with more than 10% of NAV in VAs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 1, 3]. It replaced the April 2025 version [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 1, 3].
- Circular 26EC31 does not cover a fund's exposure to Relevant Stablecoins or tokenised deposits [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2 and fn 4]. Relevant Stablecoins are stablecoins tied to one or more official currencies and issued under a licence under the Stablecoins Ordinance [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2 and fn 4]. Tokenised deposits are bank deposits recorded as digital tokens. For fund exposure to either of them, the SFC points to question 20G of its FAQs on the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2 and fn 4].
- Eligible VAs are limited to tokens that the Hong Kong public can trade on SFC-licensed VATPs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 13, 15]. VA futures must trade on conventional regulated futures exchanges [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 13, 15]. Funds should not use leverage to increase their VA exposure at fund level [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 13, 15].
- Funds should buy and sell spot VAs through one of two routes [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 17 and fn 7]. The first is SFC-licensed VATPs, either on the platform or over the counter (a direct deal off the platform) [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 17 and fn 7]. The second is AIs, or subsidiaries of locally incorporated AIs, that comply with HKMA requirements [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 17 and fn 7].
- Spot VA ETFs may create and redeem units in cash or in kind (paying with the VAs themselves) [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18]. For in-kind creations, PDs are expected to move VAs into the fund's custody accounts at VATPs or AIs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18]. The VAs may have been held in Hong Kong or overseas [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18].
- PDs for spot VA ETFs should be LCs or RIs [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8]. The SFC's Intermediaries Division may impose extra terms on them [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8]. Appendix 6 of the joint circular sets out these terms [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8].
- The trustee may delegate VA custody only to SFC-licensed VATPs, qualifying AIs, or other firms the SFC accepts [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 20–21]. Qualifying AIs are AIs, or subsidiaries of locally incorporated AIs, that meet HKMA custody standards [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 20–21]. Custodians should keep most VA holdings in cold wallets (storage kept offline) [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 20–21]. They should store seeds and private keys (the secret codes that control the VAs) securely in Hong Kong [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 20–21].
- The SFC may allow VA funds to stake through licensed VATPs or, where applicable, AIs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(d), 28(f), 31]. The conditions include disclosing how much of the fund may be staked, and reporting on staking in interim and annual reports [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(d), 28(f), 31]. The fund also needs to consult the SFC and get its approval first [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(d), 28(f), 31]. In April 2025, the SFC said it caps fund staking to manage liquidity risk (the risk of running short of cash for redemptions) [S:36753b22e10a, para 4].
- SFC-authorised VA funds are not restricted to professional investors (PIs) [S:8a016a912528, paras 9, 9.1–9.2 and fn 14]. For units listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), an intermediary can execute on-exchange orders without a suitability check or warning statements [S:8a016a912528, paras 9, 9.1–9.2 and fn 14]. This applies only where the intermediary did not solicit or recommend the trade [S:8a016a912528, paras 9, 9.1–9.2 and fn 14]. A VA-knowledge test still applies [S:8a016a912528, paras 9, 9.1–9.2 and fn 14]. Unlisted classes, and trades off exchange, need a suitability check, warnings and the knowledge test [S:8a016a912528, paras 9, 9.1–9.2 and fn 14].
- The tokenisation circular looks through the token to the product underneath (a see-through approach) [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 13]. It permits primary dealing (buying units from, and selling them back to, the product provider) in tokenised authorised products [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 13]. Product providers should use public permissionless blockchains (open networks anyone can join) only if they add proper controls [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 13].
- Retail investors may be offered secondary trading (buying and selling between investors) in tokenised products [S:a64e364fd912, paras 8, 12(a)]. This trading runs through on-screen order matching on SFC-licensed VATPs [S:a64e364fd912, paras 8, 12(a)]. One control is an alert when the price moves away from the indicative NAV (a live estimate of the fund's value) [S:a64e364fd912, paras 8, 12(a)].
- In March 2026, the Hong Kong public could buy 13 tokenised products [S:bbd10edd41cf, paras 3, 6]. Their tokenised classes held about $10.7 billion in assets [S:bbd10edd41cf, paras 3, 6]. The SFC expects the first products offered for secondary trading to be mainly tokenised money market funds [S:bbd10edd41cf, paras 3, 6].

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Apply the joint circular's selling rules when you sell any SFC-authorised VA fund [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 27; S:8a016a912528, paras 6.2, 9]. These rules include the VA-knowledge test for all clients except institutional and qualified corporate PIs [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 27; S:8a016a912528, paras 6.2, 9].
- Offer complex VA-related products (products whose terms and risks a retail investor is unlikely to understand) only to PIs [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.1, 12.3]. VA funds not authorised by the SFC are one example [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.1, 12.3]. Before offering such a fund, do the Appendix 4 due diligence on the fund and its manager [S:8a016a912528, appendices, Appendix 4]. Cover the firms the fund uses for trading and custody too [S:8a016a912528, appendices, Appendix 4].
- Be a licensed corporation or registered institution to distribute tokenised authorised products [S:58ea8520974d, para 18]. Apply the usual onboarding and suitability rules [S:58ea8520974d, para 18].
- Apply the underlying product's selling rules to tokenised products that the Securities and Futures Ordinance does not cover [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.1–3 and fn 1]. The HKMA expects this, and expects the same for tokenised deposits placed by customers [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.1–3 and fn 1]. The HKMA also expects AIs to understand and control the extra risks that tokenisation brings [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.1–3 and fn 1]. RIs that sell tokenised products other than tokenised securities should follow this HKMA guidance [S:d5ebca32c92b, fn 2].

**Controls & monitoring**
- Show clients the price-deviation alert if the bank acts as a Connecting Broker [S:a64e364fd912, paras 13, 20(c) and fn 4]. A Connecting Broker is a licensed corporation or registered institution that routes clients' trades to VATPs [S:a64e364fd912, paras 13, 20(c) and fn 4]. Tell clients they can subscribe or redeem at NAV instead [S:a64e364fd912, paras 13, 20(c) and fn 4]. Get clients to confirm the risks before onboarding them [S:a64e364fd912, paras 13, 20(c) and fn 4].
- Keep an online screen that shows the indicative NAV and the last NAV [S:a64e364fd912, para 20(b)]. The indicative NAV is usually refreshed at least every 15 seconds while trading is open [S:a64e364fd912, para 20(b)].
- Build access rights and controls into any online platform that sells VA-related products, so the platform enforces selling restrictions [S:8a016a912528, para 11].

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Give the SFC advance notice before first letting clients subscribe or redeem with VAs, including in-kind flows [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 7(a), 10(d)]. Tell the HKMA too, where relevant [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 7(a), 10(d)].
- Notify the SFC before first taking part in secondary trading of tokenised products [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13]. The SFC reminds RIs to notify the HKMA too [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13].
- Discuss with the HKMA before starting any activity covered by its tokenised-products circular [S:b2719f3dcb37, Implementation, p.6].

**Counterparty due diligence**
- Hold VAs for in-kind flows only in accounts with SFC-licensed platforms or AIs [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(b)]. For these VA transfers, the supplemental joint circular also points to the SFC's anti-money laundering (AML) guideline [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(c)]. The relevant part is Chapter 12 of the guideline for LCs and SFC-licensed VA service providers [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(c)].
- Do product due diligence on each VA-related product [S:8a016a912528, para 12.3]. Cover its risks and features, its target investors and selling restrictions, and its regulatory status [S:8a016a912528, para 12.3].
- Do due diligence on the issuer and its technology vendors for tokenised products under the HKMA circular [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3–4]. Cover smart-contract audits (independent reviews of the token's code) and private key management [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3–4]. Also cover contingency plans for the distributed ledger (the shared record behind the tokens) [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3–4].

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The VA fund circular is written for product providers [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 8, 19, 27]. Our duties as distributor or participating dealer come from the joint circular and any extra SFC terms for PDs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 8, 19, 27].
- For secondary trading of tokenised products, intermediaries notify the SFC first [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13]. RIs are reminded to notify the HKMA as well [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13].

**To business heads**
- Retail clients can trade SEHK-listed VA ETFs on exchange without a suitability check if we did not solicit or recommend the trade [S:8a016a912528, paras 9.1–9.2, fn 14]. They still need a knowledge test [S:8a016a912528, paras 9.1–9.2, fn 14]. Unlisted classes of the same fund need a full suitability check [S:8a016a912528, paras 9.1–9.2, fn 14].
- In-kind creation is allowed, and the VAs may come from overseas holdings [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18]. PDs are expected to move the VAs into custody at VATPs or AIs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18].

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong allowed futures-only VA ETFs in 2022 and spot VA funds in 2023 [S:51dac2cabe78, para 12; S:5b6530666cc2, para 5]. Fund staking followed in 2025 [S:353535602a83, para 27]. In 2026 came a pilot of 24/7 secondary trading of tokenised funds [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 4].
- As of March 2026, 13 tokenised products held about $10.7 billion in their tokenised classes [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 3].

**To Risk / CRO**
- VA funds should not use leverage for VA exposure at fund level [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 15, 20]. Custody can go only to VATPs, qualifying AIs, or firms the SFC accepts [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 15, 20].
- Staking by funds needs SFC consultation and approval first [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(f), 31]. Interim and annual reports should show staked holdings as a share of NAV, along with revenue and costs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(f), 31].

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** Hong Kong only has futures VA ETFs. **Fact:** Funds have been allowed to hold spot VAs directly since the December 2023 circular [S:5b6530666cc2, para 5].
- **Myth:** A fund holding licensed stablecoins is a VA fund. **Fact:** Circular 26EC31 excludes Relevant Stablecoins and tokenised deposits [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2].
- **Myth:** The 2023 or 2025 fund circular still applies. **Fact:** Each was replaced, most recently by 26EC31 on 27 May 2026 [S:353535602a83, para 2; S:c79c4686c5e0, para 3].
- **Myth:** Tokenised funds only allow subscription and redemption. **Fact:** The 20 April 2026 revision allows secondary trading on VATPs [S:58ea8520974d, para 6]. The 2023 version had called for more caution [S:d422282a262d, para 6].
- **Myth:** In-kind subscription makes the bank a VA dealer. **Fact:** The regulators say in-kind subscription is not treated as VA dealing [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7]. Notification, custody and AML conditions still apply [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7].

## Open items

- Question 20F of the FAQs on the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds holds the detailed staking requirements for VA funds [S:c79c4686c5e0, fn 4, fn 11]. Question 20G covers exposure to stablecoins and tokenised deposits [S:c79c4686c5e0, fn 4, fn 11]. Neither question is in the documents reviewed for this guide.
- For in-kind VA transfers, the supplemental joint circular cites the SFC's AML guideline for LCs and VA service providers [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(c)]. The circular does not say how this fits with the HKMA's own AML guideline for AIs.
- The SFC says it will review the first products offered for secondary trading [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6]. It will then consider widening the product range in due course [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6]. The SFC has given no timeline [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6].
- The 2026 Policy Address (the Chief Executive's yearly policy speech) says the SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed VATPs [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]. It will also promote their use in settling tokenised money market funds [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)].

## Check yourself

1. Which circular sets the selling rules a bank follows when it sells SFC-authorised VA funds?
   **Answer:** The joint SFC–HKMA circular sets the selling rules [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 27; S:8a016a912528, para 9].
2. Above what share of its net asset value (NAV) in VAs must a public fund consult the SFC first?
   **Answer:** A fund that wants more than 10% of its net asset value (NAV) in VAs needs to consult the SFC and get its approval first [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 31].
3. Is the December 2023 VA fund circular still current, or has it been superseded?
   **Answer:** Superseded. The April 2025 revision replaced it, and circular 26EC31 replaced that on 27 May 2026 [S:353535602a83, para 2; S:c79c4686c5e0, para 3]. 26EC31 is the version in force.
4. What should a bank do before first letting clients subscribe or redeem fund units with VAs?
   **Answer:** It should give the SFC advance notice, including for in-kind flows, and tell the HKMA too where relevant [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 7(a), 10(d)].
5. Does a fund holding licensed stablecoins count as a VA fund under circular 26EC31?
   **Answer:** No. Circular 26EC31 excludes Relevant Stablecoins and tokenised deposits from its VA fund rules [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2].
