# C2 VA funds and ETFs

For the Head of Compliance at a bank that distributes funds or acts as participating dealer: after this module you can explain which crypto funds and tokenised funds may be sold in Hong Kong, on what terms, and what the bank checks before it sells, creates or redeems units.

## In 60 seconds

In October 2022 the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) began accepting applications to authorise exchange-traded funds (ETFs) that get exposure to virtual assets (VAs) mainly through futures [S:51dac2cabe78, para 6; S:c79c4686c5e0, para 5]. A December 2023 circular let authorised funds invest in spot VAs directly [S:5b6530666cc2, paras 2, 5]. A revised version in April 2025 added staking and other VA-related activities [S:353535602a83, paras 1–2, 27]. That version was in turn replaced on 27 May 2026 (26EC31) [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 3]. A fund seeking more than 10% of its net asset value (NAV) in VAs needs prior SFC consultation and approval [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 31]. It should invest only in tokens the Hong Kong public can trade on virtual asset trading platforms (VATPs) licensed by the SFC, and should not have leveraged VA exposure at fund level [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 15]. Its trustee may delegate VA custody only to a licensed VATP, to an authorized institution (AI, such as a bank) that meets Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) custody standards, or to another entity the SFC accepts [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 20]. Separate SFC circulars allow tokenised versions of authorised products, including tokenised fund classes [S:58ea8520974d, para 5 and fn 8]. Since the 20 April 2026 revision, these may also be traded on the secondary market on licensed VATPs [S:58ea8520974d, para 6; S:a64e364fd912, para 8]. When a bank distributes SFC-authorised VA funds, the joint SFC–HKMA circular sets the selling rules [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 27; S:8a016a912528, para 9]. Participating dealers (PDs) for spot VA ETFs should be SFC-licensed corporations (LCs) or registered institutions (RIs), subject to any extra SFC terms [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8].

## Key facts

- The first VA ETFs allowed were futures-based. At first only bitcoin and ether futures traded on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange were allowed, and the manager needed at least three years' track record in managing ETFs [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 10, 12].
- The 2022 circular also capped net derivative exposure at 100% of NAV and required intermediaries to apply the VA-knowledge test when serving clients in these ETFs [S:51dac2cabe78, paras 14, 16].
- Under the December 2023 circular, funds could invest directly in spot VAs [S:5b6530666cc2, para 5]. The first spot VA ETFs listed on the Hong Kong stock exchange on 30 April 2024 [S:e4236ed2600f, para 1].
- The current circular (26EC31) applies to public funds with more than 10% of NAV in VAs. It replaced the April 2025 version [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 1, 3].
- 26EC31 does not cover fund exposure to Relevant Stablecoins (fiat-referenced stablecoins issued under a licence granted under the Stablecoins Ordinance) or tokenised deposits. For those, the SFC points to question 20G of its FAQs on its code for unit trusts and mutual funds (UT Code) [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2 and fn 4].
- Eligible VAs are limited to tokens the Hong Kong public can trade on SFC-licensed VATPs. VA futures must trade on conventional regulated futures exchanges, and funds should not have leveraged VA exposure at fund level [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 11, 13, 15].
- Funds should buy and sell spot VAs through SFC-licensed VATPs (on or off platform), or through AIs (or subsidiaries of locally incorporated AIs) that comply with HKMA requirements [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 17 and fn 7].
- Spot ETFs may create and redeem units either in cash or in kind. For in-kind creations, PDs are expected to move VAs into the ETF's custody account at a VATP or an AI. The VAs may have been held locally or overseas [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18].
- PDs for spot VA ETFs should be LCs or RIs, and are subject to extra terms the SFC's Intermediaries Division may impose, set out in Appendix 6 of the joint circular [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 19 and fn 8].
- The trustee may delegate VA custody only to an SFC-licensed VATP, to an AI (or a locally incorporated AI's subsidiary) that meets HKMA custody standards, or to another entity the SFC accepts. Custodians should keep most VA holdings in cold wallets, and store seeds and private keys securely in Hong Kong [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 20–21].
- The SFC may allow VA funds to stake through licensed VATPs or, where applicable, AIs. Conditions include disclosing how much may be used, reporting in interim and annual reports, and prior SFC consultation and approval [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(d), 28(f), 31]. When it added staking in April 2025, the SFC said fund staking is capped to manage liquidity risk [S:36753b22e10a, para 4].
- On distribution, SFC-authorised VA funds are not restricted to professional investors (PIs). For units listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), with no solicitation or recommendation, on-exchange orders can be executed without suitability or warning statements, but a VA-knowledge test still applies. Unlisted classes, or trades off exchange, need suitability, warnings and the knowledge test [S:8a016a912528, paras 9, 9.1–9.2 and fn 14].
- The tokenisation circular uses a see-through approach, looking past the token to the underlying product. It permits primary dealing in tokenised authorised products. Product providers should not use public permissionless blockchains unless they add proper controls [S:58ea8520974d, paras 5, 13].
- Retail investors may be offered secondary trading in tokenised products through on-screen matching on SFC-licensed VATPs. Controls include a price-deviation alert against the indicative NAV [S:a64e364fd912, paras 8, 12(a)].
- In March 2026 the Hong Kong public could buy 13 tokenised products, with tokenised-class assets of about $10.7 billion. The SFC expects the first secondary-trading batch to be mainly tokenised money market funds [S:bbd10edd41cf, paras 3, 6].

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- Apply the joint circular's selling rules when distributing any SFC-authorised VA fund, including the knowledge test for all clients except institutional and qualified corporate PIs [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 27; S:8a016a912528, paras 6.2, 9].
- Offer VA-related products that are complex products, such as VA funds not authorised by the SFC, only to PIs. For non-authorised VA funds, first do the Appendix 4 due diligence on the fund, its manager and its trading and custody counterparties [S:8a016a912528, paras 5, 6.1, 12.3; S:8a016a912528, appendices, Appendix 4].
- As a distributor of tokenised authorised products, be an LC or RI and apply the usual onboarding and suitability rules [S:58ea8520974d, para 18].
- For tokenised products that fall outside the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) regime, and for tokenised deposits placed by customers, the HKMA expects the selling rules for the underlying product to apply. It also expects AIs to understand and control the extra risks from tokenisation [S:b2719f3dcb37, pp.1–3 and fn 1]. RIs selling tokenised products other than tokenised securities should follow this HKMA guidance [S:d5ebca32c92b, fn 2].

**Controls & monitoring**
- As a Connecting Broker (an LC or RI routing clients' trades to a VATP), show the price-deviation alert, tell clients they can subscribe or redeem at NAV instead, and get their confirmation of the risks before onboarding [S:a64e364fd912, paras 13, 20(c) and fn 4].
- Keep an online interface showing indicative NAV, usually refreshed at least every 15 seconds while trading is open, plus the last NAV [S:a64e364fd912, para 20(b)].
- Where VA-related products are sold online, design the platform with access rights and controls that enforce selling restrictions [S:8a016a912528, para 11].

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- Give the SFC advance notice, and the HKMA too where relevant, before first letting clients subscribe or redeem with VAs, including in-kind flows [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 7(a), 10(d)].
- Before first engaging in secondary trading of tokenised products, notify the SFC. RIs are reminded to notify the HKMA too [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13].
- Before starting activities covered by the HKMA tokenised-products circular, discuss them with the HKMA [S:b2719f3dcb37, Implementation, p.6].

**Counterparty due diligence**
- For in-kind flows, hold the VAs only in accounts with SFC-licensed platforms or AIs [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(b)]. The supplemental joint circular also points to Chapter 12 of the SFC's anti-money laundering (AML) guideline for LCs and SFC-licensed VA service providers when handling these VA transfers [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(c)].
- Do product due diligence on each VA-related product: its risks and features, target investors and selling restrictions, and regulatory status [S:8a016a912528, para 12.3].
- On tokenised products covered by the HKMA circular, do due diligence on the issuer and its technology vendors, including smart-contract audits, private key management and distributed ledger technology (DLT) contingency plans [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3–4].

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The VA fund circular is written for product providers. The bank's own duties as distributor or PD come from the joint circular and any extra SFC terms for PDs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 8, 19, 27].
- For tokenised secondary trading, intermediaries notify the SFC first, and RIs are reminded to notify the HKMA as well [S:a64e364fd912, para 27 and fn 13].

**To business heads**
- SEHK-listed VA ETFs can be traded on exchange for retail clients without solicitation, with a knowledge test. Unlisted classes of the same ETF need full suitability [S:8a016a912528, paras 9.1–9.2, fn 14].
- In-kind creation is allowed, and the VAs may come from overseas holdings. PDs are expected to move them into custody at a VATP or AI [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 17(b), 18].

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong has moved from futures-only VA ETFs (2022) to spot VA funds (2023), fund staking (2025) and a pilot of 24/7 secondary trading of tokenised funds (2026) [S:51dac2cabe78, para 12; S:5b6530666cc2, para 5; S:353535602a83, para 27; S:bbd10edd41cf, para 4].
- Tokenised-class assets were about $10.7 billion across 13 products as of March 2026 [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 3].

**To Risk / CRO**
- VA funds should not have leveraged VA exposure at fund level, and custody delegates are limited to VATPs, qualifying AIs, or entities the SFC accepts [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 15, 20].
- Staking by funds needs prior SFC consultation and approval. Interim and annual reports should show staked holdings as a share of NAV, along with revenue and costs [S:c79c4686c5e0, paras 28(f), 31].

## Common mix-ups

- "Hong Kong only has futures VA ETFs." Direct spot exposure has been allowed since the December 2023 circular [S:5b6530666cc2, para 5].
- "A fund holding licensed stablecoins is a VA fund." 26EC31 excludes Relevant Stablecoins and tokenised deposits [S:c79c4686c5e0, para 2].
- "The 2023 or 2025 fund circular still applies." Each was replaced, most recently by 26EC31 on 27 May 2026 [S:353535602a83, para 2; S:c79c4686c5e0, para 3].
- "Tokenised funds can only be subscribed and redeemed." The 20 April 2026 revision allows secondary trading on VATPs; the 2023 version had called for more caution [S:58ea8520974d, para 6; S:d422282a262d, para 6].
- "In-kind subscription makes the bank a VA dealer." The regulators say it is not treated as VA dealing, but notification, custody and AML conditions apply [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7].

## Open items

- The detailed staking requirements for VA funds sit in question 20F of the UT Code FAQs, and question 20G covers stablecoin and tokenised-deposit exposure. Neither is in the documents reviewed for this guide [S:c79c4686c5e0, fn 4, fn 11].
- The supplemental joint circular cites the SFC's AML guideline for LCs and VA service providers for in-kind VA transfers [S:d5ebca32c92b, para 7(c)]. How this maps onto the HKMA's own AML guideline for AIs is not stated in the circular.
- The SFC says it will review the first secondary-trading products and consider widening the product scope in due course. No timeline has been given [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 6].
- The 2026 Policy Address says the SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed VATPs and their use in settling tokenised money market funds [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)].
