# F6 Your path to COO

This module is for a head of compliance aiming to be chief operating officer (COO) of a digital-assets business. After reading it, you can name the approvals a COO route runs into and map the skills the role draws on. As of 25 Sep 2026.

## In 60 seconds

The Banking Ordinance requires the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) to approve the directors and chief executive of a locally incorporated bank. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 12] A bank registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) needs at least two executive officers (EOs) for each regulated activity. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 23] Each executive officer (EO) directly supervises that activity and needs HKMA consent. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 24]

The SFC expects each firm it licenses to name a Manager-In-Charge (MIC) for each of eight core functions. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, paras 7-8] A bank licensed to issue stablecoins needs HKMA consent before it appoints its stablecoin manager. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.3]

For an EO, the HKMA must be satisfied the person is fit and proper and has enough authority. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 24] The SFC's standard route for a responsible officer (RO) asks for qualifications, industry experience, management experience and a regulatory exam. [S:9a95f8495c50, para 4.2.1.2] An EO must meet the same bar, and the HKMA takes the SFC's guidelines into account. [S:9a95f8495c50, para 2.2]

## Key facts

### At a bank (HKMA gates)

- The Banking Ordinance (BO) requires HKMA approval, under section 71, of each director and chief executive (including alternates) of a bank incorporated in Hong Kong. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 12]
- Under the BO, a manager is someone, other than a director or chief executive, who is principally responsible for a listed business or function. [S:7e7494d80711, para 2.1.1]
- The listed functions include accounts, risk controls, anti-money laundering (AML) controls, computer systems, internal audit and compliance. [S:7e7494d80711, para 2.2.1]
- The HKMA expects banks to notify it of new managers, departures and changed duties within 14 days. [S:7e7494d80711, para 6.1.3]
- No one may act as an EO before the HKMA gives written consent. [S:75a7c37f8b3d, Annex 1, Part II, 'Application to become an EO']
- The HKMA expects an EO to be the most senior person in the regulated activity. An EO should sit no more than one rank below a chief executive, director or manager notified under BO section 72B (a section 72B manager). [S:2baef0f142a0, para 27]
- For each regulated activity, at least one EO should be a chief executive, alternate chief executive, director or section 72B manager. [S:75a7c37f8b3d, Annex 1, Part II, 'Assessment of an application to become an EO']
- The HKMA expects a registered bank to name the senior person responsible for each listed business or function that touches regulated activities. [S:17871e6ed4e0, Q1/A1] The HKMA treats compliance, risk and internal audit as no less important than front-office roles here. [S:17871e6ed4e0, Q4/A4]

### At an SFC-licensed firm

- Senior management of a licensed corporation includes its directors, responsible officers (ROs) and Managers-In-Charge (MICs). One person can hold all three roles. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, paras 5-6]
- Four of the eight core functions are overall management oversight, key business lines, operational control and review, and risk. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, para 7] The others are finance and accounting, information technology (IT), compliance, and AML and counter-terrorist financing. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, para 7]
- The SFC generally expects the MICs for overall oversight and key business lines to be ROs. It does not expect other MICs to be ROs. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, para 23]
- The SFC may approve a long-serving head of compliance, risk, finance or operations as an RO for overall oversight, even without direct regulated-activity experience. A typical condition is to work alongside a fully competent RO. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, para 24]
- MICs are regulated persons. The SFC can hold them to account for the firm's misconduct that arises from their consent, connivance or neglect. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, paras 17-18]

### At a licensed stablecoin issuer

- The Stablecoins Ordinance requires each chief executive, director and stablecoin manager of a licensee to be fit and proper. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.2]
- A licensee that is a bank must not appoint a stablecoin manager without HKMA consent. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.3] A non-bank licensee needs HKMA consent for its chief executive and directors. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.3]
- A person may become a controller (broadly, a major shareholder or someone whose instructions the firm follows) only in the circumstances section 37(1) of the Ordinance allows. The licensee should track its controllers and seek HKMA consent under section 38. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.5]
- A licensee must tell the HKMA within 14 days when a manager is appointed, leaves or takes on new areas. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.7]

### What the regulators test

- For directors and chief executives, the standard depends on the post. A person may suit one post but not another with different duties. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 15]
- The HKMA may meet a candidate for director, chief executive or EO face to face. It uses the meeting to understand their knowledge of Hong Kong banking, the bank's business and key rules. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 16]
- Under most options, an RO or EO needs at least three years of relevant industry experience in the past six. They also need two years of management experience and a pass in the local regulatory framework paper (LRP). [S:9a95f8495c50, para 4.2.1.2]

## How it works commercially

### A competency map

A COO runs the machine that turns strategy into daily service, within the rules (concept).

| Competency | What it covers | Where the rules touch it |
|---|---|---|
| Permissions and approvals | Knowing which licence, registration and personal consent each activity needs | The SFC application fee to add a regulated activity to a bank's registration is HK$23,500 per activity. [S:13f2903631f6, para 8.4.1] |
| Governance and accountability | Clear owners, reporting lines and board papers | The HKMA expects each registered bank to name its principally responsible people. [S:17871e6ed4e0, Q1/A1] |
| Operations and technology | Accounts, systems, controls and outsourcing | These are listed functions; the person principally responsible for each counts as a manager under the BO. [S:7e7494d80711, para 2.2.1] |
| People and competence | Hiring, training and supervising staff | The HKMA expects banks not to launch new products until staff understand them and the rules. [S:6ea96c41cccc, para 3.7.4] |
| Commercial judgement | Revenue, cost, capital and the profit and loss account (P&L) of a business line | No official test applies; this is a general management skill (concept). |
| Regulator engagement | Explaining plans and controls before launch | The HKMA expects banks to discuss new digital-asset activities with it before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] |
| Integrity and track record | A clean personal and firm history | For stablecoin licensees, fitness checks look at any firm the person helped run that was disciplined or wound up. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.2(iv)] |

### Moving from second line to first line

In the three lines of defence, the first line runs the business and owns its risks (concept). The second line, such as compliance, sets standards and challenges it, and internal audit checks both (concept).

The rules keep these roles apart. For a stablecoin licensee, the HKMA expects compliance and internal audit to be independent functions. [S:240be6833157, para 7.1.9] It also expects pay for control staff to be set independently of front-line units. [S:240be6833157, para 7.1.12]

The SFC judges experience by whether its substance is directly relevant to the activity and the role. [S:9a95f8495c50, para 4.1.9]

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** In years 1–3, what evidence of hands-on first-line work could a compliance head build?
> **How to think about it:** The competence tests for ROs and EOs count hands-on management of regulated functions and their staff. Purely administrative supervision normally does not count [S:9a95f8495c50, paras 4.1.12-4.1.13]. One framing: list each regulated activity the bank runs. For each, ask whether you supervised people doing it or only reviewed their work. Which roles sit close to delivery, such as leading a launch workstream or chairing a new-product review?
> **What it depends on:** the bank's structure; whether a sponsor offers a delivery role; how the regulators judge relevant experience for your case.
> **Official signposts:** the HKMA expectation to discuss new digital-asset activities before launch [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]; the Supervisory Incubator's offer of live trials with an HKMA team [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3].
> **What this is not:** not career advice, and not a statement of what any regulator would accept.

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** In years 3–6, what would move a record from control owner to business owner?
> **How to think about it:** The HKMA's signs that a person is principally responsible include owning targets, decision rights, budget authority and representing the unit [S:7e7494d80711, para 2.3.3]. Which roles show several of these signs, such as head of operations for a digital-asset line or head of a small line with its own P&L? Under the management-accountability guidance, the HKMA expects the people named as principally responsible to be chief executives, directors or section 72B managers [S:17871e6ed4e0, Q4/A4].
> **What it depends on:** whether the move keeps you within regulated activity; the size of the line; the bank's appetite for rotation.
> **Official signposts:** manager notifications to the HKMA [S:7e7494d80711, para 6.1.3]; the HKMA view that appraisals of managers also weigh compliance, not only profit [S:7e7494d80711, para 4.1.7].
> **What this is not:** not a forecast of any bank's hiring and not advice to change roles.

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** In years 6–10, what might a record contain when a COO-level post needs regulatory approval?
> **How to think about it:** A COO title alone triggers no approval. Approval or consent attaches to posts such as director, chief executive, EO or stablecoin manager. Managers a bank notifies to the HKMA are judged by function, whatever it is called [S:7e7494d80711, para 2.2.2]. For directors and chief executives, fitness is judged against the specific post [S:2baef0f142a0, para 15]. One self-test draws on the HKMA's face-to-face meeting: could you explain the bank's business, capital, liquidity and risk practices plainly [S:2baef0f142a0, para 16]? The HKMA also looks at firms a candidate helped run [S:2baef0f142a0, para 25; S:240be6833157, para 7.2.2(iv)].
> **What it depends on:** the vehicle (bank unit, licensed corporation or stablecoin issuer); which consent each post needs.
> **Official signposts:** section 71 approvals for directors and chief executives [S:2baef0f142a0, para 12]; stablecoin manager consent [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.3]; MIC and RO expectations [S:9e24bd74d3e6, para 23].
> **What this is not:** not a promise that any profile gains approval; the regulator decides case by case.

### Compliance as a commercial enabler

The HKMA expects every bank's board to endorse a strategic plan within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The plan responds to distributed ledger technology (DLT) and other tech change. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The plan should look for ways to adapt products, revenue models, risk management and operations. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The Supervisory Incubator gives banks a dedicated HKMA team for supervisory feedback, so they can check their risk controls before a full DLT launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Intermediaries must notify the SFC when they offer virtual asset (VA) trading or asset management services. The SFC advises them to discuss such plans with it first. [S:13f2903631f6, para 9.14.1]

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** How could a compliance head measure whether compliance work speeds launches up or slows them down?
> **How to think about it:** One framework treats each approval as a dated step on the launch plan, with an owner. Simple measures include weeks from idea to regulator discussion, rework loops, and conditions attached. These can sit beside revenue and cost rather than in a separate risk report. One question for past launches: did those that began with an early regulator discussion need fewer late changes?
> **What it depends on:** early access to product plans; a business sponsor who shares the pack; data on past launch times.
> **Official signposts:** the board strategic plan expectation [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]; the staff competence expectation before launch [S:6ea96c41cccc, para 3.7.4].
> **What this is not:** not a view on any product's profit and not the HKMA's or SFC's view.

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** Only licensed or approved people carry personal regulatory risk. **Fact:** SFC-licensed firms' MICs are regulated persons even if unlicensed. [S:9e24bd74d3e6, para 17] The same applies to anyone involved in managing a bank's regulated-activity business. [S:932ceff44493, p.2]
- **Myth:** A senior title is enough to be an EO. **Fact:** The HKMA also checks sufficient authority, including rank and reporting line. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 27]
- **Myth:** HKMA consent covers only directors and chief executives. **Fact:** EOs also need HKMA consent. [S:2baef0f142a0, para 24] A bank licensed to issue stablecoins also needs consent for its stablecoin manager. [S:240be6833157, para 7.2.3]

## Open items

- The sources reviewed do not say whether years spent in a bank's compliance function count as relevant industry experience for an EO application.
- The SFC's route for a long-serving control head to become an RO is written for licensed corporations. No matching HKMA text for EOs was found.
- The sources reviewed set no COO licence and no COO-specific competence standard. Under CG-2, what counts is the underlying business or function, not what the bank calls it. [S:7e7494d80711, para 2.2.2]
- Senior-management rules for the planned virtual asset dealing, custody, advisory and management regimes are not final. On 24 June 2026 the Government said it and the SFC would introduce a bill to the Legislative Council in 2026 (a stated target). [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (3), 'In addition' para]
