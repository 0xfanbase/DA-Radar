# C4 Stablecoins

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain Hong Kong's stablecoin law, what a licensed issuer must do, and every role a bank can play: issuer, offeror, dealer or adviser, custodian, or holder on its own balance sheet.

## In 60 seconds

The Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656) took effect on 1 August 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1] In the course of business, issuing a fiat-referenced stablecoin (FRS) in Hong Kong, or issuing one referenced to the Hong Kong dollar in or outside Hong Kong, needs a Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) licence. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] Licensed issuers must back each coin type in full with segregated reserves and must not pay interest; the HKMA expects valid redemptions at par, normally within one business day, and strict private-key controls. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1, 3.3.3, 6.5.7]; [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1] Only permitted offerors, including authorized institutions (AIs), may offer these coins, and only coins issued by licensed issuers may be offered to retail investors. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]; [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1] The first two licences took effect on 10 April 2026; the HKMA says that if more are granted, the overall number will remain very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:193316443831, Follow-up work section, para 3] On 27 May 2026 the HKMA (for registered institutions, RIs) and the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) relaxed how virtual asset (VA) conduct requirements apply to these Relevant Stablecoins, while keeping advance-notification duties. [S:a0bd48559762, pp.1-2, 9]; [S:19d52ade6849, p.1] For its own holdings, a bank classifies stablecoins under the capital module CRP-1 and treats them under the liquidity module LM-1. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1-1.3.2]; [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7]

## Key facts

- History: HKMA discussion paper (January 2022; its January 2023 conclusions reported 58 submissions); FSTB-HKMA consultation (27 Dec 2023 to 29 Feb 2024); consultation conclusions (17 Jul 2024, 108 submissions). [S:c72a4b732293, para 1.4]; [S:167649d1bc28, para 3]; [S:54aa01e7396e, paras 1-2]
- The Bill was gazetted on 6 December 2024, passed on 21 May 2025, and the Ordinance commenced on 1 August 2025. [S:a9a82f2b38ce, para 1]; [S:35e1ac2b815a, para 1]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- A licence is required to issue, in the course of business, an FRS in Hong Kong, or an FRS referenced to the Hong Kong dollar in or outside Hong Kong. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]
- Only FRS count as specified stablecoins for now; bank deposits, stored value facility (SVF) float and securities are outside the definition. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 2.1.2, 2.2.1-2.2.2]
- Permitted offerors are: a licensee, an AI, an SVF licensee, an SFC-licensed VATP, or an LC licensed for SFC Type 1 (dealing in securities). [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]
- Permitted offerors may offer licensed-issuer coins to anyone; other specified stablecoins only to professional investors; VA over-the-counter shops are not permitted offerors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), paras 1-2]
- Only adverts for licensed FRS issuance may be published; the rule applied during the six-month non-contravention period too. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 3]
- An overseas-incorporated AI may apply without a local subsidiary. The financial-resources and business-activity criteria do not apply to AI applicants. No HKMA consent is needed for someone to become an AI applicant's controller, chief executive or director. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 6, 8]
- A licensee must keep paid-up capital of at least HK$25 million; an AI licensee is exempt and follows the Banking Ordinance instead. [S:240be6833157, paras 5.1.1, 5.1.3]
- Reserves must be worth at least the par value of coins in circulation at all times and must be kept segregated; the HKMA expects an effective trust arrangement and a qualified custodian. Reserve deposits should have a term of no more than three months, and debt securities a residual maturity of no more than one year. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.3.1, 2.5.1-2.5.4]
- Valid redemptions must be honoured at par without unreasonable fees or unduly burdensome conditions; unless otherwise approved, they should be processed within one business day. [S:240be6833157, paras 3.3.3, 3.3.5]
- Licensees may not pay interest or yield tied to holding a coin. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1]
- Private-key controls should cover the whole key lifecycle. Significant keys should be generated offline in an air-gapped setting. Keys should be stored in Hong Kong or another HKMA-accepted location. [S:240be6833157, para 6.5.7 and items (ii), (iv)]
- The issuer AML/CFT Guideline uses a HK$8,000 threshold for customer due diligence on occasional transactions and for the fuller data set on stablecoin transfers. Unless a licensee shows the HKMA that its risk controls are effective, each holder's identity should be verified (by the licensee, a supervised institution or a reliable third party). [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 4.4, 6.5-6.6, 5.11]
- A pre-existing issuer wishing to be licensed had to apply by 31 October 2025; one that did was not barred from carrying on its activity until 31 January 2026, unless it had entered a closing-down period. [S:6c3f349e4f90, Explanatory Note on Transitional Provisions, paras 3.3, 4.1]
- The HKMA received 36 first-batch applications and licensed Anchorpoint Financial Limited (FRS01) and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (FRS02), effective 10 April 2026. [S:193316443831, paras 1, 3]; [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- In June 2026 the Government said that, if more licences are granted, the overall number will remain very limited; it expected the first regulated stablecoin launch as early as mid-2026. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (1)-(2)]
- Complaints: the issuer should acknowledge within 7 calendar days and give a full or interim response within 30 calendar days (a full response generally within 60). The HKMA may take complaints the issuer has not properly addressed, but generally does not order compensation. [S:240be6833157, para 8.4.3]; [S:d3e75db3ddb3, Lodging Complaint with the HKMA section]
- The current Stablecoin Review Tribunal panel holds a three-year term running to 31 October 2028; a person aggrieved may refer a decision in writing within 30 days after the HKMA makes it, or within a period the Tribunal permits. [S:16699fe49364, paras 2-3]
- On 28 April 2026 the HKMA warned that tokens using the tickers HKDAP or HSBC were not issued by, or associated with, the licensed issuers, and that both licensees had confirmed they had not yet issued any regulated stablecoins. [S:275e7319a841, paras 2-3]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] As an offeror, offer only licensed-issuer coins to retail; offer other specified stablecoins only to professional investors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 4]
- [ ] For Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice and portfolio management, follow the RI standards and the Appendix A1 and A2 terms; the HKMA expects this of all AIs. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- [ ] Disclose material information on each Relevant Stablecoin's stabilisation and redemption arrangements when dealing or advising. [S:a0bd48559762, para (5), p.5 and para (10), p.7]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] To use the Relevant-Stablecoin-only relief, clearly demarcate those client accounts and deal no other VAs for those clients; dealing other VAs for a client brings back the full VA requirements, including the knowledge assessment. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4]
- [ ] Keep advice grounded in the client's use case, not commission rebates. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6]
- [ ] Classify stablecoin holdings under CRP-1 (Group 1b only if classification conditions 1-4 are met; full redeemability means redemption within five calendar days). [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1-1.3.2, 2.3.2]
- [ ] For liquidity (category 1 institutions), treat held stablecoins as non-HQLA (high-quality liquid assets) debt securities: generally a 0% inflow under the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) and an 85% required stable funding factor under the net stable funding ratio (NSFR). An issuing bank must apply a 100% LCR outflow if the coin is redeemable within the LCR period, with lower rates possible where the backing assets are HQLA-eligible. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] RIs: notify the HKMA and SFC before any Relevant Stablecoin activity or material change. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- [ ] All AIs: discuss Relevant Stablecoin services with the HKMA first. [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] Notify the HKMA on acquiring a new type of cryptoasset, and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Check a claimed issuer against the HKMA Register of licensees; the HKMA points the public to the Register to verify licensees. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]
- [ ] An AI may delegate custody of stablecoins to a licensed issuer only for that issuer's own coins, and only if the issuer has the HKMA's consent to provide that custody. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14 and fn 7]

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- Banks are permitted offerors; only licensed-issuer coins may be offered to retail investors. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]; [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]
- RIs notify the HKMA and SFC before Relevant Stablecoin activity; other AIs discuss with the HKMA first. [S:a0bd48559762, p.9]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.2]

**To business heads**
- Two issuers are licensed (effective 10 April 2026); the HKMA says any further licences would keep the overall number very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:193316443831, Follow-up work section, para 3]
- Relevant-Stablecoin-only dealing is not limited to RIs registered for Type 1, and needs no VA knowledge assessment for clearly demarcated Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts; Appendix A1 conditions still apply. [S:a0bd48559762, paras (2), (3)(a)]

**To the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- An AI can apply to issue, and the financial-resources and business-activities minimum criteria do not apply to an AI applicant. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.2.1; fns 1, 8]
- In June 2026 the Government expected the first regulated stablecoin launch as early as mid-2026. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (1)]

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- A held stablecoin is treated as a non-HQLA debt security for liquidity; an issuing bank must apply a 100% LCR outflow if the coin is redeemable within the LCR period (lower rates possible with HQLA-eligible backing). [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, para 7]
- HKMA-licensed coins can be deemed to meet CRP-1's extra permissionless-chain measures, but Group 1 still needs conditions 1-4. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1, 2.6.3]

## Common mix-ups

- "A Relevant Stablecoin is an investment product." The HKMA letter says its guidance should not be read that way. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- "Sandbox firms are licensed." Sandbox admission was not a licence; the HKMA later said it is neither a precondition nor a guarantee. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, para 2]; [S:e0b249a7eb8c, Licensing regime section, para 5]
- "A licence means coins are live." Both licensees confirmed on 28 April 2026 they had not yet issued. [S:275e7319a841, para 3]
- "The SFC's 26EC26 governs our bank." It covers VATPs and LCs; for RIs it points to the HKMA RI circular. [S:19d52ade6849, p.1, opening paragraph and fn 2]; [S:a0bd48559762, p.1]
- "Only Hong Kong firms are caught." A business that issues Hong Kong dollar-referenced coins needs a licence wherever the issue takes place. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]
- "The HKMA will get my client compensated." It generally does not order issuers to pay compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, Lodging Complaint with the HKMA section]

## Open items

- No document reviewed for this guide has a official confirmation that either licensee had launched a regulated stablecoin by 25 Sep 2026.
- The HKMA has told applicants it has no definitive inclination on the direction and timing of further licensing. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (2)]
- On 3 June 2026 the HKMA clarified (loosened) CRP-1 para 2.6.4 for tokenised traditional assets, not stablecoins, effective immediately, and said the CRP-1 wording will be updated in due course; No document reviewed for this guide has a updated CRP-1 text. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- The 2026 Policy Address says the SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed VA trading platforms and their use in settling tokenised money market funds; no detailed measures are in the documents reviewed for this guide. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- Statutory text of Cap. 656 on e-Legislation is not in the documents reviewed for this guide; section numbers here come from HKMA documents.
