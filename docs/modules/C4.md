# C4 Stablecoins

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After this module you can explain Hong Kong's stablecoin law and what a licensed issuer must do. You can also explain each role a bank can play:

- issuer of its own coins;
- offeror (a firm that sells or offers coins to the public);
- dealer or adviser;
- custodian (a firm that keeps coins safe for clients);
- holder of coins on its own balance sheet.

## In 60 seconds

A stablecoin is a digital token designed to keep a steady value against a currency or other asset. The Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656) took effect on 1 August 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1] It covers the fiat-referenced stablecoin (FRS), meaning a stablecoin tied to one or more official currencies. A business needs a licence from the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) to issue an FRS in Hong Kong. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] It also needs one to issue a Hong Kong dollar FRS, whether in or outside Hong Kong. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]

Licensed issuers must back each type of coin in full with reserves kept apart from their own assets. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1, 3.3.3, 6.5.7] They must not pay interest. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1] The HKMA expects them to pay out valid redemptions at face value (par), normally within one business day. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1, 3.3.3, 6.5.7] Its expectations also cover strict control of private keys, the secret codes that move the coins. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1, 3.3.3, 6.5.7]

Only permitted offerors may offer these coins. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1] Authorized institutions (AIs), the HKMA's term for banks and other deposit-takers, are permitted offerors. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1] Retail investors may only be offered coins from licensed issuers. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]

The first two licences took effect on 10 April 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table] The HKMA says that if it grants more, the total will remain very limited. [S:193316443831, Follow-up work section, para 3]

On 27 May 2026 the HKMA and the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) eased some conduct rules for these coins. [S:a0bd48559762, pp.1-2, 9]; [S:19d52ade6849, p.1] The circulars call them Relevant Stablecoins. The eased rules are those for virtual asset (VA) services, such as dealing and advice. The HKMA circular covers registered institutions (RIs), meaning banks registered with the SFC for securities business. [S:a0bd48559762, pp.1-2, 9] Both regulators kept the duty to notify them in advance. [S:a0bd48559762, pp.1-2, 9]; [S:19d52ade6849, p.1]

For coins a bank holds itself, the bank sorts them into a risk group under CRP-1, the HKMA's capital module on cryptoassets. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1-1.3.2] It treats them under LM-1, the HKMA's liquidity module (rules on managing liquidity risk). [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7]

## Key facts

- History: the HKMA issued a discussion paper in January 2022. Its conclusions, in January 2023, reported 58 responses. [S:c72a4b732293, para 1.4] The FSTB and HKMA then consulted the public from 27 December 2023 to 29 February 2024. [S:167649d1bc28, para 3] They published consultation conclusions on 17 July 2024, after 108 responses. [S:54aa01e7396e, paras 1-2]
- The Government published the Bill in the Gazette on 6 December 2024. [S:a9a82f2b38ce, para 1] LegCo passed it on 21 May 2025. [S:35e1ac2b815a, para 1] The Ordinance took effect on 1 August 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- A licence is required to issue an FRS in Hong Kong as a business. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] A licence is also needed to issue a Hong Kong dollar FRS, in or outside Hong Kong. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]
- For now, only FRS count as specified stablecoins (the coins the law covers). Bank deposits are outside the definition. So is stored value facility (SVF) float, the money customers hold in e-wallets. Securities are outside it too. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 2.1.2, 2.2.1-2.2.2]
- Permitted offerors are licensed issuers, AIs and SVF licensees. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1] Virtual asset trading platforms licensed by the SFC also qualify. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1] So do corporations licensed by the SFC for Type 1 regulated activity (dealing in securities). [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]
- Permitted offerors may offer licensed issuers' coins to anyone. They may offer other specified stablecoins only to professional investors (investors who meet legal wealth or institution tests). Over-the-counter virtual asset shops are not permitted offerors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), paras 1-2]
- Only advertisements for licensed FRS issuance may be published. This rule also applied during the six-month grace period for existing issuers. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 3]
- An AI incorporated outside Hong Kong may apply without setting up a local subsidiary. The criteria on financial resources and business activity do not apply to AI applicants. No one needs HKMA consent to become an AI applicant's controller, chief executive or director. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 6, 8]
- A licensed issuer must keep paid-up capital (money shareholders have paid in) of at least HK$25 million. An AI that holds a licence is exempt. The Banking Ordinance sets its capital instead. [S:240be6833157, paras 5.1.1, 5.1.3]
- Reserves must be worth at least the face value of all coins in circulation, at all times. Reserves must be kept segregated, meaning separate from the issuer's own assets. The HKMA expects an effective trust arrangement and a qualified custodian. Reserve deposits should have a term of three months or less. Debt securities should have one year or less left to run. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.3.1, 2.5.1-2.5.4]
- Issuers must honour valid redemptions at face value. They cannot charge unreasonable fees or set unduly burdensome conditions. They should process each redemption within one business day, unless the HKMA approves otherwise. [S:240be6833157, paras 3.3.3, 3.3.5]
- Licensees may not pay interest or any yield linked to holding a coin. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1]
- Private-key controls should cover every stage of a key's life. Significant keys should be created offline, on equipment never linked to a network (air-gapped). Keys should be stored in Hong Kong or another place the HKMA accepts. [S:240be6833157, para 6.5.7 and items (ii), (iv)]
- The issuer AML/CFT Guideline sets a HK$8,000 threshold for two things. One is customer due diligence (identity and risk checks) on one-off transactions. The other is the fuller data set sent with stablecoin transfers. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 4.4, 6.5-6.6, 5.11]
- Each holder's identity should be verified. The licensee, a supervised institution or a reliable third party can do this. The exception is where a licensee shows the HKMA that its risk controls are effective. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 4.4, 6.5-6.6, 5.11]
- An issuer already operating before the law had to apply by 31 October 2025 if it wanted a licence. If it applied, it could keep operating until 31 January 2026. The exception was an issuer that had entered a closing-down period. [S:6c3f349e4f90, Explanatory Note on Transitional Provisions, paras 3.3, 4.1]
- The HKMA received 36 applications in the first batch. [S:193316443831, paras 1, 3] It licensed two issuers, effective 10 April 2026 (register numbers in brackets): Anchorpoint Financial Limited (FRS01) and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (FRS02). [S:193316443831, paras 1, 3]; [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- In June 2026 the Government said that if more licences are granted, the total will remain very limited. It expected the first regulated stablecoin launch as early as mid-2026. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (1)-(2)]
- Complaints: the issuer should acknowledge a complaint within 7 calendar days. It should give a full or interim response within 30 calendar days. It should generally give a full response within 60 calendar days. [S:240be6833157, para 8.4.3] The HKMA may take up complaints the issuer has not handled properly. It generally does not order compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, Lodging Complaint with the HKMA section]
- The current Stablecoin Review Tribunal panel serves a three-year term ending 31 October 2028. [S:16699fe49364, paras 2-3] A person aggrieved by (adversely affected by) an HKMA decision may refer it to the Tribunal in writing. The deadline is 30 days after the HKMA makes the decision, or another period the Tribunal permits. [S:16699fe49364, paras 2-3]
- On 28 April 2026 the HKMA warned about tokens using the tickers (trading symbols) HKDAP or HSBC. The HKMA said the licensed issuers did not issue these tokens and had no link to them. Both licensees had confirmed they had not yet issued any regulated stablecoins. [S:275e7319a841, paras 2-3]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Offer only licensed issuers' coins to retail clients when acting as an offeror. Offer other specified stablecoins only to professional investors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 4]
- [ ] Follow the RI standards when dealing, advising or managing portfolios in Relevant Stablecoins. Follow the terms in Appendices A1 and A2 too. The HKMA expects this of all AIs. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- [ ] Disclose material information on each Relevant Stablecoin when dealing or advising. Cover its stabilisation arrangements (how it keeps its value) and its redemption arrangements (how holders get money back). [S:a0bd48559762, para (5), p.5 and para (10), p.7]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Mark clearly the accounts of clients who trade only Relevant Stablecoins, to use the lighter rules for them. Deal in no other virtual assets for those clients. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4] Dealing in other virtual assets for a client brings back the full virtual asset rules. These include the knowledge assessment, a check of the client's understanding of virtual assets. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4]
- [ ] Base advice on the client's use case (what the client needs the coin for), not on commission rebates. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6]
- [ ] Classify stablecoin holdings under CRP-1. A coin is Group 1b (a lower-risk stablecoin group) only if it meets classification conditions 1-4. Here, full redeemability means redemption within five calendar days. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1-1.3.2, 2.3.2]
- [ ] Treat held stablecoins as debt securities outside high-quality liquid assets (HQLA), if your bank is a category 1 institution for liquidity. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7] Under the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), a short-term cash stress test, the usual inflow is 0%. The bank counts no cash coming in from them. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7] Under the net stable funding ratio (NSFR), a longer-term funding test, the required stable funding factor is 85%. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7] An issuing bank must apply a 100% LCR outflow if the coin is redeemable within the LCR period. In effect, it assumes holders cash in every such coin. Lower rates are possible where the backing assets qualify as HQLA. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, paras 6-7]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] RIs: notify the HKMA and SFC before any Relevant Stablecoin activity or material change. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- [ ] All AIs: discuss Relevant Stablecoin services with the HKMA first. [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] Notify the HKMA when you acquire a new type of cryptoasset. Treat it as Group 2b (the highest-risk group) until the HKMA agrees its classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Check any claimed issuer against the HKMA Register of licensed issuers. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table] The HKMA points the public to the Register to verify licensees. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]
- [ ] Hand custody (safekeeping) of stablecoins to a licensed issuer only for that issuer's own coins. An AI may do so only if the issuer has HKMA consent to provide that custody. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14 and fn 7]

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- Banks are permitted offerors. Only licensed issuers' coins may be offered to retail investors. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]; [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]
- RIs notify the HKMA and SFC before Relevant Stablecoin activity. Other AIs discuss it with the HKMA first. [S:a0bd48559762, p.9]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.2]

**To business heads**
- Two issuers hold licences, effective 10 April 2026. The HKMA says any further licences would keep the total very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:193316443831, Follow-up work section, para 3]
- Relevant-Stablecoin-only dealing is not limited to RIs registered for Type 1 (dealing in securities). Clearly marked Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts need no virtual asset knowledge test. The Appendix A1 conditions still apply. [S:a0bd48559762, paras (2), (3)(a)]

**To the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- A bank can apply to issue stablecoins. The minimum criteria on financial resources and business activities do not apply to AI applicants. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.2.1; fns 1, 8]
- In June 2026 the Government expected the first regulated stablecoin launch as early as mid-2026. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (1)]

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- For liquidity, a held stablecoin counts as a debt security that is not HQLA. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, para 7] An issuing bank must apply a 100% LCR outflow if the coin is redeemable within the LCR period. Lower rates are possible with HQLA-eligible backing. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, para 7]
- HKMA-licensed coins can be treated as meeting CRP-1's extra measures for permissionless blockchains (public networks anyone can join). They still need conditions 1-4 to reach Group 1. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1, 2.6.3]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** "A Relevant Stablecoin is an investment product." **Fact:** The HKMA letter says its guidance should not be read that way. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- **Myth:** "Sandbox firms are licensed." **Fact:** Joining the sandbox was not a licence. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, para 2] The HKMA later said the sandbox is neither a precondition nor a guarantee of a licence. [S:e0b249a7eb8c, Licensing regime section, para 5]
- **Myth:** "A licence means coins are live." **Fact:** On 28 April 2026 both licensees confirmed they had not yet issued any coins. [S:275e7319a841, para 3]
- **Myth:** "The SFC's circular 26EC26 governs our bank." **Fact:** It covers VATPs and LCs. [S:19d52ade6849, p.1, opening paragraph and fn 2] For RIs, it points to the HKMA RI circular. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1]
- **Myth:** "Only Hong Kong firms are caught." **Fact:** A business that issues Hong Kong dollar coins needs a licence wherever it issues them. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]
- **Myth:** "The HKMA will get my client compensated." **Fact:** The HKMA generally does not order issuers to pay compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, Lodging Complaint with the HKMA section]

## Open items

- No document reviewed for this guide confirms that either licensee had launched a regulated stablecoin by 25 September 2026.
- The HKMA has told applicants it has no settled view on the direction and timing of further licensing. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (2)]
- On 3 June 2026 the HKMA loosened CRP-1 para 2.6.4 for tokenised traditional assets, not stablecoins. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1] Tokenised traditional assets are ordinary assets, such as bonds, recorded as digital tokens. The change took effect at once. The HKMA said it will update the CRP-1 wording in due course. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1] No document reviewed for this guide has the updated CRP-1 text.
- The 2026 Policy Address says the SFC will promote the trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed trading platforms for virtual assets. It will also promote their use to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)] The documents reviewed for this guide contain no detailed measures.
- This guide has not reviewed the text of Cap. 656 on e-Legislation, the official online database of Hong Kong laws. Section numbers here come from HKMA documents.
