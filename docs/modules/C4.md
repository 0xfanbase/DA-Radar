# C4 Stablecoins

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain Hong Kong's stablecoin law, what a licensed issuer must do, and every role a bank can play: issuer, offeror, dealer or adviser, custodian, or holder on its own balance sheet.

## In 60 seconds

The Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656) took effect on 1 August 2025. It requires a Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) licence to issue a fiat-referenced stablecoin (FRS) in Hong Kong, or one pegged to the Hong Kong dollar anywhere. Licensed issuers must fully back coins with segregated reserves, redeem at par (normally within one business day), pay no interest and keep keys under tight control. Only listed permitted offerors, including banks, may offer these coins, and retail investors may only get coins from licensed issuers. The HKMA granted the first two licences on 10 April 2026 and says the total will stay very limited. In May 2026 the HKMA and the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) eased how VA conduct rules apply to these Relevant Stablecoins, while keeping advance-notice duties. For its own holdings, a bank classifies stablecoins under the capital rules (CRP-1) and treats them under the liquidity rules (LM-1).

## Key facts

- History: HKMA discussion paper (January 2022; its January 2023 conclusions reported 58 submissions); FSTB-HKMA consultation (27 Dec 2023 to 29 Feb 2024); consultation conclusions (17 Jul 2024, 108 submissions). [S:c72a4b732293, para 1.4]; [S:167649d1bc28, para 3]; [S:54aa01e7396e, para 2, p.3]
- The Bill was gazetted on 6 December 2024, passed on 21 May 2025, and the Ordinance commenced on 1 August 2025. [S:a9a82f2b38ce, para 1]; [S:35e1ac2b815a, para 1]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- A licence is required to issue an FRS in Hong Kong, or an FRS referenced to the Hong Kong dollar anywhere. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]
- Only FRS count as specified stablecoins for now; bank deposits, stored value facility (SVF) float and securities are outside the definition. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 2.1.2, 2.2.1-2.2.2]
- Permitted offerors are: a licensee, an Authorized Institution (AI), an SVF licensee, an SFC-licensed VA trading platform (VATP), or an SFC Type 1 licensed corporation (LC). [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]
- Permitted offerors may offer licensed-issuer coins to anyone; other specified stablecoins only to professional investors; VA over-the-counter shops are not permitted offerors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), paras 2-3]
- Only advertisements for licensed issuance are allowed, including during the six-month transition. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 3]
- An overseas-incorporated AI may apply without a local subsidiary. The financial-resources and business-activity criteria do not apply to AI applicants. The HKMA's consent is not needed for an AI applicant's controllers or directors. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 6, 8]
- A licensee must keep paid-up capital of at least HK$25 million; an AI licensee is exempt and follows the Banking Ordinance instead. [S:240be6833157, paras 5.1.1, 5.1.3]
- Reserves must be worth at least the par value of coins in circulation at all times, and must be held on trust with a qualified custodian. Deposits in reserves are capped at three months' tenor, debt securities at one year. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.3.1, 2.5.1-2.5.4]
- Valid redemptions are honoured at par without unreasonable fees; generally within one business day unless the HKMA approves otherwise. [S:240be6833157, para 3.3.3]
- Licensees may not pay interest or yield tied to holding a coin. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1]
- Key management covers the whole key lifecycle. Significant keys are generated offline in air-gapped settings. Keys are stored in Hong Kong or another HKMA-accepted location. [S:240be6833157, para 6.5.7(ii), (iv)]
- The issuer AML/CFT Guideline sets a HK$8,000 threshold for customer due diligence and the travel rule. By default, every holder's identity should be verified unless the licensee proves its controls work. [S:4d98c6a80b4d, paras 4.4, 6.5-6.6, 5.11]
- Pre-existing issuers had to apply by 31 October 2025 to keep operating until 31 January 2026. [S:6c3f349e4f90, Explanatory Note on Transitional Provisions, paras 3.3, 4.1]
- The HKMA received 36 first-batch applications and licensed Anchorpoint Financial Limited (FRS01) and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (FRS02), effective 10 April 2026. [S:193316443831, paras 1, 3]; [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- The government says further licences will be very limited and expected launch as early as mid-2026. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (1)-(2)]
- Complaints: the issuer acknowledges within 7 calendar days and responds within 30 (generally no more than 60). The HKMA reviews escalations but does not order compensation. [S:240be6833157, para 8.4.3]; [S:d3e75db3ddb3, Lodging Complaint with the HKMA section]
- The current Stablecoin Review Tribunal panel holds a three-year term running to 31 October 2028; a review must be sought within 30 days of the HKMA decision. [S:16699fe49364, paras 2-3]
- On 28 April 2026 the HKMA warned that tokens using the tickers HKDAP and HSBC were not issued by the licensees, and that neither licensee had yet issued. [S:275e7319a841, paras 2-3]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] As an offeror, offer only licensed-issuer coins to retail; offer other specified stablecoins only to professional investors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 2]
- [ ] Apply the RI standards (Appendices A1 and A2) to Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice and portfolio management. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- [ ] Disclose each Relevant Stablecoin's stabilisation and redemption arrangements when dealing. [S:a0bd48559762, para (5), p.5]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Ring-fence Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts; mixing in other VAs brings back full VA rules. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4]
- [ ] Keep advice grounded in the client's use case, not commission rebates. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6]
- [ ] Classify stablecoin holdings under CRP-1 (Group 1b only if all conditions are met; redemption within five calendar days). [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.3.1, 2.3.2]
- [ ] For liquidity (category 1 institutions), treat held stablecoins as non-HQLA debt (generally 0% LCR inflow, 85% NSFR factor); an issuing bank generally applies a 100% outflow. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, para 7]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] RIs: notify the HKMA and SFC before any Relevant Stablecoin activity or material change. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- [ ] All AIs: discuss Relevant Stablecoin services with the HKMA first. [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] Notify the HKMA before first acquiring a new stablecoin type. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Check an issuer on the HKMA register before dealing. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 5]
- [ ] Custody delegation of stablecoins to a licensed issuer needs that issuer to hold HKMA consent for its own coins. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14 and fn 7]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Banks are permitted offerors; retail may only get licensed-issuer coins. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]; [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 2]
- RIs notify the HKMA and SFC before Relevant Stablecoin activity; other AIs discuss with the HKMA first. [S:a0bd48559762, p.9]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.2]

**To business heads**
- Two issuers are licensed (effective 10 April 2026); the HKMA says numbers will stay very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:193316443831, final paragraphs]
- Relevant-Stablecoin-only dealing needs no Type 1 registration and no VA knowledge test for ring-fenced accounts. [S:a0bd48559762, paras (2), (3)(a)]

**To the CEO**
- An AI can apply to issue and is exempt from some criteria, such as capital and business-activity limits. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, fns 1, 8]
- Launch of the first licensed coins was expected from mid-2026. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (1)]

**To Risk / CRO**
- A held stablecoin is non-HQLA debt for liquidity; an issuing bank faces a 100% LCR outflow. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3, para 7]
- HKMA-licensed coins are deemed to meet CRP-1's permissionless-chain measures, but still need conditions 1-4. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.6.3]

## Common mix-ups

- "A Relevant Stablecoin is an investment product." The HKMA letter says its guidance should not be read that way. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]
- "Sandbox firms are licensed." Sandbox admission was not a licence; the HKMA later said it is neither a precondition nor a guarantee. [S:28616d929735, para 9]; [S:e0b249a7eb8c, Licensing regime section, para 5]
- "A licence means coins are live." Both licensees confirmed on 28 April 2026 they had not yet issued. [S:275e7319a841, para 3]
- "The SFC's 26EC26 governs our bank." It covers VATPs and LCs; RIs follow the HKMA RI circular. [S:19d52ade6849, para 2]; [S:a0bd48559762, p.1]
- "Only Hong Kong firms are caught." HKD-referenced coins issued anywhere need a licence. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]
- "The HKMA will get my client compensated." It does not order issuers to pay compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, Lodging Complaint with the HKMA section]
