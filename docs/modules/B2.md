# B2 Engaging the HKMA and SFC

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): the HKMA (Hong Kong Monetary Authority) and the SFC (Securities and Futures Commission) are your two regulators. After this module you can list the main duties to talk to a regulator before acting on digital-asset plans. You can also explain the HKMA's engagement channels: the Supervisory Incubator, the 2026 business-model review and the superseded (replaced) stablecoin issuer sandbox.

## In 60 seconds

In HKMA rules, banks are called authorized institutions (AIs). Virtual assets (VAs) are crypto tokens and similar digital assets. Since January 2022, the HKMA has expected any bank planning a virtual-asset (VA) activity to discuss it with the HKMA first. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5] The bank should get the HKMA's feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5]

Later HKMA circulars (formal letters to banks) repeat this discuss-first step for:
- tokenised products (assets recorded as digital tokens on a blockchain) [S:b2719f3dcb37, Implementation, p.6]
- custody (holding digital assets for clients) [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- staking (locking up tokens to help run a blockchain, in return for rewards) [S:4641e6c404d2, p.3, Implementation]
- services in Relevant Stablecoins (stablecoins issued under an HKMA licence) [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2]

Some banks are also registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) for securities business. These banks are called registered institutions (RIs). A bank acting as a registered institution (RI) should tell both regulators before it starts or changes VA dealing, advice or asset management. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]

The capital rules (rules on how much capital a bank holds against its risks) add a separate trigger. A bank must notify the HKMA when it acquires a new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]

For distributed ledger technology (DLT) projects, the HKMA runs a Supervisory Incubator. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] DLT means shared digital records, such as blockchains. The Incubator offers a dedicated HKMA team and optional live trials. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

In March 2026 the HKMA said it expects every bank's board to endorse a strategic business plan on digital change within six months. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]

## Key facts

- A bank planning any VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA, and with other regulators where relevant, before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5] The bank should get feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5]
- Before selling or issuing tokenised products, a bank should have policies and controls in place and discuss its plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Implementation, p.6]
- A bank, or a local bank's subsidiary, that intends to offer digital-asset custody should first discuss it with the HKMA. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2] It should also satisfy the HKMA that it meets the custody standards in the guidance's Annex. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- The February 2024 custody circular asked existing custodians to confirm compliance to the HKMA within six months. [S:6bccca749ac2, Implementation, p.2] The May 2026 letter supersedes (replaces) that circular. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2] The new letter simply asks existing custodians to review and revise their controls. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- Before offering staking from custody, a bank should have adequate policies and controls and discuss its plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:4641e6c404d2, p.3, Implementation]
- Intermediaries (firms licensed or registered with the SFC, including RIs) should give the SFC advance notice before starting or changing VA activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31] Where relevant, they should also give the HKMA advance notice. [S:8a016a912528, para 31] A change in client type counts as a change. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- The September 2025 supplementary joint circular lists changes that need prior notice: [S:b79266f3da51, para 10]
  - a change in client type
  - first VA deposits or withdrawals
  - first staking service
  - first subscriptions or redemptions (buying into or cashing out of a product) paid in VAs
  - other material changes
- SB-1 is the Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) chapter on supervising RIs. It expects an RI to tell the SFC and the HKMA when it plans to start or change VA activities. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- An RI needs at least two executive officers (senior staff who directly supervise the business) for each regulated activity. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.4, 4.3.9-4.3.10] Each executive officer needs written consent from the Monetary Authority (MA), applied for through the HKMA. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.4, 4.3.9-4.3.10]
- RIs should give the HKMA and SFC advance notice before they do any of these: [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
  - distribute products linked to Relevant Stablecoins
  - deal in, advise on or manage Relevant Stablecoins
  - materially change those arrangements
- All AIs, not only RIs, should discuss Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management with the HKMA before starting. [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2]
- CRP-1 is the SPM chapter on classifying cryptoassets. Under CRP-1, a bank that acquires a new type of cryptoasset must notify the HKMA. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] It must treat the asset as Group 2b (the group with the strictest capital treatment) until the HKMA agrees to the bank's own classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- A locally incorporated AI (one set up in Hong Kong) needs the MA's written consent for some share purchases. [S:13365a31e794, para 1.1 and fn 1] Consent is needed when the shares bought are worth at least 5% of the bank's own Tier 1 capital (its core capital). [S:13365a31e794, para 1.1 and fn 1] Equity-like cryptoassets (tokens that act like shares) count as shares for this test. [S:13365a31e794, para 1.1 and fn 1]
- The HKMA launched the Supervisory Incubator on 8 January 2025. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3] The Incubator gives banks one supervisory contact point to confirm DLT risk controls before a full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3] Live trials are optional. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3] Tokenised deposits (bank deposits recorded as digital tokens) were an initial focus. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]
- Incubator participants may get some supervisory flexibility, decided case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and fn 2]
- The HKMA expects each bank's board to oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan for these technology changes. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4] The deadline is six months from 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- The same March 2026 circular repeats that banks should discuss new digital-asset activities with the HKMA before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]
- The stablecoin issuer sandbox (March 2024) let would-be issuers test their plans on a limited scale. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, paras 1-2] Admission did not mean HKMA endorsement or regulation. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, paras 1-2] A licence would need a separate application. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, paras 1-2]
- The HKMA later said sandbox participation is not a condition for a licence and does not guarantee one. [S:e0b249a7eb8c, Licensing regime section, para 5]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Discuss each new VA product or service with the HKMA before launch, and get its feedback on risk controls. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- [ ] Have the board oversee and endorse the strategic business plan that the March 2026 circular asks for. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- [ ] Get the MA's written consent for each RI executive officer, applying through the HKMA. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Track the changes that trigger prior notice: new client type, first VA deposits or withdrawals, first staking, first VA-funded subscriptions and other material changes. [S:b79266f3da51, para 10]
- [ ] If the bank already offers custody, review and revise its controls against the 2026 Annex. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] If a cryptoasset stops meeting the conditions for its HKMA-agreed group (1a, 1b or 2a), move it to Group 2b at once. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 2.1.3, 2.1.5] Any later move to another group needs the HKMA's agreement. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 2.1.3, 2.1.5]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Discuss tokenised products, custody and staking with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.6]; [S:ed61d7c335e7, p.2]; [S:4641e6c404d2, p.3]
- [ ] Notify the SFC and the HKMA in advance of RI VA activities and of changes to them. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]; [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- [ ] Relevant Stablecoin activities: if the bank is an RI, notify the HKMA and SFC. [S:a0bd48559762, p.9] Every bank: discuss them with the HKMA. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.2]
- [ ] Notify the HKMA when the bank acquires a new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Seek consent before taking a stake in equity-like tokens worth 5% or more of Tier 1 capital. [S:13365a31e794, paras 1.1, 2.9.1] Give the MA at least one month's notice of other significant acquisitions. [S:13365a31e794, paras 1.1, 2.9.1]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Do not treat a firm's sandbox status as a licence. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, para 2] Check the HKMA's Register of Licensed Stablecoin Issuers instead. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Every new VA, tokenisation, custody, staking or stablecoin launch starts with an HKMA discussion. [S:642680fa4467, p.5] For RI activities, the SFC and HKMA also expect advance notice. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- The 2026 custody letter dropped the six-month confirmation. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2] Existing custodians should review and revise their controls instead. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]

**To business heads**
- We talk to the HKMA before go-live on any new digital-asset activity. The March 2026 circular restates that step. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]
- We should flag a new client type to the SFC in advance, and to the HKMA where relevant. [S:b79266f3da51, para 10] The same goes for our first VA deposits, staking or VA-funded subscriptions. [S:b79266f3da51, para 10]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA asked each board to endorse a digital-transformation business plan within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- The Supervisory Incubator gives us a dedicated HKMA team for feedback before a full DLT launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Live trials there are optional. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

**To Risk / CRO**
- We must notify the HKMA when we acquire a new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] The asset sits in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees with our classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- Equity-like tokens count as shares for the 5%-of-Tier-1 consent test. [S:13365a31e794, fn 1]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** Telling the SFC is enough. **Fact:** SB-1 expects RIs to tell both regulators. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6] The HKMA's own circulars add a discuss-first step. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- **Myth:** The advance-notice rule in SFC circular 26EC26, on admitting Relevant Stablecoins, binds us. **Fact:** That circular is addressed to VATPs and LCs. [S:19d52ade6849, para 12] For RIs, the HKMA's RI circular sets the advance-notice duty. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- **Myth:** Every crypto trade needs a CRP-1 notice. **Fact:** Only a new type of cryptoasset does. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2 and fn 7] A first Bitcoin position triggers a notice. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2 and fn 7] Bitcoin futures after existing spot (direct) holdings do not. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2 and fn 7]
- **Myth:** The six-month custody confirmation still applies. **Fact:** It belonged to the 2024 circular. [S:6bccca749ac2, Implementation, p.2] That circular has been superseded. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- **Myth:** Incubator or sandbox entry means approval. **Fact:** Incubator flexibility is case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] Sandbox admission was not an endorsement. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, para 2]

## Open items

- The documents reviewed for this guide contain no HKMA form, template or response timeline for the discuss-first step.
- The six-month window in the March 2026 business-model circular ran to about 9 September 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4] No document reviewed for this guide shows any HKMA follow-up on these plans.
- No document reviewed for this guide has a public list of Supervisory Incubator participants.
- The December 2025 consultation conclusions on VA custodian services cover banks that safekeep private keys (the secret codes that control crypto) for client VAs. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 35(b), 65] They also cover subsidiaries of locally incorporated banks that do so. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 35(b), 65] These firms would need a licence or registration under the proposed regime. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 35(b), 65] The stated aim is to introduce a bill into the Legislative Council in 2026. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 35(b), 65] No bill text is in the documents reviewed for this guide (see A1).
