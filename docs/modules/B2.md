# B2 Engaging the HKMA and SFC

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can list the main talk-to-the-regulator-first duties that apply to a bank's digital-asset plans, and explain the HKMA's engagement channels: the Supervisory Incubator, the 2026 business-model review and the earlier (now superseded) stablecoin issuer sandbox.

## In 60 seconds

Since January 2022 the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) has expected any bank that plans a virtual-asset (VA) activity to discuss it with the HKMA and get feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5] Later circulars repeat that step for tokenised products, custody, staking and services in HKMA-licensed (Relevant) stablecoins. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Implementation, p.6; S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2; S:4641e6c404d2, p.3, Implementation; S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2] When the bank acts as a Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) Registered Institution (RI), it should also tell both regulators ahead of time before starting or changing VA dealing, advice or asset management. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6] The capital rules add a separate trigger: a bank must notify the HKMA when it acquires a new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] For distributed ledger technology (DLT) projects, the HKMA runs a Supervisory Incubator with a dedicated team and optional live trials. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] In March 2026 the HKMA said it expects every bank's board to endorse a strategic business plan on digital change within six months. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]

## Key facts

- A bank planning any VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA (and other regulators where relevant) and obtain feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5]
- Before selling or issuing tokenised products, a bank should have policies and controls in place and discuss its plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Implementation, p.6]
- A bank, or a local bank's subsidiary, that intends to offer digital-asset custody should discuss it with the HKMA first and show, to the HKMA's satisfaction, that it meets the Annex standards. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- The February 2024 circular asked existing custodians to confirm compliance to the HKMA within six months; the May 2026 letter supersedes it and simply asks existing custodians to review and revise their controls. [S:6bccca749ac2, Implementation, p.2]; [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- Before launching staking from custody, a bank should have adequate policies and controls and discuss its plans with the HKMA in advance. [S:4641e6c404d2, p.3, Implementation]
- Intermediaries, including RIs, should give the SFC, and where relevant the HKMA, advance notice before starting VA activities or changing them, including a change in client type. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- The September 2025 supplement lists changes needing prior notice: client type; first VA deposits or withdrawals; first staking service; first subscriptions or redemptions paid in VAs; other material changes. [S:b79266f3da51, para 10]
- SB-1 expects an RI to tell the SFC and the HKMA when it plans to start, or change, VA activities. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- An RI needs at least two executive officers per regulated activity, and each executive officer needs the Monetary Authority's (MA's) written consent, applied for through the HKMA. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.2.4, 4.3.9-4.3.10]
- RIs should give the HKMA and SFC advance notice before distributing Relevant Stablecoin-related products, dealing in, advising on or managing Relevant Stablecoins, or materially changing those arrangements. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- All AIs (not only RIs) should discuss Relevant Stablecoin dealing, advice or portfolio management with the HKMA before starting. [S:1babdb609fc8, Implementation, p.2]
- Under CRP-1, a bank that acquires a new type of cryptoasset must notify the HKMA and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees to the bank's own classification assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- A locally incorporated AI needs the Monetary Authority's written consent to acquire shares worth 5% or more of its Tier 1 capital; equity-like cryptoassets count as shares. [S:13365a31e794, para 1.1 and fn 1]
- The Supervisory Incubator, launched on 8 January 2025, gives banks one supervisory contact point to confirm DLT risk controls before full launch, with optional live trials; tokenised deposits were an initial focus. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]
- Incubator participants may get some supervisory flexibility, decided case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and fn 2]
- The HKMA expects each bank's board to oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan on these technology shifts within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- That circular repeats that new digital-asset activities should be discussed with the HKMA before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]
- The stablecoin issuer sandbox (March 2024) let would-be issuers test in a limited way; admission did not mean HKMA endorsement or regulation, and a licence would need a separate application. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, paras 1-2]
- The HKMA later said sandbox participation is neither a precondition for nor a guarantee of a licence. [S:e0b249a7eb8c, Licensing regime section, para 5]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Discuss each new VA product or service with the HKMA and obtain its feedback on risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- [ ] Have the board oversee and endorse the strategic business plan the March 2026 circular asks for. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- [ ] Get the MA's written consent for each RI executive officer, applying through the HKMA. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Track the changes that trigger prior notice: client type, first VA deposits or withdrawals, first staking, first VA-funded subscriptions, other material changes. [S:b79266f3da51, para 10]
- [ ] If the bank already offers custody, review and revise controls against the 2026 Annex. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] If a cryptoasset with an HKMA-agreed Group 1a, 1b or 2a classification stops meeting the conditions, move it to Group 2b at once; any later move to another group needs HKMA agreement. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 2.1.3, 2.1.5]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Tokenised products, custody, staking: discuss with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, p.6]; [S:ed61d7c335e7, p.2]; [S:4641e6c404d2, p.3]
- [ ] RI VA activities and changes: notify the SFC and the HKMA in advance. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]; [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- [ ] Relevant Stablecoin activities: RIs notify the HKMA and SFC; all AIs discuss with the HKMA. [S:a0bd48559762, p.9]; [S:1babdb609fc8, p.2]
- [ ] New cryptoasset type: notify the HKMA on acquisition. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Equity-like token stakes at or above 5% of Tier 1: seek consent; other significant acquisitions: give at least one month's notice. [S:13365a31e794, paras 1.1, 2.9.1]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Do not treat a firm's sandbox status as a licence; check the HKMA's Register of Licensed Stablecoin Issuers instead. [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, para 2]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Every VA, tokenisation, custody, staking or stablecoin launch starts with an HKMA discussion; for RI activities the SFC and HKMA also expect advance notice. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- The 2026 custody letter dropped the six-month confirmation; existing custodians should review and revise their controls instead. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]

**To business heads**
- The HKMA discussion comes before go-live for any new digital-asset activity, as the March 2026 circular restates. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]
- Changing client type, or first allowing VA deposits, staking or VA-funded subscriptions, should be flagged in advance to the SFC and, where relevant, the HKMA. [S:b79266f3da51, para 10]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA asked each board to endorse a digital-transformation business plan within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- The Supervisory Incubator gives banks a dedicated HKMA team for supervisory feedback, and optional live trials, before a full DLT launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

**To Risk / CRO**
- A new cryptoasset type must be notified to the HKMA on acquisition and sits in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees to the bank's classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- Equity-like tokens count as shares for the 5%-of-Tier-1 consent test. [S:13365a31e794, fn 1]

## Common mix-ups

- "Telling the SFC is enough." SB-1 expects RIs to tell both regulators, and the HKMA's own circulars add a prior-discussion step. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]; [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- "The SFC's 26EC26 notice rule on admitting stablecoins binds us." It is addressed to VATPs and LCs; for RIs the HKMA's RI circular sets the advance-notice duty. [S:19d52ade6849, para 12]; [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- "Every crypto trade needs a CRP-1 notification." Only a new type does: a first Bitcoin position triggers it, but Bitcoin futures after existing spot exposure do not. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2 and fn 7]
- "The six-month custody confirmation still applies." It belonged to the superseded 2024 circular. [S:6bccca749ac2, Implementation, p.2]; [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- "Incubator or sandbox entry means approval." Incubator flexibility is case by case; sandbox admission was not an endorsement. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]; [S:28616d929735, Testing in the sandbox section, para 2]

## Open items

- No HKMA form, template or response timeline for prior discussion appears in the corpus.
- The six-month window in the March 2026 business-model circular ran to about 9 September 2026; the corpus has no HKMA follow-up on submissions. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- The corpus has no public list of Supervisory Incubator participants.
- The December 2025 consultation conclusions on VA custodian services say banks, and subsidiaries of locally incorporated banks, that safekeep private keys for client VAs would need a licence or registration under the proposed regime; the stated aim is to introduce a bill into the Legislative Council (LegCo) in 2026. No bill text is in the corpus (see A1). [S:f41a543388bc, paras 35(b), 65]
