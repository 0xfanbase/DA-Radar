# A1 The Hong Kong digital-asset map

This module is for the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After it, you can name who regulates each digital-asset activity in Hong Kong. You can also say which regimes are live and which are still coming.

## In 60 seconds

Hong Kong splits digital-asset regulation among several bodies. Each body covers particular activities. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises banks, known in law as Authorized Institutions (AIs). [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.1] The HKMA also licenses stablecoin issuers (firms that issue tokens designed to hold a steady value against a currency). [S:270f1f7324ab, Register introduction] Virtual assets (VAs) are digital tokens such as cryptocurrencies. The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licenses virtual-asset (VA) trading platforms (VATPs). [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table] The SFC also sets codes of conduct for regulated securities activities. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.2] The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) sets policy. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 2] The FSTB and the SFC consult the public on new laws. [S:6186c01c6649, cover page] The Legislative Council (LegCo) passes bills. [S:b12bd88c7aee, p.1]

A bank that carries on SFC-regulated activities must register with the SFC as a Registered Institution (RI). [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] Even so, the HKMA is the bank's day-to-day, frontline supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3]

Live today (see Status board):
- the VA trading-platform regime
- the Stablecoins Ordinance
- the joint SFC–HKMA circulars
- HKMA guidance on custody (holding assets for clients) and on tokenisation (recording assets as tokens on a blockchain)
- the Basel cryptoasset capital standard (global rules on how much capital banks hold against cryptoassets)

Coming:
- statutory licensing (licensing set by law) for VA dealing, custody, advisory and management [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 36]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]
- crypto-asset tax reporting under the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section]

## Key facts

- The HKMA does not currently plan to stop banks taking on VA exposures, as long as controls and senior-management oversight are adequate. [S:642680fa4467, Prudential supervision section, p.3]
- A bank intending any VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA before launch, and get feedback on its risk controls. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals, p.5]
- A bank carrying on regulated activities (securities business the SFC regulates) must register with the SFC as an RI. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] The SFC keeps ultimate responsibility, but the HKMA supervises the RI day to day. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] The SFC may rely on that HKMA supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3]
- Banks follow the joint circular in their role as RIs, with the HKMA as frontline supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3] The circular reminds intermediaries to tell the SFC, and the HKMA where applicable, before they start or change VA-related activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- The SFC and HKMA require intermediaries to offer VA dealing services only by partnering with SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- The Stablecoins Ordinance came into force on 1 August 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- The HKMA register lists two licensed stablecoin issuers. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table] They are Anchorpoint Financial Limited (FRS01) and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (FRS02). [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table] Both licences took effect on 10 April 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- The Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury (SFST) told LegCo that the HKMA received 36 applications in the initial application period. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply para 1 and (2)] The SFST also said any further licences would keep the overall number very limited. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply para 1 and (2)]
- The SFC's online list showed 13 licensed VATPs at its 29 May 2026 update. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table; applicant section] Being on the applicant list is not the same as being licensed. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table; applicant section]
- The HKMA's 2026 custody annex expects banks to keep 98% of client VAs in cold storage (offline). [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(b), para 11(l) and fn 8] The annex also expects banks to create, store and back up seeds and private keys in Hong Kong. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(b), para 11(l) and fn 8] Private keys are the secret codes that control the assets, and seeds are used to generate them.
- The Basel cryptoasset rules on capital, disclosure and exposure limits came into force on 1 January 2026. [S:974899bbef4d, p.1]
- CRP-1 is the HKMA's Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) module on classifying cryptoassets. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] Under it, a bank that acquires a new type of cryptoasset must notify the HKMA. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] The bank must treat the asset as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's own classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] Group 2b is the capital category where the bank gets no credit for hedging.
- For the coming VA dealing and custody regimes, the Government proposed that the SFC lead licensing and standard-setting. The HKMA would be the frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- Under the proposed dealing regime, banks would need to register with the SFC to deal in VAs. The SFC would register banks in consultation with the HKMA. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 1.10]
- No arrangement is planned to let existing providers carry on while they apply once the VA dealing and custody regimes start. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51] SFC-licensed VATPs, LCs, RIs and banks already providing these services are to get a faster (expedited) approval process. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
- The CARF bill was published in the Gazette (the Government's official record) on 22 May 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section] It had its First Reading (the first formal stage of a bill) in LegCo on 3 June 2026. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section] If the bill passes, CARF duties start on 1 January 2027. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section]; [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]
- The IRD plans a dedicated CARF portal. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Reporting portal section] The bill proposes that every in-scope reporting crypto-asset service provider register an account on it. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Reporting portal section]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Discuss any planned VA activity with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- [ ] As an RI, notify the SFC and HKMA when the bank intends to start or change VA dealing, advice or asset management. This includes a change of client type. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- [ ] From 1 January 2026, classify cryptoasset exposures under Part 12 of the Banking (Capital) Rules. Read CRP-1 alongside Part 12. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Set prudent limits on VA exposures. Set conservative loan-to-value ratios (loan size compared with collateral value) when VAs are used as collateral. [S:642680fa4467, p.3]
- [ ] Keep client digital assets held in custody separate (segregated) from the bank's own assets. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 6]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the HKMA when the bank acquires any new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Discuss custody plans with the HKMA before offering custody. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For VA dealing, route business only through SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- [ ] Check a VATP's status on the SFC's licensed list, not the applicant list. [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section]
- [ ] Check a stablecoin issuer against the HKMA register. The HKMA says the register holds the latest list of licensees. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]

## Talking points

**To the chief compliance officer (CCO)**
- The bank's VA conduct duties (rules on how it treats clients) apply to the bank as an RI. The HKMA supervises the bank day to day, not the SFC directly. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- The bank must comply with the HKMA's anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-financing of terrorism (CFT) Guideline. [S:7bfdad704174, Ch.1 fn 1] As an RI, it must also take into account the SFC's AML/CFT Guideline, including its Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, Ch.1 fn 1]

**To business heads**
- Any new VA product needs an HKMA discussion before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5] RIs must also tell the SFC and HKMA in advance. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- Only two stablecoin issuers are licensed. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table] The Government says any further licences would keep the total very limited. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (2)]

**To the CEO**
- The Government proposed that the SFC lead licensing for VA dealing and custody. The HKMA would be the banks' frontline regulator. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- No arrangement is planned to let existing firms carry on while they apply when these regimes start. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]

**To Risk / chief risk officer (CRO)**
- The Basel cryptoasset standard applies from 1 January 2026. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.4.1-1.4.2, 2.1.2] Until the HKMA agrees the bank's classification, two things stay in Group 2b: exposures held before that date and any new cryptoasset type. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.4.1-1.4.2, 2.1.2]
- The HKMA's custody annex expects banks to keep 98% of client VAs in cold storage. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(l) and fn 8]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** "The SFC supervises our VA business directly." **Fact:** The SFC holds ultimate responsibility. But the HKMA is the RI's day-to-day supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- **Myth:** "The 50%/100% insurance rule applies to our custody." **Fact:** That rule is an SFC expectation for VATPs. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22] It calls for an SFC-approved compensation arrangement covering 50% of client VAs in cold storage and 100% in hot (online) and other storage. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22] The HKMA's 2026 annex takes a different approach. It makes the bank liable to clients for losses from incidents the bank is responsible for. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)] It also expects the bank to hold adequate financial resources to cover such losses. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)]
- **Myth:** "A platform on the SFC's applicant list is licensed." **Fact:** Applicants are not licensed, even those allowed to keep operating while they apply. Only the licensed list shows licensed platforms. [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section]
- **Myth:** "The 2022 joint circular still applies." **Fact:** The December 2023 joint circular replaced the January 2022 and October 2023 versions. [S:8a016a912528, note after para 3, p.1]
- **Myth:** "The dealing and custody bills are law." **Fact:** They are at the consultation-conclusions stage. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31] The Government's stated target was to introduce a bill in 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]

## Read these first

1. **Regulatory approaches to AIs' interface with VAs and VASPs** — HKMA, 28 Jan 2022. Read pp.3-5 on prudential rules (bank safety and soundness), anti-money laundering, investor protection and prior discussion. This document sets out the HKMA's base position on banks and VAs. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/20220128-1-EN
2. **SPM SB-1 Supervision of Regulated Activities of SFC-Registered AIs (V.4)** — HKMA, 12 Sep 2025. Read paras 2.1.3, 2.2.4, 4.16.1 and 5.6. This module explains why the HKMA, not the SFC, supervises your RI business day to day. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20250909-3-EN/SB-1.PDF
3. **Policy Statement 2.0 on the Development of Digital Assets in Hong Kong** — FSTB, 26 Jun 2025. Read paras 4-6 and 16. This statement sets out the LEAP framework. LEAP stands for Legal and regulatory streamlining, Expanding tokenised products, Advancing use cases and collaboration, and People and partnership development. The statement also proposes that the SFC lead the new regimes, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. https://gia.info.gov.hk/general/202506/26/P2025062600269_500089_1_1750909574183.pdf
