# A1 The Hong Kong digital-asset map

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can name who regulates each digital-asset activity in Hong Kong, which regimes are live, and which are still coming.

## In 60 seconds

Hong Kong splits digital-asset regulation among several bodies, each covering particular activities. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises banks (Authorized Institutions, AIs) and licenses stablecoin issuers. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.1]; [S:270f1f7324ab, Register introduction] The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licenses virtual-asset (VA) trading platforms and sets codes of conduct for regulated securities activities. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.2] The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) sets policy and, with the SFC, consults on new laws; the Legislative Council (LegCo) passes bills. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, cover page]; [S:b12bd88c7aee, p.1] A bank that carries on SFC-regulated activities must register with the SFC as a Registered Institution (RI), but the HKMA is its day-to-day, frontline supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3] Live today: the VA trading-platform regime, the Stablecoins Ordinance, the joint SFC–HKMA circulars, HKMA custody and tokenisation guidance, and the Basel cryptoasset capital standard (see Status board). Coming: statutory licensing for VA dealing, custody, advisory and management, and crypto-asset tax reporting under the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF). [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 36]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]; [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section]

## Key facts

- The HKMA does not currently plan to stop banks taking VA exposure, provided controls and senior-management oversight are adequate. [S:642680fa4467, Prudential supervision section, p.3]
- A bank intending any VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA and get feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals, p.5]
- An AI carrying on regulated activities must register with the SFC as an RI; the SFC keeps ultimate responsibility, while the HKMA supervises the RI day to day and the SFC may rely on that supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2-2.1.3]
- Banks meet the joint circular as RIs, with the HKMA as frontline supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3] Intermediaries are reminded to tell the SFC ahead of time (and the HKMA, where applicable) before beginning or changing VA-related activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- The SFC and HKMA require intermediaries to provide VA dealing services only by partnering with SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- The Stablecoins Ordinance commenced on 1 August 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- The HKMA register lists two licensed stablecoin issuers, Anchorpoint Financial Limited (FRS01) and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (FRS02), both effective 10 April 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- The Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury (SFST) told LegCo that the HKMA received 36 applications in the initial application period, and that any further licences would keep the overall number very limited. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply para 1 and (2)]
- The SFC's live list showed 13 licensed VATPs at its 29 May 2026 update; being on the applicant list is not the same as being licensed. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table; applicant section]
- The HKMA's 2026 custody annex expects AIs to keep 98% of client VAs in cold storage and to generate, store and back up seeds and private keys in Hong Kong. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(b), para 11(l) and fn 8]
- The Basel cryptoasset capital, disclosure and exposure-limit rules came into operation on 1 January 2026. [S:974899bbef4d, p.1]
- Under CRP-1, the HKMA's Supervisory Policy Manual module on classifying cryptoassets, a bank that acquires a new type of cryptoasset must notify the HKMA and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's classification assessment. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- For the coming VA dealing and custody regimes, the Government proposed that the SFC lead licensing and standard-setting, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- Under the proposed dealing regime, banks would need to register with the SFC, in consultation with the HKMA, to deal in VAs. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 1.10]
- No deeming (grandfathering) arrangement is planned when the VA dealing and custody regimes commence; SFC-licensed VATPs, LCs, RIs and banks already providing these services are to get an expedited approval process. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
- The CARF bill appeared in the Gazette on 22 May 2026 and had its First Reading in LegCo on 3 June 2026; subject to its passage, CARF duties start on 1 January 2027. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section]; [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]
- The IRD plans a dedicated CARF portal, and the bill proposes that every in-scope reporting crypto-asset service provider register an account on it. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Reporting portal section]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Discuss any planned VA activity with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- [ ] As an RI, notify the SFC and HKMA when intending to start or change VA dealing, advice or asset management, including a change of client type. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- [ ] Classify cryptoasset exposures under Part 12 of the Banking (Capital) Rules, reading CRP-1 alongside, from 1 January 2026. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Set prudent VA exposure limits and conservative loan-to-value ratios for VA collateral. [S:642680fa4467, p.3]
- [ ] Keep client digital assets held in custody segregated from the bank's own assets. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 6]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the HKMA on acquiring any new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Discuss custody plans with the HKMA before offering custody. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For VA dealing, route business only through SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- [ ] Check a VATP's status on the SFC's licensed list, not the applicant list. [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section]
- [ ] Check a stablecoin issuer against the HKMA register, which the HKMA says holds the latest list of licensees. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]

## Talking points

**To the chief compliance officer (CCO)**
- The bank's VA conduct duties reach it as an RI supervised day to day by the HKMA, not through direct SFC supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- The bank must comply with the HKMA's anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) Guideline; as an RI it must also have regard to the SFC's AML/CFT Guideline, including its Chapter 12 on VAs. [S:7bfdad704174, Ch.1 fn 1]

**To business heads**
- Any new VA product needs an HKMA discussion before launch, and RIs must pre-notify the SFC and HKMA. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- Only two stablecoin issuers are licensed; the Government says any further licences would keep the total very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (2)]

**To the CEO**
- The Government proposed an SFC-led licensing model for VA dealing and custody, with the HKMA as the banks' frontline regulator. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- No deeming (grandfathering) arrangement is planned when these regimes start. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]

**To Risk / chief risk officer (CRO)**
- The Basel cryptoasset standard applies from 1 January 2026; exposures held before that date, and any new type of cryptoasset, stay in Group 2b until the HKMA agrees the bank's classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.4.1-1.4.2, 2.1.2]
- The HKMA's custody annex expects AIs to keep 98% of client VAs in cold storage. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(l) and fn 8]

## Common mix-ups

- "The SFC supervises our VA business directly." No: the SFC holds ultimate responsibility, but the HKMA is the RI's day-to-day supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- "The 50%/100% insurance rule applies to our custody." That rule is the SFC's VATP expectation of an SFC-approved compensation arrangement covering 50% of client VAs in cold storage and 100% in hot and other storage; the HKMA's 2026 annex instead makes the bank liable for losses attributable to it and expects adequate financial resources. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22]; [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)]
- "A deemed or applicant VATP is licensed." Not so; only the licensed list shows licensed platforms. [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section]
- "The 2022 joint circular still applies." The December 2023 joint circular superseded the January 2022 and October 2023 versions. [S:8a016a912528, note after para 3, p.1]
- "The dealing and custody bills are law." They are at consultation-conclusions stage; the Government's stated target was to introduce a bill in 2026. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]
