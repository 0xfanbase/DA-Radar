# A1 The Hong Kong digital-asset map

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can name who regulates each digital-asset activity in Hong Kong, which regimes are live, and which are still coming.

## In 60 seconds

Hong Kong regulates digital assets activity by activity, not through one super-regulator. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises banks (Authorized Institutions, AIs) and licenses stablecoin issuers; the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licenses trading platforms and sets conduct rules for securities-type activity; the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) sets policy and drafts the laws; the Legislative Council (LegCo) passes them. When a bank does securities-type virtual-asset (VA) business, it does so as an SFC Registered Institution (RI), but the HKMA is its day-to-day supervisor. Live today: the VA trading-platform regime, the Stablecoins Ordinance, the joint SFC–HKMA circulars, HKMA custody and tokenisation guidance, and the Basel cryptoasset capital standard. Coming: statutory licensing for VA dealing, custody, advisory and management, plus crypto tax reporting (CARF).

## Key facts

- The HKMA does not currently plan to stop banks taking VA exposure, provided controls and senior-management oversight are adequate. [S:642680fa4467, Prudential supervision section, p.3]
- A bank intending any VA activity should discuss it with the HKMA and get feedback on its risk controls before launch. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals, p.5]
- An AI registered by the SFC becomes an RI; the SFC keeps ultimate responsibility, while the HKMA supervises the RI day to day and the SFC may rely on that supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- The joint circular reaches banks as RIs through the HKMA as frontline supervisor; intermediaries must tell the SFC ahead of time — and the HKMA too, if relevant — before beginning or changing VA-related activities. [S:8a016a912528, para 31]
- VA dealing by intermediaries may only be done by partnering with SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, paras 17-18]
- The Stablecoins Ordinance commenced on 1 August 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- The HKMA register lists two licensed stablecoin issuers, Anchorpoint Financial Limited (FRS01) and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (FRS02), both effective 10 April 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]
- The HKMA received 36 first-batch stablecoin applications and says the number of licences will stay very limited. [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply para 1 and (2)]
- The SFC's live list showed 13 licensed VATPs at its 29 May 2026 update; being on the applicant list is not the same as being licensed. [S:b14be2203d39, Licensed platforms table; applicant section]
- The HKMA's 2026 custody annex expects at least 98% of client VAs in cold storage and seed/key generation, storage and backup in Hong Kong. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(b), para 11(l) and fn 8]
- The Basel cryptoasset capital, disclosure and exposure-limit rules took effect on 1 January 2026. [S:974899bbef4d, para 2]
- Under CRP-1, a bank must notify the HKMA before acquiring a new type of cryptoasset and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees otherwise. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- For the coming VA dealing and custody regimes, the SFC will license and set standards, and the HKMA will be frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- Banks must register with the SFC, in consultation with the HKMA, to deal in VAs under the proposed dealing regime. [S:f673ef6dcb63, para 1.10]
- No grandfathering will apply when the VA dealing and custody regimes commence; firms already vetted get an expedited track. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
- The CARF bill was gazetted on 22 May 2026 and had its First Reading on 3 June 2026; CARF duties are set to start on 1 January 2027. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview section]; [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)]
- The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) will run CARF registration through a future portal; the Insurance Authority (IA) appears in this corpus only as a co-backer of FiNETech and a contributor to the HKMA's DLT study. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Reporting portal section]; [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 5]; [S:e0fd558b3dbc, Introduction]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Discuss any planned VA activity with the HKMA before launch. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]
- [ ] As an RI, give advance notice to the SFC and HKMA before starting or changing VA dealing, advice or asset management, including a change of client type. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- [ ] Treat cryptoasset exposures under the Banking (Capital) Rules Part 12 and CRP-1 from 1 January 2026. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Set prudent VA exposure limits and conservative loan-to-value ratios for VA collateral. [S:642680fa4467, p.3]
- [ ] Keep client digital assets segregated from the bank's own assets. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 6]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Notify the HKMA before acquiring any new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- [ ] Discuss custody plans with the HKMA before offering custody. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] For VA dealing, route business only through SFC-licensed VATPs. [S:8a016a912528, para 17]
- [ ] Check a VATP's status on the SFC's licensed list, not the applicant list. [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section]
- [ ] Check a stablecoin issuer against the HKMA register. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 5]

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- The bank's VA conduct duties reach it as an RI supervised by the HKMA, not through direct SFC supervision. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- The HKMA AML/CFT Guideline, not the SFC's, is the bank's primary AML text; RIs only need to have regard to SFC Chapter 12. [S:7bfdad704174, Ch.1 fn 1]

**To business heads**
- Any new VA product needs an HKMA discussion before launch, and RIs must pre-notify the SFC and HKMA. [S:642680fa4467, p.5]; [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]
- Only two stablecoin issuers are licensed; the HKMA says licences will stay very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (2)]

**To the CEO**
- The government plans a single SFC-led licensing model for VA dealing and custody, with the HKMA as the banks' frontline regulator. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- No grandfathering is planned when these regimes start. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]

**To Risk / CRO**
- The Basel cryptoasset standard applies from 1 January 2026; unassessed exposures sit in Group 2b by default. [S:b7d630146e6c, paras 1.4.1-1.4.2]
- Custody requires at least 98% of client VAs in cold storage. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(l)]

## Common mix-ups

- "The SFC supervises our VA business directly." No: the SFC holds ultimate responsibility, but the HKMA is the RI's day-to-day supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]
- "The 50%/100% insurance rule applies to our custody." The fixed 50% cold / 100% hot cover belongs to the SFC VATP regime; the HKMA's 2026 annex instead makes the bank liable for losses attributable to it and requires adequate financial resources. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22]; [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)]
- "A deemed or applicant VATP is licensed." Not so; only the licensed list shows licensed platforms. [S:b14be2203d39, applicant section]
- "The 2022 joint circular still applies." The December 2023 joint circular superseded the January 2022 and October 2023 versions. [S:8a016a912528, note after para 3, p.1]
- "The dealing and custody bills are law." They are at consultation-conclusions stage; the government targets a 2026 bill. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]
