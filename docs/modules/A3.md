# A3 Where policy is heading

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can say what the government, the SFC and the HKMA have officially said they will do next, and by when, using only their own words and dates.

## In 60 seconds

Hong Kong's digital-asset direction is set out in a short stack of official strategy papers. The FSTB's 2022 Policy Statement set out its founding regulatory principle (quoted in Key facts below); its 2025 Policy Statement 2.0 added the LEAP framework and made the SFC the lead licensing authority, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. The SFC's ASPIRe roadmap (Feb 2025) lists 12 initiatives, most of them still exploratory. The HKMA moved from Fintech 2025 to Fintech 2030, then published a Fintech Promotion Blueprint (Feb 2026) and asked bank boards to endorse a business-model plan. The 16 Sep 2026 Policy Address and the 2026-27 Budget add dated targets: tokenised Exchange Fund Bills, EnsembleTX settlement upgrades, and a VA licensing bill in 2026. All of these are statements of intent until a law or rule gives them force.

## Key facts

- The 2022 Policy Statement committed the government to "same activity, same risks, same regulation" and to a mandatory VA service-provider licensing regime. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6]
- It also flagged stablecoins as a priority and named three pilots: NFT issuance, a tokenised government green bond, and e-HKD. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 9-11]
- Policy Statement 2.0 replaced the government's use of *virtual assets* with *digital assets*; the legal term under AMLO remains *virtual asset*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]
- LEAP stands for Legal and regulatory streamlining, Expanding tokenised products, Advancing use cases, and People and partnership development. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 4]
- Under PS 2.0 the SFC leads licensing for digital-asset dealing and custody, while the HKMA is frontline regulator for banks' dealing and custodian activities. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- PS 2.0 also said the government would extend fund tax concessions to specified digital assets from year of assessment 2025/26, subject to LegCo approval. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- ASPIRe has five pillars (Access, Safeguards, Products, Infrastructure, Relationships) and 12 initiatives; custodian licensing preparation was targeted for end-2025. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]
- ASPIRe says the SFC will explore moving from a fixed cold-storage ratio to outcome-based custody standards. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]
- Fintech 2030 has four pillars (Data and payments, AI, Resilience, Tokenisation) and over 40 initiatives, including tokenised government bonds and the Ensemble pilot. [S:1131a8814d96, para 3 and 'Tokenisation of Finance']
- The Fintech Promotion Blueprint announced four flagship projects, including a Quantum Preparedness Index and a Fintech Cybersecurity Baseline for AI/DLT providers. [S:b0607b779270, paras 5, 7]
- The HKMA expects each AI's board to oversee and endorse a strategic business plan within six months of 9 March 2026, covering DLT and other technology change. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The 2025 Policy Address committed to introducing a bill in 2026 for licensing digital-asset dealing and custodian providers. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex item 22]
- The 2026-27 Budget repeated the 2026 bill target and said a CARF amendment bill would come in the first half of 2026, with implementation over two years. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108]
- The Budget also said CMU OmniClear would launch a digital asset platform in 2026 for digital bond issuance and settlement. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114]
- The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA will test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end, covering more than $1.3 trillion of bills. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The HKMA aims to have central bank digital currency settlement running around the clock through EnsembleTX by roughly end-2026, with more tokenised-deposit use cases to follow. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]
- The SFC will enhance VA licensing regimes, let licensed platforms trade regulated stablecoins, and switch on a system for monitoring digital-asset custody from mid-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49(i), 49(iii), 51(iii)]
- Customs and Excise plans a digital-asset transaction analytics system by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Part II item 209]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Have the board oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan addressing DLT and related technology within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Track the 2026 bill target for VA dealing and custody licensing, since banks will need SFC registration with the HKMA as frontline regulator. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]; [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- [ ] Track CARF start dates (1 Jan 2027 for CARF, 1 Jan 2028 for amended CRS) in the bill. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)-(4)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Discuss any new digital-asset activity with the HKMA before launch, as the March 2026 circular restates. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- [ ] Be ready to submit the business plan via the HKMA Survey Tool if invited. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] No counterparty duties arise from policy statements themselves; apply the rules in B2 and B3.

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Policy statements, Budgets and Policy Addresses create no obligations; the business-model circular does create a board expectation. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]; [S:3ec5b269b5dc, p.2]
- Government now says *digital assets*, but AMLO still defines *virtual asset*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]

**To business heads**
- The SFC plans to let licensed platforms trade regulated stablecoins and use them to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- EnsembleTX is to add 24/7 CBDC settlement by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA wants banks to act as trusted intermediaries between traditional finance and digital assets, and expects a board-endorsed plan. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, p.1-2]
- The government's stated 2026 target is one bill for VA dealing and custody licensing. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex item 22]

**To Risk / CRO**
- The Blueprint's Quantum Preparedness Index will measure post-quantum readiness; the HKMA's first score was 2.3 out of 10. [S:b0607b779270, para 5]; [S:a50e9dc16dc7, slide 11]
- ASPIRe may shift custody rules toward outcome-based standards, but that is still exploratory. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]

## Common mix-ups

- "Policy Statement 2.0 changed the law." It sets directions; tax concessions, for example, need LegCo approval. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- "ASPIRe items are rules." Most initiatives are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]
- "The first stablecoin licences came in March 2026 as the Budget said." The Budget said next month; the HKMA granted them on 10 April 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 105]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 1]
- "The business-model circular is about crypto only." It covers DLT, agentic AI and cloud, with a reminder on digital-asset pre-launch discussion. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, p.1-2]
