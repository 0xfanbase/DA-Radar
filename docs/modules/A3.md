# A3 Where policy is heading

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can say what the government, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) have officially said they will do next, and by when, using only their own words and dates.

## In 60 seconds

Hong Kong's digital-asset direction is set out in a short stack of official strategy papers. The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) issued a 2022 Policy Statement that set out its founding regulatory principle (quoted in Key facts below). [S:f587b828e3fa, para 4] Its 2025 Policy Statement 2.0 added the LEAP framework and proposed the SFC as the lead licensing authority for digital-asset dealing and custody, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 4-6] The SFC's ASPIRe roadmap (Feb 2025) lists 12 initiatives; half of them are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Executive Summary; Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11] The HKMA closed Fintech 2025 and launched Fintech 2030, then published a Fintech Promotion Blueprint (Feb 2026) and asked bank boards to endorse a business-model plan. [S:1131a8814d96, photo caption; S:b0607b779270, para 1; S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The 2026-27 Budget and the 16 Sep 2026 Policy Address add dated targets, including a digital-asset licensing bill in 2026, EnsembleTX upgrades and tests of tokenised Exchange Fund Bills. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 109]; [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii), 50]

## Key facts

- The 2022 Policy Statement said the government would follow the "same activity, same risks, same regulation" principle, and that its next step was a licensing regime for VA service providers. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6]
- It also named stablecoins as another key area of focus and listed three pilots: NFT (non-fungible token) issuance, tokenised government green bonds, and e-HKD (a possible retail central bank digital currency). [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 9-10]
- Policy Statement 2.0 (PS 2.0) says the government will use *digital assets* instead of *virtual assets*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] The legal term in the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO) is still *virtual asset* (section 53ZRA). [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1]
- LEAP stands for Legal and regulatory streamlining, Expanding the suite of tokenised products, Advancing use cases and cross-sectoral collaboration, and People and partnership development. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 4]
- PS 2.0 proposes the SFC as the lead authority for licensing and registering digital-asset dealing and custodian service providers, with the HKMA as frontline regulator supervising banks' dealing and custodian activities. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- PS 2.0 also said the government would propose laws to extend profits tax concessions for family-owned investment holding vehicles and privately offered funds to specified digital assets, from year of assessment 2025/26, subject to Legislative Council (LegCo) approval. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- ASPIRe has five pillars (Access, Safeguards, Products, Infrastructure, Relationships) and 12 initiatives. [S:d461c2db6af2, Executive Summary] Government-led legislative preparations for a custodian licensing regime were aimed for completion by end-2025. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]
- ASPIRe says the SFC will explore moving away from set hardware and cold-storage ratios towards technology-neutral, outcome-based custody standards. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]
- Fintech 2030 has four pillars, known as DART (Data and payment infrastructure, Artificial intelligence, Resilience, Tokenisation), and over 40 initiatives, including regular tokenised government bond issuance and the Project Ensemble pilot. [S:1131a8814d96, paras 3-7]
- The Fintech Promotion Blueprint announced four flagship projects, including a Quantum Preparedness Index and a Fintech Cybersecurity Baseline for fintech solution providers using AI (artificial intelligence) and DLT (distributed ledger technology). [S:b0607b779270, paras 3, 4, 6]
- The HKMA expects each AI's board to oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan within six months of the 9 March 2026 circular, addressing DLT and other technology change. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The 2025 Policy Address set a target to introduce a bill into LegCo in 2026 to license digital-asset dealing and custody providers. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22]
- The 2026-27 Budget repeated the plan for a licensing bill in 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104] It also said the government would change the Inland Revenue Ordinance to put into effect the amended Common Reporting Standard (CRS) and the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) over the coming two years, with a bill in the first half of 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 108]
- The Budget also said CMU OmniClear (the HKMA's Central Moneymarkets Unit operator) would set up a digital asset platform in 2026 for digital bond issuance and settlement. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114]
- The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA will test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end, to help banks use more than $1.3 trillion of these bills more efficiently and around the clock. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The HKMA plans to add settlement in central bank digital currency (CBDC), and round-the-clock operation, to EnsembleTX by around end-2026, and to keep exploring tokenised-deposit use cases. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]
- The SFC will enhance VA licensing regimes, promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms, and start running its digital-asset custody monitoring system in the second half of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49(i), 49(iii), 51(iii)]
- The Customs and Excise Department (C&ED) aims to launch a Digital Asset Transaction Analytics System by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex, New Indicators, item 209]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Have the board oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan addressing DLT and related technology within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Track the 2026 bill target for digital-asset dealing and custody licensing. PS 2.0 proposes the SFC as lead licensing and registration authority, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]; [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- [ ] Track the start dates proposed in the CARF bill: 1 Jan 2027 for the CARF part and 1 Jan 2028 for the amended CRS part. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)-(4)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Discuss any new digital-asset activity with the HKMA before launch, as the March 2026 circular restates. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- [ ] Be ready to submit the business plan via the HKMA Survey Tool if invited. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] None set by the policy documents in this module; see B2 and B3 for the current rules.

## Talking points

**To the CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)**
- The 2026 licensing regime is still a target: the bill has to be introduced into LegCo. The business-model circular, by contrast, already sets a board expectation for every AI. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22]; [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- Government now says *digital assets*, but AMLO still uses *virtual asset*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]; [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1]

**To business heads**
- The SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms and their use to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- EnsembleTX is to add CBDC settlement and 24/7 operation by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA says banks have an opportunity to act as a key intermediary between traditional finance and digital assets, and expects a board-endorsed plan. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2]
- The government's stated 2026 target is a bill for digital-asset dealing and custody licensing. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22]

**To Risk / CRO (Chief Risk Officer)**
- The Blueprint's Quantum Preparedness Index assesses banks' readiness for post-quantum cryptography; the first index reading was 2.3 out of 10. [S:b0607b779270, para 4]; [S:a50e9dc16dc7, slide 11]
- ASPIRe may shift custody rules toward outcome-based standards, but that is still exploratory. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]

## Common mix-ups

- "Policy Statement 2.0 changed the law." It sets directions; the tax concessions, for example, need LegCo approval. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- "ASPIRe items are rules." Half of the 12 initiatives are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11]
- "The first stablecoin licences came in March 2026 as the Budget said." The Budget (25 Feb 2026) said the first licences would come the next month; the HKMA granted them on 10 April 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 105]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 1]
- "The business-model circular is about crypto only." It covers DLT, agentic AI and cloud computing, with a reminder on discussing new digital-asset activities before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2]
