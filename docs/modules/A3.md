# A3 Where policy is heading

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After this module, you can say what each body has officially said it will do next, and by when. The bodies are the government, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA). This module uses only their own words and dates.

## In 60 seconds

A short set of official strategy papers sets Hong Kong's direction on digital assets. The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) issued a Policy Statement in 2022. [S:f587b828e3fa, para 4] It set out the government's founding principle for regulation (quoted in Key facts below). [S:f587b828e3fa, para 4] The FSTB's 2025 Policy Statement 2.0 added a framework called LEAP (four policy areas, spelled out in Key facts below). [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 4-6] It also proposed the SFC as the lead licensing authority for digital-asset dealing and custody (holding assets for clients). [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 4-6] The HKMA's role in that model is frontline regulator for banks, meaning the regulator that supervises them directly. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 4-6] ASPIRe (the SFC's roadmap for the digital-asset market, issued in Feb 2025) lists 12 initiatives. [S:d461c2db6af2, Executive Summary; Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11] Half of them are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Executive Summary; Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11] The HKMA closed its Fintech 2025 strategy and launched Fintech 2030. [S:1131a8814d96, photo caption] It then published a Fintech Promotion Blueprint (Feb 2026). [S:b0607b779270, para 1] It also asked bank boards to endorse a plan for their business model. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The 2026-27 Budget and the 16 Sep 2026 Policy Address add dated targets. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 109; S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii), 50] These targets include a digital-asset licensing bill in 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 109] They also include upgrades to EnsembleTX (the HKMA's live pilot for settling tokenised money between banks). [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 109; S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii), 50] The targets also cover tests of tokenised Exchange Fund Bills (short-term HKMA debt issued as digital tokens). [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35(ii), 50]

## Key facts

- The 2022 Policy Statement said the government would follow the principle of "same activity, same risks, same regulation". [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6] It said its next step was a licensing regime for virtual-asset service providers. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6]
- The 2022 Policy Statement also named stablecoins (tokens designed to hold a steady value) as another key area of focus. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 9-10] It listed three pilots: issuing non-fungible tokens, tokenised government green bonds, and e-HKD (a possible central bank digital currency for public use). [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 9-10]
- Policy Statement 2.0 says the government will use the term *digital assets* instead of *virtual assets*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] The anti-money-laundering law still uses the legal term *virtual asset*. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1] That law is the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO), section 53ZRA. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1]
- LEAP stands for: Legal and regulatory streamlining; Expanding the suite of tokenised products; Advancing use cases and cross-sectoral collaboration; People and partnership development. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 4]
- Policy Statement 2.0 proposes the SFC as the lead authority for licensing and registering digital-asset dealers and custodians. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] For banks, it names the HKMA as frontline regulator of their dealing and custody activities. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- Policy Statement 2.0 also said the government would propose laws on profits tax concessions (tax breaks). [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15] Privately offered funds and family-owned investment holding vehicles already get these breaks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15] The change is to extend them to cover specified digital assets. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15] The start date is the 2025/26 tax year, if the Legislative Council (LegCo) approves. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- ASPIRe has five pillars and 12 initiatives. [S:d461c2db6af2, Executive Summary] The pillars are Access, Safeguards, Products, Infrastructure and Relationships. [S:d461c2db6af2, Executive Summary] ASPIRe says the government-led work to prepare laws for a custodian licensing regime aimed to finish by end-2025. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]
- ASPIRe says the SFC will explore new custody standards. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3] The idea is standards that focus on outcomes and suit any technology, instead of fixed hardware and cold-storage ratios. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3] Cold storage means keeping the keys to client assets offline.
- Fintech 2030 has four pillars, known as DART, and over 40 initiatives. [S:1131a8814d96, paras 3-7] DART stands for Data and payment infrastructure, Artificial intelligence, Resilience and Tokenisation. [S:1131a8814d96, paras 3-7] The initiatives include regular issuance of tokenised government bonds and the Project Ensemble pilot. [S:1131a8814d96, paras 3-7]
- The Fintech Promotion Blueprint announced four flagship projects. [S:b0607b779270, paras 3, 4, 6] One is a Quantum Preparedness Index. [S:b0607b779270, paras 3, 4, 6] Another is a Fintech Cybersecurity Baseline for fintech firms using artificial intelligence and distributed ledger technology (DLT). [S:b0607b779270, paras 3, 4, 6] DLT is the shared-ledger technology behind blockchains.
- The HKMA expects each authorized institution's board to oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan covering DLT and other technology change. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The board has six months from the 9 March 2026 circular. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The 2025 Policy Address set a target: a bill in LegCo in 2026 to license digital-asset dealing and custody service providers. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22]
- The 2026-27 Budget repeated the plan for a licensing bill in 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104] It said the government would change the Inland Revenue Ordinance over the coming two years. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 108] The change puts into effect two international tax-reporting standards. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 108] They are the amended Common Reporting Standard (CRS) and the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF). [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 108] Both help tax authorities share information across borders. The bill was due in the first half of 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 108]
- CMU OmniClear: the company that runs the HKMA's Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU), a system for holding and settling bonds. The Budget said CMU OmniClear would set up a digital asset platform in 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114] The platform is for issuing and settling digital bonds. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114]
- The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA will test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)] The tests are meant to help banks use more than HK$1.3 trillion of these bills more efficiently. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)] Banks could then use them around the clock. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The HKMA plans to add two features to EnsembleTX by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50] One is settlement in central bank digital currency (CBDC). The other is round-the-clock operation. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50] The HKMA will also keep exploring uses for tokenised deposits (bank deposits in digital token form). [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]
- The Policy Address lists three SFC steps: [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49(i), 49(iii), 51(iii)]
  - improve its virtual-asset licensing regimes; [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49(i), 49(iii), 51(iii)]
  - promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms; [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49(i), 49(iii), 51(iii)]
  - start running its digital-asset custody monitoring system in the second half of 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49(i), 49(iii), 51(iii)]
- The Customs and Excise Department aims to launch a Digital Asset Transaction Analytics System by 2029. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex, New Indicators, item 209]
- Hong Kong's First Five-Year Plan covers 2026 to 2030. It says the government will set up a world-leading regime to license and regulate digital assets. [S:e20f75cc97c2, para 2.14, printed p.19] It keeps the 'same activity, same risks, same regulation' approach. [S:e20f75cc97c2, para 2.14, printed p.19]
- The plan's finance table makes tokenisation of finance one of four fintech pillars. [S:e20f75cc97c2, Table 3 Finance+, item 1, printed p.20] It names CBDC, stablecoins, tokenised deposits, tokenised bonds and tokenised securities products as digital assets to develop rigorously. [S:e20f75cc97c2, Table 3 Finance+, item 1, printed p.20] It also backs wider use of Project Ensemble. [S:e20f75cc97c2, Table 3 Finance+, item 1, printed p.20] These are stated targets, not rules.
- On 23 September 2026 the SFC published a strategic action plan for the Five-Year Plan and the 2026 Policy Address. [S:6f0a77fd18b1, p.1, title] In the short term it lists new digital-asset licensing regimes. [S:6f0a77fd18b1, row 'Promoting Finance+', Short Term column, p.3] It also lists surveillance of digital-asset custody, market surveillance under CrypTech (an SFC technology initiative) and anti-money laundering capabilities. [S:6f0a77fd18b1, row 'Strengthening financial security', Short Term column, p.3] For the medium to long term it lists rules for tokenised investment products, such as tokenised gold. [S:6f0a77fd18b1, row 'Promoting Finance+', Medium to Long Term column, p.3] The plan gives no dates for these items. [S:6f0a77fd18b1, pp.1-3]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Have the board oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan covering DLT and related technology. The deadline is six months after 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Track the 2026 target for a bill to license digital-asset dealing and custody. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104] Policy Statement 2.0 proposes the SFC as lead licensing and registration authority, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- [ ] Track the start dates proposed in the CARF bill. These are 1 Jan 2027 for the CARF part and 1 Jan 2028 for the amended CRS part. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)-(4)]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Discuss any new digital-asset activity with the HKMA before launch. The March 2026 circular restates this. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- [ ] Be ready to submit the business plan through the HKMA Survey Tool if invited. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] None set by the policy documents in this module. See B2 and B3 for the current rules.

## Talking points

**To the CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)**
- The 2026 licensing regime is still only a target. The bill has yet to go to LegCo. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22] By contrast, the business-model circular already sets a board expectation for every authorized institution. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The government now says *digital assets*, but AMLO still says *virtual asset*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]; [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1]

**To business heads**
- The SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)] It will also promote their use to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- EnsembleTX is to add CBDC settlement and 24/7 operation by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]

**To the CEO**
- The HKMA sees a chance for banks to link traditional finance and digital assets as a key intermediary. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2] It expects a board-endorsed plan. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2]
- The government's stated 2026 target is a bill to license digital-asset dealing and custody. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22]

**To Risk / CRO (Chief Risk Officer)**
- The Blueprint's Quantum Preparedness Index measures how ready banks are for post-quantum cryptography. [S:b0607b779270, para 4] Post-quantum cryptography is encryption built to resist future quantum computers. The first index reading was 2.3 out of 10. [S:a50e9dc16dc7, slide 11]
- ASPIRe may shift custody rules toward outcome-based standards. That idea is still exploratory. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** "Policy Statement 2.0 changed the law." **Fact:** It sets directions. The tax breaks, for example, need LegCo approval. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 15]
- **Myth:** "ASPIRe items are rules." **Fact:** Half of the 12 initiatives are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11]
- **Myth:** "The first stablecoin licences came in March 2026, as the Budget said." **Fact:** The Budget (25 Feb 2026) said the first licences would come the next month. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 105] The HKMA granted them on 10 April 2026. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 1]
- **Myth:** "The business-model circular is about crypto only." **Fact:** It covers DLT, agentic AI (systems that act on their own) and cloud computing. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2] It also reminds banks to discuss new digital-asset activities before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2]

## Open items

- The 2026 virtual-asset licensing bill was not in the documents reviewed for this guide by 25 Sep 2026. In June 2026 the government said it and the SFC would introduce the bill this year. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)] The bill is to cover providers of virtual-asset dealing, custody, advisory and management services. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- The detailed documents for the Blueprint's Fintech Cybersecurity Baseline and Quantum Preparedness Index are not in the documents reviewed for this guide.
- The 2023 Policy Address does not mention virtual assets. Its nearest items are blockchain-based e-licences and a green fintech subsidy scheme. [S:61458655f37b, paras 41, 72(iv)]

## Check yourself

1. Which body does Policy Statement 2.0 propose as lead licensing authority for digital-asset dealing and custody?
   **Answer:** Policy Statement 2.0 proposes the SFC as the lead authority for licensing and registering digital-asset dealers and custodians. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] For banks, the HKMA is named frontline regulator. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
2. How long does the March 2026 business-model circular give each board to endorse its strategic business plan?
   **Answer:** The HKMA expects the board to oversee and endorse the plan within six months of the 9 March 2026 circular. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
3. Is the 2026 digital-asset licensing bill already law?
   **Answer:** No. It is a stated target. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, Annex, New Indicators, item 22] In June 2026 the government said it would introduce the bill this year. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)] As of 25 Sep 2026, the bill is not in the documents reviewed.
4. What does the March 2026 business-model circular restate about launching a new digital-asset activity at your bank?
   **Answer:** It restates that a bank planning new digital-asset activities is expected to discuss its plans with the HKMA before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
5. Does Policy Statement 2.0's use of the term digital assets mean the law changed the legal term too?
   **Answer:** No. The anti-money-laundering law still uses the legal term virtual asset, under AMLO section 53ZRA. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1]
