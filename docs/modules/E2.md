# E2 Wholesale money, CBDC and cross-border projects

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can explain the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's (HKMA) central bank digital currency (CBDC) work, from the digital Hong Kong dollar (e-HKD) to mBridge and Ensemble, and say what compliance role the published designs give to banks.

## In 60 seconds

The HKMA has worked on an e-HKD since 2017, and its first projects looked at use by financial institutions (wholesale, bank-to-bank money). [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for financial institutions' section] It ran two rounds of market consultation on a retail e-HKD in 2021-2022 and two industry pilot phases in 2023-2025. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'HKMA's Policy Stance on e-HKD used in retail applications' and 'e-HKD Pilot Programme Phase 1' and 'Phase 2' sections] In October 2025 it said it would prioritise wholesale uses, and it has not yet made a policy decision on if or when it will introduce a retail e-HKD. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4]; [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach'] Its cross-border work grew from Project Inthanon-LionRock with the Bank of Thailand (BoT) into mBridge, a multi-CBDC platform that reached minimum viable product (MVP) stage in June 2024. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project Inthanon-LionRock' section]; [S:db56d97e2206, para 1] Project Ensemble explores interbank settlement of tokenised deposits; its pilot phase, EnsembleTX, settles first through Hong Kong's Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) system for HKD and is to move in stages to tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1 and 3] Ensemble has tie-ups with central banks in France, Thailand and Brazil and a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Shanghai bodies. [S:0ed273d6f63f, paras 2-3]; [S:aabff1db2350, paras 1 and 3]; [S:8d9f12b43ada, para 1]; [S:eb28508e5032, para 2] In the mBridge designs, each participating bank runs its own anti-money laundering (AML), know-your-customer (KYC) and sanctions checks off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]; [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]

## Key facts

- The HKMA's e-HKD work began in 2017 with Project LionRock, a proof of concept with the three note-issuing banks and Hong Kong Interbank Clearing Limited. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for financial institutions' section]
- The October 2021 technical paper proposed a two-tier design: the central bank issues e-HKD, and banks distribute it. It said the design was not a blueprint. [S:59a20457a2fc, Executive Summary points 3-4]
- The April 2022 policy paper noted that full anonymity is generally not seen as workable for a CBDC, given anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) duties, and pointed to tiered wallets as a common approach. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.3, p.34]
- In September 2022 the HKMA set out a three-rail approach and said initial legal research suggested existing law was not adequate to issue a digital legal tender with enough certainty. [S:35cb94c89a11, Rail 1 section, p.10]
- Phase 1 of the pilot (16 firms, 14 pilots) found possible value in programmability, tokenisation and atomic settlement. [S:9f8886b0fb21, Executive Summary, p.6; s.3.1, p.11]
- In September 2024 the project became Project e-HKD+, covering both e-HKD and tokenised deposits; Phase 2 ran 11 pilot groups. [S:2f93aa2b9560, paras 1-2]
- On 28 October 2025 the HKMA made wholesale payments the priority for future e-HKD work, ahead of retail use. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4]
- The HKMA's September 2026 CBDC page says it has not yet made a policy decision on if or when it will introduce an e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']
- Inthanon-LionRock was run in Q4 2019. In February 2021 the PBoC's Digital Currency Institute and the Central Bank of the UAE joined, and it was renamed the m-CBDC Bridge. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and fn 1]
- The HKMA page says the BISIH Hong Kong Centre gave 'strong support' as the project became mBridge. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project Inthanon-LionRock' section]
- In the 2022 pilot, 20 banks in four jurisdictions ran more than 160 payment and foreign exchange (FX) trades worth over US$22 million. [S:947fa49606ab, s.1 Executive Summary, p.4]
- At MVP in June 2024, the project team listed was BISIH Hong Kong Centre, Bank of Thailand, PBoC DCI, Central Bank of the UAE and the HKMA; the Saudi Central Bank was welcomed as the next full participant. [S:db56d97e2206, fn 1, para 3]
- In October 2025 the Government told the Legislative Council (LegCo) that mBridge had become part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure, and that participating Hong Kong banks set their own fees. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- Aurum built a two-tier prototype with a wholesale interbank system and retail wallets; Sela tested a design in which private intermediaries (called access enablers) connect directly to the central bank's CBDC ledger. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects'; S:f9000467b0ee, s.1 Introduction, p.7]
- Project Ensemble explores settling tokenised money between banks through the e-HKD as wholesale CBDC, starting with tokenised deposits. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2]; [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project Ensemble' section]
- The November 2025 government digital green bonds gave the HKD and RMB tranches an option to settle in tokenised central bank money, alongside traditional rails. The Government called it the world's first digital bond offering to use e-CNY and e-HKD in settlement. [S:ac6cd085a325, 'Integration of digital money in the settlement process' item]
- Tier-4 personal e-CNY wallets for Hong Kong residents cap balances at RMB10,000 and allow retail payments only, not person-to-person transfers. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2]
- About 80,000 e-CNY wallets had been registered with Hong Kong mobile numbers by end-January 2026, and 18 local banks support top-ups through the FPS. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)]

## What your bank must do

These are pilot and research designs, not binding rules. The lines below show what the published designs expect of participating banks.

**Obligations**
- [ ] On mBridge, run AML, counter-terrorist financing (CTF) and sanctions checks off the platform before sending a payment; the receiving bank runs its own check before confirming. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- [ ] On mBridge, meet each jurisdiction's AML/CTF/sanctions laws; the platform attaches a pass or fail certificate from the bank's off-platform check to each transaction. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.2(d), p.35]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep KYC, AML and CTF compliance off-bridge in the bank's own systems. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
- [ ] For any e-HKD distribution role, note that the 2022 policy paper said a two-tier design could task customer-facing institutions with customer due diligence and record-keeping. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4, p.35]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Banks may use the HKMA Supervisory Incubator for distributed ledger technology (DLT) to reaffirm their risk controls before a full DLT launch; tokenised deposits are a core focus. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] On mBridge, foreign banks can hold and use a CBDC, but only domestic banks can have it issued or redeemed against reserves. Check which role each counterparty has. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.1, pp.31-32]

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- mBridge leaves AML and sanctions screening to each bank, off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- The 2022 e-HKD policy paper said customer-facing institutions could be tasked with customer due diligence under a two-tier design. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4, p.35]

**To business heads**
- The HKMA's e-HKD focus is now wholesale payments, to support tokenisation and cross-border payments. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4]
- In Phase 2, commercial banks leaned slightly towards tokenised deposits rather than e-HKD. [S:bdefe7fc69d4, Executive Summary, p.2]

**To the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- mBridge reached MVP in June 2024; the Government calls it part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure. [S:db56d97e2206, para 1]; [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- There is no decision to issue a retail e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- The 2022 mBridge pilot ran on a centralised cloud in Hong Kong, and the report said holding data in a single cloud heightened privacy concerns. [S:947fa49606ab, s.5.1, p.16; s.7.1.3, p.33]
- In Phase 2, zero-knowledge-proof privacy tools tested on public blockchains made transactions up to 320 times more costly in gas fees. [S:bdefe7fc69d4, Section 4 'Privacy', p.30]

## Common mix-ups

- "Hong Kong is about to launch a retail e-HKD." The HKMA has made wholesale the priority and has made no launch decision. [S:6bd9e65817c4, paras 4-5]; [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']
- "mBridge screens payments for sanctions." Each bank screens off-platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- "Any bank can mint any CBDC on mBridge." Only domestic banks can request issuance or redemption of their home CBDC. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.2]
- "e-CNY wallets work like general wallets." Person-to-person transfers are not allowed. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2 'Usage']
- "Inthanon-LionRock and mBridge are different projects." mBridge is the renamed later phase. [S:3044ec37475a, Executive Summary, p.7]

## Open items

- No document reviewed for this guide has a official Hong Kong document after June 2024 describing any change in BISIH's role in mBridge. This module states BISIH's role only as of the 2024 MVP release and the HKMA's CBDC page.
- In October 2025 the HKMA said retail e-HKD groundwork would be finished in the first half of 2026. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 5] Its September 2026 page says this preparation work is completed, but gives no launch decision. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for individuals and corporates' section]
- The planned common token standards from the e-HKD Industry Forum are not yet in the documents reviewed for this guide. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 6]
- The HKMA–PBoC work on upgrading e-CNY wallets and raising their limits had no finalised proposals or timetable as of March 2026. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (3) and (5)]; [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (2)]
- Outcomes of the BDF, BoT, BCB and Shanghai collaborations are not published in the documents reviewed for this guide.
