# E2 Wholesale money, CBDC and cross-border projects

Central bank digital currency (CBDC) is money that a central bank issues in digital form. This module explains the CBDC work of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA). It runs from the digital Hong Kong dollar (e-HKD) to the mBridge and Ensemble projects. The module is for the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After it, you can say what compliance role the published designs give to banks.

## In 60 seconds

The HKMA has worked on the e-HKD since 2017. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for financial institutions' section] Its first projects looked at wholesale use: money that moves only between banks and other financial institutions. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for financial institutions' section] It ran two rounds of market consultation on a retail e-HKD (one for the public) in 2021-2022. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'HKMA's Policy Stance on e-HKD used in retail applications' and 'e-HKD Pilot Programme Phase 1' and 'Phase 2' sections] It also ran two industry pilot phases in 2023-2025. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'HKMA's Policy Stance on e-HKD used in retail applications' and 'e-HKD Pilot Programme Phase 1' and 'Phase 2' sections] In October 2025 the HKMA said it would prioritise wholesale uses. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4] It has not yet decided whether or when to introduce a retail e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']

Its cross-border work started with Project Inthanon-LionRock (a joint project with the Bank of Thailand). That project grew into mBridge, a multi-CBDC platform. A multi-CBDC platform is one shared system where several central banks' digital currencies can move. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project Inthanon-LionRock' section] The mBridge platform reached minimum viable product (MVP) stage, meaning a first working version, in June 2024. [S:db56d97e2206, para 1]

Project Ensemble explores how banks settle tokenised deposits with each other. A tokenised deposit is an ordinary bank deposit recorded as a digital token. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1 and 3] EnsembleTX — the pilot phase — first settles these payments through Hong Kong's Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) system for HKD. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1 and 3] RTGS settles each payment between banks one by one, as it happens. EnsembleTX is to move in stages to tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1 and 3] Ensemble has tie-ups with the central banks of France, Thailand and Brazil. [S:0ed273d6f63f, paras 2-3; S:aabff1db2350, paras 1 and 3; S:8d9f12b43ada, para 1] It also has a memorandum of understanding (MoU), a formal cooperation agreement, with Shanghai bodies. [S:eb28508e5032, para 2]

In the mBridge designs, each bank runs its own anti-money laundering (AML), know-your-customer (KYC) and sanctions checks. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30; S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3] Each bank runs these checks off the platform, in its own systems. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30; S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]

> **Commercial and customer benefits**
> **For customers:**
> - A 2023 Bank for International Settlements (BIS) paper said mBridge payments can settle within seconds, in central bank money. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
> - The same 2023 paper said less settlement risk and fewer repeated steps can lower overall costs. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
> - The HKMA says its EnsembleTX pilot aims to make settling tokenised deals faster and more transparent. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 2]
> **For the bank:**
> - The 2023 paper said banks on mBridge can connect directly, instead of going through correspondent banks (banks that pass payments on abroad). [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
> - A bank can earn a fee and a foreign exchange spread on each cross-border payment it handles (concept).
> - EnsembleTX first focuses on tokenised deposits for tokenised money market fund deals and real-time liquidity and treasury management. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 2]
> **Evidence so far:**
> - In a six-week pilot in 2022, 20 banks in four jurisdictions made over 160 payment and foreign exchange (FX) deals on mBridge. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project mBridge' section] They totalled more than HK$171 million, on behalf of corporate clients. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project mBridge' section]
> - A 2025 Financial Stability Board report (global, reported World Bank data) put the average cost of sending a US$200 remittance at 6.5% of the amount sent in 2025. [I:0c779bbbb00d, p.27, section 3.3]
> **Limits:**
> - In June 2024 the HKMA said mBridge had reached minimum viable product stage (a first working version), with further enhancements to follow. [S:db56d97e2206, paras 1 and 3]
> - The HKMA has not yet decided whether or when to introduce an e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']

## Key facts

- The HKMA's e-HKD work began in 2017 with Project LionRock. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for financial institutions' section] LionRock was a proof of concept, meaning a small test of whether an idea works. It involved the three note-issuing banks (the banks that issue Hong Kong banknotes) and Hong Kong Interbank Clearing Limited. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for financial institutions' section]
- In October 2021 an HKMA technical paper proposed a two-tier design. [S:59a20457a2fc, Executive Summary points 3-4] Under it, the central bank issues e-HKD and banks distribute it. [S:59a20457a2fc, Executive Summary points 3-4] The paper said this design was not a blueprint. [S:59a20457a2fc, Executive Summary points 3-4]
- The April 2022 policy paper noted that full anonymity is generally not seen as workable for a CBDC. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.3, p.34] The reason given was anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) duties. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.3, p.34] The paper pointed to tiered wallets as a common approach. Tiered wallets have limits that rise as the user passes more identity checks. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.3, p.34]
- In September 2022 the HKMA set out a three-rail approach, meaning three parallel work streams. [S:35cb94c89a11, Rail 1 section, p.10] It said early legal research suggested existing law was not adequate to issue a digital legal tender with enough certainty. [S:35cb94c89a11, Rail 1 section, p.10] Legal tender is money the law recognises for paying debts.
- Phase 1 of the pilot had 16 firms running 14 pilots. [S:9f8886b0fb21, Executive Summary, p.6; s.3.1, p.11] It found possible value in three features. [S:9f8886b0fb21, Executive Summary, p.6; s.3.1, p.11]
  - Programmability: rules built into the money about when and how it moves.
  - Tokenisation: recording money or assets as digital tokens.
  - Atomic settlement: both sides of a deal settle at the same instant, or neither does.
- In September 2024 the project became Project e-HKD+. It covers both e-HKD and tokenised deposits. [S:2f93aa2b9560, paras 1-2] Phase 2 ran 11 pilot groups. [S:2f93aa2b9560, paras 1-2]
- On 28 October 2025 the HKMA made wholesale payments the priority for future e-HKD work, ahead of retail use. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4]
- The HKMA's September 2026 CBDC page says it has not yet decided if, or when, it would introduce an e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']
- Inthanon-LionRock ran in Q4 2019. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and fn 1] In February 2021 the PBoC DCI and the Central Bank of the UAE joined. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and fn 1] The project was then renamed the m-CBDC Bridge (multiple-CBDC Bridge). [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and fn 1]
- The HKMA page says the BISIH Hong Kong Centre gave 'strong support' as the project became mBridge. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project Inthanon-LionRock' section]
- In the 2022 mBridge pilot, 20 banks in four jurisdictions made more than 160 payments and foreign exchange trades. [S:947fa49606ab, s.1 Executive Summary, p.4] These were worth over US$22 million in total. [S:947fa49606ab, s.1 Executive Summary, p.4]
- At MVP in June 2024, the listed project team was the HKMA, BoT, PBoC DCI, CBUAE and BISIH Hong Kong Centre. [S:db56d97e2206, fn 1, para 3] The Saudi Central Bank was welcomed as the next full participant. [S:db56d97e2206, fn 1, para 3]
- In October 2024 the BIS said it would hand mBridge over to the partner central banks. [I:3ca847631aa3, project page, para 6] Its then General Manager said on 31 October 2024 that the BIS was leaving because the partners could now run the project themselves. [I:96a1a8113c3f, answer to the mBridge question, para 3]
- He said mBridge was not built for the BRICS countries. [I:96a1a8113c3f, answer to the mBridge question, para 5] BRICS means a group of large emerging economies whose name comes from Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa. He also said that countries under sanctions cannot use BIS products. [I:96a1a8113c3f, answer to the mBridge question, para 6] See also [D7 Sanctions, wallet screening, blockchain analytics and fraud](#m-D7).
- The December 2025 fintech page of the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) lists the central banks conducting mBridge. They are the HKMA, the PBoC DCI, the BoT, the CBUAE and the Saudi Central Bank. The page does not list the BIS. [S:5b4fb46ce83e, 'mBridge' paragraph]
- In October 2025 the Government told the Legislative Council that mBridge had become part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)] It also said participating Hong Kong banks set their own fees. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- Project Aurum built a two-tier test version: a wholesale system between banks, plus retail wallets. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects'] Project Sela tested a different design. Private go-between firms, called access enablers, connect directly to the central bank's CBDC ledger. [S:f9000467b0ee, s.1 Introduction, p.7]
- Project Ensemble explores settling tokenised money between banks, using the e-HKD as a wholesale CBDC. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] A wholesale CBDC is central bank digital money used only by banks and other financial institutions. Ensemble starts with tokenised deposits. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project Ensemble' section]
- The Government's November 2025 digital green bonds had HKD and renminbi (RMB) tranches, or portions. [S:ac6cd085a325, 'Integration of digital money in the settlement process' item] Those tranches had an option to settle in tokenised central bank money, alongside traditional payment systems. [S:ac6cd085a325, 'Integration of digital money in the settlement process' item] The Government called it the world's first digital bond offering to use e-CNY and e-HKD in settlement. [S:ac6cd085a325, 'Integration of digital money in the settlement process' item]
- Tier-4 personal e-CNY wallets for Hong Kong residents cap balances at RMB 10,000. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2] They allow retail payments only, not person-to-person transfers. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2]
- By end-January 2026, about 80,000 e-CNY wallets had been registered with Hong Kong mobile numbers. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)] In addition, 18 local banks let users top up these wallets through the FPS. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)]

## What your bank must do

These are pilot and research designs, not binding rules. Neither the HKMA nor the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has made them rules. The lines below show what the published designs expect of participating banks.

**Obligations**
- [ ] On mBridge, run AML, counter-terrorist financing (CTF) and sanctions checks off the platform before sending a payment. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30] The receiving bank runs its own check before confirming the payment. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- [ ] On mBridge, meet each jurisdiction's AML, CTF and sanctions laws. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.2(d), p.35] The platform attaches a pass or fail certificate to each transaction, based on the bank's own off-platform check. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.2(d), p.35]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep KYC, AML and CTF checks in the bank's own systems, outside the mBridge platform ("off-bridge"). [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
- [ ] If the bank takes a role in distributing e-HKD, note the 2022 policy paper. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4, p.35] It said a two-tier design could give customer-facing institutions two tasks: customer due diligence (checking who customers are) and record-keeping. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4, p.35]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Banks may use the HKMA Supervisory Incubator for distributed ledger technology (DLT). [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3] DLT is a shared record kept in step across many parties. The Incubator lets banks reaffirm their risk controls before a full DLT launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3] Tokenised deposits are a core focus. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] On mBridge, foreign banks can hold and use a CBDC. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.1, pp.31-32] Only domestic banks can swap their reserves at the central bank for that CBDC, or swap it back. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.1, pp.31-32] Check which role each counterparty has.

## Talking points

**To the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
- mBridge leaves AML and sanctions screening to each bank, off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- The 2022 e-HKD policy paper said a two-tier design could task customer-facing institutions with customer due diligence. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4, p.35]

**To business heads**
- The HKMA's e-HKD focus is now wholesale payments, to support tokenisation and cross-border payments. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4]
- In Phase 2, commercial banks leaned slightly towards tokenised deposits over e-HKD. [S:bdefe7fc69d4, Executive Summary, p.2]

**To the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**
- mBridge reached MVP in June 2024. [S:db56d97e2206, para 1] The Government calls it part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- The HKMA has not decided to issue a retail e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']

**To Risk / Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
- The 2022 mBridge pilot ran on one central cloud system in Hong Kong. [S:947fa49606ab, s.5.1, p.16; s.7.1.3, p.33] The pilot report said holding the data in a single cloud raised privacy concerns. [S:947fa49606ab, s.5.1, p.16; s.7.1.3, p.33]
- In Phase 2, privacy tools using zero-knowledge proofs were tested on public blockchains. A zero-knowledge proof shows a fact is true without revealing the data behind it. [S:bdefe7fc69d4, Section 4 'Privacy', p.30] These tools made transactions up to 320 times more costly in gas fees, the fees paid to process each transaction. [S:bdefe7fc69d4, Section 4 'Privacy', p.30]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** Hong Kong is about to launch a retail e-HKD. **Fact:** The HKMA has made wholesale the priority. It has made no launch decision. [S:6bd9e65817c4, paras 4-5; S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']
- **Myth:** mBridge screens payments for sanctions. **Fact:** Each bank screens payments itself, off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- **Myth:** Any bank can create any CBDC on mBridge. **Fact:** Only domestic banks can request issuance or redemption of their home CBDC. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.2]
- **Myth:** e-CNY wallets work like general-purpose wallets. **Fact:** They do not allow person-to-person transfers. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2 'Usage']
- **Myth:** Inthanon-LionRock and mBridge are different projects. **Fact:** mBridge is the renamed later phase of the same project. [S:3044ec37475a, Executive Summary, p.7]

## Open items

- The official Hong Kong sources reviewed do not describe the BIS exit from mBridge. This module takes it from the BIS's own statements of October 2024. [I:3ca847631aa3, project page, para 6] The sources reviewed do not describe mBridge's work after December 2025.
- In October 2025 the HKMA said groundwork for a retail e-HKD would be finished in the first half of 2026. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 5] Its September 2026 page says this preparation work is completed. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for individuals and corporates' section] The page gives no launch decision. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'e-HKD for individuals and corporates' section]
- The e-HKD Industry Forum's planned common token standards (shared technical rules for tokens) are not yet in the documents reviewed for this guide. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 6]
- The HKMA and the PBoC have been working on upgrading e-CNY wallets and raising their limits. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (3) and (5); S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (2)] As of March 2026, this work had no finalised proposals or timetable. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (3) and (5); S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (2)]

2. When did the mBridge platform reach minimum viable product (MVP) stage?
   **Answer:** mBridge reached MVP stage in June 2024. [S:db56d97e2206, para 1]
3. Has the HKMA decided whether or when to introduce a retail e-HKD?
   **Answer:** No. Its September 2026 CBDC page says it has not yet decided whether or when to introduce a retail e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']
4. If your bank distributes e-HKD under a two-tier design, what two tasks could it get?
   **Answer:** The 2022 policy paper said a two-tier design could give customer-facing institutions customer due diligence and record-keeping. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4, p.35]

   **Answer:** No. Only domestic banks can request issuance or redemption of their home CBDC. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.2] Foreign banks can hold and use it. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.1, pp.31-32]
