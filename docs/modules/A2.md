# A2 How Hong Kong rules are made and how to read them

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can place any document on the law-making path, tell how binding it is, and cite it to the paragraph.

## In 60 seconds

The stablecoin regime shows the path a Hong Kong digital-asset rule can take: a consultation paper, published conclusions, a bill gazetted and passed by the Legislative Council (LegCo), a commencement notice in the Gazette, then a transition period. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1]; [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 6]; [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]; [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3] Below the statute sit other instruments whose force differs: rules made as subsidiary legislation, Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) modules, guidelines and circulars. Each SPM module states its own classification on the cover, so check it. [S:974899bbef4d, letter, para 1]; [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification] For a bank, a key reading rule sits in the anti-money-laundering (AML) Guideline of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA): both *must* and *should* mark a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6] HKMA circulars and SPM modules are published in its Banking Regulatory Document Repository (BRDR); circulars of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) open from its e-Distribution site; conclusions of the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) and government press releases record the legislative milestones. [S:974899bbef4d, landing page]; [S:8a016a912528, p.1]; [S:6186c01c6649, p.4, Next step]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]

## Key facts

- The stablecoin conclusions of July 2024 said a bill was being prepared for introduction into LegCo later that year. [S:54aa01e7396e, Way Forward, para 12.1, p.29]
- The Stablecoins Bill was gazetted on 6 December 2024, with its First Reading in LegCo set for 18 December 2024. [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 6]
- LegCo passed the bill on 21 May 2025; the government release refers to a six-month non-contravention period. [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]
- A commencement notice made by the SFST and gazetted on 6 June 2025 set 1 August 2025 as the start date; the notices were to be tabled in LegCo on 11 June 2025 for negative vetting. [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3, 7]
- The Banking (Capital), (Disclosure) and (Exposure Limits) (Amendment) Rules 2025 went through a 28-day LegCo negative-vetting period, which expired without extension. [S:974899bbef4d, letter, para 1]
- The Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO) amendments passed LegCo on 7 December 2022; the HKMA said the amendments relevant to banks would start on 1 June 2023, to allow time for preparation. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2]
- Transitions can be hard stops: VATPs operating before 1 June 2023 could continue for 12 months to 31 May 2024 without breaching the licensing rules, and the SFC had no power to extend the 29 February 2024 deadline for the deeming arrangement. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 20]
- For the coming VA dealing and custodian regimes, the conclusions say existing providers will not be offered a deeming arrangement under current plans. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 48]
- The HKMA AML Guideline is published under AMLO s.7 and Banking Ordinance s.7(3), and compliance is enforced through both ordinances. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.1, 1.3]
- In that Guideline, both *must* and *should* denote a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- SPM modules state their own force: SB-1 is a statutory guideline under Banking Ordinance s.7(3), while CRP-1 is a non-statutory guideline issued as a guidance note. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification]
- Where CRP-1 and the Banking (Capital) Rules (BCR) differ, the BCR prevail. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- Some HKMA papers say they are non-binding: the April 2024 distributed ledger technology (DLT) considerations are non-binding and non-exhaustive, and the HKMA's detailed expectations are discussed with each AI bilaterally. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, Introduction, 4th bullet, p.1]
- A circular can change how a module applies at once: the 3 June 2026 letter clarified that the CRP-1 para 2.6.4 condition may also be met where the tokenised asset's business activities are supervised by a financial market regulator, effective immediately, with the module's wording to be updated later. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]
- The SFC Guidelines for VATP operators are published under section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) and AMLO s.53ZTK. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1, Note]
- Later circulars can supersede earlier ones outright: the December 2023 joint circular replaced the January 2022 and October 2023 versions. [S:8a016a912528, para 3, closing sentence, p.1]
- The HKMA's 27 May 2026 custody letter supersedes its 20 February 2024 custody circular. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- Budget speeches announce plans, not rules: the 2026-27 Budget said a bill would be introduced in 2026 for dealing and custodian licensing regimes, and an amendment bill for CARF in the first half of 2026. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Read *should* in the HKMA AML Guideline as mandatory, not optional. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- [ ] Where CRP-1 and the BCR differ, follow the BCR; Part 12 of the BCR sets the classification principles. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- [ ] Reflect the 3 June 2026 clarification of CRP-1 para 2.6.4, which took effect immediately. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep a regulatory inventory that records each document's classification and supersession, e.g. the 2024 custody circular replaced by the 2026 letter. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] Track commencement dates separately from passage dates (e.g. AMLO amendments: passed December 2022, relevant changes from 1 June 2023). [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] The dealing conclusions encourage firms in, or planning, VA dealing to contact the SFC or HKMA (as applicable) early, e.g. to start pre-application; firms that do not will have to stop operating on commencement. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 33-34]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Tell a formally licensed VATP apart from an applicant or a deemed-to-be-licensed applicant; the SFC keeps them on separate lists (last updated 29 May 2026). [S:b14be2203d39, List of VATP applicants; Last update]

## Talking points

**To the CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)**
- Our AML Guideline says *should* marks a mandatory requirement, the same as *must*. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- Check each SPM module's classification: SB-1 is statutory; CRP-1 is a guidance note that yields to the BCR. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]

**To business heads**
- Conclusions papers are not law; the FSTB and SFC aim to put a bill for the dealing regime into LegCo in 2026, and the Budget said the same for dealing and custodian regimes. [S:6186c01c6649, p.4, Next step]; [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]
- The conclusions state no plan for a deeming arrangement and encourage early SFC/HKMA engagement. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 33-34]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong's path runs from consultation to conclusions, bill, commencement and transition; for stablecoins, consultation began on 27 December 2023 and the Ordinance commenced on 1 August 2025, about 19 months later. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- Budget items such as planned bills are stated intentions, not obligations. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108]

**To Risk / CRO (Chief Risk Officer)**
- The negative-vetting period for the cryptoasset capital rules expired without extension, and the rules apply from 1 January 2026. [S:974899bbef4d, letter, paras 1-2]
- HKMA letters can change how capital guidance applies with immediate effect, as in June 2026. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]

## Common mix-ups

- "Should in HKMA guidance is best practice." In the AML Guideline it is mandatory. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- "All SPM modules have the same force." SB-1 is statutory; CRP-1 is non-statutory and yields to the BCR. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- "The June 2026 CRP-1 letter tightened Group 1 tests." It widened how para 2.6.4 can be met. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, para 3]
- "Passage means in force." AMLO amendments passed in December 2022; the HKMA said the relevant changes would start in June 2023. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2]
- "Existing players will be grandfathered into new VA regimes." The dealing conclusions state no plan for a deeming arrangement. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]

## Read these first

1. **Guideline on AML/CFT (For Authorized Institutions), revised May 2023** — HKMA, 25 May 2023. Read Chapter 1, paras 1.1-1.8 and footnote 1. Defines how binding the text is and who it binds. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20230525-4-EN/AML-2.pdf
2. **SPM CRP-1 Classification of Cryptoassets** — HKMA, 27 Nov 2025 (V.1, effective 1 Jan 2026). Read the cover page and paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1-1.4.2. Shows how a guidance note sits under statutory rules. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20251125-15-EN/CRP-1.pdf
3. **Rules on cryptoassets standard and other updates** — HKMA, 15 Aug 2025. Read paras 1-2 of the letter. A short view of negative vetting and commencement. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/20250815-1-EN

**How to use paragraph references.** Cite HKMA SPM modules and guidelines by paragraph (e.g. CRP-1 para 2.1.2). HKMA circulars often have no numbered paragraphs, so cite heading plus page (e.g. Implementation, p.2). SFC circulars number paragraphs (e.g. joint circular para 31). BRDR landing pages (brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/…) link the PDF and related documents; SFC circulars open from apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb by reference number (e.g. 23EC66). [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]

## Open items

- No FAQ, code of practice or SFC Code of Conduct text on how SFO s.399 guidelines are weighed in proceedings is in the documents reviewed for this guide; this module does not state their legal effect beyond what the documents say.
- The CRP-1 text itself had not been updated for the June 2026 clarification as of the letter. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, para 4]
- As of their conclusions, the Government and the SFC were still to decide commencement dates for the VA dealing, custodian, advisory and management regimes. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 49]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- The CARF bill had its First and Second Readings on 3 June 2026 with the Second Reading debate adjourned; its passage is not recorded in the documents reviewed for this guide. [S:d5097f447cf9, para 2]
