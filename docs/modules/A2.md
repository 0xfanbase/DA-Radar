# A2 How Hong Kong rules are made and how to read them

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): after this module you can place any document on the law-making path, tell how binding it is, and cite it to the paragraph.

## In 60 seconds

Most Hong Kong digital-asset rules follow the same path: a consultation paper, published conclusions, a bill gazetted and passed by the Legislative Council (LegCo), a commencement notice, then a transition period. Below the statute sit the regulator's own instruments: rules (subsidiary legislation), Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) modules, guidelines, circulars, codes and FAQs. Their force differs, so check each document's own *classification* or *application* note. For a bank, the key reading rule is in the HKMA's anti-money-laundering (AML) Guideline: both *must* and *should* are mandatory. Most HKMA documents live in the Banking Regulatory Document Repository (BRDR); SFC circulars live on its e-Distribution site; FSTB papers and government press releases carry the legislative milestones.

## Key facts

- The legislative path is visible in the stablecoin regime: consultation conclusions in July 2024 said a bill would follow by end-2024. [S:92481c3c89ce, para 29]
- The Stablecoins Bill was gazetted on 6 December 2024 and read a first time in LegCo on 18 December 2024. [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 5]
- LegCo passed the bill on 21 May 2025; the Ordinance provides a six-month non-contravention period. [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]
- A separate gazetted notice set 1 August 2025 as commencement, and the notices went to LegCo for negative vetting. [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1-3]
- Subsidiary legislation such as the Banking (Capital) Amendment Rules 2025 goes through a 28-day LegCo negative-vetting period; for the cryptoasset rules it expired without extension. [S:974899bbef4d, para 1]
- AMLO amendments passed on 7 December 2022 did not take effect until 1 June 2023, giving industry time to prepare. [S:b12bd88c7aee, p.2]
- Transitions can be hard stops: pre-existing VATPs had a 12-month non-contravention window to 31 May 2024, and the SFC could not extend the 29 February 2024 deeming deadline. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 20]
- For the coming VA dealing and custody regimes, no deeming or grandfathering will be given. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 48]
- The HKMA AML Guideline is issued under AMLO s.7 and Banking Ordinance s.7(3), and compliance is enforced through both ordinances. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.1, 1.3]
- In that Guideline, both *must* and *should* denote a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- SPM modules state their own force: SB-1 is a statutory guideline under Banking Ordinance s.7(3), while CRP-1 is a non-statutory guidance note. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification]
- Where CRP-1 and the Banking (Capital) Rules differ, the Rules prevail. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- Some HKMA papers say they are non-binding: the April 2024 DLT considerations are non-binding, non-exhaustive and discussed bilaterally. [S:1884530ff235, Annex introduction, p.1]
- A circular can change a module at once: the 3 June 2026 letter loosened CRP-1 para 2.6.4 with immediate effect, pending a later text update. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]
- SFC VATP Guidelines are published under SFO s.399 and AMLO s.53ZTK. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1 note]
- Later circulars can supersede earlier ones outright: the Dec 2023 joint circular replaced the Jan 2022 and Oct 2023 versions. [S:8a016a912528, note after para 3, p.1]
- The HKMA's 2026 custody letter supersedes the February 2024 custody circular. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- Policy speeches are not rules: the 2026-27 Budget states intentions that need later bills or regulatory issuances. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Read *should* in the HKMA AML Guideline as mandatory, not optional. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- [ ] Apply the Banking (Capital) Rules Part 12 where they differ from CRP-1. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- [ ] Update CRP-1 classification practice for the 3 June 2026 clarification of para 2.6.4. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep a regulatory inventory that records each document's classification and supersession, e.g. 2024 custody circular replaced by the 2026 letter. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] Track commencement dates separately from passage dates (e.g. AMLO: passed Dec 2022, effective 1 June 2023). [S:b12bd88c7aee, p.2]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Engage the SFC or HKMA early on pre-application for the coming dealing regime; firms that do not may have to stop on commencement. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 33-34]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Distinguish a VATP's licensed status from deemed or applicant status. [S:c787f990209d, 'Reminder for investors']

## Talking points

**To the CCO**
- Our AML Guideline treats *should* as a requirement; there is no soft reading. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- Check each SPM module's classification: SB-1 is statutory, CRP-1 is a guidance note beneath the Capital Rules. [S:8934af8cc884, cover]; [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]

**To business heads**
- Conclusions papers are not law yet; the dealing and custody regimes still need a bill. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]
- When the new regimes start there is no grandfathering, so early SFC/HKMA engagement matters. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 33-34]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong's path is consultation, conclusions, bill, commencement and transition; the Stablecoins Ordinance took about 19 months from consultation to commencement. [S:92481c3c89ce, para 29]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- Budget and Policy Address items are stated intentions, not obligations. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104-109]

**To Risk / CRO**
- The cryptoasset capital rules passed negative vetting and apply from 1 January 2026. [S:974899bbef4d, paras 1-2]
- HKMA letters can amend capital guidance with immediate effect, as in June 2026. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]

## Common mix-ups

- "Should in HKMA guidance is best practice." In the AML Guideline it is mandatory. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- "All SPM modules have the same force." SB-1 is statutory; CRP-1 is non-statutory and yields to the Rules. [S:8934af8cc884, cover]; [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- "The June 2026 CRP-1 letter tightened Group 1 tests." It widened how para 2.6.4 can be met. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1]
- "Passage means in force." AMLO amendments passed in December 2022 took effect in June 2023. [S:b12bd88c7aee, p.2]
- "Existing players will be grandfathered into new VA regimes." The conclusions say no deeming arrangement. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]

## Read these first

1. **Guideline on AML/CFT (For Authorized Institutions), revised May 2023** — HKMA, 25 May 2023. Read Chapter 1, paras 1.1-1.8 and footnote 1. Defines how binding the text is and who it binds. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20230525-4-EN/AML-2.pdf
2. **SPM CRP-1 Classification of Cryptoassets** — HKMA, 27 Nov 2025 (V.1, effective 1 Jan 2026). Read the cover page and paras 1.1.4, 1.4.1-1.4.2. Shows how a guidance note sits under statutory rules. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/20251125-15-EN
3. **Rules on cryptoassets standard and other updates** — HKMA, 15 Aug 2025. Read paras 1-2. A one-page view of negative vetting and commencement. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/20250815-1-EN

**How to use paragraph references.** Cite HKMA SPM modules and guidelines by paragraph (e.g. CRP-1 para 2.1.2). HKMA circulars often have no numbered paragraphs, so cite heading plus page (e.g. Implementation, p.2). SFC circulars number paragraphs (e.g. joint circular para 31). BRDR landing pages (brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/…) link the PDF and related documents; SFC circulars open from apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb by reference number (e.g. 23EC66). [S:8a016a912528, para 31]; [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
