# A2 How Hong Kong rules are made and how to read them

This module is for the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets). After it, you can place any document on the law-making path. You can also tell how binding it is and cite it to the paragraph.

## In 60 seconds

Hong Kong's stablecoin regime shows the path a digital-asset rule can take. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1]; [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 6]; [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]; [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3] The path has five steps:

- A consultation paper lays out the proposals. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1]
- The Government publishes its conclusions on the consultation. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1]
- A bill is gazetted (published in the Government Gazette) and passed by the Legislative Council (LegCo). [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 6]; [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]
- A notice in the Gazette sets the date the law starts. [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3]
- A transition period follows. [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]

Below Ordinances (the main laws) sit other documents with different legal force. [S:974899bbef4d, letter, para 1] They include:

- rules made as subsidiary legislation (detailed law made under an Ordinance) [S:974899bbef4d, letter, para 1]
- Supervisory Policy Manual (SPM) modules, the supervisory guidance chapters of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification]
- guidelines
- circulars.

Each SPM module states its own classification on its cover, so check it. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification]

For a bank, one key reading rule sits in the HKMA's anti-money-laundering (AML) Guideline. In that Guideline, both *must* and *should* mark a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]

Where to find the documents:

- The HKMA publishes its circulars and SPM modules in its Banking Regulatory Document Repository (BRDR). [S:974899bbef4d, landing page]
- Circulars of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) open from its e-Distribution site. [S:8a016a912528, p.1]
- Conclusions of the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (FSTB) record the legislative milestones. [S:6186c01c6649, p.4, Next step]
- Government press releases record them too. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]

## Key facts

- The July 2024 stablecoin conclusions said a bill was being prepared for LegCo later that year. [S:54aa01e7396e, Way Forward, para 12.1, p.29]
- The Stablecoins Bill was gazetted on 6 December 2024. Its First Reading in LegCo was set for 18 December 2024. [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 6]
- LegCo passed the bill on 21 May 2025. The government release refers to a six-month grace period. [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]
- The SFST made a notice setting 1 August 2025 as the start date. It was gazetted on 6 June 2025. [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3, 7] The notices were due to go to LegCo on 11 June 2025 for negative vetting. [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3, 7] Under negative vetting, a notice stands unless LegCo amends or repeals it within a set period.
- The Banking (Capital), (Disclosure) and (Exposure Limits) (Amendment) Rules 2025 went through 28 days of negative vetting in LegCo. That period expired without extension. [S:974899bbef4d, letter, para 1]
- LegCo passed amendments to the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO) on 7 December 2022. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2] The HKMA said the changes relevant to banks would start on 1 June 2023. It said the gap allowed time to prepare. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2]
- Transition periods can end in a hard stop. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 20] VATPs operating before 1 June 2023 could carry on for 12 months, to 31 May 2024, without breaching the licensing rules. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 20] Only applications made by 29 February 2024 could qualify for the transitional arrangement that treated existing platforms as licensed. The SFC had no power to extend that deadline. [S:2e5c35b38cc3, paras 6, 20]
- Under current plans, the conclusions on the coming VA dealing and custodian regimes offer existing providers no arrangement that treats them as licensed while they apply. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 48]
- The HKMA issues its AML Guideline under section 7 of AMLO and section 7(3) of the Banking Ordinance. Compliance is enforced through both Ordinances. [S:7bfdad704174, paras 1.1, 1.3]
- In that Guideline, both *must* and *should* mean a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- Each SPM module states its own force on its cover. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification]
- SB-1 (the module on supervising banks' SFC-regulated activities) is a statutory guideline under section 7(3) of the Banking Ordinance. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification] CRP-1 is a non-statutory guideline, issued as a guidance note. [S:b7d630146e6c, cover, Classification]
- Where CRP-1 and the Banking (Capital) Rules (BCR) differ, the BCR take priority. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- Some HKMA papers say they are not binding. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, Introduction, 4th bullet, p.1] One example is the April 2024 paper on distributed ledger technology (DLT), such as blockchain. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, Introduction, 4th bullet, p.1] That paper is non-binding and does not cover every case. The HKMA discusses its detailed expectations with each AI one to one. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, Introduction, 4th bullet, p.1]
- A circular can change how a module applies at once. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4] The HKMA's letter of 3 June 2026 clarified the condition in CRP-1 para 2.6.4. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4] The condition may also be met where a financial market regulator supervises the business activities of the tokenised asset. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4] A tokenised asset is a traditional asset recorded as a token on a blockchain. The change took effect immediately, and the module's wording is to be updated later. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]
- The SFC issues its Guidelines for VATP operators under two laws. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1, Note] These are section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) and section 53ZTK of AMLO. [S:f1bbafccd556, para 1.1, Note]
- A later circular can replace earlier ones outright. [S:8a016a912528, para 3, closing sentence, p.1] The December 2023 joint circular replaced the January 2022 and October 2023 versions. [S:8a016a912528, para 3, closing sentence, p.1]
- The HKMA's custody letter of 27 May 2026 replaces its custody circular of 20 February 2024. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- Budget speeches announce plans, not rules. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108] The 2026-27 Budget said a bill would be introduced in 2026 for VA dealing and custodian licensing. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108] It gave the first half of 2026 as the timing for a CARF amendment bill. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108]

## What your bank must do

**Obligations**
- [ ] Treat *should* in the HKMA AML Guideline as mandatory, not optional. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- [ ] Follow the BCR where CRP-1 and the BCR differ. Part 12 of the BCR sets the classification principles. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- [ ] Apply the 3 June 2026 clarification of CRP-1 para 2.6.4. It took effect immediately. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]

**Controls & monitoring**
- [ ] Keep a register of regulatory documents. Record each document's classification and whether a later document replaced it. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2] For example, the 2026 letter replaced the 2024 custody circular. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2]
- [ ] Track start dates separately from passage dates. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2] For example, LegCo passed the AMLO amendments in December 2022. The relevant changes applied from 1 June 2023. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2]

**Notify / consult HKMA or SFC**
- [ ] Note that the dealing conclusions encourage firms that provide, or plan, VA dealing to contact the SFC or HKMA (as applicable) early. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 33-34] One example is to start pre-application (early talks before a formal licence application). [S:6186c01c6649, paras 33-34] The conclusions say dealing providers that do not make contact for pre-application will have to stop operating when the regime starts. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 33-34]

**Counterparty due diligence**
- [ ] Tell a formally licensed VATP apart from two kinds of applicant: an ordinary applicant, and an applicant that the law treats as licensed while its application is pending. [S:b14be2203d39, List of VATP applicants; Last update] The SFC keeps them on separate lists, last updated 29 May 2026. [S:b14be2203d39, List of VATP applicants; Last update]

## Talking points

**To the CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)**
- Our AML Guideline says *should* is mandatory, the same as *must*. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- Check each SPM module's classification. SB-1 is statutory; CRP-1 is a guidance note that gives way to the BCR. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]

**To business heads**
- Conclusions papers are not law. The FSTB and SFC aim to put a dealing bill into LegCo in 2026. [S:6186c01c6649, p.4, Next step]
- The Budget said the same for the dealing and custodian regimes. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 104]
- The conclusions state no plan to treat existing firms as licensed while they apply. They encourage early talks with the SFC or HKMA. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 33-34]

**To the CEO**
- Hong Kong rules move from consultation to conclusions, bill, start date and transition. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1]; [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1] For stablecoins, consultation began on 27 December 2023. [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1] The Ordinance started on 1 August 2025, about 19 months later. [S:0df2de5234dc, para 1]
- Planned bills in the Budget are stated intentions, not obligations. [S:654fa5eba8cf, paras 104, 108]

**To Risk / CRO (Chief Risk Officer)**
- LegCo's negative-vetting period for the cryptoasset capital rules expired without extension. The rules apply from 1 January 2026. [S:974899bbef4d, letter, paras 1-2]
- HKMA letters can change how capital guidance applies with immediate effect, as one did in June 2026. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, paras 3-4]

## Common mix-ups

- **Myth:** "*Should* in HKMA guidance means best practice." **Fact:** In the AML Guideline, *should* is mandatory. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
- **Myth:** "All SPM modules have the same force." **Fact:** SB-1 is statutory. CRP-1 is non-statutory and gives way to the BCR. [S:8934af8cc884, cover, Classification]; [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
- **Myth:** "The June 2026 CRP-1 letter tightened the Group 1 tests." **Fact:** It widened the ways to meet para 2.6.4. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, para 3] Group 1 is the class of cryptoassets that meets the CRP-1 classification conditions.
- **Myth:** "Once LegCo passes a law, it is in force." **Fact:** LegCo passed the AMLO amendments in December 2022. The HKMA said the relevant changes would start in June 2023. [S:b12bd88c7aee, letter, pp.1-2]
- **Myth:** "Existing firms can carry on under the new VA regimes while they apply." **Fact:** The dealing conclusions state no plan for that. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]

## Read these first

1. **Guideline on AML/CFT (For Authorized Institutions), revised May 2023** — HKMA, 25 May 2023. This is the HKMA's guideline for banks on anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism. Read Chapter 1, paras 1.1-1.8 and footnote 1. These parts set out how binding the text is and whom it binds. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20230525-4-EN/AML-2.pdf
2. **SPM CRP-1 Classification of Cryptoassets** — HKMA, 27 November 2025 (V.1, applies from 1 January 2026). Read the cover page and paras 1.1.4 and 1.4.1-1.4.2. They show how a guidance note sits under statutory rules. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20251125-15-EN/CRP-1.pdf
3. **Rules on cryptoassets standard and other updates** — HKMA, 15 August 2025. Read paras 1-2 of the letter. They give a short view of negative vetting and start dates. https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/20250815-1-EN

**How to use paragraph references.** Cite HKMA SPM modules and guidelines by paragraph, for example CRP-1 para 2.1.2. HKMA circulars often have no numbered paragraphs. Cite them by heading and page, for example Implementation, p.2. [S:ed61d7c335e7, Implementation, p.2] SFC circulars number their paragraphs, for example joint circular para 31. [S:8a016a912528, para 31] BRDR landing pages (brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/…) link to the PDF and related documents. SFC circulars open from apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb by reference number, for example 23EC66.

## Open items

- The documents reviewed for this guide do not show how SFO section 399 guidelines are weighed in proceedings. They include no FAQ, code of practice or SFC Code of Conduct text on this point. This module does not state their legal effect beyond what the documents say.
- The CRP-1 text itself had not yet been updated for the June 2026 clarification at the date of the letter. [S:dca53ff0a62c, p.1, para 4]
- When the conclusions came out, the Government and the SFC had still to decide start dates for the VA dealing, custodian, advisory and management regimes. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 49]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- LegCo's notice set the CARF bill's First and Second Readings for 3 June 2026. [S:d5097f447cf9, main text, para 1] The notice said the Second Reading debate (the debate on the bill's general principles) would be adjourned. [S:d5097f447cf9, main text, para 1] The documents reviewed for this guide do not record its passage.

## Check yourself

1. Who issues the notice that sets the Stablecoins Ordinance's start date?
   **Answer:** The Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury (SFST) made the notice. It was gazetted on 6 June 2025 and set 1 August 2025 as the start date. [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3]
2. Using the stablecoin regime as the example, what are the five steps from proposal to a rule in force?
   **Answer:** Consultation paper, Government conclusions [S:54aa01e7396e, Introduction, para 1], a gazetted bill passed by LegCo [S:a9a82f2b38ce, paras 1, 6]; [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3], a Gazette notice setting the start date [S:0df2de5234dc, paras 1, 3], then a transition period. [S:35e1ac2b815a, paras 1, 3]
3. Does the word should in the HKMA's AML/CFT Guideline mean a requirement is optional?
   **Answer:** No. In that Guideline, both must and should mark a mandatory requirement. [S:7bfdad704174, para 1.6]
4. Which text prevails when CRP-1 and the Banking (Capital) Rules (BCR) differ?
   **Answer:** Follow the BCR. Where CRP-1 and the BCR differ, the BCR take priority. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 1.1.4]
5. Do the dealing conclusions let existing VA firms be treated as licensed while they apply under the planned regime?
   **Answer:** No. The conclusions offer existing providers no such arrangement. [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 48] They encourage early contact with the SFC or HKMA, for example pre-application. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 33-34]
