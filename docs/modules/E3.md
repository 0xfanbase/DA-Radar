# E3 Glossary and timeline

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): a quick reference. Look up a term in plain English, or place any document in the 2017–2026 sequence, with the official source for each line. As of 25 Sep 2026.

## How to use this page

Common abbreviations: HKMA = Hong Kong Monetary Authority; SFC = Securities and Futures Commission; FSTB = Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau; LegCo = Legislative Council; VA = virtual asset; DLT = distributed ledger technology; CMU = Central Moneymarkets Unit; BIS = Bank for International Settlements; BISIH = BIS Innovation Hub; AML = anti-money laundering; CFT = counter-financing of terrorism; ML/TF = money laundering / terrorist financing; KYC = know your customer; STO = security token offering; MoU = memorandum of understanding; OECD = Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development; IRD = Inland Revenue Department; MVP = minimum viable product; FATF = Financial Action Task Force.

- Part A defines about 80 terms. Each points to the official document and paragraph where the idea is set out.
- Part B lists about 100 dated events from 2017 to September 2026. The events are facts about what was published or done. Targets are shown only as the source states them.
- Dates are those the source gives. Where a source gives only a month or year, so does this page.

| Registered Institution (RI) | An AI that the SFC has registered for one or more regulated activities. The HKMA is the day-to-day frontline supervisor; ultimate responsibility stays with the SFC. | [S:8934af8cc884, paras 2.1.2, 2.1.3, 2.2.1] |

| FSTB | Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau. It issued Policy Statement 2.0 and runs the VA licensing consultations jointly with the SFC. | [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 2]; [S:7395a53cc85a, para 1] |

**Virtual assets and platforms**

| Term | Plain definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual asset (VA) | The legal term, defined in s.53ZRA of the AMLO. Policy Statement 2.0 (June 2025) says the Government will now say 'digital assets' instead. | [S:8a016a912528, footnote 1]; [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] |
| Digital asset (HKMA custody usage) | Covers VAs, tokenised securities and other tokenised assets, and the keys, seeds and backups used to reach them. | [S:ed61d7c335e7, footnote 1, p.1] |
| VASP | VA service provider. The HKMA expects banks to assess each VASP customer's ML/TF risk on its own facts, with extra due diligence where needed. | [S:642680fa4467, p.4, item (b)] |
| VATP | VA trading platform. In SFC usage, a corporation licensed for Types 1 and 7 under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO), or holding an AMLO VA service licence. | [S:19d52ade6849, footnote 1] |
| Deemed-to-be-licensed | A platform that was operating before 1 June 2023, met the deeming conditions and may keep operating while its licence application is decided. It is not licensed and may never be. | [S:c787f990209d, Note 1, 'Reminder for investors', Note 2] |
| Non-contravention period | The 12-month window (1 June 2023 to 31 May 2024) in which pre-existing VATPs with a genuine Hong Kong presence could operate without breaching the AMLO licensing rule. | [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 6] |
| Associated Entity | A wholly owned Hong Kong subsidiary of a VATP that holds client assets on trust and does no other business. | [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 1.1, 10.1] |
| Cold storage | Offline key storage. The SFC expects VATPs to keep 98% of client VAs in cold storage. | [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.6(c)] |
| Compensation arrangement | An SFC-approved arrangement a VATP should hold to cover potential loss of 50% of client VAs in cold storage and 100% in hot and other storage. For banks' custody, the HKMA's May 2026 guidance instead expects liability to clients plus adequate financial resources. | [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22]; [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(n)] |
| Professional investor | Defined in the SFO (Schedule 1, Part 1, s.1). Any other person is a 'retail client'. | [S:19d52ade6849, footnote 4] |
| Complex product | A product that triggers the SFC's complex-product rules, including a suitability check even without a solicitation. VA-related products are very likely complex. | [S:8a016a912528, paras 5-6.1] |
| Joint circular | The SFC–HKMA circular on intermediaries' VA activities. The December 2023 version replaced those of January 2022 and October 2023, and was supplemented in September 2025. | [S:8a016a912528, note after para 3, p.1]; [S:b79266f3da51, para 1] |
| Staking | Locking a client's VAs in a proof-of-stake network's validation to earn rewards. | [S:b79266f3da51, para 4] |
| Shared order book | An arrangement letting an SFC-licensed VATP pool orders with an overseas affiliate platform, using fully pre-funded orders and delivery-versus-payment settlement. | [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 1, 3, 10, 13] |
| Perpetual contract | A leveraged VA derivative with no expiry. Under the SFC's February 2026 framework, licensed VATPs may propose them, and they should be offered to professional investors only. | [S:49f642bdee28, 'Scope of application', 'Eligible investors and reference assets'] |
| Funding rate | Periodic payments between long and short holders of a perpetual contract that keep its price near spot. The SFC expects settlement at least once every 24 hours. | [S:49f642bdee28, 'Scope of application', 'Trading and settlement'] |
| Travel Rule (VA transfers) | For banks, the HKMA Guideline sets customer due diligence for occasional VA transfers of HK$8,000 or more and points to AMLO Schedule 2 s.13A. RIs must also have regard to Chapter 12 of the SFC AML Guideline for VA provisions. | [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1(b)(iii), fn 15; para 1.3, fn 1] |
| Unhosted wallet | Software or hardware through which a person holds and moves VAs, keeping the private key themselves. The HKMA's May 2025 consultation proposed that licensed stablecoin issuers check whether a customer's wallet is custodial or unhosted. | [S:0d6e6d0253ff, footnote 4, paras 3.2.2-3.2.4] |
| Suspicious VATPs list | A dedicated SFC list that helps the public spot suspicious platforms doing business in Hong Kong. | [S:ea8889eb1fc4, item 2]; [S:dddd919625e9, para 3] |

**Coming VA regimes**

| Term | Plain definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| VA dealing service | Under the June 2025 proposal, dealing in VAs as a business in Hong Kong, from simple conversion to brokerage and block trades. Banks would register with the SFC, with the HKMA as frontline regulator. | [S:27d27650e820, para 1.10] |
| VA custodian service | Under the December 2025 conclusions, safekeeping for others, as a business, the private keys or similar instruments that can move VAs. Trustees or managers that only delegate custody are out of scope. | [S:f41a543388bc, paras 6-8] |
| VA advisory / VA management | Proposed regimes whose scope will match the SFO regimes for advising on securities (Type 4) and asset management (Type 9). | [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2] |
| Deeming / grandfathering | Automatic transitional licensing for incumbents. None is planned for the VA dealing or VA custodian regimes. | [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:f41a543388bc, para 48] |
| Expedited approval | A faster approval track promised for SFC-licensed VATPs, and for LCs and RIs already providing VA dealing services. | [S:6186c01c6649, paras 35-36] |
| MPC (multi-party computation) provider | A technology firm that splits control of private keys. Whether it needs a custodian licence is decided case by case, mainly on whether it can move client VAs on its own and whether clients can always reach their assets independently. | [S:f41a543388bc, para 11] |
| VA OTC regime | The February 2024 proposal for the Commissioner of Customs and Excise to license spot VA trading businesses, including shops and ATMs. The June 2025 dealing proposal revised it. | [S:efcdb0ae3849, para 2.6]; [S:27d27650e820, para 1.1] |

| e-CNY tier-4 wallet | A personal digital renminbi wallet opened with a Hong Kong mobile number, without real-name checks. Balance is capped at RMB10,000; retail payments only, no person-to-person transfers. | [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2] |
