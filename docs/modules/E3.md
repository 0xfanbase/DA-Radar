# E3 Glossary and timeline

For the bank's Head of Compliance (digital assets): a quick reference. Look up a term in plain English, or place any document in the 2017–2026 sequence, with the official source for each line. As of 25 Sep 2026.

## How to use this page

Common abbreviations: SFC = Securities and Futures Commission; LegCo = Legislative Council; BIS = Bank for International Settlements; BISIH = BIS Innovation Hub; ML/TF = money laundering / terrorist financing; KYC = know your customer; CFT = counter-financing of terrorism; STO = security token offering; MoU = memorandum of understanding; OECD = Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development; IRD = Inland Revenue Department; MVP = minimum viable product; FATF = Financial Action Task Force.

- Part A defines about 80 terms. Each points to the official document and paragraph where the idea is set out.
- Part B lists about 100 dated events from 2017 to September 2026. The events are facts about what was published or done. Targets are shown only as the source states them.
- Dates are those the source gives. Where a source gives only a month or year, so does this page.

**Institutions and legal status**

|---|---|---|

**Virtual assets and platforms**

| Term | Plain definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual asset (VA) | The legal term under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO). Since 2025 the government uses 'digital assets' in policy papers. | [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] |
| Digital asset (HKMA custody usage) | Covers VAs, tokenised securities and other tokenised assets, and the keys, seeds and backups used to reach them. | [S:ed61d7c335e7, footnote 1, p.1] |
| VASP | VA service provider. The HKMA expects banks to judge each VASP customer's risk on its own facts. | [S:2b4123d71a07, p.1] |
| VATP | VA trading platform. A corporation licensed for Types 1 and 7 under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO), or holding an AMLO VA service licence. | [S:19d52ade6849, footnote 1] |
| Deemed-to-be-licensed | A VATP applicant allowed to keep operating during transition. It is not licensed and may never be. | [S:c787f990209d, 'Reminder for investors'] |
| Non-contravention period | The 12-month window (1 June 2023 to 31 May 2024) in which existing VATPs could operate before needing a licence. | [S:2e5c35b38cc3, para 6] |
| Associated Entity | A wholly owned company of a VATP that holds client assets on trust and does no other business. | [S:f1bbafccd556, paras 1.1, 10.1] |
| Cold storage | Offline key storage. VATPs must keep at least 98% of client VAs in cold storage. | [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.6(c)] |
| Compensation arrangement | A VATP's cover for client VAs: 50% of cold-stored and 100% of hot-stored assets. | [S:f1bbafccd556, para 10.22] |
| Professional investor | Defined in section 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the SFO. Anyone else is a 'retail client'. | [S:19d52ade6849, footnote 4] |
| Complex product | A product needing extra suitability steps. VA-related products are very likely complex. | [S:8a016a912528, paras 5-6.1] |
| Joint circular | The SFC–HKMA circular on intermediaries' VA activities. The December 2023 version replaced those of January 2022 and October 2023. | [S:8a016a912528, note after para 3, p.1] |
| Staking | Locking a client's VAs in a proof-of-stake network's validation to earn rewards. | [S:b79266f3da51, para 4] |
| Shared order book | An arrangement letting a VATP pool liquidity with an overseas platform, using pre-funded, delivery-versus-payment settlement. | [S:f009f1a6d615, paras 7, 10] |
| Perpetual contract | A leveraged VA derivative with no expiry. Licensed VATPs may offer it to professional investors only. | [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'] |
| Funding rate | Periodic payments between long and short holders of a perpetual contract, settled at least every 24 hours. | [S:49f642bdee28, 'Trading and settlement'] |
| Travel Rule | The duty to collect and pass originator and recipient details with VA transfers. For banks, the HKMA Guideline points to AMLO Schedule 2 s.13A. | [S:7bfdad704174, para 4.2.1, fn 15] |
| Unhosted wallet | A wallet the holder controls directly, not held by an institution. Stablecoin issuers were asked to tell it apart from a custodial wallet. | [S:0d6e6d0253ff, paras 3.2.2-3.2.4] |
| Suspicious VATPs list | An SFC list that helps the public spot problem platforms. | [S:ea8889eb1fc4, item 2] |
