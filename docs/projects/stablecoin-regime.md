# Stablecoin issuer regime (Stablecoins Ordinance, Cap. 656)

Hong Kong's licensing law for issuers of fiat-referenced stablecoins, from the 2024 sandbox through the Ordinance, the first licences, the public register, the review tribunal and complaint handling.

## What it is

The Ordinance requires an HKMA licence to issue a fiat-referenced stablecoin (FRS) in Hong Kong. The same applies to an FRS referenced to the Hong Kong dollar, wherever it is issued. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] Licensed issuers must back coins in full with segregated reserves, and must meet the reserve value of coins in circulation at all times. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.3.1] Valid redemptions are met at par, generally within one business day. [S:240be6833157, para 3.3.3] Only listed permitted offerors may offer coins, and an Authorized Institution (AI) is one of them. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1] The HKMA licensed the first two issuers with effect from 10 April 2026. It says further licences will stay very limited. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 1]; [S:193316443831, final paragraphs]

## What it means for your bank

- As a permitted offeror, the bank may offer licensed-issuer coins to anyone. Other specified stablecoins go to professional investors only. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), paras 2-3]
- An AI can apply to issue. Overseas-incorporated AIs need no local subsidiary, and some criteria, such as financial resources, do not apply to AI applicants. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 6, 8]
- Before dealing, check that the issuer is on the HKMA register. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 5]
- Sandbox admission was never a licence or an endorsement. [S:f2a668eb3b93, Annex, Objectives, p.1]
- For its own holdings, the bank must notify the HKMA before first acquiring a new stablecoin type. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- The HKMA reviews escalated complaints about issuers but does not order compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, 'Lodging Complaint with the HKMA' section]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Retail may only get coins from licensed issuers; we are a permitted offeror. Check the register before any deal. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 2]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 5]
- **To business heads:** Two issuers hold licences effective 10 April 2026. The HKMA says the total will remain very limited. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:193316443831, final paragraphs]
- **To the CEO:** An AI may apply to issue and is spared some criteria, including the capital and business-activity limits. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, fns 1, 8]
- **To Risk:** A licensee must redeem at par, generally within one business day, and hold reserves on trust with a qualified custodian. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.5.1-2.5.4, 3.3.3]
