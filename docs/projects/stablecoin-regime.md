# Stablecoin issuer regime (Stablecoins Ordinance, Cap. 656)

Hong Kong's licensing law for issuers of fiat-referenced stablecoins, from the 2024 sandbox through the Ordinance, the first licences, the public register, the review tribunal and complaint handling.

## What it is

The Ordinance requires a business that issues a fiat-referenced stablecoin (FRS) in Hong Kong to hold an HKMA licence. The same applies to an FRS referenced to the Hong Kong dollar, wherever it is issued. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] A licensee must keep a separate reserve pool for each coin type, and that pool's market value must always be at least the par value of the coins in circulation. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1] Holders must have a right to redeem at par. Valid requests should be processed within one business day unless the HKMA approves otherwise. [S:240be6833157, paras 3.3.1, 3.3.3] Only permitted offerors may offer these coins, and an Authorized Institution (AI) is one of them. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1] The HKMA licensed the first two issuers with effect from 10 April 2026. It says that if more licences are granted, the total will remain very limited. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 1]; [S:193316443831, 'Follow-up work', para 3]

## What it means for your bank

- As a permitted offeror, the bank may offer coins from licensed issuers to retail and professional investors. Other specified stablecoins may go to professional investors only. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]
- An AI can apply to issue. An AI incorporated overseas needs no local subsidiary, and some minimum criteria, such as financial resources, do not apply to AI applicants. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 8]
- The HKMA keeps a public register of licensees and tells the public to use it to check claims about them. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]
- Sandbox admission did not mean HKMA endorsement, and participants had to apply separately for a licence. [S:f2a668eb3b93, Annex, Objectives, p.1]
- For its own holdings, the bank must notify the HKMA when it acquires a new type of cryptoasset, and treat it as Group 2b until the HKMA agrees its classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- The HKMA may assess complaints about an issuer that the issuer has not handled properly. It generally does not order an issuer to pay compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, 'Lodging Complaint with the HKMA' section]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Only coins from licensed issuers may be offered to retail investors, and AIs are permitted offerors. The HKMA register lists who is licensed. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]
- **To business heads:** Two issuers hold licences effective 10 April 2026. The HKMA says any further licences will stay very limited in number. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table]; [S:193316443831, 'Follow-up work', para 3]
- **To the CEO:** An AI may apply to issue, and the financial-resources and business-activities criteria do not apply to it. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.2.1; fns 1, 8]
- **To Risk:** A licensee must give holders a right to redeem at par, and should process valid requests within one business day. Reserves should be held under trust arrangements with qualified custodians. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.5.2, 2.5.4, 3.3.1, 3.3.3]
