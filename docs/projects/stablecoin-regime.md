# Stablecoin issuer regime (Stablecoins Ordinance, Cap. 656)

The Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656, its chapter number in Hong Kong's laws) is Hong Kong's licensing law for firms that issue fiat-referenced stablecoins. These are digital tokens meant to keep a steady value against an official currency, such as the Hong Kong dollar. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) grants the licences. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] This page traces the regime from the 2024 sandbox (a supervised test run) to the first licences and the first coin launch. It also covers the public register, the review tribunal and complaint handling. Why it matters to your bank: authorized institutions (AIs) may offer licensed coins to customers. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1] AI is the legal term for banks and deposit-taking companies authorized by the HKMA. An AI can also apply to issue its own coins. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 8]

## What it is

The Ordinance requires any business that issues a fiat-referenced stablecoin (FRS) in Hong Kong to hold an HKMA licence. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2] The same applies to an FRS linked to the Hong Kong dollar, wherever it is issued. [S:ad1082eb2ea9, para 2]

A licensee must keep a separate reserve pool (the assets that back the coins) for each type of coin. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1] Each pool's market value must always be at least the par value (face value) of the coins in circulation. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1]

Holders must have a right to redeem (cash in) their coins at par. [S:240be6833157, paras 3.3.1, 3.3.3] The issuer should process valid requests within one business day, unless the HKMA approves otherwise. [S:240be6833157, paras 3.3.1, 3.3.3]

Only permitted offerors may offer these coins. An AI is one of them. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 2.10.1]

The HKMA licensed the first two issuers with effect from 10 April 2026. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 1] It says that if it grants more licences, the total will stay very limited. [S:193316443831, 'Follow-up work', para 3]

On 16 September 2026 a Government spokesman said the first regulated stablecoin in Hong Kong had launched in August 2026. [S:8be6d15c6641, para 10] The official sources reviewed do not name the issuer.

> **Commercial and customer benefits**
> **For customers:**
> - Issuers must give holders a right to redeem at par (face value), without an unreasonable fee. [S:240be6833157, paras 3.3.1, 3.3.3]
> - Each coin type needs its own reserve pool, always worth at least the par value of coins in circulation. [S:240be6833157, paras 2.2.1, 2.5.1]
> - The HKMA says both licensees plan efficient, transparent and cost-effective cross-border payments, and fast local payments. [S:193316443831, 'Use cases and strategic priorities' section]
> **For the bank:**
> - A licensed bank can be a custodian of reserve assets. [S:240be6833157, para 2.5.4] Reserves may include bank deposits of up to three months. [S:240be6833157, para 2.3.1]
> - A bank holding reserve deposits earns a margin on them (concept).
> - An AI may offer licensed coins to retail and professional investors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]
> - An AI may apply to issue coins, and some minimum criteria do not apply to it. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 8]
> **Evidence so far:**
> - The HKMA received 36 first-batch applications and licensed two issuers from 10 April 2026. [S:193316443831, 'Assessment of licence applications', paras 1-2]
> - On 28 April 2026 both licensees confirmed they had not yet issued any regulated stablecoin. [S:275e7319a841, para 3]
> - On 16 September 2026 a Government spokesman said the first regulated stablecoin in Hong Kong had launched in August 2026. [S:8be6d15c6641, para 10] The official sources reviewed do not name the issuer. [S:8be6d15c6641, para 10]
> - A Bank for International Settlements 2026 report (global, reported estimate) put the market value of all stablecoins at about US$320 billion at end-May 2026. [I:efa37f6d77e9, p. 88]
> **Limits:**
> - One coin has launched, a Government spokesman said in September 2026. [S:8be6d15c6641, para 10] The payment benefits above are still the issuers' plans, not reported results. [S:193316443831, 'Use cases and strategic priorities' section]
> - Issuers must not pay interest to holders. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.13.1]

## What it means for your bank

- As a permitted offeror, the bank may offer coins from licensed issuers to retail and professional investors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1] Other specified stablecoins (a class defined in the law) may go to professional investors only. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1]
- An AI can apply to issue coins. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 8] An AI incorporated overseas needs no local subsidiary. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 8] Some minimum criteria, such as financial resources, do not apply to AI applicants. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, paras 3.2.1-3.2.3; fns 1, 8]
- The HKMA keeps a public register of licensees. It tells the public to use the register to check claims about licensees. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]
- A place in the sandbox did not mean the HKMA endorsed the firm. Sandbox firms had to apply separately for a licence. [S:f2a668eb3b93, Annex, Objectives, p.1]
- For its own holdings, the bank must notify the HKMA when it acquires a new type of cryptoasset. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2] The bank must treat the asset as Group 2b (the crypto group with the strictest bank capital treatment) until the HKMA agrees its classification. [S:b7d630146e6c, para 2.1.2]
- The HKMA may assess complaints about an issuer that the issuer has not handled properly. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, 'Lodging Complaint with the HKMA' section] The HKMA generally does not order an issuer to pay compensation. [S:d3e75db3ddb3, 'Lodging Complaint with the HKMA' section]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Only coins from licensed issuers may be offered to retail investors. AIs are permitted offerors. [S:7b6c8dc63140, reply (1), para 1] The HKMA register lists who is licensed. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, paras 4-5]
- **To business heads:** Two issuers hold licences that took effect on 10 April 2026. [S:270f1f7324ab, Register table] The HKMA says any further licences will stay very limited in number. [S:193316443831, 'Follow-up work', para 3]
- **To the CEO:** An AI may apply to issue coins. The financial-resources and business-activities criteria do not apply to it. [S:fe4baea8dd7d, para 3.2.1; fns 1, 8]
- **To Risk:** A licensee must give holders a right to redeem at par. It should process valid requests within one business day. Reserves should be held under trust arrangements with qualified custodians (firms that safekeep assets). [S:240be6833157, paras 2.5.2, 2.5.4, 3.3.1, 3.3.3]
