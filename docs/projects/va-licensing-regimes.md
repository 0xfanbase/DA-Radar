# New VA licensing regimes: dealing, custody, advisory and management

Four planned statutory licensing regimes for virtual-asset (VA) intermediaries, to be brought in by one bill under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO, Cap. 615).

## What it is

The regimes will replace today's approach of attaching VA terms and conditions to SFC licences and registrations under the joint circular. The joint circular's current requirements become the baseline. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62] VA dealing will align with Type 1 (dealing in securities). Dealers must keep client VAs with SFC-regulated VA custodians. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3-5, 26] A custodian licence attaches to whoever safekeeps private keys and can move client VAs. Custody of tokenised securities falls outside it. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 6-8, 12] VA advisory will mirror Type 4 and VA management Type 9. Banks may register for these rather than be licensed. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10] An earlier plan for a Customs-run licence for VA-for-cash trading was folded into the SFC dealing regime. [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.9-1.10]

## What it means for your bank

- A bank will register with the SFC for these activities; the HKMA stays its frontline regulator. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]
- No deeming or grandfathering is planned. Firms already vetted by the SFC or HKMA get an expedited track. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]
- A bank VA custodian would use existing HKMA capital rules, not the custodian capital baseline. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 41-42]
- VA management has no de minimis threshold. Any discretionary VA exposure needs a licence or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 32]
- Advice on a mixed VA and securities portfolio still needs the VA advisory permission for the VA part. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 21]
- Once the regimes apply, no one may actively market these services to the Hong Kong public without SFC licensing or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 70]
- Early pre-application talks with the SFC, or the HKMA as applicable, are encouraged. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 6]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** All four regimes have conclusions; one bill is targeted for 2026 and no grandfathering is planned. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]
- **To business heads:** Inventory every VA dealing, custody, advisory and management line now; even small discretionary VA exposure will need registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 32]
- **To the CEO:** Commencement dates are not set; the Government and SFC will weigh how long the market needs to adjust. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]
- **To Risk:** Under the dealing regime, client VAs must sit with SFC-regulated custodians only. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]
