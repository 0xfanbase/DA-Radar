# New VA licensing regimes: dealing, custody, advisory and management

VA stands for virtual asset, such as a crypto token. Hong Kong has four planned licensing regimes for firms that provide dealing, custody, advisory or management services in virtual assets (VAs). One bill under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615) is to bring in all four. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 3] This matters to banks because a bank offering VA dealing or custody would register with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is set to supervise the bank day to day. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]

## What it is

Today the SFC adds VA terms and conditions to the licences and registrations of firms it already oversees. The dealing, advisory and management regimes will replace that practice. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62] The current HKMA–SFC joint circular (a letter both regulators issued together) sets the requirements. Those requirements will become the starting point for these three regimes. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62]

The four regimes cover different services:

- **VA dealing** will line up with Type 1 (dealing in securities). [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3, 26] The SFC will require dealers to keep client VAs with VA custodians the SFC regulates. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3, 26]
- **VA custody** means safekeeping VAs for others. The custodian licence will cover firms that keep private keys for others. Private keys are the secret codes that let someone move a VA. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 6-7, 12] The licence will also cover firms that keep other tools that let someone transfer VAs for others. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 6-7, 12] Custody of tokenised securities alone falls outside the licence. Tokenised securities are securities recorded as digital tokens. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 6-7, 12]
- **VA advisory** will line up with Type 4 (advising on securities). **VA management** will line up with Type 9 (asset management). [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]
- **Banks only:** the further consultation said only banks could register with the SFC for advisory and management, rather than be licensed. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]

An earlier proposal had the Commissioner of Customs and Excise license VA OTC spot trading. OTC means deals agreed directly between two parties, not on an exchange. The SFC dealing proposal replaced that approach. [S:efcdb0ae3849, para 2.6]; [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.9-1.10]

## What it means for your bank

- For VA dealing and custody, a bank would register with the SFC. The HKMA is set to be its frontline regulator, meaning the HKMA supervises the bank day to day. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]
- None of the four regimes is planned to let existing firms carry on while they apply. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 63, 67]
- Firms the SFC or HKMA already oversee for these services will get a faster approval process. That group includes registered institutions, which are banks registered with the SFC. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 63, 67]
- Banks are exempt from the custodians' rule on minimum financial resources. The reason is that HKMA capital requirements already apply to banks. [S:f41a543388bc, para 41]
- VA management will have no small-amount exception. It covers any business of managing a VA portfolio for others where the manager makes the investment decisions. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32-33]
- Advice on a portfolio that mixes VAs and securities will still need a VA advisory licence or registration for the VA part. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 21]
- Once the regimes apply, no one may actively market these services to the Hong Kong public without an SFC licence or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 70]
- The FSTB and SFC encourage firms to talk early with the SFC, or the HKMA where relevant, before applying. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 65]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 7]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** All four regimes now have conclusions, and one bill is targeted for 2026. There are no plans to let existing firms carry on while they apply. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 63]
- **To business heads:** Each of the four activities will need a licence or registration once its regime starts. VA management has no small-amount exception. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 63]
- **To the CEO:** Start dates are not set yet. The Government and SFC will weigh how long the market needs to adjust. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- **To Risk:** Under the dealing regime, the SFC will require client VAs to be held with custodians the SFC regulates. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]
