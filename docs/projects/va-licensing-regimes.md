# New VA licensing regimes: dealing, custody, advisory and management

Four planned statutory licensing regimes for virtual-asset (VA) intermediaries, to be brought in by one bill under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO, Cap. 615). [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 3]

## What it is

The dealing, advisory and management regimes will replace today's practice of imposing VA terms and conditions on SFC licensees and registrants. The current requirements in the HKMA–SFC joint circular will become their baseline. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 62] VA dealing will align with Type 1 (dealing in securities). The SFC will require dealers to keep client VAs with SFC-regulated VA custodians. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 3, 26] The custodian licence will cover firms that safekeep private keys or other instruments that enable transfer of VAs for others. Custody of tokenised securities alone falls outside it. [S:f41a543388bc, paras 6-7, 12] VA advisory will align with Type 4 and VA management with Type 9. The further consultation said only banks could register with the SFC, rather than be licensed, for these two services. [S:4a0342268bf2, para 2]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10] An earlier plan for a licence from the Commissioner of Customs and Excise for VA OTC spot trading was replaced by the SFC dealing proposal. [S:efcdb0ae3849, para 2.6]; [S:27d27650e820, paras 1.1, 1.9-1.10]

## What it means for your bank

- For VA dealing and custody, a bank would register with the SFC, with the HKMA as its frontline regulator. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]
- No deeming arrangement is planned for any of the four regimes. Firms the SFC or HKMA already oversees for these services, including registered institutions (RIs), will get an expedited approval process. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 31, 35-36]; [S:f41a543388bc, paras 48, 51]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 63, 67]
- Banks are carved out of the custodian financial-resources baseline because they are already subject to HKMA capital requirements. [S:f41a543388bc, para 41]
- VA management will have no de minimis threshold. It covers any business of managing a VA portfolio for others with discretion. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32-33]
- Advice on a mixed VA and securities portfolio will still need VA advisory licensing or registration for the VA part. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 21]
- Once the regimes apply, no one may actively market these services to the Hong Kong public without SFC licensing or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 70]
- The FSTB and SFC encourage early pre-application talks with the SFC, or the HKMA as applicable. [S:6186c01c6649, para 33]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 65]; [S:4a0342268bf2, para 7]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** All four regimes have conclusions; one bill is targeted for 2026 and no deeming arrangement is planned. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]; [S:6186c01c6649, para 31]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 63]
- **To business heads:** Each of the four activities will need a licence or registration once its regime starts, and VA management has no de minimis threshold. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 32, 63]
- **To the CEO:** Commencement dates are not set; the Government and SFC will weigh how long the market needs to adjust. [S:6186c01c6649, para 32]; [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- **To Risk:** Under the dealing regime, the SFC will require client VAs to be held with SFC-regulated custodians. [S:6186c01c6649, para 26]
