# CMU OmniClear digital asset platform and tokenised Exchange Fund Bills

CMU OmniClear (the company that runs Hong Kong's central bond settlement system) plans a digital asset platform. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] That system is the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU), Hong Kong's central securities depository. [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 2] A central securities depository is where bonds are held and settled. Separately, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) intends to test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] "Tokenised" means recorded as digital tokens on a shared ledger. Exchange Fund Bills are short-term debt issued by the HKMA. Why banks care: the Policy Address links the bill tests to how banks manage their assets and liabilities. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)] The platform is meant to be a one-stop service for issuing and settling digital bonds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(iv)]

## What it is

The HKMA set up CMU OmniClear to run the CMU under a contract with the HKMA. [S:d43a14fd959f, paras 1-2] At set-up, the Exchange Fund (Hong Kong's official reserves) wholly owned the company. [S:d43a14fd959f, paras 1-2] The HKMA stays the owner and system operator of the CMU. The HKMA also holds the legal relationships with CMU participants (the firms that use the CMU) and makes CMU policy decisions. [S:d43a14fd959f, para 3]; [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 6]

Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing (HKEX) is taking a 20% stake in the holding company, CMU OmniClear Holdings. The Exchange Fund keeps 80%. [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 2]

The 2026-27 Budget said CMU OmniClear would set up a digital asset platform in 2026, for issuing and settling digital bonds. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114] The Budget said the platform would later cover other digital assets and link to other tokenisation platforms in the region. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114]

The 2026 Policy Address repeats the platform target. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] It also says the HKMA will test tokenising Exchange Fund Bills by year-end. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] The purpose is to let banks use more than HK$1.3 trillion of these bills at any hour. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)]

The Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation (HKMC) issued digital bonds in June 2026. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 4] The bonds were created on a distributed ledger technology (DLT) platform run by the CMU. DLT is a shared digital record kept in step across many computers. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 4] The CMU acted as the clearing and settlement system for these bonds. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 4]

## What it means for your bank

- Nothing is binding yet. Both items are stated targets. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] The sources reviewed show no platform rulebook and no test design. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)]
- The Policy Address links the Exchange Fund Bills tests to how banks manage their assets and liabilities. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- The Digital Bond Grant Scheme is an HKMA subsidy for digital bond issues. Issuing on a DLT platform run by the CMU meets one of its two alternative Basic Requirements. [S:5a0563de8142, s.2 and fn 6, p.3] The HKMA decides case by case whether a platform counts. [S:5a0563de8142, s.2 and fn 6, p.3]
- The law treats the CMU as a designated clearing and settlement system. So its settlements have statutory finality. [S:a0b44be27c40, paras 36-38, pp.14-15] Another DLT platform gets statutory finality only if the HKMA designates it. Otherwise, the parties can agree on finality in their contract. [S:a0b44be27c40, paras 36-38, pp.14-15]
- The HKMA's February 2024 circular on tokenised products does not cover products regulated under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (the main securities law). [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Coverage' and fn 2, p.1] Those products follow the relevant Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and HKMA requirements. [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Coverage' and fn 2, p.1]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** No rules are out yet for the platform or the Exchange Fund Bills tests. Both are stated targets. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)]
- **To business heads:** The platform is meant to be a one-stop service for issuing and settling digital bonds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(iv)]
- **To the CEO:** The HKMA plans tests of tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by end-2026. This is to help banks use more than HK$1.3 trillion of these bills round the clock. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- **To Risk:** A DLT platform has statutory finality only if the HKMA designates it. Otherwise, finality rests on contract. [S:a0b44be27c40, paras 37-38, p.15]
