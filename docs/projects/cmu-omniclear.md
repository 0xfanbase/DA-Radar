# CMU OmniClear digital asset platform and tokenised Exchange Fund Bills

Plans to add a digital-asset platform to Hong Kong's bond settlement system, the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU), and to test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|

| Status as of 25 Sep 2026 | Stated target [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] |
| Started | CMU OmniClear set up 18 Oct 2024 [S:d43a14fd959f, paras 1-2] |
| Next official date | Platform "this year" (2026); Exchange Fund Bills tests by end-2026 (both stated targets) [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] |
| Covered in | C5, A3, E1, E3 |

## What it is

CMU OmniClear is owned through the Exchange Fund and runs the CMU under contract to the HKMA. [S:d43a14fd959f, paras 1-2] The HKMA keeps ownership of the CMU system, its legal ties with members and its policy decisions. [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 4, p.1] Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing (HKEX) is taking a 20% stake in the holding company; the Exchange Fund keeps 80%. [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 2, p.1] The 2026-27 Budget said CMU OmniClear would set up a digital asset platform in 2026 for digital bond issuance and settlement. It would later cover other digital assets and link to regional tokenisation platforms. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114] The 2026 Policy Address repeats the platform target. It also says the HKMA will test tokenised Exchange Fund Bills by year-end, covering more than $1.3 trillion of bills, so banks can use them round the clock. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)] The CMU already runs a DLT platform: the HKMC's June 2026 digital bonds were created and settled on it. [S:6aa27ee8b400, para 4]

## What it means for your bank

- Nothing binding yet. Both items are stated targets, and the corpus holds no platform rulebook or test design. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)]
- The Exchange Fund Bills tests are aimed at banks' round-the-clock asset and liability management, so treasury and liquidity teams are the likely users. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- A bond issued on a CMU-operated DLT platform meets one of the basic conditions of the Digital Bond Grant Scheme. [S:5a0563de8142, s.2, p.3]
- CMU settlement carries statutory settlement finality. A DLT platform gets that only if the HKMA designates it; otherwise finality must be agreed by contract. [S:b7e1bb6b44ae, report paras 37-38]
- For customer-facing tokenised bond products, the HKMA tokenised-products circular and prior HKMA discussion still apply. [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Implementation', p.6]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** No new rules yet; watch for CMU platform terms and any HKMA note on the Exchange Fund Bills tests. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii), (iv)]
- **To business heads:** The platform is meant as a one-stop service for digital bond issuance and settlement. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(iv)]
- **To the CEO:** The HKMA plans to test tokenising more than $1.3 trillion of Exchange Fund Bills by end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35(ii)]
- **To Risk:** Check whether any DLT settlement venue has designated status before relying on statutory finality. [S:b7e1bb6b44ae, report paras 37-38]
