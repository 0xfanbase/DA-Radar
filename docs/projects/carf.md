# Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) and amended CRS

Hong Kong's bill to bring in the OECD's yearly crypto-asset tax reporting standard and to update the Common Reporting Standard (CRS) that banks already follow.

## What it is

CARF is a standard of the OECD (Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development) for yearly exchange of crypto-asset tax data between tax authorities. Hong Kong said it would legislate by 2026 and start exchanges from 2028. [S:ea81f92ea327, paras 1, 4] The bill defines a Reporting Crypto-Asset Service Provider (RCASP) as a business that carries out crypto exchange transactions for customers. That includes acting as counterparty, broker or trading venue. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M "reporting crypto-asset service provider"] A Hong Kong RCASP would register, run due diligence, file returns and keep records. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, ss.50N-50O] Central bank digital currencies and specified e-money products are outside CARF but join the amended CRS. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 1.5, 4.3] If passed, CARF starts on 1 January 2027 and amended CRS on 1 January 2028. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(2)-(4)]

## What it means for your bank

- Map whether any group entity, as a business, carries out crypto exchange transactions for customers, including as an intermediary. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M]
- Test each such entity, including branches, against the Hong Kong nexus rules. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50N(1)-(2)]
- A Hong Kong RCASP must register by 31 January of the year after it first meets a nexus test. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50O(1)-(2)]
- New customers need tax-residence self-certifications; existing users within 12 months of start. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Due diligence requirements']
- Under amended CRS, crypto-linked derivatives become financial assets and CBDCs and specified e-money join the depository-account concept. [S:275d5cc93a88, paras 4.3-4.5]
- Returns and registration go to the IRD, not the HKMA or SFC. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, 'Reporting portal and mandatory registration requirement']
- Using a service provider is not, on its own, a reasonable excuse for a failure. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(2)]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** The bill is not yet law. If passed, CARF duties start 1 January 2027 and amended CRS 1 January 2028. [S:d70ae2cbc9d1, Overview]; [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, Clause 1(3)-(4)]
- **To business heads:** Any customer crypto exchange service, even as intermediary, can make the entity an RCASP. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.50M]
- **To the CEO:** Hong Kong committed to CARF in December 2024, with first data exchanges targeted from 2028. [S:ea81f92ea327, para 4]
- **To Risk:** Fines can scale: $500 a day for continuing failures, or $1,000 per affected user for due-diligence breaches. [S:e8c0a71f0cc5, s.80R(4)-(5)]
