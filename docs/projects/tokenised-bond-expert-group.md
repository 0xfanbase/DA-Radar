# Tokenised Bond Expert Group and the DLT fixed-income law review

An HKMA industry panel on tokenised bonds, working alongside a joint FSTB–HKMA review of whether Hong Kong law fits bonds held on a distributed ledger (DLT).

## What it is

Policy Statement 2.0 said the FSTB and HKMA would review the legal and regulatory position on tokenisation, starting with bonds. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 9] The 2026-27 Budget said the Government would issue guidance confirming that debenture-holder registers may be kept on a DLT. It also said it would explore e-signatures and digitised bearer bonds. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 107] The HKMA convened the TBEG in June 2026. Its members come from industry bodies, financial institutions, law firms and infrastructure and technology providers. [S:fc3eb72031c6, paras 1-2] The group's May 2026 discussions fed into the joint review. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 3] Phase 1 concluded that current law is already flexible enough for tokenised bond issues. [S:279f10054c6e, opening paragraph] On the same day, the Companies Registry published FAQs. They confirm that a DLT-based register of debenture holders can meet Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) record-keeping rules. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Issuing clarifications on DLT record keeping' section]

## What it means for your bank

- No new obligation arises. Phase 1 found the existing framework adequate, so current securities and company rules keep applying to tokenised bonds. [S:279f10054c6e, opening paragraph]
- If the bank keeps or relies on a DLT debenture register for a Hong Kong issuer, the Companies Registry FAQs are the reference point. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Issuing clarifications on DLT record keeping' section]
- Phase 2 will look at electronic signatures for trusts linked to tokenised bonds and funds. It will also look at how possession and transfer work for tokenised fixed-income instruments. Trustee and custody teams should track it. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Next phase' section]
- The HKMA says it will review and update the group's membership when needed. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 4]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Phase 1 changed no law; it confirmed flexibility and added Companies Registry FAQs on DLT registers. [S:279f10054c6e, opening paragraph and 'Issuing clarifications on DLT record keeping' section]
- **To business heads:** Phase 2 targets e-execution of issue documents, which could speed tokenised bond launches. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Next phase' section]
- **To the CEO:** The TBEG is the HKMA's industry channel on tokenised bond policy and practice. [S:fc3eb72031c6, paras 1-2]
- **To Risk:** Possession and transfer of tokenised instruments remain under legal review; findings are not yet published. [S:279f10054c6e, 'Next phase' section]
