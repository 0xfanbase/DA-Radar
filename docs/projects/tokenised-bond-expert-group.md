# Tokenised Bond Expert Group and the DLT fixed-income law review

An HKMA industry panel on tokenised bonds, working alongside a joint FSTB–HKMA review of whether Hong Kong's legal and regulatory regime supports using distributed ledger technology (DLT) in the bond market. [S:fc3eb72031c6, paras 1, 3]; [S:279f10054c6e, para 1]

## What it is

Policy Statement 2.0 said the FSTB and HKMA would lead a legal and regulatory review on tokenisation, with an initial focus on bonds. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 9] The 2026-27 Budget said the Government would issue guidance confirming that registers of debenture holders may be kept on a distributed ledger. It also said it would explore e-signatures for bond documents and digitised bearer bonds. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 107] The HKMA announced the TBEG on 5 June 2026. Its members come from industry bodies, financial institutions, law firms and infrastructure and technology providers. [S:fc3eb72031c6, paras 1-2] The group's May 2026 discussions fed into the joint review. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 3] Phase 1 concluded that current law and regulation are already flexible enough for tokenised bond issues. [S:279f10054c6e, para 1] On the same day, the Companies Registry published FAQs. They confirm that a DLT-based register of debenture holders can meet Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) record-keeping rules. [S:279f10054c6e, para 6]

## What it means for your bank

- Phase 1 created no new obligation. It found the current legal and regulatory environment already flexible enough for tokenised bond issues. [S:279f10054c6e, paras 1, 3]
- If the bank keeps or relies on a DLT debenture register for a Hong Kong company, the Companies Registry FAQs are the reference point. [S:279f10054c6e, para 6]
- Phase 2 will look at electronic signatures for trusts linked to tokenised bonds and funds. It will also look at how possession and transfer work for tokenised fixed-income instruments. [S:279f10054c6e, paras 9-11]
- The HKMA says it will review and update the group's membership when needed. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 4]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Phase 1 changed no law; it confirmed flexibility and added Companies Registry FAQs on DLT registers. [S:279f10054c6e, paras 1, 6]
- **To business heads:** Phase 2 will explore electronic execution of tokenised bond issue documents, aimed at a more efficient and automated issuance process. [S:279f10054c6e, para 10]
- **To the CEO:** The HKMA set up the TBEG to explore policy measures, market practices and innovations with industry. [S:fc3eb72031c6, paras 1-2]
- **To Risk:** Possession and transfer of tokenised instruments are topics for the phase 2 review, set for H2 2026. [S:279f10054c6e, paras 8, 11]
