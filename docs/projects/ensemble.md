# Project Ensemble

Project Ensemble is a Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) project on wholesale central bank digital currency. It supports settling tokenised money (money held as digital tokens) between banks. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 1] It matters to your bank because it is now a live pilot with real-value deals (EnsembleTX). [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1] The HKMA invites parties with real-value uses for tokenised deposits to take part. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 5]

## What it is

Ensemble explores new market infrastructure, meaning the shared systems that banks use to settle deals. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] It tests how banks can settle tokenised money with each other using wholesale CBDC (wCBDC). [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2; S:460620ab7f2b, footnote 1] Wholesale CBDC is digital central bank money that only financial institutions use. Ensemble's first focus is tokenised deposits. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] These are digital versions of ordinary commercial bank deposits.

The HKMA set up the Architecture Community in May 2024 to develop shared industry standards. [S:0169bc8fbb4d, paras 1-2] A Sandbox (a test environment) followed in August 2024. [S:460620ab7f2b, paras 1-2] Banks in the Architecture Community connected their tokenised-deposit platforms to it. [S:460620ab7f2b, paras 1-2] They were then ready to run two kinds of interbank test. [S:460620ab7f2b, paras 1-2]

- Payment versus payment (PvP): both sides of a currency deal pay at the same moment.
- Delivery versus payment (DvP): an asset and the payment for it change hands at the same moment.

In November 2025 the project moved to EnsembleTX, a pilot that uses real value. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-2] It starts with trades in tokenised money market funds and with real-time treasury and liquidity uses. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-2] At first, banks settle with each other through the Hong Kong dollar real-time gross settlement system. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3] That system settles each interbank payment in full, one by one, as it happens. The HKMA plans a gradual move to round-the-clock settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]

## What it means for your bank

- The HKMA lists seven Tokenised Deposit Banks. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A, p.1] These banks are going to offer tokenised deposits to Hong Kong customers. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A, p.1] They will also settle interbank tokenised-deposit deals through EnsembleTX with real value. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A, p.1] The HKMA invites others with real-value uses for tokenised deposits to take part. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 5]
- When customers place tokenised deposits, the HKMA says banks should also meet its circular on tokenised products. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1, p.1; sections (A)-(C), pp.3-5; 'Implementation', p.6] That circular expects due diligence, disclosure, risk controls and early discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1, p.1; sections (A)-(C), pp.3-5; 'Implementation', p.6]
- The liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) tests whether a bank holds enough cash-like assets to get through a short period of stress. Under the LCR, a bank may treat qualifying tokenised deposits as deposits or unsecured funding. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4, pp.89-90] "Qualifying" means a legally binding claim on the bank that can be cashed in at full face value. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4, pp.89-90] These deposits cannot count as stable retail deposits. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4, pp.89-90]
- The HKMA has a non-binding note on distributed ledger technology (shared digital record-keeping). It sets out what the HKMA would expect in four areas. [S:1884530ff235, Introduction, 4th bullet; items 2-4 and 8] These are network choice, smart contracts, private-key security, and legal risks such as settlement finality. [S:1884530ff235, Introduction, 4th bullet; items 2-4 and 8] Smart contracts are programs that run by themselves on the ledger. Settlement finality is the point at which a payment becomes final and irreversible.
- Before a full launch, a bank can use the Supervisory Incubator to check its risk controls with a dedicated HKMA team. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3] Tokenised deposits are a core focus of the Incubator. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** EnsembleTX is a pilot, not a rulebook. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1] Customer tokenised deposits still fall under the HKMA's tokenised-products circular, which expects early discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1; 'Implementation', p.6]
- **To business heads:** The pilot starts with tokenised money market funds and real-time treasury uses. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 2 and 5] The HKMA invites proposals for new real-value uses of tokenised deposits. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 2 and 5]
- **To the CEO:** The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA plans 24/7 operations and CBDC settlement for EnsembleTX by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]
- **To Risk:** Tokenised deposits cannot get the LCR treatment for stable retail deposits. If holders cannot always be identified, they count as other unsecured wholesale funding. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4, p.89]
