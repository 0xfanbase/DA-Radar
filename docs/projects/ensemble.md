# Project Ensemble

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority's (HKMA's) wholesale central bank digital currency (wCBDC) project for settling tokenised money between banks, now in a real-value pilot called EnsembleTX. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 1]; [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1]

## What it is

Ensemble explores new market infrastructure so that banks can settle tokenised money with each other through wCBDC. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2]; [S:460620ab7f2b, footnote 1] Its first focus is tokenised deposits, meaning digital versions of ordinary commercial bank deposits. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] The HKMA set up an Architecture Community in May 2024 to develop shared industry standards. [S:0169bc8fbb4d, paras 1-2] A Sandbox followed in August 2024. Community banks connected their tokenised-deposit platforms to it, ready to run interbank PvP (payment versus payment) and DvP (delivery versus payment) experiments. [S:460620ab7f2b, paras 1-2] In November 2025 the project moved to EnsembleTX, a pilot using real value. It starts with tokenised money market fund trades and real-time treasury and liquidity uses. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-2] At first, interbank settlement runs through the HKD RTGS (real-time gross settlement) system. The HKMA plans a gradual upgrade to round-the-clock settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]

## What it means for your bank

- The HKMA lists seven Tokenised Deposit Banks. It describes them as banks that are going to offer tokenised deposits to Hong Kong customers and settle interbank tokenised-deposit deals through EnsembleTX with real value. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A, p.1] The HKMA invites others with real-value use cases for tokenised deposits to take part. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 5]
- When customers place tokenised deposits, the HKMA says banks should also meet its tokenised-products circular. That circular expects due diligence, disclosure, risk controls and discussion with the HKMA in advance. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1, p.1; sections (A)-(C), pp.3-5; 'Implementation', p.6]
- For the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), a bank may treat qualifying tokenised deposits (a legally binding claim on it, redeemable at par) as deposits or unsecured funding. They cannot count as stable retail deposits. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4, pp.89-90]
- The HKMA's non-binding note on distributed ledger technology (DLT) sets out what it would expect on network choice, smart contracts, legal risks such as settlement finality, and private-key security. [S:1884530ff235, Introduction, 4th bullet; items 2-4 and 8]
- Before a full launch, the Supervisory Incubator lets a bank check its risk controls with a dedicated HKMA team; tokenised deposits are a core focus. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** EnsembleTX is a pilot, not a rulebook. Customer tokenised deposits still fall under the HKMA tokenised-products circular, which expects discussion with the HKMA in advance. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 1]; [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1; 'Implementation', p.6]
- **To business heads:** The pilot starts with tokenised money market funds and real-time treasury uses, and the HKMA invites new real-value use cases for tokenised deposits. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 2 and 5]
- **To the CEO:** The 2026 Policy Address says the HKMA plans to bring 24/7 operations and CBDC settlement to EnsembleTX by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]
- **To Risk:** Tokenised deposits cannot get stable retail deposit LCR treatment. If holders cannot always be identified, they count as other unsecured wholesale funding. [S:69600121310a, Annex 3 para 4, p.89]
