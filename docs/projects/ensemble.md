# Project Ensemble

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority's (HKMA's) programme to let banks settle tokenised deposits and tokenised assets with each other, now running live-money trades as EnsembleTX.

## What it is

Ensemble looks at new market infrastructure so that banks can settle tokenised money with each other using wholesale central bank money. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] Its first focus is tokenised deposits, meaning digital versions of ordinary commercial bank deposits. [S:5a9cad53f2be, para 2] The HKMA set up an Architecture Community in May 2024 to agree shared industry standards. [S:0169bc8fbb4d, para 2] A Sandbox followed in August 2024. Community banks linked their tokenised-deposit platforms to it and tested two settlement modes: PvP (payment versus payment) and DvP (delivery versus payment). [S:460620ab7f2b, para 2] In November 2025 the project moved to EnsembleTX, a pilot using real value. It starts with tokenised money market fund trades and treasury and liquidity uses. [S:8b67e8dc510f, paras 1-2] At first, banks settle with each other through Hong Kong's HKD RTGS (real-time gross settlement) system. The HKMA plans a gradual move to round-the-clock settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]

## What it means for your bank

- Participation is by invitation and commitment. The seven named Tokenised Deposit Banks each agree to offer tokenised deposits to Hong Kong customers and settle interbank deals in EnsembleTX with real value. [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex A] The HKMA invites firms with new real-value use cases to join. [S:8b67e8dc510f, CE quote]
- Offering tokenised deposits to customers brings in the HKMA's tokenised-products circular, which expects due diligence, disclosure and early discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1; 'Implementation', p.6]
- For the liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), tokenised deposits that are a legal claim on the bank can be reported like other deposits, but never get the stable-deposit outflow rate. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
- The HKMA expects DLT risk controls on network choice, smart contracts, settlement finality and key security. [S:1884530ff235, Annex items 2-8]
- Before launch, the Supervisory Incubator lets a bank check its tokenised-deposit risk controls with a dedicated HKMA team. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, paras 2-3]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** EnsembleTX is a pilot, not a rulebook. Customer-facing tokenised deposits still fall under the HKMA tokenised-products circular and need prior HKMA discussion. [S:b2719f3dcb37, footnote 1; p.6]
- **To business heads:** The pilot starts with tokenised money market funds and real-time treasury uses, and the HKMA is inviting new real-value use cases. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 2 and CE quote]
- **To the CEO:** The Policy Address targets CBDC settlement and 24/7 operation in EnsembleTX by around end-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 50]
- **To Risk:** Tokenised deposits never get stable-deposit LCR treatment; outflows are reported separately if holders cannot always be identified. [S:f4e64fda4011, para 31]
