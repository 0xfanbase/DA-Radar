# Project mBridge

A shared platform on which several central banks' digital currencies can settle cross-border payments and foreign exchange (FX) trades between banks.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Run by | HKMA with the Bank of Thailand (BoT), the People's Bank of China Digital Currency Institute (PBoC DCI), the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) and, as of the 2024 MVP, the BIS Innovation Hub (BISIH); the Saudi Central Bank joined in 2024 [S:db56d97e2206, fn 1, paras 3-4] |
| Status as of 25 Sep 2026 | Pilot; the Government calls it part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)] |
| Started | Q4 2019 as Inthanon-LionRock; renamed in Feb 2021 [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and fn 1] |
| Next official date | None stated; no timetable for wider participation [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (3) and (5)] |
| Covered in | E2, E3 |

## What it is

The project began as Inthanon-LionRock, run by the HKMA and the BoT. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1] In February 2021 the PBoC DCI and the CBUAE joined, and it became the m-CBDC Bridge. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and fn 1] The platform tests real-time, round-the-clock FX payment-versus-payment deals across several jurisdictions. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project mBridge' section] In the 2022 pilot, 20 banks in four jurisdictions made more than 160 payment and FX trades worth over US$22 million. [S:947fa49606ab, s.1 Executive Summary, p.4] The platform reached minimum viable product (MVP) stage in June 2024, with a validating node in each founding jurisdiction. [S:db56d97e2206, paras 1, 3] In October 2025 the Government told LegCo that participating banks set their own fees. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]

## What it means for your bank

- Each bank runs its own sanctions, anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF) checks before sending or confirming a payment. The platform does not. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- The platform only records a pass or fail certificate from the bank's off-platform check. Each jurisdiction's AML, CTF and sanctions laws still apply. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.2(d), p.35]
- Know-your-customer (KYC), AML and CFT work stays in the bank's own systems, off the bridge. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
- Only a domestically licensed bank can ask for issue or redemption of its home jurisdiction's CBDC. Check each counterparty's role. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.1, pp.31-32]
- Private firms can propose add-on technology or use cases through a BISIH form. [S:db56d97e2206, para 5]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** mBridge leaves AML and sanctions screening to each bank, off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- **To business heads:** Participating banks set their own mBridge service fees. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- **To the CEO:** mBridge reached MVP in June 2024; the Government calls it part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure. [S:db56d97e2206, para 1]; [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- **To Risk:** The 2022 pilot ran on one cloud environment in Hong Kong, flagged as a privacy and governance issue to revisit. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.3, p.33]
