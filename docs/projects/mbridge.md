# Project mBridge

A shared platform on which several central banks issue their own central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), so that banks can settle cross-border payments and foreign exchange (FX) trades directly. [S:947fa49606ab, s.1 Executive summary, p.4]

## What it is

The project began as Inthanon-LionRock, a CBDC study for cross-border payments started by the HKMA and the BoT. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and footnote 1] In February 2021 the PBC DCI and the CBUAE joined, and it was renamed the m-CBDC Bridge. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1] The platform tests real-time, round-the-clock FX payment-versus-payment deals across several jurisdictions. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project mBridge' section] In the 2022 pilot, 20 banks in four jurisdictions made more than 160 payment and FX trades worth over US$22 million. [S:947fa49606ab, s.1 Executive summary, p.4] The platform reached minimum viable product (MVP) stage in June 2024, with a validating node in each founding central bank's jurisdiction. [S:db56d97e2206, paras 1-2] In October 2025 the Government told the Legislative Council (LegCo) that participating Hong Kong banks set their own fees for mBridge payments. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]

## What it means for your bank

- Before sending or confirming a payment, each bank runs its own compliance checks, including anti-money laundering (AML), counter-terrorist financing (CTF) and sanctions, off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30]
- Each participating bank must follow the AML, CTF and sanctions laws that apply to it. The platform only attaches a pass or fail certificate from the bank's off-platform check. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.2(d), p.35]
- Banks keep know-your-customer (KYC), AML and CFT (counter-financing of terrorism) compliance off the bridge, under each jurisdiction's rules. [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.3]
- Foreign banks can hold and use another jurisdiction's CBDC, but only that jurisdiction's domestic banks can ask for it to be issued or redeemed. [S:947fa49606ab, s.7.1.1, p.32]; [S:89b8fcadfaa8, p.2]
- Private firms can propose add-on technology or use cases through a BISIH participation form. [S:db56d97e2206, para 4]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** On mBridge, AML and sanctions checks stay with each bank, off the platform. [S:947fa49606ab, s.6.5.2, p.30; s.7.2(d), p.35]
- **To business heads:** Participating Hong Kong banks set their own fees for mBridge payments. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- **To the CEO:** mBridge reached MVP in June 2024; the Government calls it part of Hong Kong's financial market infrastructure. [S:db56d97e2206, para 1]; [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (4)]
- **To Risk:** The 2022 pilot ran on a centralised cloud in Hong Kong. The report says keeping data in one cloud raised privacy concerns. [S:947fa49606ab, s.5.1, p.16; s.7.1.3, p.33]
