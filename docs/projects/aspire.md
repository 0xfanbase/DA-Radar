# ASPIRe: the SFC's virtual-asset roadmap

The Securities and Futures Commission's (SFC's) February 2025 roadmap of five pillars and 12 initiatives for Hong Kong's virtual-asset (VA) market. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 1]

## What it is

ASPIRe stands for five pillars: Access, Safeguards, Products, Infrastructure and Relationships. Together they hold 12 initiatives. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 1] Access covers new licensing regimes for VA over-the-counter (OTC) dealing and custody; legislative preparation for custodians was aimed for completion by end-2025. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1] Safeguards includes exploring technology-neutral, outcome-based custody standards and a more flexible approach to cold-storage ratios. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3] Products looks at new token listings and derivatives for professional investors, margin financing, staking, and borrowing and lending. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar P, Initiatives 6-8] Infrastructure covers regulatory reporting and surveillance tools such as wallet tracing. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar I, Initiative 9] Many initiatives are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11]

## What it means for your bank

- The SFC describes the roadmap as an evolving plan, not a final destination. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 5] Its initiatives take effect through separate circulars and guidance, such as those on staking and VA financing. [S:36753b22e10a, paras 2-3]; [S:089ec86e2407, para 2]
- VA financing now reaches banks too. Registered institutions (RIs) providing VA dealing services may offer financing for VA dealing to their securities margin clients, following standards similar to the SFC's. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', items (1)-(2)]
- Intermediaries, including RIs, may offer staking to clients they deal in VAs for, but should use only segregated accounts held at an SFC-licensed platform or at an authorized financial institution. [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4-5]
- Under the SFC's framework, VATP perpetual contracts should be offered only to professional investors. Margin collateral should be fiat money, or Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)-regulated stablecoins or tokenised deposits. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management']
- Pillar A's OTC and custody plans became proposed VA dealing and custodian regimes. Banks would register rather than be licensed, with the HKMA as frontline regulator. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]; [S:7395a53cc85a, paras 3-4] Two further proposed regimes, for VA advisory and VA management, would also let only banks register. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 52, 58 and fns 9-10]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** ASPIRe is a roadmap; its initiatives take effect through separate SFC circulars and guidance, so track those. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 5]; [S:089ec86e2407, para 2]
- **To business heads:** Delivered items so far include staking, shared order books, VA financing and professional-investor perpetuals. [S:36753b22e10a, para 2]; [S:f09e57feb89b, para 2]; [S:089ec86e2407, paras 2-3]
- **To the CEO:** The SFC's Executive Director of Intermediaries called the roadmap-based approach essential to scaling Hong Kong's digital asset market. [S:089ec86e2407, para 5]
- **To Risk:** Outcome-based custody standards are still framed as something to explore; the SFC's August 2025 VATP custody circular took immediate effect. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]; [S:562c0f8addce, 'Way forward', p.7]
