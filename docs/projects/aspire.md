# ASPIRe: the SFC's virtual-asset roadmap

The Securities and Futures Commission's (SFC's) February 2025 plan of five pillars and 12 initiatives for Hong Kong's virtual-asset (VA) market.

## What it is

ASPIRe stands for five pillars: Access, Safeguards, Products, Infrastructure and Relationships. Together they hold 12 initiatives. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 1] Access covers new licensing regimes for VA over-the-counter (OTC) dealing and custody, with custodian legislative preparation targeted for end-2025. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1] Safeguards includes exploring outcome-based custody standards in place of a fixed cold-storage ratio. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3] Products looks at professional-investor-only derivatives, margin financing, staking and lending. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar P, Initiatives 6-8] Infrastructure covers regulatory reporting and surveillance tools such as wallet tracing. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar I, Initiative 9] Most items are framed as things the SFC will explore or consider, not rules. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]

## What it means for your bank

- The roadmap itself binds no one. Bank duties come only from the circulars and conditions that implement it. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]
- VA financing now reaches banks too. Registered institutions (RIs) dealing in VAs may offer VA-backed margin financing to existing securities margin clients, under HKMA conditions. [S:e64b7b0267b2, paras (2), (4) and fn 5]
- Staked client assets must sit in segregated accounts at an SFC-licensed platform or an Authorized Institution. [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4-5]
- VA trading platform (VATP) perpetual contracts are for professional investors only, and margin may be posted in HKMA-regulated tokenised deposits. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements']
- Pillar A's licensing initiatives are now the four VA intermediary regimes, in which banks would register rather than be licensed. [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]; [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** ASPIRe is a roadmap; watch for the circulars that implement it, since those carry the obligations. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]
- **To business heads:** Delivered items so far include staking, shared order books, VA financing and professional-investor perpetuals. [S:36753b22e10a, para 2]; [S:089ec86e2407, paras 2-4, p.1]
- **To the CEO:** The SFC calls its structured, roadmap-based approach essential to growing the market. [S:089ec86e2407, para 2, p.1]
- **To Risk:** Outcome-based custody standards remain exploratory; today's VATP custody circular already applies. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]; [S:562c0f8addce, 'Way forward', p.7]
