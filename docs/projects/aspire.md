# ASPIRe: the SFC's virtual-asset roadmap

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) published ASPIRe in February 2025. It is a roadmap of five pillars and 12 initiatives for Hong Kong's virtual-asset (VA) market. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 1] Some roadmap items, such as VA financing, now reach banks through Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) guidance. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', items (1)-(2)] ASPIRe's licensing work also led to planned regimes under which banks would register with the SFC. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]; [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4]

## What it is

ASPIRe stands for five pillars: Access, Safeguards, Products, Infrastructure and Relationships. Together they hold 12 initiatives. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 1]

- **Access** covers new licensing regimes for over-the-counter (OTC) VA dealing and for VA custody. OTC deals are agreed directly between two parties, not on an exchange. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1] ASPIRe says the government-led work to prepare the law for custodians aimed to finish by end-2025. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]
- **Safeguards** includes exploring custody standards that focus on results and do not favour any one technology. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3] It also includes a more flexible approach to cold-storage ratios, meaning the share of client assets kept offline. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3]
- **Products** looks at: [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar P, Initiatives 6-8]
  - new token listings
  - derivatives (contracts whose value tracks another asset) for professional investors
  - margin financing (lending to clients so they can buy assets)
  - staking (locking up tokens to help run a blockchain, for a reward)
  - borrowing and lending
- **Infrastructure** covers tools for regulatory reporting and surveillance. One example is wallet tracing, which follows tokens as they move between wallets. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar I, Initiative 9]

The SFC frames many initiatives as things it will explore or consider. [S:d461c2db6af2, Initiatives 3, 6-9, 11]

## What it means for your bank

- The SFC calls the roadmap a plan that will keep changing, not a final destination. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 5] Its initiatives take effect through separate circulars (SFC letters to firms) and guidance. Examples include the circulars on staking and VA financing. [S:36753b22e10a, paras 2-3]; [S:089ec86e2407, para 2]
- VA financing now reaches banks too. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', items (1)-(2)] Registered institutions (RIs) are banks registered with the SFC. RIs that provide VA dealing services may lend to their securities margin clients for VA dealing. Margin clients are clients who borrow from the firm to invest. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', items (1)-(2)] The HKMA expects these RIs to observe standards similar to the SFC's. [S:e64b7b0267b2, p.2, 'HKMA's guidance for RIs', items (1)-(2)]
- Licensed and registered firms, including RIs, may offer staking to clients for whom they deal in virtual assets. [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4-5] They should use only segregated accounts, kept apart from the firm's own assets. [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4-5] Those accounts should sit at an SFC-licensed platform or at an authorized financial institution. [S:d5ebca32c92b, paras 4-5]
- The SFC's framework covers perpetual contracts on VATPs. These are derivatives with no expiry date. VATPs should offer them only to professional investors. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management'] Margin collateral is what clients post to back their trades. It should be fiat money (ordinary currency), or stablecoins or tokenised deposits regulated by the HKMA. [S:49f642bdee28, 'Eligible investors and reference assets'; 'Margin arrangements and loss allocation management']
- Pillar A's plans for OTC trading and custody became the proposed VA dealing and custodian regimes. Banks would register rather than be licensed, with the HKMA as frontline regulator. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar A, Initiative 1]; [S:7395a53cc85a, para 4] Two further proposed regimes cover VA advisory and VA management. Under those regimes too, only banks could register with the SFC. [S:6186c01c6649, paras 52, 58 and fns 9-10]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** ASPIRe is a roadmap. Its initiatives take effect through separate SFC circulars and guidance, so track those. [S:cfe8a53186c3, para 5]; [S:089ec86e2407, para 2]
- **To business heads:** Items delivered so far include staking, shared order books, VA financing and perpetual contracts for professional investors. [S:36753b22e10a, para 2]; [S:f09e57feb89b, para 2]; [S:089ec86e2407, paras 2-3]
- **To the CEO:** The SFC's Executive Director of Intermediaries called the roadmap-based approach essential to scaling Hong Kong's digital asset market. [S:089ec86e2407, para 5]
- **To Risk:** The SFC still frames custody standards based on results as something to explore. [S:d461c2db6af2, Pillar S, Initiative 3] But its August 2025 custody circular to VATPs took effect immediately. [S:562c0f8addce, 'Way forward', p.7]
