# Digital bonds: Genesis, Evergreen and the public-sector issues

Hong Kong's run of bond tokenisation work, from the 2021 Project Genesis test to three Government green bond deals and the Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation's (HKMC's) 2026 digital bond.

## What it is

The HKMA began bond tokenisation in 2021 with a proof of concept alongside the BIS Innovation Hub Hong Kong Centre. [S:fc3eb72031c6, para 5] Project Evergreen then produced a HK$800 million tokenised green bond in February 2023, described as the first tokenised bond from any government. [S:a0841967c871, para 1] It settled on a T+1 basis through the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU), which carries statutory settlement finality. [S:a0841967c871, paras 3-4] The HKMA's August 2023 report found that existing Hong Kong law prescribes no paper form for debt securities. [S:a0b44be27c40, para 28, pp.11-12] A multi-currency issue of about HK$6 billion followed in February 2024. [S:4290ec1111e3, key features] In November 2025 a HK$10 billion issue became the first digital bond to offer settlement in e-HKD and e-CNY. [S:ac6cd085a325, paras 4-5] In June 2026 the HKMC priced about HK$12 billion of digital bonds created on a CMU-run platform. [S:6aa27ee8b400, paras 1, 4]

## What it means for your bank

- In Evergreen, only custodians that were CMU participants and onboarded to the platform could open token accounts. Other investors held through the custodian's own books. [S:a0b44be27c40, para 45, p.16]
- Where investors join the platform directly, the operator and any custodian are expected to run know-your-customer (KYC) checks and support AML/CFT compliance. [S:a0b44be27c40, para 44, p.16]
- Statutory finality comes only from a designated clearing and settlement system; otherwise finality rests on contract. [S:b7e1bb6b44ae, report paras 37-38]
- Existing securities licensing and offering rules apply to digital bond distribution as they do to ordinary bonds. [S:a0b44be27c40, paras 23-24, p.11]
- Selling tokenised bonds to customers falls under the HKMA tokenised-products circular, including prior discussion with the HKMA. [S:b2719f3dcb37, 'Implementation', p.6]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Digital bonds carry no new licence category; ordinary securities rules apply. [S:a0b44be27c40, paras 23-24, p.11]
- **To business heads:** Sixteen financial institutions arranged the HKMC deal. Investors could use existing CMU links to Euroclear and Clearstream, and settlement fell from T+5 to T+3. [S:6aa27ee8b400, paras 5, 8]
- **To the CEO:** The Policy Address says Hong Kong-issued digital bonds took almost half the global market from 2025 to mid-2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35]
- **To Risk:** Evergreen flags untested DLT platform risk and asks for continuity plans covering disruption and platform migration. [S:a0b44be27c40, paras 46-47, p.17]
