# CBDC research and cross-border digital money pilots

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority's (HKMA's) research projects on central bank digital currency (CBDC) and tokenised money (Aurum, Sela, Genesis, Inthanon-LionRock), plus the cross-boundary e-CNY (digital renminbi) pilot and the 2026 Shanghai cooperation. ## What it is

Project Aurum, with the BISIH and a local research institute, built a two-tier prototype. It paired a wholesale interbank system with retail e-wallets, and its report came out in October 2022. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects'] Project Sela, with the Bank of Israel, tested letting non-bank intermediaries, called Access Enablers, connect directly to a central bank's CBDC ledger. [S:8146b4a87d45, paras 2 and 5] Sela's report says it reflects no HKMA policy decision on a potential e-HKD. [S:f9000467b0ee, Section 1, footnote 5, p.5] Project Genesis built prototype platforms for green bond tokenisation with the BISIH. [S:5a9cad53f2be, footnote 4] It concluded in 2021, and the Government's 2023 tokenised green bond built on it. [S:a0841967c871, quote from Eddie Yue] Inthanon-LionRock, with the Bank of Thailand, studied CBDC for cross-border payments and grew into mBridge. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and footnote 1] The cross-boundary e-CNY pilot lets Hong Kong residents set up personal e-CNY wallets with a Hong Kong mobile number and top them up through the Faster Payment System (FPS). [S:08caafe0cfe7, paras 1-2; Annex 1]

## What it means for your bank

- Sela's report says the project reflects no HKMA policy decision on a potential e-HKD. [S:f9000467b0ee, Section 1, footnote 5, p.5]
- Pilot wallets are tier-4 personal wallets. Limits are RMB10,000 balance, RMB2,000 per transaction, RMB5,000 a day and RMB50,000 a year, with no person-to-person transfers. Operating institutions may set lower limits. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2, pp.1-2]
- In Hong Kong, 18 local banks support FPS top-ups. The PBoC (People's Bank of China) and the HKMA are exploring wallet upgrades to raise limits, but proposals and timing are not finalised. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)-(2)]
- Under the Shanghai MoU, cross-border financial cooperation and electronic bills of lading will be explored under Project Ensemble. Trade finance using cargo and trade data will be studied through linked data platforms. [S:eb28508e5032, para 2]
- The Government says stablecoins, CBDCs such as e-CNY, and tokenised deposits each have their own features and maturity. Market forces will largely decide their prospects. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (3)]
