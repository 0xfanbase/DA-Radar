# CBDC research and cross-border digital money pilots

CBDC means central bank digital currency: money issued in digital form by a central bank. This page covers the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) research projects on CBDC and tokenised money (money recorded as digital tokens on a shared ledger). The projects are Aurum, Sela, Genesis and Inthanon-LionRock (a joint study with the Bank of Thailand). [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects' and 'Project Inthanon-LionRock' sections] The page also covers the cross-boundary pilot of e-CNY (the Mainland's digital renminbi). [S:08caafe0cfe7, para 1] It also covers a 2026 memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Shanghai bodies. [S:eb28508e5032, para 1] Why it matters to your bank: 18 local banks in Hong Kong support top-ups of pilot e-CNY wallets. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)]

## What it is

Project Aurum built a two-tier prototype with the BISIH and a local research institute. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects'] It linked a wholesale system (for payments between banks) with retail e-wallets (for the public). [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects'] Its report came out in October 2022. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects']

The HKMA ran Project Sela with the Bank of Israel. [S:8146b4a87d45, paras 2 and 5] Sela tested whether non-bank firms, called Access Enablers, could connect directly to a central bank's CBDC ledger (the record of who holds what). [S:8146b4a87d45, paras 2 and 5] Sela's report says it reflects no HKMA policy decision on a possible e-HKD (a digital Hong Kong dollar). [S:f9000467b0ee, Section 1, footnote 5, p.5]

Project Genesis built test platforms with the BISIH for tokenising green bonds (issuing them as digital tokens). [S:5a9cad53f2be, footnote 4] Genesis ended in 2021. The Government's 2023 tokenised green bond built on its work. [S:a0841967c871, quote from Eddie Yue]

Inthanon-LionRock studied CBDC for cross-border payments with the Bank of Thailand. It later grew into mBridge. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1 and footnote 1]

The cross-boundary e-CNY pilot lets Hong Kong residents open personal e-CNY wallets with a Hong Kong mobile number. [S:08caafe0cfe7, paras 1-2; Annex 1] They top up the wallets through the Faster Payment System (FPS), Hong Kong's instant payment system. [S:08caafe0cfe7, paras 1-2; Annex 1]

> **Commercial and customer benefits**
> **For customers:**
> - The Government says e-CNY gives residents on both sides of the boundary an extra safe and convenient way to pay. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply, 2nd paragraph]
> - Since May 2024, Hong Kong residents can open a pilot wallet with a local mobile number and top it up through FPS. [S:08caafe0cfe7, paras 1-2; Annex 1]
> - The HKMA says its 2023 Sela test showed that direct ledger access for private payment firms could feasibly promote competition. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects']
> **For the bank:**
> - By March 2026, 18 local banks supported e-CNY top-ups, up from 17 at the start. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)] A top-up service keeps the bank in its customer's payment flow. (concept)
> - Some banks let users top up from Hong Kong dollar accounts, converting to renminbi. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2, p.1] This is a foreign exchange (FX) service. (concept)
> - The HKMA chief executive said the 2023 tokenised green bond built on Project Genesis. [S:a0841967c871, quote from Eddie Yue] Two of its four selling banks also acted as investor custodians. [S:a0841967c871, para 2]
> **Evidence so far:**
> - Citing Mainland central bank data, the Government said about 80,000 wallets were registered by end-January 2026. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)] Local shops accepting e-CNY grew from about 300 to about 5,200. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)]
> - The 2023 bond's primary issue settled one business day after the trade (T+1). [S:a0841967c871, para 3]
> **Limits:**
> - Pilot wallets have limits, such as 2,000 renminbi per transaction. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2, p.1] Plans to raise them have no final timetable. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (2)]
> - Direct ledger access for non-bank firms means more payment competition for banks. (concept)

## What it means for your bank

- Sela's report says the project reflects no HKMA policy decision on a possible e-HKD. [S:f9000467b0ee, Section 1, footnote 5, p.5]
- Pilot wallets are "tier-4" personal wallets. Their limits, in Chinese renminbi (RMB), are:
  - RMB 10,000 balance;
  - RMB 2,000 per transaction;
  - RMB 5,000 a day;
  - RMB 50,000 a year. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2, pp.1-2]
- Pilot wallets do not allow person-to-person transfers. Operating institutions may set lower limits. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2, pp.1-2]
- 18 local banks in Hong Kong support FPS top-ups. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)-(2)] The People's Bank of China (PBoC) and the HKMA are exploring wallet upgrades to raise limits. The proposals and timing are not final. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)-(2)]
- The Shanghai MoU says the parties will explore cross-border financial cooperation and electronic bills of lading (digital shipping documents) under Project Ensemble. [S:eb28508e5032, para 2] They will also study trade finance that uses cargo and trade data from linked data platforms. [S:eb28508e5032, para 2]
- The Government says stablecoins, CBDC such as e-CNY, and tokenised deposits each have their own features and maturity. It says market forces will largely decide their future. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (3)]
