# CBDC research and cross-border digital money pilots

The HKMA's research projects on central bank digital currency (CBDC) and tokenised money (Aurum, Sela, Genesis, Inthanon-LionRock), plus the cross-boundary e-CNY pilot and the 2026 Shanghai cooperation.

## What it is

Project Aurum, with the BISIH and a local research institute, built a two-tier prototype. It paired a bank-to-bank layer with retail e-wallets, and its report came out in October 2022. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Collaborative Projects'] Project Sela, with the Bank of Israel, tested letting non-bank "Access Enablers" connect directly to a central bank ledger. [S:8146b4a87d45, press release para 2] Sela's report says it reflects no HKMA policy decision on e-HKD. [S:8146b4a87d45, report Section 1, footnote 5] Project Genesis was a 2021 bond tokenisation proof of concept with the BISIH, and the 2023 Government tokenised bond built on it. [S:a0841967c871, quote from Eddie Yue, p.1] Inthanon-LionRock, with the Bank of Thailand, tested CBDC cross-border payments and grew into mBridge. [S:6b0fae230518, para 1] The cross-boundary e-CNY pilot lets Hong Kong residents open personal Mainland digital renminbi wallets and top them up through the Faster Payment System (FPS). [S:08caafe0cfe7, para 1; Annex 1]

## What it means for your bank

- Research projects create no obligations; their designs are not policy decisions. [S:8146b4a87d45, report Section 1, footnote 5]
- If the bank is an FPS top-up bank, note that tier-4 e-CNY wallets cap balances at RMB10,000 and allow no person-to-person transfers. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2]
- Per-transaction and yearly e-CNY limits are RMB2,000 and RMB50,000; any relaxation has no confirmed date. [S:2ffb8727bb2f, Reply (3) and (5)]
- The Shanghai MoU will look at a cross-border platform and electronic bills of lading under Project Ensemble, linked to trade data. Trade-finance teams may be asked to take part. [S:eb28508e5032, para 2]
- The Government expects stablecoins, e-CNY and tokenised deposits to develop different roles, driven by market forces. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (3)]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Sela and Aurum are research, not rules; Sela says so expressly. [S:8146b4a87d45, report Section 1, footnote 5]
- **To business heads:** About 80,000 e-CNY wallets were registered with Hong Kong numbers by end-January 2026, with 18 banks supporting FPS top-ups. [S:062b0a1547b9, Reply (1)]
- **To the CEO:** The Shanghai MoU links Ensemble to Mainland trade-finance data work. [S:eb28508e5032, para 2]
- **To Risk:** e-CNY wallets block person-to-person transfers and cap balances, which limits misuse paths to monitor. [S:08caafe0cfe7, Annex 2]
