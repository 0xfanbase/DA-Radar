# Digital Bond Grant Scheme (DBGS)

An HKMA subsidy that refunds part of the cost of issuing a digital bond in Hong Kong.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Run by | Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) [S:5a0563de8142, p.1] |
| Status as of 25 Sep 2026 | In force (initial three-year period) [S:5a0563de8142, p.1 fn 1] |
| Started | Applications opened 28 Nov 2024; covers bonds issued on or after 16 Oct 2024 [S:633ce27d4f9b, para 1, footnote 1] |
| Next official date | End of the initial three years from 28 Nov 2024 (no renewal decision in the corpus) [S:633ce27d4f9b, para 3] |
| Covered in | C5, E3 |

## What it is

The DBGS refunds half of eligible issuance costs for a digital bond. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1, p.2] The cap is HK$1.25 million for a Half Grant, where only the basic conditions are met. It rises to HK$2.5 million for a Full Grant, where the additional conditions are also met. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1, p.2] Each issuer group can claim for no more than two issues. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1, p.2] To qualify at all, at least half of the lead arrangers must be recognised Hong Kong arrangers. The bond also needs a DLT team with real Hong Kong presence, or must use a CMU-run DLT platform. [S:5a0563de8142, s.2, p.3] The HKMA keeps full discretion over eligibility and grant amounts, and may change the scheme. [S:5a0563de8142, p.1]

## What it means for your bank

- If the bank arranges bonds, arranger roles matter: half or more of lead arrangers must be recognised in Hong Kong for the issue to qualify. [S:5a0563de8142, s.2, p.3]
- The Full Grant needs a DLT platform provider outside the issuer's group of associates. A bank issuing on its own group platform would not meet that test. [S:5a0563de8142, s.3, p.4]
- The Full Grant also needs at least HK$1 billion in size, five or more unrelated investors, and a listing on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) or an SFC-licensed VA trading platform. [S:5a0563de8142, s.3, p.4]
- Fees paid to associates are not eligible expenses. [S:633ce27d4f9b, Guideline s.4, p.5]
- A green digital bond can claim general issuance costs under the DBGS or Track I of the Green and Sustainable Finance Grant Scheme, not both. [S:5a0563de8142, s.4, p.5]
- The formal application must be filed within three months of issue. An optional pre-application consultation with the HKMA is available. [S:5a0563de8142, s.5, p.6]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** The grant is a subsidy, not a licence or approval. The HKMA keeps absolute discretion on eligibility and amount. [S:5a0563de8142, p.1]
- **To business heads:** Clients can recover up to HK$2.5 million per issue, for at most two issues per issuer group. [S:5a0563de8142, s.1, p.2]
- **To the CEO:** The Government links the scheme to its aim of more digital bond issues in Hong Kong. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 97]
- **To Risk:** Using an associated DLT platform caps the grant at the Half Grant level. [S:5a0563de8142, s.3, p.4]
