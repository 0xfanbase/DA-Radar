# e-HKD (digital Hong Kong dollar)

The HKMA's long-running work on a possible Hong Kong central bank digital currency (CBDC), now focused on wholesale use by financial institutions.

## What it is

The HKMA began in 2017 with a wholesale proof of concept, Project LionRock. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Project LionRock' section] It then consulted on a retail e-HKD through a 2021 technical paper and a 2022 policy paper. The technical paper proposed a two-tier model in which banks distribute e-HKD. [S:59a20457a2fc, Executive Summary points 3-4] In September 2022 the HKMA set a three-rail approach. It found existing law not certain enough for issuing digital legal tender. [S:35cb94c89a11, Rail 1 section] Industry pilots ran in two phases, and the project was renamed e-HKD+ in September 2024 to cover tokenised deposits too. [S:2f93aa2b9560, paras 1-2] In October 2025 the HKMA said the near-term priority is wholesale payments, not retail. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4] It has not decided whether or when to launch a retail e-HKD. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']

## What it means for your bank

- There is no binding e-HKD rule. The designs are research papers and pilot findings. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']
- The April 2022 paper ruled out full anonymity so that e-HKD could support AML/CFT compliance, and floated tiered wallets. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.3]
- In any retail distribution role, the designs place customer due diligence and record-keeping on the intermediary. [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4]
- Phase 2 found the public sees e-HKD and tokenised deposits in a similar light, because of high trust in banks. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 3]
- The HKMA says it will publish common token standards from the e-HKD Industry Forum. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 6]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** No retail e-HKD decision exists; designs expect distributing banks to run customer due diligence. [S:d51dc8884c8a, 'Policy Stance and Three-Rail Approach']; [S:3b42966a786e, Section 5.4]
- **To business heads:** The HKMA's e-HKD focus is now wholesale: tokenisation and cross-border settlement. [S:6bd9e65817c4, para 4]
- **To the CEO:** The Government's November 2025 digital bond was the first to settle using e-HKD and e-CNY. [S:ac6cd085a325, para 4]
- **To Risk:** Phase 2 found privacy tools raised gas fees by up to 320 times. [S:bdefe7fc69d4, Section 4 'Privacy', p.31]
