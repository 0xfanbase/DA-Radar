## What it is

The 2022 Policy Statement set the principle of treating the same activity and risks under the same regulation. It also promised mandatory licensing for VA service providers. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6] It named three pilots: NFT issuance, a tokenised Government green bond and e-HKD. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 10-11] Policy Statement 2.0 (June 2025) set out the LEAP framework. LEAP means legal and regulatory streamlining, expanding tokenised products, advancing use cases, and people and partnership development. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 4] It named the SFC as lead licensing authority for digital-asset dealing and custody, with the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] It also switched the Government's term to *digital assets*. The legal term in the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO) stays *virtual asset*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] The 2026 Policy Address adds dated targets for the HKMA and SFC. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35, 49-51]

## What it means for your bank

- Policy statements and Policy Addresses create no obligations; they are delivered later through laws, guidelines and circulars. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 49-51]
- Under PS 2.0 banks will register with the SFC for dealing and custody, with the HKMA as frontline regulator. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- The 2025 Policy Address said the HKMA would encourage banks to issue tokenised deposits and settle tokenised money market fund trades with them. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40]
- The same paragraph said the HKMA would push banks to use the supervisory sandbox to improve their risk controls. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40]
- The HKMA has separately asked each bank's board to endorse a digital-transformation business plan within six months of 9 March 2026. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Government now says *digital assets*, but AMLO still defines *virtual asset*; use the legal term in policies. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]
- **To business heads:** The SFC plans to let licensed platforms trade regulated stablecoins and use them to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- **To the CEO:** The Government's stated 2026 target is one bill for VA dealing, custody, advisory and management licensing. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- **To Risk:** Customs plans digital-asset transaction analytics by 2029, feeding a wider financial-crime data platform. [S:ef3648ff1610, Part II item 209]
