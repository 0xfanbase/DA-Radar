## What it is

The 2022 Policy Statement adopted the principle of treating the same activity and risks under the same regulation. It also announced work on a licensing regime for VA service providers. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6] It listed three pilots: a non-fungible token (NFT) issue, a tokenised Government green bond and e-HKD. [S:f587b828e3fa, para 10] Policy Statement 2.0 (PS 2.0, June 2025) set out the LEAP framework. LEAP means legal and regulatory streamlining, expanding tokenised products, advancing use cases and cross-sector collaboration, and people and partnership development. [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 4] It proposed the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) as lead authority for licensing and registering digital-asset dealing and custody providers, with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] It also switched the Government's term to *digital assets*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] *Virtual asset* remains the term defined in the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO). [S:7395a53cc85a, Note] The 2026 Policy Address adds targets for the HKMA and SFC, some with dates. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35, 49-50]

## What it means for your bank

- PS 2.0 said the HKMA would be the frontline regulator for banks, supervising their digital-asset dealing and custody, with the SFC as lead licensing and registration authority. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- The 2025 Policy Address said the HKMA would encourage banks to introduce tokenised deposits, and would promote live deals such as settling tokenised money market funds with them. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40]
- The same paragraph said the HKMA would encourage banks to use the supervisory sandbox to improve their risk management. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40]
- Separately, the HKMA expects each Authorized Institution's board to oversee and endorse a strategic business plan on technology change within six months of its 9 March 2026 circular. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4, pp.1-2]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Government now says *digital assets*, but *virtual asset* is still the defined term in AMLO. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]; [S:7395a53cc85a, Note]
- **To business heads:** The 2026 Policy Address says the SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms and their use to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- **To the CEO:** The Government's stated 2026 target is one bill for VA dealing, custody, advisory and management licensing. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- **To Risk:** Customs plans a digital-asset transaction analytics system by 2029, as part of its anti-money laundering big-data platform. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex (I) New Indicators, item 209]
