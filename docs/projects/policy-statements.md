# Government policy statements and Policy Address items

These are the Government's own strategy papers on digital assets. They include the 2022 Policy Statement, Policy Statement 2.0 in 2025, and the digital-asset targets in the 2025 and 2026 Policy Addresses. [S:f587b828e3fa, p.6, date line]; [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 2, 4] A Policy Address is the Chief Executive's yearly speech setting out Government policy. For banks, Policy Statement 2.0 proposed the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as frontline regulator for banks' digital-asset dealing and custody. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]

## What it is

The 2022 Policy Statement adopted a simple principle: the same activity and the same risks get the same regulation. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6] It also announced work on a licensing regime for VA service providers. [S:f587b828e3fa, paras 4, 6] It listed three pilots:

- an issue of non-fungible tokens (unique digital tokens) [S:f587b828e3fa, para 10]
- a tokenised Government green bond, meaning a bond recorded as digital tokens [S:f587b828e3fa, para 10]
- e-HKD (a possible digital Hong Kong dollar) [S:f587b828e3fa, para 10]

Policy Statement 2.0 (June 2025) set out the LEAP framework. LEAP stands for four areas of work: [S:a29b51bffc6b, para 4]

- **L**egal and regulatory streamlining
- **E**xpanding tokenised products
- **A**dvancing use cases and cross-sector collaboration
- **P**eople and partnership development

Policy Statement 2.0 proposed the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) as lead authority to license and register digital-asset dealers and custodians. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] It proposed the HKMA as frontline regulator for banks. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] Policy Statement 2.0 also switched the Government's term to *digital assets*. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1] *Virtual asset* is still the term defined in the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO). [S:7395a53cc85a, Note] The 2026 Policy Address adds targets for the HKMA and SFC, some with dates. [S:ef3648ff1610, paras 35, 49-50]

## What it means for your bank

- Policy Statement 2.0 said the HKMA would be the frontline regulator for banks. The HKMA was to supervise banks' digital-asset dealing and custody. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6] Policy Statement 2.0 proposed the SFC as the lead authority for licensing and registration. [S:a29b51bffc6b, paras 5-6]
- The 2025 Policy Address said the HKMA would encourage banks to introduce tokenised deposits. These are bank deposits recorded as digital tokens. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40] The HKMA would also promote live deals in tokenised assets, such as settling tokenised money market funds with tokenised deposits. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40]
- The same paragraph said the HKMA would encourage banks to use the supervisory sandbox to improve their risk management. The sandbox lets banks test new technology with HKMA oversight. [S:cc2fa9fc0772, para 103, p.40]
- Separately, the HKMA expects the board of each authorized institution to oversee a strategic business plan on technology change. Authorized institutions are the banks and deposit-takers the HKMA authorizes. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4, pp.1-2] The board should sign off the plan within six months of the HKMA's 9 March 2026 circular. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4, pp.1-2]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** The Government now says *digital assets*. But *virtual asset* is still the defined term in AMLO. [S:a29b51bffc6b, footnote 1]; [S:7395a53cc85a, Note]
- **To business heads:** The 2026 Policy Address says the SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms. It will also promote their use to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- **To the CEO:** The Government's stated 2026 target is one bill for VA dealing, custody, advisory and management licensing. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply part (3)]
- **To Risk:** Customs plans a system to analyse digital-asset transactions by 2029. The system is part of the Customs anti-money laundering big-data platform. [S:ef3648ff1610, Annex (I) New Indicators, item 209]
