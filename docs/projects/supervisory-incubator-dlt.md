# Supervisory Incubator for DLT

The Supervisory Incubator for distributed ledger technology (DLT) is a Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) scheme for banks. DLT is a shared digital record kept in step across many computers, such as a blockchain. A bank can use the Incubator to check the risk controls for a DLT project with a dedicated HKMA team before full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Why banks care: the HKMA says six banks have launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section] Tokenised deposits are bank deposits recorded as digital tokens.

## What it is

The Incubator is a supervisory arrangement. It helps banks manage the risks of adopting DLT. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] It pays close attention to risks in services that span DLT and older, existing systems. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] Tokenised deposits were a core focus from the start. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2]

For each bank, the Incubator offers a single contact point at the HKMA. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Through it, the bank can reconfirm that its risk controls are adequate before launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] The bank works with a dedicated HKMA team and can choose to run live trials. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Trials take a hands-on, step-by-step approach. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]

For the industry as a whole, the Incubator is to share good practice through guidance, sharing sessions and research. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 4] In an HKMA inSight article on 24 October 2025, the HKMA said six banks had launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]

> **Commercial and customer benefits**
> **For customers:**
> - The HKMA says the Incubator aims to foster DLT banking services that are safe, efficient and useful to the wider community. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 5]
> - When setting trial flexibility, the HKMA considers whether a bank can follow sandbox operating principles, including customer protection measures. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2]
> - Tokenised deposits were a core focus when the Incubator started. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2]
> **For the bank:**
> - A dedicated HKMA team gives supervisory feedback on a bank's DLT proposals before full launch. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]
> - The HKMA is prepared to give some supervisory flexibility during trials, decided case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]
> - The HKMA's March 2026 circular says a bank's strategic plan may use small real-world Incubator trials to test new business models. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4, p.2]
> - Feedback from a supervisor before launch can lower the risk of costly redesign later. (concept)
> **Evidence so far:**
> - In October 2025 the HKMA said six banks had launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]
> - The same HKMA article says DLT adoption rose 50% in recent years. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]
> - Around 60 banks attended a March 2026 HKMA event on the Incubator and Project Ensemble. [S:38e1f67c38df, para 3 and closing note]
> **Limits:**
> - The flexibility depends on the bank managing trial risks in line with the trial's scale, scope and nature. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]
> - The HKMA article gives the number of banks, but no client numbers, volumes or cost savings. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]

## What it means for your bank

- Joining is voluntary. AIs that want to use the Incubator contact the HKMA. [S:fae36c380174, p.2] The purpose is to reconfirm risk controls before a full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]
- The HKMA is prepared to give AIs in the Incubator some supervisory flexibility (room in how rules apply). The HKMA decides this case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2] One factor is whether the bank can meet operating principles similar to those for Fintech Supervisory Sandbox trials. The Sandbox is an existing HKMA scheme for limited fintech pilots. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2]
- That flexibility depends on the bank managing trial risks in line with the trial's scale, scope and nature. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]
- Some existing rules might not fit DLT well. Where that mismatch is unintended, the HKMA says it will consider whether rules need adapting or some flexibility is needed. [S:fae36c380174, p.2] The HKMA will do the same where the mismatch goes against the principle of "same activity, same risk, same regulation". [S:fae36c380174, p.2]
- The HKMA's non-binding DLT considerations remain a reference. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, Introduction; items 1-4, 8] They cover:
  - board responsibility;
  - network choice;
  - smart contracts (code on the ledger that runs by itself);
  - legal risks such as settlement finality (the point when a transfer is legally final);
  - private key security (protecting the secret codes that control digital assets).
- The HKMA expects AIs planning new digital-asset activities to discuss them with the HKMA before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** The HKMA decides any supervisory flexibility in the Incubator case by case. It depends on how the bank manages trial risks. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2]
- **To business heads:** The HKMA says six banks have launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]
- **To the CEO:** The HKMA's business-model circular says a bank's strategic plan may factor in small real-world trials through the Incubator. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- **To Risk:** The HKMA's DLT considerations say the board and senior management carry full responsibility for DLT adoption and its risks. [S:1884530ff235, Annex item 1]
