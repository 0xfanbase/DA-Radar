# Supervisory Incubator for DLT

An HKMA channel where a bank can test a distributed ledger technology (DLT) product with a dedicated supervisory team before full launch.

## What it is

The Incubator is a supervisory arrangement to help banks manage the risks of adopting DLT. It pays particular attention to services that span DLT and legacy systems. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] Tokenised deposits were a core focus from the start. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] For a single bank, it offers one contact point to confirm that risk controls are adequate before launch, with a dedicated HKMA team and optional live trials. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Trials are run in a hands-on, step-by-step way. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] At industry level, it shares good practice through guidance, sharing sessions and research. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 4] In October 2025 the HKMA Chief Executive wrote that six banks had launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]

## What it means for your bank

- Use is optional. It is a way to confirm DLT risk management before full launch, not a licence. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]
- A bank in the Incubator may get some supervisory flexibility, decided case by case, on factors like those used in the Fintech Supervisory Sandbox. [S:fae36c380174, p.1, footnote 2]
- That flexibility assumes the bank manages trial risks in line with the trial's scale and nature. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]
- Where a legal or regulatory construct is inadvertently incompatible with DLT, the HKMA says it will consider adapting its approach. [S:fae36c380174, p.2]
- The HKMA's DLT risk-management expectations still apply: board responsibility, network choice, smart contracts, settlement finality and key security. [S:1884530ff235, Annex items 1-8]
- New digital-asset activity still needs prior discussion with the HKMA. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Incubator entry is not approval; any flexibility is case by case and tied to trial controls. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2]
- **To business heads:** Six banks have launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator, according to the HKMA. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]
- **To the CEO:** The HKMA's business-model circular invites banks to use the Incubator for small real-world trials of new models. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- **To Risk:** The HKMA holds the board and senior management fully responsible for DLT adoption and its risks. [S:1884530ff235, Annex item 1]
