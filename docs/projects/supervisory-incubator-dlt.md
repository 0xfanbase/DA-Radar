# Supervisory Incubator for DLT

A Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) arrangement where a bank can check its risk controls for a distributed ledger technology (DLT) initiative with a dedicated HKMA team before full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]

## What it is

The Incubator is a supervisory arrangement to help banks manage the risks of adopting DLT. It pays particular attention to risks in services that span DLT and legacy systems. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] Tokenised deposits were a core focus from the start. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] For a single bank, it offers one contact point to reconfirm that risk controls are adequate before launch, with a dedicated HKMA team and optional live trials. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3] Trials are run in a hands-on, step-by-step way. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] At industry level, it is to share good practice through guidance, sharing sessions and research. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 4] In an HKMA inSight article of 24 October 2025, the HKMA said six banks had launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]

## What it means for your bank

- Access is voluntary: AIs that want to use the Incubator contact the HKMA. [S:fae36c380174, p.2] Its purpose is to reconfirm risk controls before a full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]
- The HKMA is prepared to give AIs in the Incubator some supervisory flexibility, assessed case by case. One factor is whether the bank can meet operating principles similar to those for Fintech Supervisory Sandbox (FSS) trials. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2]
- That flexibility rests on the bank managing trial risks in line with the trial's scale, scope and nature. [S:fae36c380174, p.1]
- Where an existing rule does not fit DLT well and that mismatch is inadvertent, or runs against the "same activity, same risk, same regulation" principle, the HKMA says it will consider whether regulatory adaptations or flexibility are needed. [S:fae36c380174, p.2]
- The HKMA's non-binding DLT considerations remain a reference: board responsibility, network choice, smart contracts, legal risks such as settlement finality, and private key security. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, Introduction; items 1-4, 8]
- AIs planning new digital-asset activities are expected to discuss them with the HKMA before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 5]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** Any supervisory flexibility in the Incubator is decided case by case and depends on how the bank manages trial risks. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and footnote 2]
- **To business heads:** Six banks have launched tokenised deposit services through the Incubator, according to the HKMA. [S:418769c01edc, 'Supervisory Incubator for DLT – where traditional finance and tokenisation meets' section]
- **To the CEO:** The HKMA's business-model circular says a bank's strategic plan may factor in small real-world trials through the Incubator. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, para 4]
- **To Risk:** The HKMA's DLT considerations say the board and senior management carry full responsibility for DLT adoption and its risks. [S:1884530ff235, Annex item 1]
