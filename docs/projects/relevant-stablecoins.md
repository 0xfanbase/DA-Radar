# Relevant Stablecoins: dealing, advice and trading

The May 2026 Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) package that eases how virtual-asset conduct rules apply to stablecoins from HKMA-licensed issuers, plus the stated plan for trading on licensed platforms.

## What it is

A Relevant Stablecoin is a specified stablecoin whose issue is authorised under a Stablecoins Ordinance licence. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1] After the Ordinance started, the two regulators reviewed how the joint virtual-asset (VA) circulars should apply to these coins, given their currency-pegged profile. [S:a0bd48559762, Background, pp.1-2] The HKMA's circular to registered institutions (RIs) sets out the changes, with updated terms and conditions in Appendices A1 and A2. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance', p.2] A separate HKMA letter points all Authorized Institutions (AIs) to the same standards. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1] The SFC's circular 26EC26 covers VA trading platforms (VATPs) and licensed corporations (LCs), and refers RIs to the HKMA circular. [S:19d52ade6849, opening paragraph and fn 2, p.1] The HKMA letter says its guidance should not be read as treating these coins as investment products. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

## What it means for your bank

- An RI may now deal in a Relevant Stablecoin through the licensed issuer as well as through a VATP. [S:a0bd48559762, para (1), p.3]
- Relevant-Stablecoin-only dealing needs no Type 1 registration, but the Appendix A1 conduct terms still apply. [S:a0bd48559762, para (2), p.3]
- For accounts clearly ring-fenced as Relevant-Stablecoin-only, no VA knowledge test is needed. Adding any other VA brings back the full rules. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4]
- When dealing or advising, the bank should disclose material information on each coin's stabilisation mechanism and redemption arrangements. [S:a0bd48559762, para (5), p.5; para (10), p.7]
- Advice should rest on thorough analysis, such as the client's use case. Commission rebates or other benefits should not be the main reason to recommend a coin. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6]
- The HKMA reminds RIs to give it and the SFC advance notice before starting these activities or materially changing them. All AIs are reminded to discuss these activities with the HKMA in advance. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]; [S:1babdb609fc8, 'Implementation', p.2]
- The changes do not apply to other stablecoins. The joint-circular rules and this circular also do not apply to AIs that are not RIs, or to a licensed issuer for its own coin; the HKMA expects these to meet similar standards it issues separately. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance' and footnote 3, p.2]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** As an RI, we follow the HKMA RI circular; 26EC26 sets standards for VATPs and LCs. RIs are still reminded to notify both regulators in advance. [S:19d52ade6849, opening paragraph and fn 2, p.1]; [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- **To business heads:** For Relevant-Stablecoin-only clients, no VA knowledge test and no VA exposure limit are needed. Offering those clients any other VA brings back the full rules. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a)-(b) and fn 4, pp.4-5]
- **To the CEO:** The Policy Address says the SFC will promote licensed-platform trading of regulated stablecoins, including for tokenised money market fund settlement. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- **To Risk:** Only coins issued under an HKMA licence qualify, and the HKMA register lists the licensees. [S:19d52ade6849, para 2]; [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 4]
