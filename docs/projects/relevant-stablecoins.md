# Relevant Stablecoins: dealing, advice and trading

Relevant Stablecoins are stablecoins issued under a Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) licence. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1] A stablecoin is a digital token meant to keep a steady value, usually against an official currency. In May 2026, the HKMA and the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) eased how their virtual-asset (VA) conduct rules apply to these coins. [S:a0bd48559762, p.1; S:19d52ade6849, p.1] This page also covers the stated plan for trading them on licensed platforms. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)] Why it matters to your bank: some VA rules are lighter for these coins. For example, dealing only in these coins needs no Type 1 registration (the SFC registration for dealing in securities). [S:a0bd48559762, para (2), p.3]

## What it is

A Relevant Stablecoin is a specified stablecoin (a class defined in the law) issued under a licence granted under the Stablecoins Ordinance. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

After the Ordinance came into force, the HKMA and the SFC reviewed how their joint circulars on VA activities should apply to these coins. [S:a0bd48559762, Background, pp.1-2] They took into account that the coins are pegged to a currency. [S:a0bd48559762, Background, pp.1-2]

The HKMA's circular to registered institutions (RIs) sets out the changes. RIs are banks that the SFC has registered for securities business. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance', p.2] Updated terms and conditions are in Appendices A1 and A2. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance', p.2]

A separate HKMA letter points all authorized institutions (AIs) to the same standards. AIs are the banks and deposit-taking companies the HKMA authorizes. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

The SFC's circular 26EC26 covers virtual asset trading platforms (VATPs) and licensed corporations (LCs), meaning firms licensed by the SFC. [S:19d52ade6849, opening paragraph and fn 2, p.1] It refers RIs to the HKMA circular. [S:19d52ade6849, opening paragraph and fn 2, p.1]

The HKMA letter says its guidance should not be read as treating these coins as investment products. [S:1babdb609fc8, p.1]

## What it means for your bank

- RIs may now deal in a Relevant Stablecoin through the licensed issuer, not only through a VA trading platform. [S:a0bd48559762, para (1), p.3]
- Dealing only in Relevant Stablecoins needs no Type 1 registration. [S:a0bd48559762, para (2), p.3] The conduct terms in Appendix A1 still apply. [S:a0bd48559762, para (2), p.3]
- No VA knowledge test (a check that the client understands VA) is needed for accounts clearly ring-fenced (kept separate) for Relevant Stablecoins only. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4] If the account adds any other VA, the full rules apply again. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a) and fn 4, p.4]
- When dealing or advising, the bank should disclose material information on how each coin keeps its value stable. It should also disclose how redemption (cashing in) of the coin works. [S:a0bd48559762, para (5), p.5; para (10), p.7]
- Advice should rest on thorough analysis, such as what the client wants to use the coin for. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6] Commission rebates or other benefits should not be the main reason to recommend a coin. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6]
- The HKMA reminds RIs to give it and the SFC advance notice before they start these activities or change them materially. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9] The HKMA reminds all AIs to discuss these activities with it in advance. [S:1babdb609fc8, 'Implementation', p.2]
- The changes do not apply to other stablecoins. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance' and footnote 3, p.2] The joint-circular rules and the HKMA circular to RIs also do not apply to:
  - AIs that are not RIs;
  - a licensed issuer, for its own coin. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance' and footnote 3, p.2]
- The HKMA expects both of these groups to meet similar standards that it issues separately. [S:a0bd48559762, 'Detailed guidance' and footnote 3, p.2]

## Talking points

- **To the CCO:** As a registered institution, we follow the HKMA circular to RIs. Circular 26EC26 sets standards for VATPs and LCs. [S:19d52ade6849, opening paragraph and fn 2, p.1] RIs are still reminded to notify both regulators in advance. [S:a0bd48559762, Implementation, p.9]
- **To business heads:** Clients with Relevant-Stablecoin-only accounts need no VA knowledge test. They also need no VA exposure limit (cap on a client's VA holdings). Offering those clients any other VA brings back the full rules. [S:a0bd48559762, para (3)(a)-(b) and fn 4, pp.4-5]
- **To the CEO:** The Policy Address says the SFC will promote trading of regulated stablecoins on licensed platforms. That includes their use to settle tokenised money market funds. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 49(iii)]
- **To Risk:** Only coins issued under an HKMA licence qualify. [S:19d52ade6849, para 2] The HKMA register lists the licensees. [S:afb6cd0da6ff, para 4]
