# Start here: the map and reading order

This page is your way into the book. The map lists the digital-asset activities a Hong Kong bank may run. For each, it gives the regulator, legal basis, status and module. The reading order then gives a short core path.

For general information only. Not legal or regulatory advice. Always check the official source.

## The map

Terms used in the table: HKMA = Hong Kong Monetary Authority. SFC = Securities and Futures Commission. VA = virtual asset. RI = registered institution, a bank registered with the SFC. FSTB = Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau. IRD = Inland Revenue Department. For an RI, the SFC keeps final responsibility and the HKMA is the day-to-day supervisor. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.1.3]

## Reading order

In module titles, CFT means countering terrorist financing. Virtual asset service providers (VASPs) are firms such as crypto exchanges.

1. **A1 The Hong Kong digital-asset map.** The regulators and what is in force.
2. **B1 Your bank's legal status for digital assets.** How SFC registration shapes what the bank may do.
3. **C1 Selling, dealing and advising on virtual assets.** The joint circular rules.
4. **C4 Stablecoins.** Issuers, and bank services in licensed stablecoins.
5. **D3 AML/CFT for digital assets at a bank.** Customer checks and monitoring for crypto flows.
6. **D6 Travel Rule, counterparty VASPs and unhosted wallets.** Sender and recipient details, other firms and private wallets.
7. **D7 Sanctions, wallet screening, blockchain analytics and fraud.** Screening names and wallets, and scams.
8. **C3 Custody of digital assets and key management.** Holding client assets and keys.
9. **D1 Capital and prudential treatment of cryptoassets.** Capital held against crypto exposures.

When you need them:

- **Other A to E modules:** reference, such as C2 on funds and E1 on the pipeline.
- **Project profiles:** one per official initiative, such as digital bonds.
- **Part F:** the business view.
- **E3 Glossary and timeline:** terms and dates.
