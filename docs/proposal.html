Design proposal · 25 September 2026

Praya: Hong Kong digital assets, for the bank compliance desk

A free, calm reading guide that takes a bank's head of compliance for digital assets from the basics to expert level, in about six hours of reading. Every sentence is checked against the HKMA, SFC or government document it comes from, and every citation names the paragraph.

Working name: Praya, the old waterfront road Hong Kong's banks were built on. Alternatives are listed at the end.

What you will be able to do

After the "Start here" path, you can explain the moving pieces clearly to each person you work with. Every module ends with short talking points for each of them. These examples come from the custody module.

To the CCO

The HKMA's 27 May 2026 custody annex replaced the 2024 one. Existing services must review and revise their controls. HKMA · Updated custody guidance · Implementation, p.2

To business heads

With HKMA consent, a licensed stablecoin issuer can now hold its own coins for us as custody delegate. HKMA · Custody annex 2026 · para 14(iii)

To the CEO

Fixed insurance percentages are gone for banks. We are liable for losses attributable to us and must hold adequate resources. HKMA · Custody annex 2026 · para 11(n)

To Risk / CRO

The SFC says choosing HSM, MPC or multi-signature does not by itself fix wallet weaknesses. Process controls decide it. SFC · 25EC44 · p.1

What reading everything caught

Bank custody rules changed in May 2026.The HKMA's updated annex dropped the fixed 50% cold / 100% hot compensation cover for banks and replaced it with liability plus adequate financial resources. The 50%/100% figure now applies only to SFC-licensed platforms.
AML scope for banks.A bank's own AML rulebook is the HKMA Guideline for Authorized Institutions. Its virtual-asset hooks are the HK$8,000 walk-in transfer threshold (para 4.2.1(b)(iii)), the travel rule through AMLO Schedule 2 section 13A, and checks on overseas VASP customers (paras 11.23–11.25). The SFC's Chapter 12 applies only to the bank's SFC-registered activities.
"EnsembleTX" is official.The HKMA named Project Ensemble's live pilot phase EnsembleTX on 13 November 2025, with seven participating banks listed in its annex.
The June 2026 CRP-1 letter loosened, not tightened.It lets supervision of tokenisation by a financial regulator count, not only regulator approval.
A site quirk that would have misled readers.HKMA pages carry a "Latest Speeches" sidebar. Our first fetch attached today's newest speech to 26 older pages. It is fixed, and the live site's link checker will compare content, not just check that links load.

Not reachable from the research environment: the Insurance Authority and Cyberport sites (blocked), parts of LegCo, and the statute text on e-Legislation. These are listed as gaps. The live site's own checks will cover them.

Citations you never have to hunt for

Each fact carries a chip that names the document and paragraph in words, for example "HKMA custody annex 2026 · para 11(n)". Tap it and a panel opens with the full official title, issuer, date, status, the paragraph again, and a button to the official page or PDF. On desktop the same details sit in a side column next to the text.

Status you can see at a glance

Green means in force. Amber means proposed, in consultation, or a stated target. Grey means superseded or historical. Each chip carries an "as of" date. Targets whose date passes switch automatically to "target date passed".

Desktop

Three columns: contents on the left, reading column at 65 characters in the middle, sources on the right. On phones the contents fold into a bottom bar and sources open as a sheet.

Beyond the modules

  • The map. One page showing every regime, who runs it, and its status: live, coming, or superseded. This is the home page.
  • What changed. A weekly list of new HKMA, SFC and FSTB publications. Each item has a two-line summary and flags which modules it affects. Modules touched by a change show a "changed since you read this" note.
  • Source library. All 737 documents, searchable and filterable by regulator, topic, status and "binds banks directly".
  • Review deck. Three short questions per module that come back after a few days. Progress stays on your device, with no account.
  • Glossary in English and Chinese. Terms as the regulators use them, each linked to where it is defined.

Deliberately left out: points, badges, leaderboards, a chat tutor, videos, and a long branching story. The earlier game-like ideas have been cut back to optional short scenarios for later, because you asked for a reading resource that makes you fluent fast.

How it stays accurate and current

The research pipeline becomes the site's maintenance engine. Plain code does the watching and checking, and AI writes only what the checks can verify.

WatchDaily listing from the same official indexes used in the research: HKMA API and repository, SFC databases, GovHK.
ReadEach new document is fetched in full and summarised into a registry entry with paragraph references.
VerifyA separate verifier re-checks every sentence against the source text. Anything unsupported is removed.
GateAutomatic checks: every citation resolves, official domains only, no copied text, quotes of 15 words or fewer, links load and still match.
Publish"What changed" updates. Affected modules are flagged. Module rewrites go to a pull request for you to approve.

A monthly job re-fetches every source and compares its content, because regulators sometimes revise documents in place at the same address. Source text is used only for checking and is never published: we link, we don't republish.
