# What changed

Newest first. Each entry is written by the update routine or by hand when the content changes.

## 26 Sep 2026 (plain English)
- Every module and project page was rewritten in plain English. Sentences are shorter (about 11–13 words on average; almost none over 25). Every term and abbreviation is explained the first time it appears on a page. Vague lines were replaced with direct ones.
- Facts, numbers, dates, citations and the force of each rule (must / should / may / proposes) were kept. A checker compared every rewritten page with the original. A second reviewer then read each rewrite against the original and, where in doubt, the official text. That review found and fixed 186 places where simpler wording had shifted the meaning, for example a rule stated more widely than the source, or "expects" turned into a bare instruction.
- One correction came from this review. Clients served through a general virtual-asset account still need the knowledge test even if they trade only Relevant Stablecoins (HKMA circular, footnote 4).
- The first 30 document summaries were also rewritten in plain English. The rest will follow.

## 26 Sep 2026 (later)
- Usability fixes: shorter citation chips; the source panel now closes when you change page, keeps keyboard focus and returns you to where you were; "See in Documents" works for every citation; your document filters are no longer reset by following a citation; the Back button returns you to the same place in a list; clearer status colours (conclusions and targets are amber, not green); a tidier filter panel on phones; a "More" menu on tablets; page titles for bookmarks.

## 26 Sep 2026
- HKDA Brief launched: 21 modules, 15 project profiles and 737 official documents, reflecting publications up to 25 Sep 2026.
- Every line was fact-checked a second time against the official text. This covered all 21 modules (about 1,750 claims, about 800 of them tightened), all 15 project profiles (each checked twice) and all 737 document summaries.
- The main corrections: "must" softened to "should" or "expects" where the source says so; exact paragraph and page references; statuses brought up to date (for example, September 2025 HKMA consultation drafts now show as superseded by the final rules in force from 1 January 2026); and the licensed-platform count fixed at 13 (SFC list, 29 May 2026).
- New tabs: Obligations (checklist), Briefings (talking points by audience), Timeline and Glossary.
