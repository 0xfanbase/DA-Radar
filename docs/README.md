# Design and research

- [`proposal.html`](proposal.html) — the design proposal (also published as a private Claude artifact).
- [`modules/`](modules/) — 21 verified module briefs: the content backbone of the guide. Every claim is
  cited to a source-registry entry and paragraph, and checked by a separate verifier
  ([`modules/verification/`](modules/verification/)).
- [`research/source-registry.jsonl`](research/source-registry.jsonl) — 737 official Hong Kong
  digital-asset publications (HKMA, SFC, FSTB, LegCo/GovHK and others, 2014–September 2026), each
  summarised in our own words with paragraph references and a bank-compliance angle.
- [`research/METHOD.md`](research/METHOD.md) — how the corpus was listed, read and checked, and the
  known gaps.
- [`research/owner_requirements.md`](research/owner_requirements.md) — the owner's requirements.
