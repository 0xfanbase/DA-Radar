# Advice and asset management under the new regimes

A bank can advise clients on virtual assets (VAs) and manage funds or client accounts that hold them, for a fee. (concept)

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |

| Value chain | Advise and manage |

## What the bank does

- Intermediaries, including banks, should give VA advice only to clients of their securities dealing (Type 1) or securities advice (Type 4) business. [S:8a016a912528, para 26] This limit does not apply to a bank that advises only on stablecoins licensed by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA). [S:a0bd48559762, para (7), p.6]
- Before recommending a VA to a retail client, the adviser should check that it is a large, liquid token. [S:8a016a912528, para 28(a)] That means it is in at least two acceptable indices (published lists that track major tokens) from two different index providers. [S:8a016a912528, para 28(a)]
- SFC terms for VA fund managers apply in two cases. A fund states a VA aim, or it plans to hold at least 10% of its total assets in VAs. Total assets here means gross asset value (GAV). [S:40148cf96f41, Part I, de minimis threshold, p.3]
- For retail discretionary accounts (where the manager decides the trades), the manager should trade only large-cap VAs (tokens with a large total market value). [S:40148cf96f41, Schedule 2, para 2] Stablecoins issued by HKMA licensees are exempt from that liquidity test. [S:40148cf96f41, Schedule 2, para 2]
- A registered institution (RI) is a bank registered with the SFC. Holding HKMA-licensed stablecoins, and no other VAs, in the portfolios it manages does not on its own make an RI need an asset management registration. [S:a0bd48559762, para (6), p.5] If a portfolio meets the 10% test above, the SFC's VA manager terms still apply. [S:a0bd48559762, para (6), p.5]
- Under the proposed new regimes, only banks could register with the SFC instead of taking a licence. [S:6186c01c6649, fns 9-10]

## Revenue levers

- A management fee is a yearly charge on the value of assets under management (AUM). (concept)
- A performance fee is a share of gains above an agreed level. (concept)
- An advice fee is paid by the client. A commission or rebate is paid by the product provider, and can create a conflict of interest. (concept)
- For HKMA-licensed stablecoins, a bank should base advice on thorough analysis. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6] It should not use commission rebates as the main reason to recommend one. [S:a0bd48559762, para (8), p.6]
- A licensed VA manager that deals in VAs only to run its portfolios would not need a separate VA dealing licence. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 38] For example, it could accept VAs as fund subscriptions and convert them. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 38]

## Cost and capital drivers

- A VA fund manager should place fund assets with a separate, independent custodian and keep them segregated. [S:40148cf96f41, para 4.1] It should disclose plans to keep over 10% of GAV with one custodian or in self-custody. [S:40148cf96f41, para 4.11]
- A VA fund manager should tell the SFC, and the HKMA for an RI, at least 7 business days before any significant business change. [S:40148cf96f41, para 7.2]
- The new VA management regime is to have no small-amount exception. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 32] So managing even a small VA portfolio for others would need a licence or registration. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 32] Advice on a mixed portfolio of VAs and securities would still need VA advisory licensing or registration for the VA part. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 21]
- Under current SFC fees, an RI pays HK$23,500 to apply and HK$35,000 a year for each of advice and asset management. [S:6186c01c6649, para 67] The May 2026 conclusions tie the new VA fees to those fees. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 59]
- Non-bank licensees holding client assets would need HK$5 million paid-up capital and HK$3 million liquid capital. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 25, 48] Those figures exclude banks. [S:01c5c5c4045f, paras 25, 48]
- Every RI must appoint at least two executive officers (EOs) to supervise each regulated activity directly. [S:8934af8cc884, para 2.2.4]

## Official signals

- Hong Kong's asset and wealth management AUM rose 20% to HK$42,202 billion at end-2025. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 1]
- Assets in private banking and private wealth management rose 24% to HK$12,945 billion at end-2025. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 5] Forty-seven registered institutions (RIs) reported doing that business. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 45]
- The SFC survey cites a study commissioned by Invest Hong Kong, the Government's investment promotion agency. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 30] It counts over 3,380 single-family offices (firms that manage one family's wealth) in Hong Kong at end-2025. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 30] It notes strong interest in digital assets among their alternative investments. [S:6d9deb07299d, para 31]
- The consultation on the VA advisory and management regimes drew 51 responses. [S:4a0342268bf2, Note 2, p.1]
- SFC-licensed firms and registered banks that already give VA advice or management are to get a faster approval process. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 67] The start date is not set yet. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- The Government and the SFC do not plan to treat existing VA advisers or managers as licensed while they apply. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 63] The regimes take full effect on their start date. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 63]
- The Government said one bill covering VA dealing, custody, advisory and management would go to the Legislative Council (LegCo) in 2026. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (3), 'In addition' para]

## Industry benchmarks

- HashKey Holdings Limited (HashKey), a Hong Kong-listed platform group, reported 2025 asset management revenue of HK$117.1 million, up from HK$78.2 million. [I:786f45f6ea11, PDF p.12] It said the rise came mainly from carried interest, a share of fund profits. [I:786f45f6ea11, PDF p.12]
- HashKey's 2025 annual results reported asset management AUM of HK$7.2 billion at end-2025. [I:786f45f6ea11, PDF p.5] These are group figures and include markets outside Hong Kong.
- Bosera Asset Management (International) published a Hong Kong prospectus in March 2026 (reported figures). It sets a current management fee of 0.60% a year for the listed shares of its bitcoin exchange-traded fund (ETF). [I:d81073907a14, PDF p.96]

## Analysis

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** Is VA advice and management a new fee line, or a way to keep private-banking wealth inside the bank?
> **How to think about it:**
> - Fee income is roughly AUM times the fee rate. AUM moves with VA prices, so income moves too.
> - Costs include registration, independent custody, staff with VA skills and extra suitability checks.
> - Once the new regime applies, the VA rules cover even a small VA part of a mixed portfolio.
> - A question to test: do clients who want VA exposure move assets elsewhere when the bank offers none?
> **What it depends on:** client demand; VA prices; the start date; the speed of approval; custody costs.
> **Official signposts:**
> - Faster approval for firms already in the business. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 67]
> - No small-amount exception for VA management. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 32]
> - The bill planned for 2026. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (3), 'In addition' para]
> **What this is not:** not a forecast, not advice and not the regulator's view.
