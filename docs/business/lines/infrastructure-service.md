# Infrastructure as a service

A bank can run, or sell access to, the technology behind digital assets: shared ledgers, token-issuing tools, custody systems and settlement links. (concept)

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Bank role | Platform operator; technology provider; settlement bank; custodian for other firms |
| Client segments | Corporate, institutional, fintechs |
| Value chain | Issue, Hold, Settle and pay |
| Regulatory gate | Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) expectations on distributed ledger technology (DLT) and custody. The HKMA also expects banks to discuss new digital-asset activities with it before launch [S:1884530ff235, Annex, items 1-4 and 8; S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] |
| Status as of 25 Sep 2026 | Pilot [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3] |
| Related modules | B3, C3, C5, D4, E2 |
| Related cases | 4-vatp-incident |

## What the bank does

- A bank can test DLT projects with a dedicated HKMA team in the Supervisory Incubator. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] The HKMA may give some supervisory flexibility, decided case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1 and fn 2]
- In the Ensemble Sandbox, banks connected their own tokenised deposit platforms to an HKMA settlement set-up. [S:460620ab7f2b, para 2] A tokenised deposit is a bank deposit recorded as a token on a shared ledger. (concept)
- As a general principle, a bank may hand virtual-asset (VA) custody to only three kinds of firm. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14] The first is another bank, or a subsidiary of a locally incorporated bank. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14] The second is a VA trading platform holding a Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) licence. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14] The third is an HKMA-licensed stablecoin issuer, for its own coins and with HKMA consent. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14]
- Issuing on a DLT platform run by the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU) is one route into the Digital Bond Grant Scheme (DBGS). [S:5a0563de8142, s.2 and fn 6, p.3]
- The HKMA formed the Ensemble Architecture Community in May 2024 to write industry standards. [S:0169bc8fbb4d, paras 1-2] The standards aim to link wholesale central bank digital currency (CBDC), tokenised money and tokenised assets. [S:0169bc8fbb4d, para 2]

## Revenue levers

- Software as a service (SaaS) means a client rents a platform instead of building its own, often for a base fee plus a usage fee. (concept)
- Other banks can outsource VA custody to a bank, which makes custody a service one bank can sell to another. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 14]
- DBGS grants can repay issuers' fees to DLT platform providers that are not the issuer's associates (firms linked to the issuer). [S:5a0563de8142, s.4(a), p.5] The full grant, up to HK$2.5 million per issue, needs such an outside platform, among other conditions. [S:5a0563de8142, s.3(a), p.4]
- A settlement bank can charge for moving tokenised money between parties, or keep the balances that clients hold with it. (concept)

## Cost and capital drivers

- The HKMA's DLT note puts responsibility on the board and senior management. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, items 1-4 and 8] It covers network choice, smart contracts, settlement finality and key security. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, items 1-4 and 8]
- A hardware security module (HSM) is a tamper-resistant device that stores keys. (concept) Banks should check HSM vendors before onboarding and review them regularly. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 11(a)] A bank's custody monitoring should cover vendors, blockchain protocols, encryption methods and shared code libraries. [S:ce05e67bcdce, para 24]
- For tokenised products, banks should check issuers' technology, including smart-contract audits, key management and plans for ledger failure. [S:b2719f3dcb37, Section A, pp.3-4]
- For SFC-authorised tokenised products, the product provider stays responsible for the token set-up even if it outsources it. [S:58ea8520974d, para 10] On SFC request, it should get an outside audit of the set-up and its smart contracts. [S:58ea8520974d, para 15]
- The HKMA has launched a Quantum Preparedness Index to guide the industry on upgrading encryption. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 51(ii)]
- The HKMA plans a Fintech Cybersecurity Baseline for technology firms that serve banks with DLT and artificial intelligence tools. [S:b0607b779270, paras 3-6]

## Official signals

- EnsembleTX (the live pilot of the HKMA's Project Ensemble) is to run throughout 2026. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3] It is to move step by step to round-the-clock settlement in tokenised central bank money. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]
- EnsembleTX named 13 other industry pioneers besides its banks, including asset managers, technology firms and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing (HKEX). [S:d193f12a5f78, Annex B, p.2]
- In November 2025 the HKMA announced that HKEX would take a 20% stake in the holding company of CMU OmniClear (the CMU's operator). [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 2] The Exchange Fund would hold 80%. [S:9c8855ec55a1, para 2] Assets held in the CMU were about HK$5 trillion at end-September 2025. [S:9c8855ec55a1, About CMU OmniClear section]
- CMU OmniClear is to set up a digital asset platform in 2026 for issuing and settling digital bonds. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114] It is to be widened to other digital assets and linked to other platforms in the region. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114]
- HKEX is building a blockchain-based tokenisation platform for many asset types, which already holds carbon credits. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 46(v)]
- In an HKMA stock-take run in January 2025 and published in July 2025, 45% of respondents had adopted DLT, up from 30% in 2022. [S:738405ae090d, p.11]

## Industry benchmarks

- OSL Group Limited (OSL) is a Hong Kong-listed platform group. It reported 2025 SaaS and related income of HK$133.1 million, against HK$91.6 million in 2024. [I:e2df03a8c8a6, PDF p.126, Note 5] These are group figures, not Hong Kong only.
- OSL's 2025 annual report says its SaaS fees are typically based on a base fee plus the trading volume of the client's platform. [I:e2df03a8c8a6, PDF p.91, Note 2.2.3]
- KPMG (a professional services firm) published a 2026 Hong Kong banking report through KPMG China. [I:1dfc7a0205ff, p.42] Its commentary says spending on technology, talent and cybersecurity can look like cost rather than value in the short term. [I:1dfc7a0205ff, p.42] KPMG sells advisory services to banks.

## Analysis

> **Analysis — not official**
> **Question:** When does running shared digital-asset infrastructure pay back for a bank, compared with renting it?
> **How to think about it:**
> - Building a platform has a high fixed cost and a low cost per extra client. So the number of clients decides whether it pays back.
> - Public platforms, such as the planned CMU OmniClear platform, can compete with a bank platform or connect to it.
> - Clients that rent the platform stay answerable to their own regulators. How much due diligence and audit work then comes with each sale?
> - Renting avoids the build cost but leaves the bank reliant on a vendor that then needs close monitoring.
> **What it depends on:** the number of client firms; standards that let platforms connect; vendor and audit costs; how liability for failures is shared.
> **Official signposts:**
> - The CMU OmniClear digital asset platform. [S:654fa5eba8cf, para 114]
> - Round-the-clock settlement in EnsembleTX. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 3]
> - Standards from the Ensemble Architecture Community that let platforms connect (interoperability). [S:3f0fcc104e61, Reply (3)]
> **What this is not:** not a forecast, not advice and not the regulator's view.
