# Case 5: The board's strategic plan

*Fictional case. All people and firms are invented. Facts about Hong Kong rules are real and cited.*

## The situation

On 9 March 2026 the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) wrote to every authorized institution (a licensed bank or deposit-taker). [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.1] It expects each board to oversee and endorse a formal strategic business plan within six months. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The plan answers technology change, including distributed ledger technology (DLT), a shared digital record kept across many computers. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2]

Lanternfield Bank is a fictional, mid-sized Hong Kong bank. It is also a registered institution (RI): a bank registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) to carry on securities business. You are its head of digital assets. The chief executive asks you to write the plan. The board meets in August, before the six-month mark.

## Cast

- **You**: head of digital assets, formerly head of compliance for digital assets.
- **Ada Quillon**: chief executive (CEO). She wants a plan the board can act on.
- **Ravi Tessel**: chief financial officer (CFO). He owns the budget and the profit and loss account (P&L), the record of income and costs.
- **Mona Brackwater**: chief risk officer (CRO). She owns the risk appetite, the amount of risk the bank is willing to accept.
- **Leo Fenwick**: head of wealth and markets. He wants new products this year.
- **Tidewell Ledger Systems**: a fictional vendor selling a ready-made token platform.
- **The supervisor**: the bank's HKMA case team. It acts only as the HKMA's published expectations describe.

## Exhibit A: the rules that apply

- The plan should look for chances to change products, revenue models, customer engagement, risk management and operations. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The bank may build in small, real-world trials through the HKMA's Supervisory Incubator for DLT. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The HKMA says it will invite some banks to send their plans through its Survey Tool. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- A bank adding new digital-asset activities is still expected to discuss them with the HKMA before launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- The board and senior management should understand firm-wide risks, especially from new activities such as cryptoassets. [S:349a543ae2f0, para 4.2.8]
- The HKMA expects the board and senior management to take full responsibility for DLT adoption and its risks. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, item 1, p.2]
- An RI should tell the SFC and the HKMA before it starts or changes any virtual-asset (VA) activity, including the client types it serves. [S:8934af8cc884, para 5.6]

## Act 1: What is the plan for?

At the first working meeting, views split. Leo wants a digital-assets product plan. Mona wants a risk document. Ada notes that the circular also names agentic artificial intelligence, cloud and high-performance computing. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.1]

### Decision 1: How wide does the plan reach?

- **A.** A bank-wide plan covering every technology the circular names, with digital assets and DLT as one chapter.
- **B.** A plan for digital assets and DLT only, owned by your team.
- **C.** A short statement of principles now, with a detailed plan after new VA laws pass.

### Discussion notes

- **Trade-offs.** Option A spreads ownership across many business heads, which is slower to write. Option B is faster and deeper, but covers fewer technologies than the circular names. Option C saves effort now and leaves the detail open.
- **What the rules require.** The HKMA expects a formal plan, endorsed by the board within six months. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The circular frames the plan around products, revenue models, customer engagement, risk management and operations. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] The circular also describes banks as a potential link between traditional finance and digital-asset markets. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, pp.1-2]
- **What a strong answer covers.** It shows how each option maps to the five areas. It says who owns each chapter and how the board tracks progress. It explains how a principles paper would meet the word "formal".
- **Traps.** Treating endorsement as a one-off signature. Treating the plan as product approval: pre-launch discussion with the HKMA is still expected. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] Assuming the six-month timetable waits for new laws. The circular counts six months from its own date. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2] Separately, the Government said in June 2026 that a VA bill would go to the Legislative Council (LegCo) this year. [S:c6df52e680de, Reply (3)]

## Act 2: Which lines lead?

Ravi asks for no more than three priority lines. Tidewell offers a platform discount if the bank signs by June. You shortlist three candidates.

### Decision 2: Which business line leads the plan?

- **A.** Tokenised funds: selling tokenised fund units and settling trades with tokenised deposits (bank deposits recorded as tokens).
- **B.** Digital bonds: arranging, selling and holding bonds recorded on DLT.
- **C.** VA advice and discretionary management (managing clients' VA portfolios for them), once the new SFC regimes start.

### Discussion notes

- **Trade-offs.** Option A builds on existing fund sales and the deposit base. Option B earns issuer fees and suits corporate clients. Option C is closest to wealth clients but depends on laws not yet passed.
- **What official sources say (A).** The SFC expects sellers of tokenised SFC-authorised products to be regulated firms that check each client's suitability. [S:58ea8520974d, para 18] By March 2026 the public could buy 13 tokenised products, whose tokenised classes held about HK$10.7 billion. [S:bbd10edd41cf, para 3] EnsembleTX (the HKMA's live pilot for tokenised deposits) first targets tokenised money market fund trades. [S:8b67e8dc510f, para 2]
- **What official sources say (B).** The 2026 Policy Address says Hong Kong-issued digital bonds made up nearly 50% of the global market. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35] That share covers the period from January 2025 to June 2026. [S:ef3648ff1610, para 35] The Digital Bond Grant Scheme offers up to HK$2.5 million per eligible issue. [S:5a0563de8142, introduction, p.1] The SFC reminds intermediaries to check the issuer and its tokenisation vendors. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 17]
- **What official sources say (C).** Under the December 2025 proposals, VA advisers would need an SFC licence or registration, and only banks could register. [S:6186c01c6649, para 52 and fn 9] In May 2026, the Government and the SFC said they would still consider the start date for the advisory and management regimes. [S:01c5c5c4045f, para 64]
- **What a strong answer covers.** It links each line to a named client need. It separates confirmed rules from proposals. It shows which line the bank could start under rules in force today. It tests the vendor deadline against the board timetable.
- **Traps.** Letting a vendor discount set the order of priorities. Counting grant money as recurring income. Treating consultation conclusions as law.

## Act 3: How to test, and what to tell the supervisor

The board draft is nearly ready. Mona asks how the bank proves its controls work before a full launch. Ada asks what the supervisor sees, and when.

### Decision 3: How does the plan move from paper to practice?

- **A.** Apply to the Supervisory Incubator and run a small live trial first.
- **B.** Hold the pre-launch discussion with the HKMA, notify the SFC, and plan a full launch.
- **C.** Endorse the plan with no launch in its first year, and review it when the VA bill passes.

### Discussion notes

- **Trade-offs.** Option A adds time but tests controls on a small scale. Option B is faster to revenue and relies on controls being ready on day one. Option C keeps cost low and leaves the plan's actions thin.
- **What the rules require.** The Incubator gives banks a dedicated HKMA team for feedback and the option of live trials. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] The HKMA assesses any supervisory flexibility case by case. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] Its factors include the trial's scale, scope and nature. [S:fae36c380174, p.1] Tokenised deposits were a core focus when the Incubator opened. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 2] The HKMA expects a bank to get its feedback on risk controls before launching VA products. [S:642680fa4467, AIs' VA-related proposals section, p.5] Before tokenised securities work, a registered institution should notify the SFC and the HKMA. [S:a9aa06fbcc12, para 35 and fn 8]
- **What a strong answer covers.** It sets trial limits: client numbers, amounts and an exit plan. It names the executive who reports trial results to the board. It prepares the plan for a possible Survey Tool request. It updates technology, continuity and outsourcing policies where DLT changes them. [S:1884530ff235, Annex, item 1, p.2]
- **Traps.** Reading Incubator entry as approval to launch. Reading case-by-case flexibility as a waiver of rules. Forgetting that the HKMA may invite the bank to submit the plan itself. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]

## What this case teaches

- The March 2026 circular asks for a board-owned plan, not a product launch. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- Endorsing a plan does not replace pre-launch discussion with the HKMA. [S:3ec5b269b5dc, letter body, p.2]
- A plan that separates rules in force from proposals shows the board which priorities could start under today's rules (concept).
- Incubator trials let a bank check its risk controls on a small scale before a full launch. [S:1b1a0de5de5a, para 3]
- Commercial choices look different when deposit income, fees and control costs sit side by side (concept).
