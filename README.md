# HKDA Brief

A private learning guide to Hong Kong digital-asset regulation for a bank's compliance function:
HKMA first, the SFC where its rules reach banks, and FSTB policy and legislation where relevant.

**Status:** design approved in principle (September 2026). The site is hosted privately by Claude and kept current by a scheduled Claude routine; this repository is its private storage and history. The design proposal, research registry and module briefs are in [`docs/`](docs/).
The earlier multi-jurisdiction "Global Digital Asset Radar" is preserved on the
`archive/global-da-radar-2026-09-25` branch.

Content is for general information only and is not legal or regulatory advice.
